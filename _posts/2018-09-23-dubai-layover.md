Quick update. I spent the day in Chicago, went on a bike ride with my mom in the morning and then met my grandparents for lunch and dinner and hung out until my flight which left Chicago around 8:45. Here's everything I packed, I tried to keep it to just one backpack but that proved to be impossible. Thankfully I was able to borrow Jamie's backpack to fit everything and also be stylin (it's purple).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1059.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-827" height="4032" width="3024">

The airplane was a 777-300 and the interior was decked out I had a window seat and no one sitting next to me so I was able to spread out and get comfortable. The flight to Dubai was 13.5 hours which left plenty of time to watch movies. I watched Altered carbon, Breaking and exiting, Tomb raider, True grit, and Gringo. Surprisingly I was able to get some sleep which I usually can't do on an airplane.

We arrived in Dubai around 6:30 pm and had an 18 hour layover until our next flight. Of course as we're going through immigration Patryk realized he forgot his kindle and charger on the plane. He went back to try to see if he could get it before the plane left but didn't have any luck. Hopefully he can get it on the way back. We left the airport and made our way to the hotel by way of the metro. It became immediately apparent this place was in he desert...it was hot! I'd like to think I'm used to the heat after living in Savannah for so long but this was a whole other level. The sun was down and it was still 96 degrees outside!

The hotel was surprisingly nice. We checked in and left our bags in the room to head to downtown Dubai. Unfortunately the Uber helicopter wasn't available so we had to settle for a regular Uber. Our first stop was the Dubai mall. It was filled with a ton of shops (surprisingly very westernized). The mall was massive and seemingly built as a maze to keep you inside as long as possible. We made our way through the mall to the outside promenade which had awesome views of the Burj kahlifa (currently the tallest building in the world). The place reminded me of a mix between Las Vegas and Epcot. The man made lake right in front of the Burj had a pretty cool water fountain show every 30 min accompanied by a light show on the buildings.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2263.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-828" height="1932" width="2576">

We hung outside for ~ a half hour at a cafe and had some smoothies. Afterwards we made our way back to the hotel.

The next day we booked tickets to go up to the top of the Burj Khalifa. The hotel provided breakfast which was surprisingly awesome. It was a buffet with tons of different options. After we made our way by under back to the Burj. The observation deck was outside and it was a relatively clear day so there were some great views of the city. It really does look like an oasis in the desert.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2266.jpg" class="wp-image-829 size-full" height="1932" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2277.jpg" class="wp-image-830 size-full" height="3024" width="4032">

Afterwards we made our way back to the hotel and chilled before catching the metro back tor the airport. What we didn't realize was that this time we were flying out of terminal 2 which is on the complete other side of the airport so we had to take another taxi to get there. On top of that going through security the tried to confiscate my hiking poles even though I didn't have any issues getting them on the previous flight. I had to convince the police officer that they were harmless. Luckily he let me keep them.

The plane to Kilimanjaro was the opposite of the previous flight. It was super old and even though it had TVs they charged you to watch movies. We also realized that no food or snacks were provided on the flight...what a ripoff! To top it off we ended up having to fly to dar es salaam first and then onto Kilimanjaro so the whole trip took ~8 hours.

We eventually made it and met up with our driver outside the airport. It was about a 45 min drive to where we're staying. We got in around 10 at night so I'm pretty exhausted. The hike starts tomorrow at 9am!
