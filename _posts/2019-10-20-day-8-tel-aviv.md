Today we woke up around 8 and got our stuff packed up to head out to Tel Aviv. Since today is Saturday (day of rest) the buses were not running...We didn't plan for this and even Google had it wrong saying the buses were running. I'm not sure how they overlooked that since it's literally every week that they shut down. We walked around about a mile to try and find one of the unofficial busses called a shurut that we read about. It's supposedly a van they fill up with people to take to Tel Aviv. We were unsuccessful in finding one so we ended up just taking a taxi. Tel Aviv was about a 45min drive from Jerusalem.

The taxi dropped us of at our hostel around 10. The hostel was super packed and had a cool vibe. Check in wasn't until 2 so we dropped off our luggage and had breakfast. Breakfast wasn't much just bread and hummus and veggies. After eating we walked down to beach which was a 15 min walk and hung out till 11. At 11 we went to the nearby bell tower in old Jafa to meet up with a free walking tour our hostel told us about. The walking tour went through the old city of Jafa. One of the interesting stories was the clock tower, it was donated to the city by one of its citizens who apparently owned the only clock in town and up until that point everyone constantly asked him what time it was. Naturally he got tired of this so he donated the clock tower to the city so that no one would ask him for the time again 😂. We also walked through some gardens which had a good view of the city and the coastline.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2983.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1483" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2984.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1484" width="3088" height="2320">

From the gardens we went over the "wishing bridge" towards the port. Legend has it that if you stand by your zodiac symbol (carved into the rail of the bridge) and look at the sea and make a wish it will come true. I'll let you know if it works! We finished up the tour at the port. There was also a really weird orange tree that was suspended by wires that we stopped to see. Apparently oranges were a big thing in Tel Aviv, the tour guide wasn't very engaging and it was hard to hear some of the details of the stories.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2985.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1485" width="3024" height="4032">

After the tour we walked back to the dog beach to hang out and relax. There were a ton of dogs at the beach. Israel is actually super dog friendly so you see dogs walking around almost everywhere you go, makes me miss having a dog! Sam decided it'd be fun to dig a hole at the beach so he spent a good hour digging, this attracted the attention of a few dogs who stopped by to try and help.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191019_133622_original.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1486" width="2592" height="1458"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/8ffb8f53-258b-48ff-a122-426086cf0b19.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1487" width="2160" height="3840">

We stayed at the beach for a few hours and then went to a shawarma place nearby that was recommended by Jamie's coworker. The shawarma was good as usual!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191019_161724_original.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1488" width="2592" height="1458">

After eating we walked back to the hostel to checkin and rest for a bit. We hung out at the hostel till dinner and then left to go to a nearby restaurant called Florentine 10. I decided to change it up and have a burger and it turned out to be much better than I expected!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191019_193914_original.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1489" width="2592" height="1458">

After dinner we went back to the hostel for happy hour. The hostel was a mostly younger crowd and definitely the party atmosphere. We met some cool people from Germany and the Netherlands that we hung out with for a while. After happy hour was over a huge group went to go out to the bar. We decided to go with but it took forever to walk there. The first stop was a bar with cheap drinks. We stayed for a little while and then ended up heading back to the hostel.

I really didn't want to walk all the way back so we decided to go back on electric scooters. For some reason Jamie's app wasn't working and I was the only one able to get a scooter so we decided to share. This turned out to be a little more difficult than we first anticipated and we totally crashed within the first minute 😂. That didn't stop us though, we tried again and we're able to make it back to the hostel in one piece
