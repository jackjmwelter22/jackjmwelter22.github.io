<p>Today we had a slow morning, we ate in the room, packed everything into the car, and left around 9. We were heading down to stay at the south end of the island!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>On the way to the south we stopped to do a little hike called Hoopii falls. The hike is in a neighborhood and you have to park along the side of the street. There was a cute trail dog who helped us find the start of the hike. </p>

<p>We ate the food at the picnic area by the beach being careful to not let any chickens steal it. I don’t think I mentioned this yet but Kauai is absolutely infested with chickens. They’re seriously everywhere - at the beach’s, on all the trails - and they aren’t afraid of humans at all.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The food was good after eating we went down to the beach which was fairly small and much more crowded than all the other beaches we had been to so far. There was also another sleeping seal on the beach, this one was roped off by volunteers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://i.imgur.com/hooSmpY.jpeg","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"embed-handler","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We learned that these are monk seals and there are only about 1600 of them currently (their population is growing). They come up to the beach during the day to sleep and rest for around 10 hours and then they forage and hunt at night. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On the other side of the beach were a bunch of turtles who do the opposite, they come up to the beach to sleep at night. There were only two turtles when we first got to the beach but more and more kept coming up. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up laying out by the turtles and relaxing. The beach was in a little bay/cove which made it good for snorkeling. Becca and I each took a turn snorkeling. There wasn’t a whole lot to see except for the big turtles. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We relaxed at the beach until 4 and then drove to our VRBO, the Ashton at Poipu, which was only a minute away. We checked in and got our keys and unit number and then drove to the condo. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This VRBO was so MUCH nicer than where we stayed in the north. It was huge, there was a full kitchen, living room, two beds, and two baths and most importantly there were blinds :D. After settling in we decided to go walk around. There were two different pools we could use. One was much more secluded and the other was bigger and had a hot tub! We also found out that the complex is connected to the berneccke beach and poipu beach by a nice walking trail that was only a few blocks away.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After exploring the resort area we decided to make a quick run to the Big Save grocery store for some snacks and dinner. After driving back and stocking the refrigerator we walked down to poipu beach to watch the sunset by the seal.</p>
