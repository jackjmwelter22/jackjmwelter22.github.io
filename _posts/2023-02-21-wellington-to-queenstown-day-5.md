<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 5am, ate a quick breakfast and then hit the road. The drive to Wellington was about 4 hours. Unfortunately we had to go to Wellington because there wasn’t any closer airports besides Rotorua (2.5hrs) however Rotorua didn’t have an enterprise car rental so we were stuck with Wellington. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The drive was long but the views were scenic. I was pretty exhausted, the bed in our Airbnb was terrible so I didn’t get much rest. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We arrived in Wellington around 9:45a. We passed by the New Zealand parliament building (its a circular looking building not that special) and made our way to mount Victoria lookout which had some great views of the city as well as the airport. </p>

<p>After some pictures we drove down to Weta cave. Weta is a famous workshop in New Zealand that builds lots of movie props, costumes, and sets. They are basically the best movie prop creation company and have been used by tons of massively popular films. Unfortunately all the tours of the actual workshop were sold out so we just stopped at the “cave” where they had a mini museum with lots of movie memorabilia. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next we drove to a brewery called parrot dog (Wellington us known for its craft beer). Unfortunately the brewery was closed so we walked down to the coast where there was a dog beach and surfers. After walking down the beach we stopped at a cafe called the spruce goose. I had a chocolate milkshake. We watched some airplanes land and take off until it was time to drive to the airport. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unfortunately after dropping off the car I realized I had left my sunglasses in the car. I ran back quickly to try and get them but the car was gone and no one from enterprise was around or answering the phone :(. Luckily the glasses were super old. We took our air New Zealand flight to queenstown and landed around 3:30. We got the car and drove about 15 min to our Airbnb. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It just so happened that Nate and Jamie were staying in Queenstown the same time as us and I had no clue until the day before. Jamie has been traveling for a few weeks before returning to work in the US. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up meeting up with Jamie and Nate around 5p after getting settled in our Airbnb. Since our Airbnb had frisbees we decided to try out the local disc golf course. The course was fun and on a peninsula in the lake which meant good views but also that it was super windy! Even though most holes were 100m or less it was really difficult with the wind…or at least that’s my excuse! We ended up playing 11 holes before deciding to call it quits and get some dinner. </p>

<p>We went to a “famous” burger place called Fergburger. The line was super long we had to put our order in (I got the ferg deluxe) and then wait ~20 min. Once we all got our burgers we walked back toward the pier and sat down to eat. The burgers definitely lived up to the hype! They were delicious!</p>

<p>After dinner we had some ice cream at a nearby shop for Becca’s birthday today! and then we went back to our Airbnb to play some board games with everyone. We played some 9 tiles panic (highly recommend) then a new game called floatsam (not that fun), and finally a game called cat in a box (pat didn’t remember all the rules so we didn’t really play this one so tbd if it’s good). Overall we had a great day, it was really fun to catch up with Nate and Jamie!</p>
