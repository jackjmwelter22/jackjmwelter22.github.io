<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up and had breakfast at 7:30. It was the same breakfast as the day before. After eating we got packed and loaded up into the van to start another longgg day of driving (8 hours in total). We drove for about an hour and a half before stopping in the town of ifran, which is a “Swiss themed town” it really wasn’t much. We basically just stopped for a quick bathroom break and then continued on. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next stop was a little further along just on the side of the road there were tons of monkeys that you could feed peanuts to. They were really funny and really liked the peanuts!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the monkeys we stopped in a town about halfway for lunch. There wasn’t much notable about the town. I had a “taco” for lunch but it was really more like a mix between a burrito and a panini. It tasted good though!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2286,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0467.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2286" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch we continued on and made one final stop before our destination for another toilet break in the town of Edfu. Known as the gateway to the Sahara. We drove another hour from there to Merzouga where we proceeded on camel into the desert. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The desert was really cool with two MASSIVE dunes. We stopped the camels about halfway to camp to watch the sunset and sand board down the dune. The dune was tiny so the sand boarding wasn’t that great but it was still fun. We got a ton of cool pictures with the camels and the sunset. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After sunset we continued on the camels to the camp. We had some tea and snacks and then dropped our luggage off in our tents. The tents are really nice and all have their own bathrooms in them. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2290,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0615.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2290" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Afterwards we had dinner in the main dinning tent. It was a bunch of different dishes with the main one being chicken. After dinner we sat by the campfire while our hosts played a variety of drums and instruments. It was a good time they were all super into it and made us dance along. After the festivities we walked a little outside of camp to look at the stars. There was lighting in the distance which was added a cool effect. </p>
