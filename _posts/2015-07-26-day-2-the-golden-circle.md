<div style="text-align:justify;">Today we woke up around 7:30. I felt a lot better finally getting some rest. We ate breakfast in the hostel downstairs. I ended up getting a free breakfast because jake paid for 2 tickets but the guy accidentally gave him 3. Breakfast was a small buffet, I had toast and cereals and also made a to-go sandwhich to eat for lunch later.</div>
<div style="text-align:justify;"></div>
<div style="text-align:justify;">After eating we checked out of the hostel and got on the road to begin our tour of the golden circle. The golden circle is basically a popular tourist loop that goes to a couple main sites.</div>
