Today was another early day, Jamie woke up around 7am to get some breakfast in the lobby of our hostel. I was definitely feeling the baijiu and lack of sleep from night before! Today we were going to see an unrestored section of the Great Wall and hike more of it. Our driver came and picked us up at our hotel around 7:30.

It took about 2 and a half hours to get to the start of our hike. It was at the base of this mountain. Our driver, who spoke essentially no English got out of the car and motioned for us to follow him down this path through the dense trees and up the mountain. As we started we noticed a sign that said "this section of the wall is closed to the public"... yes, I already knew this was going to be cool!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1798.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-752" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1794.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-753" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1804.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-754" width="3024">

The area was very remote. We followed our driver/guide for about an hour up the mountain. He was very out of breath during the hike and we had to stop a few times to rest (kinda weird because I figure he does this all the time). After about an hour we finally made it to the top where jiankou tower was.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1819.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-756" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1829.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-755" width="2576">

Because this section of the wall was unrestored it was partly grown over by bushes. It was really cool to see the effects of nature over time. We hiked from Jiankou all the way to Mutianyu (a popular section of the wall). It was cool to see the wall get more and more restored the farther we hiked.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1842.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-758" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1844.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-759" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1848.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-757" width="2576">

Certain sections of the wall were super steep! And because it wasn't restored there were a ton of loose stones so you had to be really careful not to trip!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1857.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-760" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1859.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-763" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1865.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-762" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1871.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-761" width="4032">

After probably 2 hours of hiking through bushes we finally made it to the more restored area of the wall! There were a ton more people at this section.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1890.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-764" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1898.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-765" width="3024">

In total I think we hiked around 6 miles to get to the end of the wall and about 4.5 hours. It's still incredible to think that they built all of it so long ago. In order to get all the way down we took a tobaggon slide! It was a ton of fun! The people in front of us were slow though so we had to keep slowing down and stopping :(.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1915.jpg" height="2576" class="wp-image-766" width="1932"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1921.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-767" width="3024">

At the bottom of the slide we took a short shuttle ride down to the town where our driver was waiting for us. We stopped at a restaurant in the town that our driver recommended. We were starving so we ordered way too much food! Some dumplings, chicken, and fried rice. It was all super delicious.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1922.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-768" width="4032">

After lunch we made the drive back to Beijing. The traffic in the city was pretty bad so it took a little longer. It was kinda funny because our driver kept giving us drinks. I think he gave each of us about 5 waters, a coke, and iced tea 😂.

Once we got back to the hostel we rested for a couple hours. Once it got dark we walked to the Hutongs to walk through some of the shops there. We also got some froyo (I think it's better in the states honestly haha). After walking around for a while we took a short metro ride up to the Olympic park.

It was really cool to see the birds nest and water cube all lit up!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1925.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-769" width="2576">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1926.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-771" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1929.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-770" width="2576">

The whole area was massive and very crowded. We also saw the wall that listed out all the medal winners from the Beijing Olympics.

After walking around we made our way back to the hostel. We leave tomorrow around lunch!</p>
