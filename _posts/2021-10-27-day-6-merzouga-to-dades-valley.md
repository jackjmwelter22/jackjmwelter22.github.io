<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up at 7 and walked a few minutes outside of our camp to watch the sunrise at 7:30. The sunrise was really nice. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2295,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0640.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2295" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It was a bit chilly in the morning but was warming up quickly. After the sunrise we had breakfast in the dining tent, which consisted of a bunch of pastries and an omelet again. After eating we got packed up and then took the camels through the desert back to the car (about an hour away). Of course we took more pictures with the camels on the way. Jamie, myself, and Becca were on the back of the line and at one point along the way Jamie’s camel (along with ours) came detached from the group. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2296,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_1699.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2296" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Back at the van we loaded up our luggage and then drove to another nearby town to ride dune buggies in the dunes. The ride was pretty fun but they made you follow the guide of course who went kind of slow. I got yelled at a few times to slow down going over the dunes. On the top of the dunes we took a bunch of pictures. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2297,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0718.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2297" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0793.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2298" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Becca drove for a little while to the top of another dune and then our buggy broke down. The whole tour you could tell it was struggling to stay running and it finally gave out on the side of a dune. They ended up having to call someone to bring us another buggy. They were definitely holding out on us because the replacement buggy was wayyyy nicer and more powerful. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After dune buggying we got back in the van and drove about an hour and a half to stop for lunch. The food was okay I had a chicken kebab, Becca had a chicken tagine that wasn’t very good. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch we got back in the van again to continue on to our next stop the Tondra valley gorge. We stopped at two panorámica of the city that had really cool views of the belly that was lush with palm trees and other greenery. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2305,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0850.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2305" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After a few pictures we made our way into the canyon and we’re dropped off to walk through it. The walls of the gorge were huge and kind of reminded me of the narrows in Zion. Unfortunately the weather went from really sunny in the desert to raining so the walk was a little cold and wet. Near the end of the gorge there was a cool built in climbing trail up the rock face. I climbed a little way up but no one else wanted to go so I went back down. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2306,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0864.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-2306" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After our little walk through the gorge we jumped back into the van and drove another hour to the dades gorge where we were staying the night. The drive was a bit slower, because of all the rain the roads were getting washed out by flash flooding. At a few points it was almost like we were driving through a river!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2307,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0867.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2307" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our hotel had a nice view of The Valley. We had the customary tea and then got settled into our rooms. Dinner was around 8:30 and was a few courses consisting of soup with bread, meat rice and vegetable platter, and fruit. The meat was really good! After dinner we talked with Siad about the plans for tomorrow and then went to sleep. </p>
