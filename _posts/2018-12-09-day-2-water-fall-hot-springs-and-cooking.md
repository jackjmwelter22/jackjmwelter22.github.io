Today we woke up around 8 and went to eat breakfast at a nearby restaurant. I had an omelet and fruit. After breakfast pat and I went to go try and hike an old volcano called Cerro Chato however we were turned back by the guy at the start of the trail because it's technically illegal to hike and supposedly the park rangers were on the mountain stopping people. I was pretty sad we weren't able to hike it 😢. Disheartened we drove back to the hotel and met up with the girls to go do a sloth tour.

It was cool to see the sloths but I probably could've just spent 15min there...not the full hour and a half the tour lasted. i wouldn't recommend unless you're really into sloths.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1552.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-968" width="3024" height="4032">

Sloth chillin in the tree.

After the tour we got our bathing suits and went to the La Fortuna water fall. To get there you hike down about 500 steps. It was an incredible view on the way down. Once we got there we jumped in, the water was freezing! I tried swimming as close to the waterfall as I could but the current was incredibly strong.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1558.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-972" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1565.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-969" width="3024" height="4032">

We dried off and made our way all the way back up to the top and decided to drive over to the hot springs. Instead of paying $70+ to go to a fancy "spa" hot spring we decided to try and find the free hot spring the locals go to. We drove about 20 min away from the city and found a spot with a ton of cars parked on the side of the road so we figured we were close. While parking I nearly flipped the car because of how steep the side of the road was 😳. <img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1568.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-973" width="3024" height="4032">

We walked down the road a ways and quickly found the spot. It was just off the side of the road down a bit of a hill. You had to got through a cut portion of a barbed wire fence to get to the water. It was worth it though! The water was nice and warm and it was really relaxing! We hung out there for a little and then drove back to our hotel.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1572.jpg" class="wp-image-975 size-full" width="3024" height="4032">

Once at the hotel we got ready to get picked up for our cooking lesson. We drove to a house that was actually really close to where we did the canyoning yesterday. The house was on a little farm that had all kinds of animals and things on it. We got a little tour and saw monkeys, emus, turkeys, ducks, bulls, goats, and some other weird little rodent thing I'm not sure what it was. After the tour we began our lesson. We learned how to make a traditional dish that consisted of tortillas, chicken, rice and beans, and a mix of veggies. I was already a really good chef according to the lady so I had to help out the others 😜. The meal ended up not being half bad!

After the meal we returned to our hotel and then went down to the main city to eat ice cream and play a board game. Afterwards we went back to the hotel to sleep. Tomorrow we head out to Monteverde.
