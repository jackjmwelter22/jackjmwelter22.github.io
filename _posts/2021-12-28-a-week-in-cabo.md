<p>Little delay in posting this but better late than never! After hanging out with my Family for Christmas 2021 I went down and met Becca and her family in Cabo. Here’s a quick recap of the trip:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 1 </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Luckily I was feeling fine and tested negative for Covid twice after my sister tested positive for Covid on Christmas day and potentially being exposed. My dad drove me to the airport in the morning (it was somewhat busy, but with precheck I got through security in two minutes). The flight from Chicago to Cabo San Lucas was direct which was nice and only took about four hours and 45 minutes. Luckily the flight went off without a hitch, a ton of other flights were getting canceled because of airline employees coming down with Covid. I arrived in Cabo around 12:30 PM Becca was already down here with her family so she came and picked me up from the airport. She drove us about an hour back to the Airbnb in Todos Santos, north of Cabo. The Airbnb was really nice however we had to quarantine from everyone else because I was exposed. It ended up working out nicely because we got our own private room. At the Airbnb I took a quick dip in the pool which was really cold and then we walked to the beach which was literally 100 feet away. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<p>At the beach I went for a quick 3 mile run to see the north end of the beach where there were a bunch of shops and surfers. On my run back I met back up with Becca and we walked back to the Airbnb. We then drove to a beach farther north that’s a popular surf spot for professionals we watched the surfers and the sunset and had a toast with Becca‘s family for our engagement. The sunset had some really great views. </p>

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<p>After the sunset we drove back to the Airbnb and had leftover prime rib rice and green beans for dinner Becca‘s family had this for Christmas dinner  The day before. After dinner we hung out in our room and then went to sleep.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 2</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Woke up and went for early morning run on the beach, this time I ran south and the sand was thick! I ran for about 4 1/2 miles so I was really tired after. I took a quick dip in the pool at the Airbnb to cool off. After, we packed up the car to drive out to a hike. On the way we stopped at cafe elix for breakfast. I had a breakfast burrito strawberry smoothie it was really good. </p>

The hike was really fun it was probably about 5 miles in total along the way we saw some seals sunbathing on the rocks and barking. We also went a little off trail at certain points in scrambled up and down the rocks as a shortcut which was fun. </p>

<p>After eating we drove back to the Airbnb. We hung out at the beach for a half hour and then got ready to go to a restaurant called El Mirador about 20 minutes away. You could actually see where we hiked from the restaurant. Becca and I were way too full to eat so we just had drinks and watched the sunset, unfortunately it was a cloudy day so the sunset was not very good, but there were still good views. After sunset we drove back to the Airbnb and hung out in our room before going to sleep. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 3</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we slept in till around 8 AM Becca wasn’t feeling too great she thinks the sushi she ate a few nights ago cause her stomach to have problems. Plan was to do a snorkel with whale sharks and sea lions tour along with a picnic at Balandra beach. However it took some (a lot of) convincing to get Becca to go because she was feeling so bad, but eventually she caved and decided to give it a shot. We drove about an hour and a half to the city of La Paz which is on the opposite side of the Baja peninsula, the gulf side. When we arrived we parked near the La Paz marina and waited for our tour guide to arrive. The guide arrived a little later than planned around 11:15 and shortly after we were on the boat heading out at 11:30. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Lt0ZRZE.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The boat ride out of whale sharks took a longgggg time, but the experience was worth it! We were so close to the whale sharks and they were huge, around 30 feet long, it was insane. We swam with them three separate times for probably about 5 to 10 minutes each time. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/IU0HKZf.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After snorkeling we got back onto the boat and drove again a long time to swim with the sea lions. All the sea lions hung out on one big rock that we snorkeled around there were a bunch sleeping in the water because they’re mostly nocturnal and there were others that were swimming around and some that were sunbathing on the rock itself. We were pretty close probably about 20 feet away but not nearly as close as we were to the whale sharks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/zMpvoSm.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We also saw a bunch of fish and coral reef. After snorkeling around the small rock we got back in the boat and rode a short trip over to Balandra beach. This is considered one of the most beautiful beaches in all of Mexico. We also saw a famous mushroom rock that is what the beach is known for. On the beach we had a small picnic of tamales and chicken salad croissant. We hung out for probably about 30 minutes to an hour and got to explore a little bit of the beach and then got back on the boat and made our way back to the La Paz marina. </p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 4</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today was a pretty relaxed day. We woke up and went for a walk down to the north end of Cerritos Beach. We walked a little bit on the volcano rocks on the point. There were a ton of surfers in the water so we watch them a little bit from the point as well. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/rzxotPD.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made our way back to the Airbnb and hung out there for a little bit we saw a whale breaching which was really cool. For lunch we had leftover steak and cheese sandwich. After lunch we drove back up to the north end of the beach and rented a surfboard to try and surf. Apparently the lessons we took in El Salvador didn’t pay off because neither Becca nor I were able to stand up once lol. To be fair the waves were really rough and breaking really hard, also the board we had was much less stable than the normal boards we trained with. After surfing we stopped for a quick bite of ice cream on the way back to our car and went back to the Airbnb to get ready for dinner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before dinner we drove to a beach north of Todos Santos to watch them release baby sea turtles into the ocean. The sunset was really nice and then afterwards is when they let the sea turtles out. There was only maybe two dozen turtles and it was very dark so it was really hard to see them. Overall it was somewhat underwhelming. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the sea turtles we went to a restaurant called Briquetta. The food was really good I had the short ribs and Becca had a grilled octopus. Also the restaurant was really cozy and interesting, there was a table inside a boat! The only problem was the portion size was really small.</p>

That’s right it has nothing to do with this song but the Eagles sued them because they were pretending to be the hotel the song is based on, in reality there is no hotel. Ice cream was really good I had brownie and chocolate peanut butter. Afterwards we drove back to the Airbnb and went to bed. </p>

<p>Day 5</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up kind of early for breakfast I had cereal avocado toast and eggs. After breakfast we packed up and got the car loaded and drove Becca‘s parents to the airport for their flight home. Becca and I were staying another two nights in Cabo. However we can check into the hotel until four so we decided to go do a bungee jump at a nearby extreme park. We almost didn’t do it at first because they wouldn’t give us the online discount since we were booking it at the park, but as we were about to leave they conveniently changed their mind and decided that they would give us the discount. The jump was really fun very similar to the one I did in Costa Rica where you jump from a gondola that is suspended above the canyon. </p>

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/2JJI8tH.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After jumping we drove downtown and tried to find a spot to park to do a hike that would take us to lovers Beach and the famous Cabo arch. However, we were unsuccessful in finding a parking spot so we ended up driving to the resort. We were about three hours early but the hotel let us check in anyway which was awesome. After we got settled in our room Becca and I went for a walk down the beach back towards the main marina. Speech is way more crowded and filled with people trying to sell you anything. On the way back we stopped at a restaurant called cocos you Camerons where we had some beef tacos for an early dinner. </p>

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/o4at9S0.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After we ate we made our way back to the hotel as it started to rain. We ended up making some drinks at our room and hanging out in the hot tub for an hour or so. After the hot tub we went back to the room and hung out the remainder of the night (we’re lame) from our balcony we watched the show put on by the hotel (it was the greatest showman musical with a bunch of dancing). We barely made it to 10 local time to celebrate the Savannah new year and then promptly passed out!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 6</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 8, after lounging around the room for a bit we took our COVID tests. Of course there was an issue (this time with the test supposedly being expired), but after a minute of arguing the lady changed her mind and allowed me to use the test (it was negative). After we made our way down to the beach to take a water taxi over to lovers beach. After talking to the front desk we haggled the water taxi guy down from $20 to $12 and made the short hop across the bay. As a part of the shuttle service the boat driver gave us a quick tour showing us a few of the rock formations, most notably the famous arco (sea arch), and then dropped us off at lovers beach around 11:30.</p>

Both beaches are really only accessible by boat but there is a way to climb over some rocks to get to a few of resorts further up divorce beach, so of course we climbed over them and walked down the beach. </p>

<p>Day 7</p>

Overall no issues getting home, we had one stop in Dallas and made it back to Savannah around. Another great trip to close out 2021 and kick off 2022, looking forward to what this next year brings!</p>
