Last night I slept terrible, I went to be around 11pm and woke up at 1:50am 😫. The ground is super hard and even though we have a sleeping pad it feels like we’re sleeping on concrete. I just couldn’t get comfortable. I ended up watching a couple movies to try and pass the time and fall asleep but I wasn’t really able to. The reception here is absolutely terrible. Not that I was expecting much but it does make me realize how much I take for granted modern technology, and my comfy bed for that matter.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1097-1.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-852" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1100.jpg" class="wp-image-853 size-full" height="3024" width="4032">

We ended up getting up for tea around 6am and the started getting our things packed back up and organized. Another thing, the bathrooms here are absolutely disgusting...I’ll just leave it at that. For breakfast we had omelet, sausage, porridge, and bread. It was really filling and good! After breakfast our guide briefed us and checked our pulse and oxygen levels then we started day 2 of the hike around 8am.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1104.jpg" class="wp-image-854 size-full" height="4032" width="3024">

Today we went from Machame camp to shira camp. The distance was less than day 1, but the hike was much steeper. Luckily the weather was perfect, it was sunny with awesome visibility. Also today we went at a muchhhh slower pace. At first I was kind of annoyed because I like to hike fast but after a little bit I enjoyed it. Much more of a relaxing pace, I didn’t sweat at all. It also gave me more time to enjoy the views which were awesome today! Since it was sunny I decided to rock the sunhat and I must admit even though it looks goofy it’s pretty awesome.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2354.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-857" height="4032" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2370.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-856" height="1932" width="2576">

Along the trail we ran into some Chinese lady who asked Patryk if he could tie her shoelace because “she didn’t know how to”......she was at least 30, how the hell do you hike Kilimanjaro and not know how to tie your shoes?!? My mind is still blown.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2390.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-858" height="1932" width="2576">

After about 4 hours we made it to shira camp!

I forgot to mention this section of the hike is moorland which is a whole different set of vegetation compared to the jungle that we hiked through yesterday.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1125.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-860" height="4032" width="3024">

At camp we took of our gear and had some lunch. It was soup, some sort of meat epenanda, fries, and bread. It was pretty good and really filling! After lunch we took a nap. I completely passed out for like an hour and a half, I don’t think I’ve ever slept so hard in my life. We were supposed to go on a small hike at 4 to help acclimate us,however it started pouring rain so we ended up staying in our tent till 5.

After the nap we walked like 5 min to see the shira cave. It’s a pretty tiny cave but has a really interesting story.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1138.jpg" class="wp-image-861 size-full" height="3024" width="4032">

So before 1998 the tour companies didn’t give the porters tents to sleep in. So because of that they would race to the cave so that they would have shelter from the rain and wind. Otherwise they would have to sleep under a tree or worse. They fit 60+ people in that tiny cave. But in 1998 during a storm one of the other caves on the mountain collapsed and killed a bunch of the porters. So the government made a law that requires tents for everyone.

After the cave we walked around the camp a bit and then had dinner around 7:45. Dinner was leek soup, fried chicken, vegetable sauce, rice, and fried bananas. It was really good! I’ve been eating a ton of food here. I probably eat better than I do back home!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1131.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-862" height="2320" width="3088">

After dinner our guide briefed us on tomorrow’s hike and chatted a bit. It turns out our guide has met a lot of famous people who’ve hiked Kilimanjaro. He was the assistant guide for a bunch of the Philadelphia eagles after they won the super bowl! Another interesting thing we learned was when he married his wife he paid his father in law a goat!

After dinner we got ready for bed. Here’s to hoping I sleep better tonight than yesterday! Tomorrow we wake up again at 6 and start hiking at 8.
