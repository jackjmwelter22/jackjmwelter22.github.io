<p>Today we woke up at 5am, we hung out in the room and got all ready until just before 7am. At 7 we walked up to the reception area to eat our breakfasts. I had the continental breakfast which was eggs, toast, fruit, coffee, and juice. Becca had the Nicaraguan and Bennett had the pancakes. It was all tasty!</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast we finished getting ready and loaded into the car. We made a stop at the nearby supermarket to get some cash and a few snacks for the horseback riding trip. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the quick stop we drove about 25 minutes to the south side of the island to Finca Montana to meet our tour guide and horses. They took some time to get us sorted out they originally had Bennett and I on different horses, but then decided to switch us because Bennet and I weighed too much for the small horse :/.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After getting comfortable on the horses and taking a few pictures we made our way to the main road to start the trip around 9 am.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After literally 20 minutes my horse was not having a good time we ended up switching horses with the guide - who was on a much bigger white horse. This horse was much more comfortable, however he was also more "wild" so the guide had to hold onto him with a rope (instead of letting us ride free).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://i.imgur.com/Np3n3k9.jpeg","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"embed-handler","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We rode on the road which went along the coast with lots of nice views and then turned inland into a nature preserve where the trail part began. We chose this trip because it went to a very popular waterfall (which you can also hike to), it was a little longer, but because we were able to knock out two things at once (horseback riding, and waterfall hike) it was worth it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Becca was really good at riding - she had some practice from when she was little.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>We made it back to the farm around 1pm and got the horses put away and everything settled up. We got back in the car and stopped for lunch at a restaurant on the way back to the lodge. I'm writing this blog well after the fact and can't quite remember what I had. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bennett was starting to fuss and the waitress brought him a mango to eat and he absolutely loved it! It may be his new favorite food - she gave us a bunch more to bring back with us for later.</p>

<p>After the massages we ate dinner at the lodge. I had a chicken plate which was good.</p>
