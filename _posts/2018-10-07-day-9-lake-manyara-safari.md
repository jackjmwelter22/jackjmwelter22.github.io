Today is our last Safari day! It's getting kinda old so I'm ready to get to Zanzibar and relax on the beach. I'd definitely recommend a two day safari unless you're super into animals.

Breakfast was at 8 this morning with the usual eggs, sausage, toast, etc. we ended up leaving for the safari around 9am. The drive to lake Manyara only took 20min. This park is a lot smaller than the last two we visited. It basically consisted of a really big lake and Forrest surrounding it.

Unfortunately we really didn't see any new animals this trip and actually saw a lot fewer animals than the other two parks. We ended up mostly driving around the lake. For lunch we stopped by the lake/hot spring (which really just looked like a mash) and had the standard lunch box meal.

After lunch we drove around a bit more and then made our way back to the campsite to drop some people off. Afterwards we made the ~2 hour drive to Arusha to meet a driver from our hotel to take us back to Moshi. Our driver was very aggressive and overtaking anyone he could. The car we were in was super old and rickety, definitely one of the less safe drives in my life 😂.

Side note: apparently they have never heard of traffic lights in Africa so the traffic around even small cities/towns is horrible! It was a Tuesday night and it took us forever to get out of Arusha. There also appears to be no real traffic laws, at least none that are enforced. The only police presence we really saw were the random checkpoints they have on the roads which were told they're looking for drugs and guns. Another thing is apparently there is no form of EPA here because sitting in traffic you can smell nothing but gasoline and exhaust fumes. By the time we made it back to the hotel my head was pounding.

Once back at the hotel we walked over to The Kitchen restaurant for another "American Burger" and a milkshake (delicious) and then went back to bed. Tomorrow we make our way to Zanzibar by plane to enjoy the beach and relax!
