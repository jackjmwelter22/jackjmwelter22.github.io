Today I woke up around 5:30 for some reason. I fell asleep pretty early so I guess that's why. I spent the morning getting everything packed back up and then cooking and eating some eggs for breakfast and then watching some Netflix till 9:30 when our Airbnb host came to take us to the airport. The airport is super close and really small one of the smallest I've seen. We took an ATR (propeller plane) for a 40 minute flight back to Athens.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2520.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1268" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2521.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1269" width="4032" height="3024">

Once we arrived we got our luggage and took the metro to downtown to find our Airbnb. We got there right on time for our 2PM checkin. Our Airbnb was a super big apartment and had an awesome view of the acropolis.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/62e5c0fc-c268-4671-8a96-dd12a92f3696.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1270" width="1440" height="2560">

After resting for a bit we made our way to the main square (monstraki) and had some gelato along the way. After walking through the street markets for a bit we went to a rooftop for a beer before our cooking class at 4:30.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2527.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1271" width="3088" height="2320">

Our finally activity for the trip was a tradiontional Greek cooking class that was highly recommended by my sister. We walked to the restaurant it was in which was about 10min away. Our teachers name was Rena and there were about 10 of us total in the class, besides us there was a family from Atlanta and two couples from England. The restaurant was very fancy and had a really nice view of the acropolis. We learned how to make a bunch of different dishes. The main dish was lamb and potatoes and we also made some taziki, a spinach pie, fried zucchini balls, Greek salad, and a Greek yoghurt desert. They all have very Greek names for the dishes that I'm not even going to try and spell.

The class was really fun and afterwards we got to eat everything and it all tasted delicious! After the class we walked back and watched a Netflix movie called contagion (which was recommended by one of the people in our class who worked for the CDC).

After that I passed out. Tomorrow I start the long trip back home to reality with an overnight stop in New York and then back to Savannah. Overall the trip was a blast and I'm extremely grateful I was able to do it with my friends.

Update on the trip home since I don't want to write another whole post. Woke up around 7 and left the Airbnb around 8:30 to go to the main square with pat and Sarah for brunch. I had some pancakes with chocolate syrup. After we ate went went to a nearby coffee and gelato shop for one last scoop! Pat and Sarah's flight was two hours earlier so they left before me. I ended up walking around the market and agoras for a bit and then hung out in the main square before grabbing a gyro to go and hopping on the 11am metro to the airport.

Getting through security was pretty painless. The flight ended up taking about 10 hours I watched a couple movies one called the upside was really good. I stayed up the whole flight to try and get back on EST time. Once I landed I got an Uber to the Airbnb I'm staying at since my flight doesn't leave till tomorrow.

The Airbnb was interesting to say the least. I guess I didn't really read the fine print that it was actually a shared room lol. The bed I had a curtain around it which was nice but I was not expecting a hostel type setup. Luckily it was only for one night but some Asian lady was in the same room and woke me up turning the light on and talking on the phone. Super annoying!

I left the Airbnb around 6:30 in the morning and got to the airport and my gate with no issues. The flight to Savannah left on time and I got in at 11am...back to reality.

Stay tuned for more adventures in about a month when I go to Jordan, Israel, and Egypt with my sister and her friend Sam!
