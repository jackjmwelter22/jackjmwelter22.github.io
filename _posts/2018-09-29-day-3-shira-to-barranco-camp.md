Today I woke up at 1:45 again! 😡 this time it was because Patryk had to go to the bathroom. Figuring it’d be difficult to fall back to sleep I used some ear plugs and eye mask I grabbed from the plane. Luckily I was able to fall back asleep and pat had to shake me to wake me up at 6 for the hot tea and “washy washy” as our guide likes to say. There are no showers on the mountain of course so basically they give you a bowl of hot water to attempt to wash yourself. We had egg omelet, porridge, sausage, and fruit for breakfast. Luckily the sun was out this morning and the weather was awesome.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1145.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-864" height="3024" width="4032">

Side note: the other mountain in the photo is mount Meru. There were some really nice views of it during the hike.

After breakfast we packed up and began our hike. We hiked from shira camp up to lava tower (around 15,000’) for lunch around 12:30. It started to rain while we were there which kinda sucked. Lunch was very simple, just a lunch box with bread, chicken, juice, fruit, and chocolate.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1155.jpg" class="wp-image-865 size-full" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1164.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-866" height="4032" width="3024">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2420.jpg" class="wp-image-872 size-full" height="1932" width="2576">After lava tower we made our way down to barranco camp which was only a few hundred feet higher than yesterday’s camp. There were some cool views along the way. We saw some really interesting trees.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1180.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-867" height="4032" width="3024">

This one was over 150 years old (25 years for each branch)!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2432.jpg" class="wp-image-873 size-full" height="1932" width="2576">

For some reason our guide decided to set up camp far away from the main camp, which he referred to as the “refugee camp” 😂. The annoying thing was we were in the opposite direction of where we’re hiking tomorrow.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1187.jpg" class="wp-image-868 size-full" height="3024" width="4032">

When we got to camp we changed and set up our tent and then had some peanuts, popcorn, and tea for a snack. After the snack we chilled and watched oceans 8, horrible movie would not recommend, and then had dinner around 7.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1196.jpg" class="wp-image-869 size-full" height="4032" width="3024">btw that's not the peak, not even close<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1195.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-870" height="2320" width="3088">

For dinner we had pumpkin soup, spaghetti, green beans, a pancake type thing, and pineapple. Mosses (our guide) told us a lot of really interesting and funny stories during dinner. But one of the craziest things he told us was what people in his town do to thief’s if they catch him. Basically he said they pour gasoline on them and then light them on fire....and the police don’t care! I guess this is a normal thing here? Note to self: don’t steal anything in Tanzania. After dinner we went to bed. Hoping to get some better sleep tonight!
