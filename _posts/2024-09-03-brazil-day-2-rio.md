<p>Today I woke up around 6:30 AM, I took a shower and hung out while Becca and Bennett slept another hour. Once they woke up, we got ready and went to a nearby breakfast café a block away from our place. At breakfast I had eggs, sausage and toast. We also got some eggs for Bennett. While we were eating, some ladies came up to say hi to Bennett. They loved him, we had no idea what they were saying though.</p>

<p>I also got a few pastries to try back at the Airbnb. We walked back and got ready for hiking. We left around 9 AM for a hike called Pedra da Gavea which was about a 20 minute drive west of Ipanema. We were able to drive right to the start of the trailhead and park there which was nice. The last bit of road is a little rough, but otherwise it’s not too bad. Here’s a really good blog. Post with lots of details if you want to do the hike.</p>

<p>We started the hike around 9:30 am after checking in at the guard shack. The hike was relatively short-  only 3.3 miles round-trip. However it was very steep  with a 2600 ft elevation gain. I carried Bennett strapped in the chest carrier. And carrying an extra 23 pounds of Bennett definitely made it more of a challenge!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There were also a few spots where we had to scramble up using our hands and legs. I had to be really careful to make sure Bennett didn't hit his head and that I didn’t fall. There were a few spots that had chains bolted to the rock that you could hold on to.</p>

<p>Bennett pooped right before we left so we thought we would be OK. However he pooped again while we were hiking so we had to change him on the trail 😅. My sister did the hike last year so we knew there was one spot that was particularly challenging, called the carrasqueira, and she had to use ropes to get up.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When we got to the start of the carrasqueira there were no ropes, but it’s seemed manageable to climb up so I strapped the carrier up tight and started climbing. Bennett and I climbed up. However, Becca was worried about how much further the climbing area went on, and about how we would get down with Bennett, so she wanted to turn back. So I started to down climb back to her, which was much more difficult than climbing up. Fortunately, we made it back down to the start where Becca was going to wait for me while I went to the top. But then we ran into the rope guy who was just getting there and setting up, and he told us we could easily do the ropes with Bennett.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We hung out at the top for about 20 minutes before making our way back down. We ended up taking the ropes down as well, which was far easier than trying to down climb.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got back to the start around 2:30pm, so about five hours of hiking. Going with Bennett definitely slow us down a bit. I think 3 1/2 to 4 hours would definitely be doable. It was a super fun hike. I highly recommend it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made the 20 minute drive back to our Airbnb where we showered and relaxed for a minute. Then we walked to the Ipanema beach which is only a block from where we staying. The clouds rolled in, so it was a lot cooler, but there were still a ton of people on the beach. They had a bunch of volleyball courts, but not everyone was playing traditional volleyball. A lot of people were playing with their heads, chest and legs (no arms).</p>
