Woke up around 8 this morning. It's a little annoying in the morning because they have the church bells going off for what feels like forever and I think it's every hour starting at like 5am! For breakfast we ended up stopping at a shop just outside our hostel for some pastries and water. I had a chocolate filled crossiant and a cheese filled bread thing, both were delicious. While we were eating a random Jewish guy came up to Sam and started talking to him about the cubs (he was wearing a cubs hat) he thought Sam was Jewish and was trying to see if we wanted a tour of one of the synagogues.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2951.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1438" width="3088" height="2320">

After eating we walked a different route to the western wall. Along the way I stopped at a shop to get a frozen coffee slushy, it was really good. Our plan was to do the tunnel tour of the walls, however all the tours were sold out because of the holiday.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3485.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1448" width="2576" height="1920">

So instead we went out of the city gate and walked 10min down to the city of David. David was one of the kings of Israel, he's also the David from the story of David and Goliath. The city ended up being super cool. It had its own underground tunnel system, but this one was filled with water. So after a short hike through the city you get to the entrance of the tunnel system. I ended up taking off my shoes because the water was going to be up to thigh level at some points. The cave was super tight at some points and completely dark. Luckily I had my headlamp which made it easy to see. Definitely wouldn't recommend if you're clostraphobic! The tunnel system went on for more than 500 meters and took us at least 20-30min to walk through. The ceilings weren't very high either so most of that I was hunched over.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2955.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1439" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2957.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1441" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2961.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1440" width="3088" height="2320">

The tunnel finally let us out at the pool of the city. This was the end of the route so we made our way back out of the city and then walked back up the hill to mount Zion. Mount Zion had the tomb of king David. For those that don't know, David is also responsible for writing the psalms (a book of the Bible). Also right above David's tomb was the room of last supper where Jesus ate with his 12 disciples. I'll be honest the room didn't look anything like the painting it was just a plain room. Afterwards we walked back into the old city towards the western wall and stopped for lunch at a small cave restaurant called al buraq. I had shawarma which was tasty but not as good as the shawarma I had yesterday.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2965.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1442" width="3088" height="2320">

After we finished eating we walked down Via Dolorosa and out of the old city through the lion gate and up the mount of olives. Walking up the mountain we saw the birth place of Mary, the church of Mary, and the tomb of Mary. They weren't anything super unique, but still pretty cool to see. We also saw the massive Jewish cemetery that faces the Temple Mount. Apparently it is almost impossible to get a grave there. The reason it's so popular is because the Jewish faith believes that during the end of the world the mount of olives will split open and the souls of the riteous will be able to get up and walk to the Temple Mount which is where heaven on earth will begin so they want to be as close as possible.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2967.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1443" width="3088" height="2320">

Next we walked a little further up mount olives to another church that had a great view of the Temple Mount from the Eastern side. After hanging out there for a bit we walked back to the hostel going through the suqs, Sam bought some souvenirs.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3489.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1449" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2969.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1446" width="4032" height="3024">

Back at the hostel we relaxed and watched the movie True Lies. If you haven't seen it it's a completely ridiculous action movie with Arnold Schwarzenegger. It was a little after 5 when we finished the movie and we had booked an escape room for 6 so we walked out of the old city near where we were last night to get there. We were there a little early so we decided to stop at the nearby Irish pub for a drink. It ended up being happy hour (buy one get one free) so we had two!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2973.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1444" width="4032" height="3024">The escape room was really fun. We made it out really fast and only needed one extra clue. The room was set in the 1930s during prohibition and the point was to find out the secret recipes of the drinks. The nice thing about the room was there was food and drinks that you were allowed to eat and drink! Needless to say we destroyed all the chocolate 😂. We ended up escaping in a little under 40 min.<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2977.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1445" width="4032" height="3024">After the room we ended up going back to the Irish pub to get some food. Jamie and I ended up splitting the large crunchy chicken and fries. After eating we made our way back to the hostel and went to sleep.
