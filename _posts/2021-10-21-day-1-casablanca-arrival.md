<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We left Savannah Thursday after work connecting in Atlanta. On arrival at the airport we had to check in at the Delta desk because we booked the flight through KLM (it was randomly $100 cheaper to book through them instead of Delta). At the desk the agent told us our flight was cancelled, for some reason she thought we were still booked through Amsterdam, I guess they didn’t get the update from KLM that we changed it. It took like 30 min for the agent to get it all sorted out and confirm us through Paris. On top of that the security line was ridiculous! Luckily I have pre check and was able to get through in less than 5 min but Becca had to wait an hour to get through! Thankfully we got to the airport super early, Becca barely made it to the gate right when we were boarding. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The flight to Atlanta was uneventful and the flight from Atlanta to Paris was great we each had a complete row to ourselves and we’re able to lay out and get some good sleep. On arrival we had a small anxiety inducing moment when our KLM app said that our flight was cancelled!! We went to the Air France desk and they told us the flight was not cancelled but our booking was?? The agent had no clue why but he was able to put us back on the flight and since we had a long layover (arrived at 7:45 departing at 12:30) we had plenty of time to figure it all out and get to our gate. In short, the airlines need to get a better system!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next flight Paris to Casablanca was a quick 2-3 hour flight. We also learned that my sister and her friends were denied getting on their flight because they didn’t have COVID PCR tests (supposedly the rules change that morning, however all of the government websites and even the airline website said no test was needed if you’re vaccinated…super frustrating!!). They ended up having to book a different flight and will be getting in a day later than us and will need to drive on their own to meet us in Chefchaouen. Once Becca and I arrived, we grabbed the free shuttle to our hotel ~5min from the airport. The room we got was HUGE it had a living room, bedroom, and 2 full bathrooms.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After we settled in we went back down to the lobby to catch a taxi to the airport. It was around 5pm and the drive to the city took about an hour. Our first stop was the old Medina, a covered market that sold random stuff clothes, jewelry, etc. we were both hungry, but the only food we could find was boiled snails which didn’t sound very appetizing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We spent about 15 minutes wandering through the market, it was like a maze with tons of winding streets. We met back up with our taxi who took us to the Hassan II Mosque, this is the tallest mosque in the country named after one of the old kings. It was really impressive and built right on the coast. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2227,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0034.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2227" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We walked along the coast by the mosque and tried this fried potato sliced kebab thing as a snack, it was pretty good and only cost like 60 cents US. After walking around we got back to our taxi who took us to an area called la corniche, there wasn’t much to do where we stopped but there were some nice views of the beach/coast. Once back at the taxi we tried to ask about getting food somewhere, but he had no clue what I was talking about (I even tried to us the google translate app but had no luck). He just kept telling us something that sounded like “cover fee” and showing us the number 23?? Btw they speak mostly French and Arabic here, which neither of us know. We ended up making a final stop at the Morocco mall (apparently it’s a big attraction). There was this massive aquarium in the middle of it that had a bunch of sharks, fish, and whale ray swimming around in it. </p>

<p>We ended up finding some food, nothing amazing as it was mall food. I ended up grabbing a burger and fries and Becca had sushi. Then after we both got some ice cream. After the mall we drive back to the hotel. We were both exhausted so we passed out quickly. </p>
