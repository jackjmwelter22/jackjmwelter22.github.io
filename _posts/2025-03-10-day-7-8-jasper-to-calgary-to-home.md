<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I woke up around 6:30 and wrote my blog. Becca and Bennett got up around 8:30 am. We had breakfast at the airbnb and then packed up and loaded up the car at 10. We started the trek back to Calgary. On the way out of Jasper we saw a bunch of crews working on cutting the dead burnt trees down and we also saw more Elk hanging out by the side of the road. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our first stop on the way back was Sunwapta falls about 45 minutes away from Jasper. No sign of fire damage here but it was raining a bit so we did our best to bundle Bennett up. I wore him on the chest carrier and wrapped him in my rain jacket. We also used Becca’s backpack cover on Bennetts head to keep him dry. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There were two parts of the falls: the upper and lower. The upper was right off the road with a bridge that went over the falls. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After hiking back we got in the car and drove another 45 minutes to the Toe of the Athabasca glacier. The rain had stopped but it was kind of hailing now and windy. The hike was super short, under a mile total. We originally thought the trail went into the glacier but unfortunately it only went up to it and not on it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It was still a super cool view but also very cold with the wind and hail blowing in our face. Becca carried Bennett for this one and had him wrapped up tight. </p>

<p>After taking some pictures we went back to the car and decided to check out the visitor center that was across the road. The visitor center was super crowded and super touristy. They sold glacier hiking tours but it didn’t look that cool plus Becca and I had been on a few glaciers already with much smaller groups. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>We left the center and continued on the road. As we drove farther the weather improved. We were planning to stop at Crow glacier (about an hour away) but ended up deciding to skip it because it was only a viewpoint and we were able to see it and take some pictures as we drove by in the car. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The rest of the drive to Calgary was about 2.5 hours. We made a stop at Tim hortons of the highway and ended up getting Wendy’s frosties (it was a combined store). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up getting to the hotel in Calgary around 5 and checking in and getting settled. We relaxed for a bit then left around 6pm to get dinner at a nearby Korean restaurant. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I ended up getting a fried rice dish and milk tea flavored bubble tea, it was really good. Bennett had a bunch of the fried rice and liked it!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After dinner I dropped Becca and Bennett back off at the hotel and then drove to the gas station to fill up and clean the car up before driving to the airport to drop the car off. After dropping the car off I got the shuttle back to the hotel. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up hanging out at the hotel before going to bed. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 8</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up early to make our way to the airport. There was a really fun play area near our gate with a tunnel that Bennett enjoyed crawling through. We connected through JFK and then to Savannah without any issues. Unfortunately when we arrived in Savannah it was right after hurricane Helene hit and our house was without power for a week!</p>
