<p>Today was a lazy day, we slept in and had the buffet breakfast at the hotel - it was delicious. They had so much food! After eating we hung out in the room and then down at the pool. Our hotel is on the beach so you can walk from the pool directly out onto the beach. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At 12 I walked to meet my kiteboarding instructor. I had learned a little bit about kiteboarding from a friend/coworker previously and wanted to learn more (lessons here are super cheap relative to the US). My instructor was the "english speaking" instructor, however his english was very basic - which I actually think was good, he explained things to me in the simplest way possible.</p>

<p>We practiced flying the kite on the beach to start and then made our way into the water to practice body dragging. Unfortunately after about two hours it started storming so we had to stop. Originally I planned for 5 hours (since the lessons are so much cheaper here than in the us). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After paying Becca and I hung out in our hotel room during the storm. The rain was supposed to let up around 4 so we decided to book a free walking tour in downtown Cartagena at 4pm (free walking tours are great by the way and most major cities have them). We took another taxi from our hotel to downtown, the roads were flooded driving there but luckily the rain completely stopped when we arrived. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before the tour we made a quick stop for some ice cream and then met the tour group, it was only two other people besides Becca and I. The tour explored most of the old city of Cartagena we started on the walls and then made our way to the plazas and churches. The old city area is relatively small maybe a mile by a mile so we were able to see almost all of it on foot.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>We walked through the neighborhood through one of the main squares and through the "flag street" before finding a place to eat dinner. We ended up stopping at an Italian restaurant. I had a baked zitti dish and Becca had a steak. The food was really good!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we walked through the "umbrella" street (I swear they have these everywhere) and then we made our way back to the gate of the old city.</p>

<p><strong>Day 11</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Final day in Colombia! I did some work in the morning and then we had a big breakfast at the hotel buffet.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/h6VdTaB.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We decided to hang out at the hotel all day, we grabbed some snacks at the nearby grocery store and went to the pool for a few hours.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the pool we went back to our room and packed up our stuff. My shoes were basically destroyed from the hike so I ended up leaving them. We took a quick cab ride from our hotel to the airport. Our flight was at 3pm and went to Miami. Becca got flagged for extra screening for some reason. The Cartagena airport is pretty terrible, no a/c and one small terminal for all the international flights which was jam packed while we were there. I had a quick snack from the one food shop which took about 20 minutes to get because of the long line.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We landed in Miami on time and decided to eat at an Irish pub for dinner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/ArK8XRz.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our flight to Jacksonville was at 10:30pm - very late. Luckily we got bumped up to first class for some reason, which was nice even though it's only a 45 minute flight. I was actually able to sleep on the plane. We arrived 12:30 and hustled to catch our shuttle which was a quick 10 minute ride to our car. We then made the 2 hour drive home and reunited with the dogs and then finally go to sleep! Unfortunately I had work the next day, Becca was smart and had the day off.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Overall a super fun trip and as always excited for the next adventure!</p>
