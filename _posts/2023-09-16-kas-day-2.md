<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up at 7:20. We had breakfast again downstairs at the hotel before packing up and checking out. We had a taxi pick us up at 8:30 to take us to the airport (there are two airports in Istanbul, this one was on the Asia side). The taxi driver tried to charge us a different amount than what the hotel told us which caused a bit of drama, but we worked it out. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our flight left around 11:30 am, and we arrived in Antalya at 1 pm. Overall a quick smooth flight, we flew in over the Mediterranean on our approach and had nice views of the coast. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Kf8rV70.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I got the rental car- we had to wait 20 minutes or so which would not have been so bad, however it was scorching hot outside. After we finally got the car we were off to Kas which is about a 3 Hour drive along the coast with beautiful views the whole way. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before going to our hotel, we made a stop in the town to check in with the local dive shop and grab some food. The lady at the shop recommended a restaurant for dinner called ora kebab. The food part was OK (I had an adan kebab) but somewhat pricey. I also had a chocolate milkshake which literally tasted like water, it was probably the worst milkshake I’ve ever had, I didn’t even drink half of it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/KebXF2A.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating, we went to the main Square to grab some ice cream before heading to our hotel and checking in. We are staying at Hotel Cachet, which is on the Kas peninsula. The hotel is pretty sweet we have our own pool &amp; patio outside our room and view of the Mediterranean. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/B1o3WH2.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Our private pool</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After unloading our luggage we went down to the cliffs/beach area of the hotel, and jumped in to test the water, it was refreshing and super clear. Afterwards, we hung out in our pool before going to bed.</p>
