<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we got up around 7:30. We just checked into our flight online when less than an hour later we got notified that the flight was delayed from 9pm to 1am!! Which of course meant we would miss all our connections. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Fortunately we were able to work with delta and we got on an earlier flight home at 2:30pm. This time going to Honolulu and then to Atlanta and finally Charleston. It honestly worked out a lot better!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up making a big breakfast finishing all the eggs and bacon. </p>

<p>From the beach we stopped at the pool. I hung out in the hot tub for a while talking to a friendly guy who’s lived on the island for 10 years. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the hot tub we got everything packed up into the car and checked out of our condo. We left at 10:30 to go to the Kauai plantation railway at kilohana tour. We had booked the tour to kill some time before our late flight. The tour was a train ride around the plantation. It was interesting, we made a stop to feed some pigs, horses, a cow, and a donkey which was fun!</p>
