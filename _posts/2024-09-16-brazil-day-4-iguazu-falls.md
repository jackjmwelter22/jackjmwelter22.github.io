<p>Today we woke up at 3am, loaded up the car and left for the airport. We made a quick stop for gas before dropping off the car and then taking the shuttle from the car rental place. We checked in with Azul Airlines, and then made our way through security  to our gate. We stopped at "The House of Cheese Bread" for some breakfast. It was good! I had the traditional and a dulce de leche filled ball as well as a comically small panini.</p>

<p>While waiting for our flight our names were called to come to the desk. Using the translate app the guy at the desk told us they found a battery in my checked bag so I had to follow him to get it. We took a bunch of back hallways and eventually made it to my bag so I could grab the battery. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Literally no more than 10 minutes later they call us again and tell us that they switched us to a direct flight. We were extremely confused- I knew there was a direct flight with Gol airlines that we were trying to avoid (their website was terrible and they had tons of bad reviews)...also even though it was direct the flight arrived an hour later. The guy at the desk told us there were maintenance issues with the connection flight and it was expected to be delayed, so this new flight would get us there earlier (using a translate app).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So we left the gate and picked up our bags and went back to the Azul check in desk. They told us it would only be “a few minutes” while they finished up our new reservation- it ended up taking 45 minutes. While waiting Becca found out that one of the flight attendants called in sick and they had to reduce the number of people on the flight - the real reason. Also after numerous times of asking the desk lady why it was taking so long and to check on the progress- they told us they were going to put us in a flight for the next day! Absolutely not! After getting very frustrated with her she came back 10 minutes later with our reservation for the direct flight at 8:40 we were originally promised.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After that stressful ordeal we checked in with Gol Airlines and thankfully had an uneventful flight to Foz. Once at Foz we took a shuttle to the car rental (this time was much faster because we knew to look for Localiza). Once we got the car we drove straight to the falls which was only 5 minutes away.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the falls we parked and took a double decker bus to the main hiking trail about 15 minutes away. We originally were on the top of the bus, but moved to the lower level because it was cold! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hiking trail was short and relatively flat along a ridge that overlooked the river and the falls. The views were amazing ! Although it was a little colder the weather was perfect- sunny and not a cloud in the sky.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The trail ended after about a mile at a place called the devils throat. There was a platform that went out into the basin that catches the tallest part of the waterfall. It was the most impressive/powerful part of the falls. The platform was a bit crowded but we were able to get some good pictures (in general we were told it’s usually much much busier than this).</p>

<p>There are two options for the tour: wet or dry. We elected to do the wet tour:).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The whole boat ride lasted probably 30 minutes . You first head upstream through some crazy rapids, then right up next to some of the falls which soaked us! The guide gave us a plastic poncho to hold over Bennett so he didn’t get too wet. They also had a lifejacket for him. </p>

After kind of unpacking and relaxing for a bit we drove about half a mile to a steak house called Park Foz that Jamie and Nate recommended. Becca and I shared the waygu steak platter which was pretty good though a bit overcooked.</p>
