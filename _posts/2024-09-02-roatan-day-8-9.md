<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After some droning we made our way to the airport. Check in was fairly slow and the security was even slower, but we eventually made it through. We ended up getting some food at the airport. I tried Baleadas - which were not good! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our flight from Roatan to Miami left on time. My mom was on the same flight as us again, Jamie and Nate were flying out the next day (since it was a cheaper flight). Once we made it to Miami we said by to my mom, her flight was before ours which was scheduled for 10:30pm.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unfortunately there were some crew and maintenance issues with our flight (we can't catch a break!), so our flight was cancelled after about an hour delay. So we made our way to the American help desk (around midnight at this point). The line was massive, but thankfully someone from American saw we had a baby and pulled us to the front of the line. We ended up getting a hotel that was farther away because all the nearby ones were booked up (due to other flight cancellations I think). We got to the hotel via a Lyft around 1:30 am. Thankfully the hotel was really nice. The other downside was that the next flight back to Savannah that they rebooked us on was a full 24 hours later - the next day at 10:30 pm :/.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 9 Wednesday</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We slept in late today - since we had all day in Miami we stayed at the hotel until 11 am (checkout). I did some work at the hotel. We had already planned a day in Miami on our way back from Brazil (our next trip) so we ended up going and doing all the things we had previously planned. Thankfully we were able to store all our bags at the hotel while we went out and explored. Our first stop was Little Havana. We stopped at a store called dos croquetas. Becca wasn't able to eat anything, but I tried a few different types. After that we had lunch at a cuban restaurant called El Pub. I had the cuban sandwich and Becca had the ropa vieja. Both were really good!</p>
