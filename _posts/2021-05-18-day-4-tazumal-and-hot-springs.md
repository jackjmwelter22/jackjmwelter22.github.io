<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we slept in till ~8 and ate breakfast at the hotel again. After breakfast we packed up the car and checked out of the hotel. We drove further west to another Mayan ruin site called Tazumal that was about an hour away. Tazumal was a Mayan city the was uncovered in the 1920s. It was cool to see the size of the structure, much bigger than the ones at San Andres. We spent about a half hour exploring the ruins. It was fun because here they actually let you climb up on most of the ruins. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After Tazumal we went to another ruin site only half a mile away called casa Blanca. It wasn’t as impressive as Tazumal (more like mounds of dirt than anything) but since we were here we figured we might as well see it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1938,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_1267.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1938" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>They also had an area they were excavating that you could go down into. It was crazy to see how deep the layers of bricks went. The guide mentioned that there are likely hundreds of other ruins still buried beneath the dirt. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1939,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_1269.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1939" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After casa Blanca we drove another 30 min or so to get to the Santa Teresa hot springs. At first glance the place looked pretty run down and the parking lot looked like a pile of rubble. However, once inside we were greeted with countless pools ranging in temperature from super hot 46C (~115F) to cold. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1931,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_1274.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1931" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We enjoyed the pools for a bit and then ate at the on-site restaurant called Manhattan. I had a grilled chicken sandwich that was surprisingly good! Becca had a chicken salad and for dessert we had a brownie with ice cream, nut delicioso!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1953,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_5225.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-1953" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch we took a nap in one of the hammocks (it was super relaxing). And then hung out in the hot springs till ~5pm. We left for our next destination, El Carmen coffee plantation in the city of Ataco. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1936,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_1270.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-1936" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We had a bit of trouble finding the hotel because the plantation is very big however luckily we ran into someone who spoke English and was able to direct us to the right building. It was a very unassuming red gate on an alley with no signs whatsoever. After unpacking and settling in to the hotel we walked across the Main Street to the town to get dinner. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We stumbled upon a Mexican restaurant where the food was dirt cheap. We spilt a plate of loaded nachos and I had a pork torta (apparently it’s a burrito but on a bun instead of a tortilla). The food was really good! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1937,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_1275-1.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1937" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After dinner we grabbed ice cream bars from a nearby street shop on our way back to the hotel. We ended up watching some of the god father part 3 (I had downloaded) before going to sleep. </p>
