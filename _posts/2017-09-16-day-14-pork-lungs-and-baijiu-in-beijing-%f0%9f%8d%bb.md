Today we woke up around 7, made our way downstairs for another all you can eat America breakfast. After breakfast we quickly got packed and took the metro to the train station to catch our train to Beijing. Xian to Beijing is fairly far away but we were on one of China's bullet trains to we were able to make the trip in 5 hours going 200+ MPH!

I was able to finish my blog on the train and get some much needed rest. I watched some British chick flick on Jamie's iPad called "Love, Rosie" which, if I'm honest, actually wasn't too bad haha!

We ended up getting into Beijing a little before 2pm and then caught the metro to our hostel which took ~an hour.

We dropped our bags off at the hostel and then went out looking for food. I was hungry and didn't want to walk that far so we stumbled into the first place we saw that looked decent. Everything was in Chinese but there were pictures so I figured we'd be fine....I've never been more wrong.

I ordered what I thought was some kind of a beef and noodle soup...nope! The meat looked a little weird but I'd tried to keep an open mind. I'll be honest, It wasn't the best, sometimes when go for something new it's awesome and other times it's not. This time it definitely was not. I wasn't about to let the food go to waste so I eat just about the whole bowl when Jamie decides it would be a good idea to google what we're eating and see what it really is. That was the worst idea!

So it turns out we had order a pork soup...doesn't sound bad right? Wrong! We his was not your typical pork. The soup consisted of Pork lung and intestines (what I thought was some kind of sausage)! 😖 after gaining that knowledge I couldn't eat another bite haha. We quickly left the restaurant, I'm sure the waitress thought we were crazy 😂.

<img class="wp-image-733" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1771.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" />

From the restaurant we ended up taking the metro out to the summer palace. It was quite the hike to get to and took over an hour.

The palace itself was really cool! It surrounds this lake and the main part of the palace is on a hill. We needed up walking around for about 1 and a half hours.

<img class="wp-image-735" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1772.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /><img class="wp-image-734" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1781.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" />

We ended up taking a bus back to the metro and then the metro back to our hostel for dinner. I ended up getting the sweet and sour chicken, which was delicious and was the first food I've had over here that I would think of as Chinese food back in the states 😂.

<img class="wp-image-736" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1784.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" />

After dinner we ended up walking about 30 minutes to meet our new friends from New Jersey out at a nearby tiki hut bar. We ended up going to a few places with them and stayed our way later than we probably should have! We also tried China's liquor which was called baijiu (pronounced "bye-Joe") which I would highly recommend 😂😂. It tasted like rubber and industrial chemicals!

Tomorrow we head back to the Great Wall to hike the "wild" unrestored section.
