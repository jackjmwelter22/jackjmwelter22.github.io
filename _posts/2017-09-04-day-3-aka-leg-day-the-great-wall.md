<p style="text-align:justify;">Today I woke up around 4am (I guess I didn't beat the jet lag after all!) which worked out well because I didn't have time to finish my blog from the previous day so I was able to get that done. We got the 'American' breakfast again in the lobby at 6:45 and waited to be picked up by our tour guide at 7. The 'guide' met us and a few other people in the lobby of the hostel. He clearly didn't speak much English and motioned for us to follow him. He the proceeded to sprint down the street, occasionally looking back to motion us to keep following him 😂.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-421" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0941.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" />Right before he decided to make a run for it!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">We were all so confused but did our best to keep up. It turns out he was rushing ahead in order to get to the main road and signal the bus to stop for us. We ended up getting on the bus and settled in for the 2 hour ride to the Simatai section of the Great Wall! On the way the tour guide gave us a brief history of the wall, only about 20% of which I was able to actually comprehend 😂 (his English wasn't the best but he was very enthusiastic!).</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-423" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0942.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" /><img class="wp-image-424" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0944.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /> The Simatai section of the wall has never been restored (supposedly...I have my doubts). We arrived at the base of a mountain where the entrance to a trail that took you up to the wall began. The area at the base was like a resort. Everything was clearly brand new and looked really nice. Even though this section of the wall is one of the least busy sections it seems like they're able to handle a lot more visitors.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-425" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0949.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /><img class="wp-image-426" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0952.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /> The hike up to the wall was very quick, it only took about 15 minutes till we got on the wall. The morning was a bit foggy so it was more difficult to see the sections of the wall but it cleared up as the day went on.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-427" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0953.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" /><img class="wp-image-429" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0954.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /><img class="wp-image-428" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0961.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /> The views on the wall were incredible. It's amazing to think something so massive was built so long ago, high up on the mountain ridge. By the way...why the hell did they need a wall on the mountain?! I don't get it! It would've been ridiculously hard to move an army through the mountains as it is so was the wall really adding any extra protection? I asked the guide and I don't think he understood my question 😂 he just said it was to stop the Mongolians.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">Anyway! Once on the wall we hiked up the ridge going through 10 watch towers. The hike on the wall took another 45 minutes allowing for plenty of time to stop and take selfies. The selfie stick definitely paid for itself at this point. I'm not sure how high we hiked but it was pretty strenuous. Apparently Jamie is a professional mountain climber or something because she practically sprinted up the wall! I was struggling to keep up with her at times! 😂 it was good training to help prepare us for the 10 mile spartan race were doing next month. here are a few of the many selfies we took on our trek up to the top!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-437" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0975.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" /><img class="wp-image-430" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0987.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /><img class="wp-image-436" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0995.jpg" width="1932" height="2576" /><img class="wp-image-435" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1006.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /><img class="wp-image-434" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1007.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" /><img class="wp-image-433" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1087.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /><img class="wp-image-432" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1109.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" /><img class="wp-image-431" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1126.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /> Once we got to the final watch tower we had about two hours before we had to get back to the entrance so we hung out at the top for a while and enjoyed the view and talked with some of the other people in our tour group. We met two different couples from the Netherlands so I talked to them for a while about my recent trip there for work. They were both extremely nice and well traveled. I'm envious of all the vacation time they get (50 days a year for the one couple!). I also met a group of people from France (which is where I was for work for 8 weeks last year) so we talked to them for a while. After about an hour we slowly decided to make our way back down the wall.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">Before the tour started they tried to sell us on taking the cable car on the way down the mountain, we decided not to and I'm glad we didn't! The view coming down was much better. You could actually look out at the wall and scenery (not your feet like you had to do on the way up). Also the fog had mostly cleared so it was much easier to see the wall and how far it extended down the mountain ridge. The hike down was much less strenuous.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">When we reached the bottom we regrouped and all went to lunch at a nearby restaurant. The food was all laid out on a big lazy Susan on each table. There was a ton of different food and it was all really delicious!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-439" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1129.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /> After lunch (around 3:30) we got back on the bus and made the 2 hour ride back to Beijing. I took a quick nap on the bus which was probably not the best idea, I felt super groggy when I woke up. The bus ended up letting us of a subway station. We (aka I) decided we should continue our quest to find the ever elusive central perk cafe from friends. We took the subway to this big mall (the new address to where it was supposed to be). The mall was huge and we had to go all the way up to the 6th floor in building A (there were 4 buildings) and we found it!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-443" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1136.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" />building the cafe was hidden in</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-444" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1137.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /> I was way more excited than I probably should have been that we found it 😂. We ended up getting drinks and cheesecake and watched an episode or two of friends.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-442" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1140.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" /> Afterwards we took the subway back to our hostel and called it an early night. Tomorrow we have to get up super early tomorrow to make our flight to Hong Kong!</p>
