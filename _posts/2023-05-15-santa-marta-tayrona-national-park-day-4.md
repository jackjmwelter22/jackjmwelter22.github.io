<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we had another very early day, we woke up around 3:30am to catch our ride to the airport for our 6am flight to Santa Marta. When trying to get cash from the ATM I found out that my card was locked - after calling the bank they said there were some fraudulent charges and they had to lock out the card so now I have no way to get cash out :(. Lucky Becca’s card still works though so we’ll be fine. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the gate we got a small breakfast from one of the shops - it was pretty bad. The flight to Santa Marta was another short hop ~55 minutes. When we arrived we grabbed a taxi from the airport to our hostel, the Masaya hostel. One of the roads was closed and our driver ended up driving reverse for like 4 blocks down a tight one way street to get us there 😂. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Since our room wasn’t ready, we checked our bags and hung out in the lobby area by the pool. The hostel has two pools! Around 9am we walked to the Marina for our sailing trip. Our original trip was cancelled on us the night before, but luckily we were able to rebook with another company (Tayrona sailing) last minute. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We met the tour operator at the dock and then waited for everyone to arrive. We got in the boat and started sailing around 10 to tayrona. </p>

The little kids working at the beach were super interested in the drone so I showed them how it worked and let the one kid fly it for a little. I ended up making friends with two of the kids named Hannes (sp?) and Diego both are 13 and from the area. They each walk 3 hours to get to the beach!</p>

<p>We left the beach around 3pm to sail back to Santa marta. The ride back took twice as long, I think the captain was trying to mostly use the sail, which is why it took longer. Along the way back we passed by a random island that had a bunch of goats on the top - I have no idea how they got there. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once we were back on land we walked back to our hostel. Our room was finally ready so we moved our bags unpacked and showered. After getting settled in we went to the downtown Santa Marta area for food. It turned out there was some sort of burger competition going on called burgermastsers that a bunch of the restaurants were competing in and each restaurant involved was serving only one item, their best burger. We stopped at one of the restaurants and got their burger (the used lettuce instead of a bun for Becca). It was really good! It had a delicious sauce and pulled pork on it. </p>
