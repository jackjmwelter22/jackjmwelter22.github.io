<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today, since we decided to book the canyoning tour, we had to wake up early to get to Wanaka by 9am. We woke up at 5a, ate a quick breakfast and hit the road. We ended up making great time and arrived at Wanaka around 8a. Since we had some time to spare we decided to take a short walk to “that one Wanaka tree”. Basically a lone tree in the middle of the lake. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The walk there only took 10 min and after taking some pictures we walked back to the car and drove to the meeting point for our tour. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/vWQqZkS.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We met our guide, Robin, and one other person who booked the tour before getting in the tour van and heading to the canyon.  The drive to the canyon took about an hour and a half. We did the intermediate trip which was a bit longer and more difficult. The canyon we were exploring is called Robinsons creek. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once we got to the canyon we got suited up in our wetsuits, harnesses and other canyoning gear.  We had to hike from the van about 20 minutes up to the start point. The hike was really cool very mossy and looked like a fairyland if that makes sense lol. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/c4gb8Jl.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The water was super cold but the trip down the canyon was a blast! Lots of jumps from 8 m high to 2 m high and lots of rappels/abseils.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Q1MebdB.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Jumping off one of the smaller jumps</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pat had his GoPro but the battery didn’t last very long so we only had a few pictures from the trip. Unfortunately we didn’t get a good video of the biggest jump either because Pat was too scared to go first 😂. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The coolest abseil was like 100’ into a big cavern!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/oqYK2K4.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Abseiling into the cavern</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/EuMrtCb.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Group selfie</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The whole trip lasted a few hours. After we got out of the canyon we ended right at the van. We changed into some warm clothes and drove about 20 minutes to a lunch picnic spot. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/bDSKjo9.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The lunch was okay, it was sandwich’s but all veggies and no meat so it was lacking a bit of protein. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we drove back into town and got back around 4. Overall the tour was a blast and our guide was great! Would recommend if you’re near wanaka check out deep canyon tours. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Wanaka is a super quaint lake front town surrounded by mountains. Much less touristy than Queenstown and a really cool vibe. After we got to the car we checked into our Airbnb and then walked down to the lake to relax. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/9P0k3MV.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We hung out at the beach for an hour and a half soaking in the sun and people watching. There’s a ton of things you can rent at the lake if you want to (paddle boats, sail boats, jet skis etc) there’s also a pier you can jump off of. We didn’t bring suits so I only went in knee deep to feel the water. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After hanging out in the beach we made our way to a craft brewery bar called Kai Whakapai (right next to the lake). Apparently Wanaka has the most breweries per capita than any other NZ town. I had the hazy IPA which was really good!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After a beer the girls went to get food from a food truck place while pat and I decided to hike the mount iron cross (surprisingly Pat suggested hiking as he’s trying to lose 1kg so he weighs less than 100kg and doesn’t have to pay extra to skydive). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike was about a mile from our Airbnb. We made a quick stop there to change shoes and then walked to the trailhead. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/b8i2xsi.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Walking up to mt iron cross</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the trailhead there was really ominous signs posted about “random nightly shootings” occurring. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/ViBuEYk.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At least the police are aware? We weren’t really sure what it meant but it was about 7:20 when we started the hike so we had about an hour and a half before sunset to complete the hike and avoid being shot 😂. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/mL4SOoD.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Pat enjoying the hike</figcaption></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike was fairly quick about 2 miles. It took us about 30-40 min up. I did some droning and we took some pictures at the top (there were great views of the town and lake). The wind was really strong but the drone did well. </p>
