<p>Day 6 Sunday</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up at the usual time and had a quick breakfast at the VRBO (eggs) before heading with Nate and Jamie to the other side of the island to do some diving. We were diving with a different shop called Key Divers - based out of little French key, which is a small island on the south/east side of the island. Normally people will pay to go hang out at the island for the day (like $90) however because we were diving we got to hang out there for free. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got picked up from the dock and shuttled over to the island by the dive shop. We stopped at their small dive hut and got our gear ready. We were supposed to dive at 9 but there was another family that was diving and they didn't get there until 9:30 so we ended up leaving late.</p>

<p>The first dive we were doing was the Prince Albert wreck - this was another big cargo ship that was intentionally sunk. It's at a shallower deck than the other dives we did which is why we were able to do the dive with Nate and Jamie.</p>

<p>After the dive we went back to little French Key to hang out and do our 1 hour surface interval. After seeing it, I would not recommend anyone to pay to visit little french key. It looked very run down. There was a tiger in a cage pacing around - apparently it was waiting to be taken somewhere where they could properly care for it :/. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our second dive was another, smaller, wreck dive called Mr. Bud. This wreck was much smaller than the last one - but I actually thought it was cooler with all the fish inside of it.</p>

We drove about 15 minutes (back towards our VRBO) and stopped at the Roatan Island Brewery. I had the meat platter (which wasn't very good) and two beers - unfortunately they didn't have the citrus IPA that I had before and really liked.</p>
