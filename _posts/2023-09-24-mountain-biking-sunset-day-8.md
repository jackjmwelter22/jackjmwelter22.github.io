<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 5:20 to go and watch the hot air balloons. We were attempting to go to a spot that the two Swiss people I met from SCUBA diving recommended. Unfortunately we realized very quickly that google maps could not be trusted! It ended up trying to get us to drive up this tiny path carved into the rock that only hikers were going up. At first I was going to try and drive it, but it became clear very quickly that there was just no way the car would fit. We looked at the map and found another route that looked promising so we circled back and went around the city to get to the other road. Fortunately we were able to get to the spot!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There was one other group of people there but there were plenty of secluded spots so Becca and I made our way to the precipice of one of the ridges, and laid out a blanket to watch the balloons. The views were great and of course we took a ton of pictures… and I did some drone flying.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/6b44pHo.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After watching the balloons for about an hour we made our way back to the hotel for a quick nap until 8:30, when we went up to the terrace for breakfast. The buffet was mostly the same as yesterday, very good and filling.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we rested a bit more in the room before I got my things ready and made my way down into town to rent a mountain bike. Of course Becca was not going to be able to mountain bike so she booked herself a Hamam (Turkish bath/massage) to do while I was away. Unfortunately they didn’t have any other day bike tours going (I knew this because I called in advance) but they did have bikes so I was able to do a “self-guided” tour. They did give me a <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1N9ua1EZqKFncvqShjHtPYD4wjog&amp;ll=38.64076777622986%2C34.85952002774098&amp;z=12">google map</a> of the bike routes and some general guidance of which ones to try.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The bike was super nice, a newer Trek mountain bike, they also gave me a fairly large bag of repair items and tools just in case I blew out a tire or something. I started off by going to the valley just north of the city. The bike ride up to the top of the ridge was strenuous, some guy tried to stop me and get me to pay for the viewpoint but after some </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Q8Bn2Oz.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>discussion he let me go. The views at the top of the ridge were nice. Trying to follow the directions using the GPS map was difficult - there are so many different paths you can take! Before making my way down the valley I took out the drone to try and get it to follow me as I went. It worked…sort of I could hear the controller hitting buttons in my backpack as I hit bumps. I got some good footage before it stopped recording.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/cecdEfG.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Snapshot from the drone footage</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/5pEeUbI.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Near the bottom of the first valley</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The ride down was a lot faster than the way up. This first valley was relatively easy. I decided the drone footage wasn’t worth the hassle so I put it back in my bag and continued to follow the GPS. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next ridge was even more of an uphill slog to get to it turns out I had to go up the same path we tried to take the car up! It was difficult enough just to ride the bike up it! In general the trails had a lot of ruts in the center you had to look out for (caused by the rain). I eventually made it to the top of the ridge overlooking the valley. This valley was called Meksender valley, at the top of the ridge I looked down at the trail and thought no way - it was way too steep for me (especially by myself), so I decided to stay on the ridge and ride over to the next valley. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The ride over wasn’t too bad as it was mostly flat and not too far - the next valley over was called the red valley, it was much more touristy, so I had to pass some people on the way down, but there were a ton of different paths to take so it mostly wasn’t an issue.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/QMoQwZV.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Top of the red valley</figcaption></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This valley was a little bit steeper and more technical so it took a bit longer to get down. After I made it down it was mostly flat and I ended up right near the town of causvin. I decided I would do one more valley (I think I had been riding for an hour and a half and about 10 miles at this point).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/WFGzo99.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Bottom of red valley</figcaption></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The last valley the shop recommended was the love valley. Unfortunately the directions were not clear and I ended up going  the wrong way and going up the love valley instead of up the road next to it (and then down). The way up the valley was quite a slog to get through. I had run out of water and was cramping which made it even more challenging. As a plus though there were some cool rock formations and I also found a cool tunnel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/w3du1Nf.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">The start of love valley</figcaption></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Thankfully I finally made it out of the valley and to the top of the ridge, there was a guy there who was selling drinks, but of course he didn’t have any water, only pomegranate juice, so I decided to keep going until I found another shop that had some water. Since I was on top of the ridge it was mostly flat. I made it to the main road and passed by the sign for Uchisar (the castle town) so I took a quick selfie. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/cScyoRk.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Made it to Uchisar</figcaption></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Just down the road from the Uchisar sign I finally found a small little restaurant so I stopped and had to waters and a flavored tea drink to rehydrate and rest.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/D6ovgtE.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Drinks at the Goerme viewpoint</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I was pretty spent at this point and was ready to meet Becca for lunch. I only had a short way back on the road to the bike shop and it was all down hill so it was super easy and went quickly. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/XktQLCX.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Starva route summary</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>All said and done the ride was just over 15 miles and 2,000 feet of elevation gain- pretty solid. It definitely would have been better with a guide but still worth doing self guided.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After I dropped the bike off at the shop I met Becca at the main strip down town and we decided to go to a restaurant called China Quick, it had a bunch of different Asian cuisine. We both had the chicken pad Thai and I also ordered some fried rice (I was super hungry).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Y9NQptj.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">So happy to have food!</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating lunch we took the car back up to the hotel and rested for a bit until ~30 minutes before sunset. We took our hotel concierge’s advice and took a short hike up to an antenna at the top of a ridge overlooking the city. The hike was super short and the views of the city were great. We stayed until a few minutes after sunset. I brought the rest of the wine up to enjoy with the views.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/mtBTykI.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Antenna tower viewpoint of the city</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the sunset we made our way to dinner at the top deck cave restaurant (we made reservations for the day before when we tried to go but they were too busy). The restaurant was in a cave which was cool and was super busy again. The food was really good. I had the Turkish ravioli and we also shared a plate of mezze (basically hummus and other dips with bread/veggies). Becca had the lamb. The food was really good and it was also the cheapest dinner we’ve had yet.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
