<p>I didn’t get much sleep tonight, as planned we woke at 11pm. I didn't feel too great because of sleeping at altitude and unfortunately, Becca was feeling terrible from the altitude, the refugee is almost 16,000’ where there is only about 55% oxygen compared to sea level so basically every two breaths is equivalent to one at sea level. She decided she wasn’t going to try the summit hike which was a smart decision because it would just get worse the higher we climbed. I think next time we have to spend more time acclimating. </p>
<p>Once I had my gear on I ate a quick breakfast, really just bread rolls with jam and then double checked everything and made sure that Becca was okay and had a way to get back down to the car (at a lower altitude) if she needed. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_4541.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1769" width="4032" height="3024"></p>
<p>We finally started hiking around 12 the first part of the hike was on rocky dirt with lots of switchbacks, I used my hiking poles. After about an hour and a half we finally made it to the glacier. There we switched out the hiking poles for an ice pick and put our crampons on our boots (basically big spikes to give you traction on the snow/ice). We also had harnesses on so that we could be tethered to our guides, in case we fell into a crevasse or slipped and started sliding off the mountain. </p>
<p>The rest of the hike up the glacier was super steep! We climbed over some steep crevasses that went down at least 100'. I lost my footing a few times and slipped but nothing serious. We ended up taking a few ~15 min rest breaks because we were hiking too fast and would reach the summit before sunrise. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/6f95016b-95b8-47a2-bc58-e4de075ae977.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1776" width="960" height="1280"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/8ba8b78d-d5b3-4811-b0b0-6d283fefab60.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1775" width="960" height="1280"></p>
<p>I don't have too many pictures on the way up because it was pitch black outside and you couldn't see much. The weather was absolutely perfect, the sky was completely clear and you could see tons of stars above and the lights from Quito below, not a bad spot to rest. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1002.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1777" width="3024" height="4032"></p>
<p>After what seemed like forever, the sun started to come up and we could finally see the summit! We made one last push up the steep slope. There was a strong smell of sulfur in the air as we got closer. Cotopaxi is the second highest peak in Ecuador and is the highest active volcano in the world! We finally summited around 5:30am.</p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/e1370cd5-db4c-4e68-b847-bf4879caecc6.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1778" width="1280" height="960"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/6216fc0d-6cf9-4230-8a9f-75c5c65bde5c.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1779" width="1280" height="960"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1007.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1780" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1018.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1781" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1005.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1787" width="10000" height="2214"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1025.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1786" width="10000" height="2318"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/c274fd85-0bc7-4dc3-b178-c487b32a179f.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1782" width="1280" height="960"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/68769301-4a42-463d-9b54-4999f9c24257.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1783" width="1280" height="960"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/1bd59e16-3804-4854-8a31-681618a9f89f.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1784" width="1280" height="960"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/458d33b4-a71c-4513-87c4-ec0d5b354804.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1785" width="1280" height="960"></p>

<p>We stayed at the top enjoying the view for about 20 minutes. Edgar pointed out all the other mountains in the area you could see. Some notable ones were Chimborazo (highest in Ecuador and farthest point from the center of the earth), Ruccu Pinchicha (which I hiked the first day), Pasachoa, Antisana, and many others. </p>
<p>Afterwards we're started our trek back down the mountain. It actually wasn't as bad as I thought it would be. The crampons made it super easy. Now that it was light out we were able to see everything we passed on our way up. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1027.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1788" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1031.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1790" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1032.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1789" width="3024" height="4032"></p>
<p>The whole way back down took less than 2 hours, even with a couple breaks. Once at the refugee we checked on Becca who still wasn't feeling great and then had some chocolate pancakes with fruit and hot chocolate, it was delicious!</p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1033.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1791" width="3024" height="4032"></p>
<p>After eating I packed up all my stuff and Becca's. Once we checked we had everything we loaded up and made the short hike back down to the car. I ended up carrying both mine and Becca's bag so she wouldn't have as hard of a time descending. At the car we loaded everything up, I took off my boots finally which felt amazing! And then we drove back down to where our car was parked. </p>
<p>After giving back all our gear we followed our guides to Machachi which was about another hour away to get to an ATM so that we could pay for the hike. The roads were much better on this drive!</p>
<p>Afterwards, we said our goodbyes and drove another hour and a half to papallacta, a famous hot springs about 45 minutes from Quito. </p>
<p>This place was awesome! It was super fancy with tons of little cabanas and hot pools outside. They also had some cold pools so you could go back and forth between them. Our cabana was by far the most luxurious place we've stayed on this trip. We ended up getting room service, I had a steak (which was only $16) and then we hung out in the hot pools. Exactly what I needed after a long day of hiking. Becca was also feeling much better now that we were lower altitude (although still 10,600'). </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_1095-1.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1799" width="2320" height="3088"></p>
<p>After relaxing for a bit we went to the spa for chocolate massage wraps...it was weird and awesome haha. Basically they give you a massage with warm melted chocolate (it's edible) and then they wrap you up with saranwrap and blankets 😂. Afterwards to get all the chocolate off you they give you a "hydro-therapy" massage which basically is spraying you with a fire hose, it felt quite nice actually! And finally they gave you a bar of chocolate to eat. Def recommend trying if you ever get the chance!</p>
<p>For dinner we went to the on-site restaurant I had llapingachos again. After we went back to our cabana and get some much needed sleep!</p>
<p>The next day we woke up and ate breakfast at 8 at the onsite restaurant. It was fried eggs, variety of bread, and fruit. Then we hung out at the hot springs until lunch. After lunch we explored a little trail from the resort that followed the river. It took about 1 hour to do the loop. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_4550.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1795" width="3088" height="2320"></p>
<p>After the hike we hung out at the hot pools till our massages at 3:30. The massages lasted about an hour and were very relaxing! We were already checked out of our room at this point so we got dinner at a nearby restaurant, I had the Ecuadorian version of BBQ ribs (they weren't very good haha).</p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_4553.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1796" width="3024" height="4032"></p>
<p>We drove the final hour to the airport, gassed up the car, and then dropped it off at the rental company. They said I should get a cost of the damaged rims in a few days so 🤞🏻 it's not too bad. </p>
<p>Finally we made it to the airport for our flights back. I was able to do my global entry interview on arrival in Miami, so next time I travel I'll get to skip the lines!</p>
<p>Overall it was an amazing trip and we can't wait for the next one! Our flights to Hawaii are booked for the end of the year!</p>
