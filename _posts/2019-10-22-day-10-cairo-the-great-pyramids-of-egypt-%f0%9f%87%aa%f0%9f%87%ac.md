Today we woke up around 8am it was a little rough getting in so late last night, but the hotel is super nice! Something kinda cool we figured out was that on our way to Egypt we ended up being in 3 different continents in like 6 hours. We went down stairs for the complimentary breakfast buffet. It was really good and had a ton of food.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2998.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1506" width="3088" height="2320">

Our tour guide picked us up at 9am from the hotel to take us over to see the great pyramids. On the way we drove through downtown Cairo. The traffic here is horrible and they also have no idea what lane lines mean 😂. On the way we passed some mosques and the "city of the dead" which is a HUGE area that is filled with old graves and tombs that date back thousands of years. It's weird because people still live in there today among all the graves.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2999.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1507" width="4032" height="3024">

After about an hour drive we made it to the pyramids. On the way our guide told us all about the history of Egypt and the pyramids. It's really fascinating! There are three main kingdoms of Egypt: the old kingdom, Middle Kingdom, and new kingdom with dark ages (times of disorder) in between each. The overall span of time is incredible. The beginning of the old kingdom dates back thousands of years to 2700 BCE. The great pyramids of Giza were made during the old kingdom which makes them even more impressive. Although there are 3 "great" pyramids, Egypt has 100s of pyramids.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3004.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1509" width="4032" height="3024">

Our guide, Ola, was great! She had been a guide for 10 years and really knew her stuff, she also was into taking lots of pictures and told us all the best spots and poses to do. We went inside the first pyramid to see the burial chamber. In order to get there you had to crawl up this tiny shaft (maybe 4x4 feet) it was really cramped and people were going out the same shaft. Luckily it wasn't super crowded otherwise it would've taken forever! The burial chamber was in the middle of the pyramid and it was ridiculously hot, it felt like a sauna, there was absolutely no air circulation. It's insane to imagine how they built it so long ago.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3033.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1510" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3037.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1511" width="3088" height="2320">

After exploring the inside of the main cave we went for a camel tour around the pyramids. This was way more fun than the one we did in Jordan! Our camel guide was super animated and took tons of photos. We were even able to "drive" the camels ourselves. My camels name was Michael Jackson 😂, Jamie's was named California and Sam's was Charlie.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3047.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1516" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3050.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1515" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3052.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1513" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3071.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1512" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3082.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1514" width="4032" height="3024">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3074.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1530" width="4032" height="3024">

After the camel ride we went to the nearby Solar boat museum. This was a restoration of a boat that they found buried next to the second pyramid. The Egyptians used to believe you took what was buried with you to the after life and they thought having a boat would help you on the journey.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3500.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1538" width="2576" height="1920">

Next we made our way down to see the Sphinx. The Sphinx is also in front of the second pyramid. The Sphinx is partly destroyed (missing its nose and beard). There are many theory's to why the nose is missing but no one really nose (get it?)😂. The beard was taken by the British as a spoil of war and is currently in the British museum.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3139.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1517" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3215.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1518" width="4032" height="3024">

We made a quick stop at a papyrus store on the way to lunch where they showed us how papyrus is actually made. Papyrus was the first form of paper and is surprisingly durable. That being said the guy showing us how it's made and doing the demonstration accidentally ripped the papyrus trying to show how strong it was 😆. After the presentation he of course tried to sell us some paintings on papyrus. They were all really cool but not really my style.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3223.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1519" width="3024" height="4032">

We also made a stop at a cotton store. Egyptian cotton is a big thing here. The shirts were mostly super colorful with crazy patterns. Sam ended up buying a T-shirt.

For lunch we stopped at a shawarma place that had a view of the pyramids. It's really crazy how close the city is to the pyramids. This shawarma was more of a grilled panini style that had cheese, it was delicious.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3226.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1520" width="4032" height="3024">

Once we finished lunch we drove to Saqqara which was about an hour south of the city. Saqqara has some of the oldest original pyramids and hieroglyphic tombs. The main pyramid there is a step pyramid that was built for the pharaoh Djoser and was originally just a rectangular structure that ended up being built up into a pyramid.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3228.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1521" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3229.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1528" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3235.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1525" width="4032" height="3024">

We also went through some the of nearby tombs to see some of the hieroglyphics which tell stories of the Gods.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3246.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1522" width="3088" height="2320">

Next we drove a few minutes to Memphis which is an open air museum that had a bunch of statues and monuments. There were two large monuments of king Ramses as well as a smaller version of the great Sphinx

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3248.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1524" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3256.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1527" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3259.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1529" width="3024" height="4032">

After seeing Memphis we started our way back to the hotel. We made one last stop at a carpet shop to see how they created rugs by hand. The process is ridiculously tedious. They create the patterns by tying colored threads in individual knots. Most rugs have 144 knots per square inch and can have over 1 million knots in a whole rug.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3260.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1526" width="3024" height="4032">

After the rug museum we made our way back to the hotel. The hotel is super huge so we decided to explore. Sam and I ended up going to the sauna and jacuzzi while Jamie went back to the room to rest. For dinner we ended up eating at the Italian restaurant. The hotel probably had 20 individual restaurants, basically anything you could think of. I ended up getting a calzone and splitting a bottle of wine with Jamie

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3262.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1523" width="3088" height="2320">

We ended up going to bed early at 10 to get some sleep. Tomorrow we have a super early flight to Aswan to start our nile cruise!
