Today we woke up around 7:30 we were flying to a city in China called Guilin today so we didn't have much planned. We decided to go check out Wan Sin Temple which was a few miles north of the hostel. We packed up all our stuff and headed to the metro. On the way we stopped at the bakery we ate at yesterday and got some more tasty pastries!

Once we got off the metro we tried following the GPS to the temple only to realize we walked about 10 minutes in the wrong direction...thanks again google! We quickly made our way back to where the temple was.

<img class="wp-image-538" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1290.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" />

Honestly the temple was less than impressive, I guess they all kinda look the same 😂. This one did have a big garden which was pretty cool I guess. Just as I was about to tell Jamie how it wasn't worth the trip a very excited Asian man yelled at us "hey hey!!!" And frantically motioned for us to come over. At first we were super confused and then realized he wanted us to join him and what in guessing is his family and friends. The dude was super happy and thankful we took a picture with them. It was a pretty funny experience that made the trip to the temple worth it 😂.

<img class="wp-image-541" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1298.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /><img class="wp-image-539" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1294.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /><img class="wp-image-540" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1293.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /> We made our way back to the downtown area to get lunch. It turned out that an old friend from grade school (who was also my setter in high school) had just got into Hong Kong! So we ended up meeting up with him for lunch at 10:30. The original place we planned to meet was closed (even though they had multiple signs saying their hours as being open at 10). Since the place we wanted to try fell through we walked around the city and found a small noodle restaurant to eat at. I got some weird looking beef with vermicelli noodles, it actually tasted a lot better than it looked!

<img class="wp-image-542" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/output-1.jpg" width="2560" height="1920" /> It was great to catch up with Nick, I hadn't seen him since high school! After eating we had to run to the airport to catch our flight so we took the metro to the airport train. The trip went smoothly and we used up exactly all the rest of our Hong Kong money on the train ticket (pretty good planning if you ask me!).

Getting through passport control they questioned my passport again! This is the third time it's happened on this trip! I can't really blame them though - here's what my passport photo looks like 😂.

<img class="wp-image-543" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1305.jpg" width="2061" height="3030" /> Back when I thought I was a surfer and long hair was cool 😎😂

No wonder they questioned me every time. It's almost time for a new one! Also Jamie for her nail clippers confiscated, on last flight they took her shampoo so I'm pretty sure they've got it out for her too.

Once we got to our gate we had some time to kill. Hong Kong airport is on its own little island and our gate had these massive windows facing the runway so I got to need out and watch planes fly in 🤓.

<img class="wp-image-544" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1307.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /> We boarded our flight on time only to learn that we were going to have to sit on the taxiway for an hour because the airspace in china was too busy? Ugh frustrating!

<img class="wp-image-550" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1312.jpg" width="4032" height="3024" /> We eventually landed in Guilin and took the shuttle bus downtown. There was some lady speaking in Chinese who I assume was trying to sell tours to see some of the different sites. The us dropped us off about a mile from the hostel. Akon the way we passed by the elephant hill but didn't stop because we were way too hungry and just wanted to drop our bags off and eat. We also walked through this really cool lake and a night market. Even though Guilin is a 'small' city there's really no such thing as a small city in china 😂, Guilin has a population of around 5 million and is a huge tourist destination (mostly Asian tourists) because of its really cool landscape (hopefully I'll get some pictures that do it justice in the next few days).

<img class="wp-image-548" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1314.jpg" width="2576" height="1932" /><img class="wp-image-547" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1317.jpg" width="720" height="1280" /><img class="wp-image-549" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1318.jpg" width="3024" height="4032" /> Once we got to the hostel we got a quick meal there and then went out to walk around the market some more. We also booked our tour of the rice terraces for a day trip tomorrow. The market was similar to most of the other ones we've seen. I ended up getting this bubble tea from a stand that didn't speak any English. I don't really know what it was but it tasted really good! it's much less common to speak English here is seems which has made it a little more difficult to communicate. We made it back to our hostel to get some rest. Our hostel is actually really cool it's on the rooftop of a building in the middle of downtown with a great view of the city! Tomorrow we leave for our tour at 7am, another early morning! Today we only did 7.7 miles of walking, gotta step it up tomorrow!
