<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 7:30 and were greeted with a nice sunrise over The valley. We are a quick typical breakfast at 8:30 and then Jamie, Becca, and I drove up the road for a quick hike through the gorge. The other 3 in our group weren’t interested in hiking so they just slept in and waited at the hotel. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike was really fun, it was a really tight slot canyon/gorge that had some difficult parts you had to maneuver around. A few points you had to wedge yourself between the walls to avoid going through the shin deep water. </p>

<p>We made it about halfway down the gorge (which is a loop) and then turned around since we didn’t have enough time for the whole loop. On the way back we took a short cut and had to cross a river. Said ended up trying to build us a small rock bridge to help get us across without getting our shoes soaked 😂. </p>

<p>After crossing the river we climbed up to the van and then made our way back to the hotel to get the others and then continue on towards Marrakech. We drove for about an hour before stopping at the “Moroccan Hollywood” in Ourzazate. The place had tons of different movie sets where they filmed over 400 movies and TV shows including Gladiator, Game of Thrones, Cleopatra, Ben Hurr, and a ton more. The guide was really funny and even filmed some scenes for us 😂. </p>

<p>After the movie set we drove shortly to another stop to get some lunch just outside Ait Ben Haddou. For lunch I had kefta (basically a meatball with different meat mixed together). It was good!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2319,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch we walked across the street to cost the kasbah, which is the term for a fortified village or castle. The village was perched on a hill in the middle of the oasis riverbed. The actually filmed a lot of the fighting scenes from gladiator right in front of the village. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After hiking back down we returned to the van again for another drive up through the Atlas Mountains to Marrakech. We made a stop at a nice panoramic vista at the top of the pass and then a final stop for the bathroom before finishing the drive to Marrakech. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2321,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Arriving in Marrakech we said our goodbyes to Said and our driver Ibrahim and then walked with our Airbnb host to our Riad inside the Medina. The Medina was very nice and modern. After settling in we decided to try and find a grocery store, unfortunately the first store we tried to get to with google maps didn’t exist so we ended up walking all the way across the maze of the Medina to a Carrefour where we got some groceries. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After making it back to our Riad we ate and went to bed. Another exhausting day!</p>
