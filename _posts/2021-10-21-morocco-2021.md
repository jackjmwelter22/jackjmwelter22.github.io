Another last minute trip! We were originally intending to go to Turkey for this trip, but due to some changes with our flights (they switched us to a 48 hour + travel time) we decided to call an audible and go to Morocco instead (Thanks again Covid)! Coincidentally my sister and her friends were already planning a trip to Morocco so we're joining up with them. The switch wasn't without it's own drama, less than 24 hours before our flight the government of Morocco suspended all flights from the UK, Germany, and Netherlands. This caused my sister and all her friends to have to rebook their flights so that they connected from Istanbul and Becca and I happened to be connecting in Amesterdam so we had to rebook our flight there too so that we had a layover in Paris instead...what a mess.
