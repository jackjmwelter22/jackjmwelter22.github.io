<p>We got extremely lucky with the weather, hurricane Ian missed us completely and left us with amazing weather all day Saturday. We had an amazing time with all of our close family and friends celebrating the weekend. Which brings us to now, our mini honeymoon.</p>

<p>We put our names in the lottery to hike half dome (a popular hike in Yosemite national park) and my name got picked! This spurred planning and that’s how we ended up with this mini-honeymoon trip after our wedding to Yosemite and Sequoia national parks in Northern California. </p>

<p><strong>Day 0 - Monday</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After saying goodbye to our family and friends on Sunday, we woke up early to catch our 7am flight from Savannah to Charlotte and then from Charlotte to San Francisco. All the flights went super smoothly, we made it to SFO ahead of schedule and picked up our car from the airport.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We were hungry from flying all day so we made a quick stop at in-n-out for some animal style burgers and fries and then continued the 4.5 hour drive to Yosemite. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made it Curry village (in The Valley of Yosemite) around 5:30pm to check in to our heated tent :). We settled into our tent then explored the village for a bit before showering and getting ready for bed (we were exhausted).</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The start of the hike was dark so we had our head lamps on. The first interesting site was Vernal falls (on the mist trail which we got on right before they closed for contstruction for the day). </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<p><strong>Day 2 - Wednesday - El Capitan</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 5:15, got dressed and ate some more egg and sausage skillet. Once we were ready we loaded up in the car and drove about 2 miles further down and on the other side of The Valley to start our hike.</p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once we got to the top of the ridge we hiked over to the to of El Capitan (the famous wall that lots of rock climbers climb). The views from the top were nice, Lots of good views of half dome and further down The Valley. It was around 10:30 at the top, we rested for a bit and ate some snacks before making our way back across the ridge to the next stop.</p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The top of the falls was really cool! Nice views and there was a little river with some pools. We ended up relaxing at the top near the pools. I ended up taking a dip in the water, it was freezing, but refreshing! Also while laying out to dry/warm up I accidentally dropped my phone in the water. Luckily it was super clear and not that deep I was able to retrieve it without an issue and the phone was fine.</p>

<p><strong>Day 3 - Yosemite and on to Sequoia </strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we slept in till 8, had some breakfast and got everything packed up in the car to check out. We did a really short hike to mirror lake. Unfortunately the lake was basically completely dried out, but there were some really good views of half dome.</p>

<p>We got back to Yosemite village and drove to the Yosemite village store and walked to the museum. The museum was cool, the most interesting section to me was how the granite formed (it was pushed up out of the ground over time by volcanic activity) and also the section on rock climbing. After the museum we made a quick stop at the ansel Adam photo house (right next door) and then picked up a few snacks from the store and Becca got herself a Yosemite hat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next we drove down The Valley to the El Capitan viewing turnoff and walked the short walk over to the wall. Seeing it up close was impressive, I can’t imagine how difficult it would be to climb the whole thing (Becca and I downloaded the free solo movie to watch later about the guy who climbed it, all 3,000’, without any rope or aid).</p>

Our first hike was to a place called the watch tower.</p>
