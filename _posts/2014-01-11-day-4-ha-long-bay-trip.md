<p style="text-align:justify;">Day 4 was another early start, we had to be up for a 4 hour bus and boat trip to get to our final destination, Cat Ba Island. After breakfast we took a quick taxi ride to the bus station to start our trip. The bus was much nicer than I expected and was only about half full (winter is the slow season for tourism). The bus took us to this huge industrial port with shipping containers everywhere and we boarded the speedboat. The boat ride was nice and only took 30-45min to get to the island. One last bus to the main town and we were at our hotel. The hotel was very nice and right in the center of the town. After getting settled in we are some lunch and decided to rent motorbikes.</p>

<div style="text-align:justify;">The motorbikes were awesome! They only cost 4 dollars to rent for the day and about 2-3 dollars in gas. There was no paperwork to sign or anything, the guy renting them to us didn't even get our names! Needless to say it was much less strict than the US. Anyway the first stop we made was cannon fort which had awesome views of the island.</div>

This was in the middle of the island and is literally a hospital type bunker that was built inside a cave during the war.
