Today I woke up around 8:30 and got all my stuff packed up and ready to go. Pat and Sarah slept a little longer and we ended up leaving the house at around 10:30 and made one final stop at the nearby family bakery for breakfast. I had a really good ham and cheese bagel and pastry filled with chocolate. After the quick breakfast we drove our ATVs to Athinios port which was close by but very busy. There was a huge line of buses trying to get down so it ended up taking like 20 min. We ended up leaving the ATVs parked at the bottom of the road to be picked up and then waited for our ferry.

The port area was a complete cluster, nothing was organized at all. Luckily we were able to find someone who knew what was going on and made it on the correct ferry. The ferry itself was actually really nice with big seats and lots of legroom, and it was really fast! We left a little after 12:15 and arrived around 1:30 at the Naxos port.

Once we arrived we walked across the street to meet our host who took us on a quick car ride to our Airbnb. The Airbnb we got was the best so far! It's in an awesome location close to the city and beach and is really big with an awesome rooftop! After unpacking and settling in we made a 5 min walk to the nearby Saint George beach.

The beach was the best we've seen in Greece so far! It actually had sand instead of pebbles/rock and also crystal clear water. Also the weather this whole trip has been perfect which made it perfect for relaxing on the beach.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2394.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1185" width="3088" height="2320">

After a couple hours of relaxing I decided to go for a 3 mile run on the beach then take a dip in the water. The water is a little cold but felt really good after the run.

After the beach we went to grocery store to pick up some breakfast food and of course some more wine! Then we made our way back to Airbnb to shower and then went to a nearby restaurant that our host recommended. I had the "special" chicken which was chicken and tomatoes, taziki sauce, and fries. It seems like just about everything in Greece comes with fries, but hey I'm not complaining!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190903_190406.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1186" width="2160" height="3840">

After dinner we got lost on our way back to our Airbnb but luckily found it again and we're able to get to the roof just before sunset to enjoy the amazing views of the city and water.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190903_200516.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1189" width="3840" height="2160"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190903_194608.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1187" width="2688" height="1512"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2398.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1188" width="4032" height="3024">

After chatting for a bit we decided to walk back into town and get some gelato! We went to a restaurant called Waffle House and I got the waffle cream flavor, it was delish. After eating we went back (without getting lost this time) and hung out at the Airbnb watching some Netflix.

Tomorrow the plan is to rent a scooter and ATV to go hike Mount Za then hit the beach again!
