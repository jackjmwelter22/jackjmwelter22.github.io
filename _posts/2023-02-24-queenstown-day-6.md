<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I woke up around 6a. Jamie messaged me to see if I wanted to do a quick hike with her and Nate before they left. Everyone was asleep so I went by myself to meet them at the trailhead (which was only about a 10 min walk from our Airbnb). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike was the Queenstown hill walkway hike and took about 40 minutes to get up to the “basket of dreams” (a weird sculpture thing). The view was pretty nice from there of the lake. We hiked back down and then Jamie and Nate drove me back to the Airbnb since I had to leave shortly for our bungy jump. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/kZUBekz.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After meeting back up with Becca, Pat, and Sarah I had a quick breakfast and then got ready. We left around 8:30 to go bungy jumping! We went to the Kuwarau river bridge for the jump. It’s a super picturesque bridge with a 43m jump. Becca and I decided to jump tandem (which we’ve never done before). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The jump was a blast! Not as scary as the others I’ve done (probably because it was shorter and over water but nevertheless thrilling! We wanted to get our heads dunked but I guess the bungee was new so they weren’t failed in to getting the settings right and we ended up about a half meter above the water at our lowest. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the jump we decided to do the “swing” which was another thrill seeking activity with the same company (also the same ones who did the Auckland sky tower jump). The swing is a 300m swing that is strung across a valley in Nevis. We took an off-road  shuttle bus about 15min to get there. </p>

<p>That swing was a blast! Not scary at all but a ton of fun. After swinging we got out of the harnesses and bussed back down to the bridge where are car was parked. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the car I did a little bit of droning to get some cool arial shots before we loaded back up. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove the car to the airport, I had to drop off our rental and pick up our other car rental. The short story is I messed up the booking dates and it ended up being cheaper to book a one day rental than add a day to my other rental, it was a pain but it worked out smoothly and the newer rental was nicer!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After swapping cars we were able to get a last minute spot for the shot over jet boat tour so we drove over there around 1:15 and got checked in for our 1:30 tour. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The jet boat was a ton of fun! Basically it’s a super charged boat with 2 massive engines putting out 700 Ho with a very flat hill that allows it to glide and spin across the water. It’s highly maneuverable and super fast. The tour took up through the tight shot over canyon barely missing rocks and walls and doing 360s! Lots of fun, would recommend if you’re in the area. </p>
