<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 6a and ate some breakfast at the Airbnb. We loaded up the car and hit the road for Milford sound at 7. The drive out took about an hour and a half. It’s a two lane road the whole way with little passing opportunities which made it slower but we still made good time. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We arrived at 8:30 and parked, our boat tour was at 10:45 so we had some time to walk around and explore. Becca and I did the viewpoint hike, which wasn’t much of a view 😂. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Then we did the peninsula hike. The peninsula hike was really cool because the tide was low we were able to go way out on the rocks and get some cool pictures as well as watch the planes and helicopters land (there must have been like 20 or more of them, probably all returning from the tour loop). </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After hiking around we went to the dock and waited to board our cruise. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When we originally planned this part of the trip we booked a kayak and cruise tour, however two days before the tour we got an email saying they canceled the kayak part of the trip because the guide got sick :(. The nice part was we got. Free cruise (usually $100/person) but we didn’t get to kayak. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The cruise itself was ok. The weather was great and the views were spectacular but it was very touristy and not super adventurous:). The drive did pull the boat up to a couple of waterfalls and soaked everyone who was on the front deck of the ship. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/LK26kb0.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Fairy falls</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Luckily Becca and I managed to stay relatively dry. Some fun facts about Milford sound - it’s not actually a sound it’s a fjord, the main difference being that a fjord is carved out by glaciers instead of rivers.  </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We travelled all the way through the sound to the outlet at the Tasman sea and the returned back through the sound. Besides the waterfalls and fjords we also saw penguins, dolphins, and seals. Overall a fun tour but I’m glad it was free!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/HnSWJrm.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the tour we decided to stop by the airport and we’re able to book a helicopter tour through the fjords on top of one of the glaciers!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We had about an hour to kill before the flight so we went back by the water and had lunch. We met back at the airport at 2:30 for our tour. It was my first helicopter ride and it was awesome! It felt like droning but in real life instead of a screen. The pilot, Andrew, took us up through the fjords and around some of the alpine lakes before landing us on the Tutoko glacier. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the flight we drove ~1.5 hours back to Te Anau. We stopped at the Airbnb for a while before heading to do the second escape room at 6pm. This room was about the digging of the hormel tunnel and was just as good as the first room. We didn’t use any hints or clues and got our in just over 45 minutes! I won’t spoil any puzzles but I definitely recommend checking it out if you’re in the area. We met the owner who designed and built the rooms as a side/passion project. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the room we walked back down the main strip and decided to get dinner at an Indian restaurant cleverly named Thai Anau. I had the butter chicken and garlic naan. Both were good! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we got back to the Airbnb and decided to book a canyoning trip for tomorrow in Wanaka. This meant we have to wake up early so we got everything we could prepacked so we can eat and leave quickly tomorrow morning. </p>
