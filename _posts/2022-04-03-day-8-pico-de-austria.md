<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 7am, grabbed some food from the breakfast buffet, and met our guide and driver in the hotel lobby at 7:30. We left La Paz and drove about 2.5 hours into the mountains north of the city in an area known as Condoriri (named because the mountains resemble a condor). At least half of the drive was on a super bumpy dirt road out into the mountains.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When we finally arrived, the area was very picturesque and had lots of wild life, alpacas, lamas, donkeys, and rabbits. The hike was basically all uphill but the views were fantastic. We passed by some small waterfalls early in the hike.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After about 45 minutes we made a quick stop at a small refugee next to a lake that our guide told us had a bunch of trout and salmon in it. We took a quick break for some coca tea and then continued on the hike. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As we hiked higher up the mountain the terrain became less dirt/plants and more shale rocks. It took us around 4 hours to reach the summit at 5,350 m or 17,552 feet. Becca some had stomach issues on the way, but was able to push through it and make it to the summit a few minutes behind me. The views from the top were awesome we could see the whole valley from where we parked all the way to lake Titicaca. Becca and I had went ahead of Pat and Sarah so we waited at the summit for ~30 minutes until Pat and Sarah arrived. We took some more pictures as a group and enjoyed the views for a few minutes before starting our descent back down the mountain. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Becca and I descended faster so we arrived back to the refugee first. At the refugee we had lunch finally, it consisted of guacamole (which was delicious), pita bread, tuna, cheese, juice, and coca tea. After we finished eating we walked the rest of the way down back to the van, we made it just as the sun set and the temperature started to drop. There were some little kids from the nearby community by our van so we gave them some candy, they were really shy but grateful. By the time we left it was dark. There were some stubborn llamas sleeping in the road on our way out and the driver had to get out and shoo them off the road so we could continue. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We finally arrived back at the hotel around 9pm, we ended up eating some dinner at the upstairs restaurant again since we were too tired to go out and get something else. Pat and Sarah took their COVID tests since they were leaving tomorrow (a day earlier than us). We said goodbye to them and went to bed. </p>
