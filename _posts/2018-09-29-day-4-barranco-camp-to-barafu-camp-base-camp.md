Today was even worse sleep than yesterday! I first woke up around 11:30....pm and then was pretty much in and out of sleep the rest of the night. We finally got up around 6 for tea and then 6:30 for breakfast. For breakfast we had the usual pouridge, fruit, and toast. Patryk is notorious for being late so it wasn’t a surprise that we ended up waiting an extra 25 min for him to get ready to go, our guide was starting to get mad 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1204.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-875" height="2320" width="3088">

Our first stop today was karanga camp, to get there we had to climb over this massive wall called barranco wall which required some actual climbing using your hands which was pretty fun. It was crazy to see the porters running up the wall with a bunch of stuff easily. On top of the wall had some pretty sick views

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1218.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-876" height="3024" width="4032">

After the wall we hiked another 3 hours or so to the camp. Along the way another Chinese person came up to pat to ask him for a snack. Idk why but it seems like all the Chinese people love him haha.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1226.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-877" height="4032" width="3024">

We got to karanga camp earlier so we decided to keep going to barafu camp instead of stopping for lunch. On the way to barafu camp it started pouring rainy which really sucked. Even though I had on all my “waterproof” clothes, everything got soaked! As we started to get higher the rain turned to snow/icy pellets which honestly was better than rain. We hiked for what felt like forever until we finally got to the camp. We arrived a little earlier than expected so our tents weren’t ready yet so we waited in the ranger tent attempting to dry ourselves. Once the tents were ready we had some popcorn and tea and then went back to our tent to relax.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1228.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-878" height="2320" width="3088"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1242.jpg" class="wp-image-879 size-full" height="2320" width="3088">

At 5:30 we had dinner which consisted of cucumber soup, pasta, and pineapples. After we went to bed. “Tomorrow” we’re waking up at 11 to get ready, eat “breakfast” at 11:30, and begin the summit hike at midnight! Reallyyyy hoping my clothes dry.
