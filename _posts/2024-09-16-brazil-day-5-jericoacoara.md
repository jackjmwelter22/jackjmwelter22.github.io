<p class="has-text-align-left">We got woken up around 10:45 pm by the phone ringing in our room. Earlier I had asked the front desk about getting a to-go breakfast for the morning, and they decided to call at nearly 11pm and let me know they had it ready and could bring it up. I asked if we could pick it up in the morning and the guy said okay. However, about 45 min later the lights in our room flashed and there was a knock on the door - a guy with our breakfast…at 11:30pm ugh.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unfortunately both the phone call and the knocking woke up Bennett, and and made waking up at 4 am for our flight even more painful. When we finally woke up for real I ate quickly (Becca didn't want anything), it was mostly fruit and some pastries (not worth the late night wake up!), then we got our car and got packed and drove about 20 min to the airport. Everywhere here has a “priority line by law” if you’re pregnant or have an infant or are handicapped - which is awesome (traveling with an infant has its perks!). So we went in that line for checkout. Unfortunately the priority line was extremlyyy slow! Otherwise though all the flights went smoothly today (thank god!). Our first flight was only an hour over to São Paulo. </p>

<p>In the car! It was nice and spacious and our diver was very friendly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made a quick stop about 30 minutes into the drive to have a bathroom break and to get some food. We picked some snacks for the ride, nothing too substantial- Becca and I still weren’t feeling the greatest. Overall the drive went smoothly. We made one more stop just before the park entrance to deflate the tires (the park is all sand/dunes and no paved roads). We also had to stop at the entrance to Jericoacoara city to pay the tourism tax. We finally made it to our hotel around 6:30 pm. We unpacked, showered (the shower was amazing - the most water pressure I've ever experienced), and then relaxed before going to bed.</p>
