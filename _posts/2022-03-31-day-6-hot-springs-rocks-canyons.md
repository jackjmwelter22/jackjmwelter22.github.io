<p>Today we woke up super early to leave at 4am. It didn’t help that I had some stomach issues (from the spaghetti?) and woke up at like 11:30pm. Also I checked my blood oxygen level with my watch and hit a new record 79% 😧 (we were at ~15k feet) Anyway, we drove around 45 minutes to the sol de mañana geysers. They’re called geysers but they were really more like steam vents.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<p>They were cool to see, we took a few pictures and made our way another 30 minutes to the next stop the auguas termales de polques (a hot spring). It was around 5am and it was still dark so the stars were out in full force and it was awesome to see. We hung out in the hot spring for around and hour and a half to watch the sunrise. </p>

<p>After the hot springs we changed and went to the nearby building for breakfast. Today was pancakes they were pretty decent. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast we waited a bit longer, Pat, Sarah, Becca, and I played a quick board game. Then we loaded up the car (around 9am) to head towards our next stop. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove about an hour and a half, passing out through the park checkpoint, to get to the green lagoon. The lagoon looked mostly white near us but farther off you could see it looked really green. Apparently when it’s windy it’s looks more green. The reason the water is green because it contains arsenic, copper, and silver…don’t drink it!</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After pictures we loaded up for our next stop. We stopped at a town called mallku for lunch. There were a bunch of lamas around the town. Lunch was pretty bad it consisted of rice, canned corn, and the most un appetizing fish I’ve ever seen. When we asked what type of fish our guide said “normal” lol. We joked and asked if it came from one of the nearby lakes (they’re all filled with metals and other nasty chemicals) and our guide said maybe hah! I had some stomach issues the night before so I didn’t have any, but Becca was brave enough to try some. I think the guide realized we didn’t like the fish so he got us some ham and bread to eat sandwiches. After dinner the guide also brought us some beer for “dessert” and then we loaded up the car and left for our next stop around 12:45</p>

<p>The next stop was a rock formation that looked like a camel. The resemblance was actually pretty good! We were able to climb on top of the camel rock and take some cool pictures before driving another 10 minutes to the next stop. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This stop was called “Italians lost” it was a bunch of tall rock formations that were fun to climb. It got its name when two Italians went climbing here and lost their passports so they went to the nearby village for help and the whole village came and helped them find their passports. We ended up climbing one of the bigger formations, it was a really fun and easy climb! We left around 2 pm to head to our next stop. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/XHMLysL.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<p>We drove for around 30 minutes to the trailhead of hidden lake. The hike to the lake was only like 10 min and then at the lake there were more rocks that formed a sort of canyon around the lake that was really fun to climb around! There were a bunch of lamas on one end on the lake on the grass. </p>

<p>We loaded up the car again to head back to Uyuni to catch our night bus back to La Paz. On the way we made a quick pit stop again in San Cristóbal. We arrived in Uyuni around 7pm, we picked up the bags we left at the tour office and then went across the street to a restaurant that had showers. We quickly showered, repacked and walked about 3 blocks to the bus station. I was finally able to catch up on most of my blogs (the reception was terrible on the tour) and then we made our way back to La Paz leaving a little after 8pm. </p>
