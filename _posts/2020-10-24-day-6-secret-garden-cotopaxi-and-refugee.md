<p>Today we woke up kind of early, we have a really cool room called “the bird house that has a nice panorama of the surrounding mountains. It was a bit cloudy so you couldn't see the mountains that well. This hostel/Airbnb also has little hobbit rooms you can stay in and a bunch of alpacas that roam around the property. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_4525.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1747" width="4032" height="3024"></p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_0949.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1758" width="4032" height="3024">After playing with the alpacas it was time for breakfast (7:30) . I had scrambled eggs, toast, and yoghurt. All the meals are included with the price of the Airbnb, probably because there's no where else you can easily go haha. </p>
<p>On our drive to the Airbnb yesterday we managed to arrange a last minute trip to hike to the summit of Cotopaxi. Apparently you usually need to give them 3 days notice but because someone else was already going it worked out. We started drinking lots of water in preparation so the altitude wouldn't affect us as much. </p>
<p>After breakfast we relaxed and got our things organized and then went on a little hike from our hostel to check our two nearby waterfalls. The hike was really fun and short with plenty of scrambling and climbing over rocks. The it was a short hike that took like and hour and a half but the waterfalls were cool!</p>

<p>When we got back we explored around the Airbnb some more, it's a super cool location with amazing views of the surrounding mountains including Cotopaxi. They had these really massive nets that were sort of like hammocks that you could lay in and enjoy the views, they were super comfy. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_0956.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1762" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_0986.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1763" width="3088" height="2320"></p>
<p>For lunch (1pm) we had a traditional potato cheese dish thing with sausages that was really good, I think it's called llapingachos. After lunch our guides came so we could try on all the gear we needed to borrow for the hike. It's was super convenient that they provided everything we needed. Once we confirmed that everything fit, we followed them in our car to a parking lot close to the mountain so we wouldn't have to drive all the way back. We drove the rest of the way with our two guides, Edgar and Segundo, and another hiker, Lillia to the refugee parking lot. The roads were very rough but our poor little car somehow managed. From the parking lot we had to hike up for about 45 minutes with all of our gear to reach the Refugee. The place is a huge lodge that can fit up to like 80-100 hikers but we were the only ones staying tonight! (Thanks again COVID). </p>

<p>We had a quick dinner in the refugee around 5:30. It was soup, salad, chicken, and rice. We also discussed the plan with our guides, we were going to go to sleep at 7 and then wake up at 11 to get ready and start hiking at midnight. The hike would take all night and then we would arrive at the summit to catch the sunrise!</p>
