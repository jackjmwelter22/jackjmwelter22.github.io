<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today was another slow morning. We woke up around ate and went downstairs to try and get breakfast at the cafe. The host was super rude to us again so we ended up walking across the street to get breakfast groceries and snacks to eat in our apartment instead.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast I spent some time catching up on the blog while the others rested and relaxed. Around 11:30 we made our way 30 minutes by car to do some more klettersteigs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The two we were doing are called <a href="https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/mein-land-dein-land-klettersteig/">Mein Land Dien Land</a> and <a href="https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/leadership-klettersteig/">Leadership</a>. They’re both on the same cliff so we figured they would be easy to find… that was wrong!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We parked the car in a parking lot near a hotel and followed the topographic map to the best of our ability to try and find the start of the first Klettersteig. We made a few wrong turns and had to climb off trail (there really weren’t great trails to start). We eventually ran into a big group of Germans that were also going to the Klettersteig, so we followed them to the start.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The whole rout was pretty short so we finished it quickly. Pat and Sarah were slow to get their gear on and were stuck behind the large group so Becca and I decided to try and get back to the beginning of the Klettersteig. That was a bad idea, we ended up on the side of the mountain that was really steep and hard to traverse.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We eventually made it to the start and did the run again. By the time we were done Becca was tired and didn’t want to do the second Klettersteig. Also it started to rain a bit :(. Becca waited at the top under the trees while I hiked down and across to try and find the second Klettersteig. Luckily Pat had followed the German group and was able to send me his location. I had to do some more scrambling and off trail hiking but I eventually made it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The route was short but fun and had another small slot canyon type segment. It was raining harder the whole time which wasn’t as fun. I didn’t take any photos of this one because of the rain but I didn’t get GoPro video. I met back up with everyone at the top of the route and we hiked back down to the car.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove back to the apartment and hung out for a bit. Everyone was tired so rest was needed! Around 6:30 we walked into town and went to dinner at the same restaurant we ate at the night before. This time I had the pork schnitzle, which was really good.</p>
