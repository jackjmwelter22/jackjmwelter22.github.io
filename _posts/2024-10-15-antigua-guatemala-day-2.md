<p>I woke up pretty early today, around 4 am - to be fair it’s 6 am Savannah time! I wrote the blog and researched some things to do before waking everyone up at 7. Once everyone was up we made our way upstairs to the hotel rooftop terrace for breakfast. I ended up getting the Guatemalan breakfast which consisted of eggs guava and cheese on a hard flat taco,  it was pretty good!</p>

<p>After breakfast we went back to the room and got our stuff ready and walked to the tour office to start our atv tour. Jimmy and I ended up going to the main square and ended up finding a store that sold sunglasses since we both forgot to bring ours. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We were doing the Sky High ATV tour today with our guide Alejandro from Simoon tours. We got checked in, got our helmets and then walked a few blocks to where the ATVs were parked. After a brief explanation we got on the ATVs and started the tour. It was just the 3 of us and the guide which was nice, we were able to go a lot faster and stay however long we wanted at each stop. </p>

<p>The first stop was a place called Alta Mira. Basically a “park” with a bunch of photo ops and random things to do. We had to park our ATVs and take a truck some of the way up because it “was too steep” seemed fine to me but I’m sure someone got hurt or something so they don’t do it anymore.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the top was the entrance to the park and so nice views of the town and volcanoes. Apparently in Guatemala they like their outstretched hands (there were 3 of them at this park) that you walk out onto to take a picture. </p>

After hanging out for a bit we met our guide took the truck back to the ATVs and drove about 5 minutes to the next stop for lunch at an avocado farm called earth lodge. Again we had to park our ATVs amd walk a good ways down to the farm.</p>

The menu had lots of vegan options which wasn’t a surprise, jimmy and I got the avocado chicken tacos which were actually amazing and fries which were also really good! I also had a smoothie which unfortunately wasn’t that good. </p>

<p>The final stop was a place lower down the mountain called cerro de la Cruz. It was a nice viewpoint of the city with a big stone cross that was placed there in the 1930s. It replaced an earlier wooden cross, which symbolized the Christianization of the region during the Spanish colonial era.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One of the volcanos next to the city is called the agua or water volcano and used to have a big lagoon at the top but after an earthquake a big crack caused the water to flow down closer to the city. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The other volcano fuego (next to acatenango) had its last major eruption in 2018 which caused hot lava, gas, and ash to flow down the slope and ended up killing hundreds of people in the nearby city. According to our guide the people had a few days warning to evacuate however many chose to stay (kinda sounds like what happens with hurricanes in the us), either way very sad.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After some pictures we got back in the ATVs and drove into town. We walked back to the tour office to return the helmets and then went back to the room for a bit to relax.</p>
