Today we woke up super early to head out for the safari. We left the hotel around 5am to drive to the town of Arusha which is about an hour and a half from where we were in Moshi. We got to Arusha around 6:45 and found out from he safari company that we wouldn't be leaving until 8:30! We could've slept at least another hour!

Arusha is a much bigger city than Moshi but still small for US standards. Since we had some time to kill we walked a couple blocks to a nearby breakfast place to eat and chill. I had eggs and sausage. Apparently there was a race going on in the city which we could see from the restaurant. Finally around 8 we made our way back to the safari office and loaded up into the car.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1328.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-906" height="3024" width="4032">

It was us and two girls from Spain going. The drive to tarangire took a little over two hours so I took a nap in the car.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1331.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-907" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1333.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-908" height="3024" width="4032">

Once we got there the drive got the permits and then we went on a game drive through the park. The vehicle we rode in is pretty cool it had a ceiling that popped open so you could stand up and see all the animals. On the trip we saw elephants, giraffes, zebras, water bucks, impalas, pumbas, wildebeest, pumbas, and ostriches. It was cool to see the animals up close in they're natural environment.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1366.jpg" class="wp-image-909 size-full" height="2320" width="3088"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1382.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-910" height="2320" width="3088"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1384.jpg" class="wp-image-911 size-full" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1386.jpg" class="wp-image-912 size-full" height="3024" width="4032">

We ate lunch in the park which consisted of chicken, a burger, and banana. There were monkeys at the lunch spot who would run up and try and steal your food so you had to be careful! After lunch we went on another game drive. The landscape here is really beautiful but also super dusty! Mostly because it's he dry season. After we drove to the campsite called panorama view, which actually had a pretty good view! 😂 since were only doing a 3 day safari we had to transfer to another safari car.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1378.jpg" class="wp-image-913 size-full" height="2320" width="3088">

We met our new driver and other people in the new car who were all really nice. For dinner we had the typical rice with meat. After dinner we went to sleep. We're sleeping in these weird igloo looking huts. I'm not complaining though because we were supposed to be sleeping in tents. Tomorrow we start again at 7am.
