<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the village one of the tribesmen gave us a demonstration and told us about the culture of his tribe. One of the scared items they have is called a poporo, it’s a dried out gourd that they give to boys when they become a man. They fill the poporo with crushed sea shells. The man will then chew coca leaf and use his spit and the crushed shells to create a plaster like coating on the outside of the poporo. You can tell how “wise” someone is by the size of their poporo. </p>
