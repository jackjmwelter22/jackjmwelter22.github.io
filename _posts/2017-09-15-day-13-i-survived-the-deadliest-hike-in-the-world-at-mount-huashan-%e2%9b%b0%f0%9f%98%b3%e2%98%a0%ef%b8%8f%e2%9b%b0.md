Today I woke up to Jamie shaking me at 6:00am I was a little hung over from last night (it was only $1.50 US for a big beer 🍻!). I slowly gathered my things and got ready for the day. The plan was to hike Mount Huashan, the one of the largest sin China and also know as "the deadliest" in the world!

We walked to the metro which took us to the train station. While trying to figure out where to by the train tickets (the first 3 offices were closed) we ran into a young couple (David and Noelle) from New York City who were also trying to hike the mountain. Together we were able to find the ticket office and caught the 7:25 bullet train to the mountain.

The train took about 40 minutes which was just enough time for me to finish my blog for the day before. When we arrived we took the free shuttle which brought us to the base of the entrance to the mountain (only a few minutes from the train).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1649.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-693" width="4032">

We bought our tickets for the park entrance and got on yet another bus which took us to the north peak cable car. It is possible to hike up the mountain from the base. Our hostel receptionist said we had enough time to hike it...yea right! To get up to where the cable car took you was over a 5 hour hike and literally tens of thousands of stairs! And then you still have to hike the peaks when you get there! So we opted to take the cable car up with David and Noelle.

The views of the mountain were absolutely incredible. We were lucky that the day wasn't too cloudy which allowed us to see for miles.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1653.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-695" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1655.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-696" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1659.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-694" width="4032">

The mountain has a trail that loops around to the four highest peaks. We started at the North peak then made our way to the East, South, and West peaks.

The hike up to the North peak was thousands of steps...definitely a good leg workout! Hopefully I'll gain a couple inches in my vertical by the time I get home 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1662.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-699" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1663.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-700" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1673.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-701" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1674.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-702" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1675.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-698" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1678.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-697" width="4032">

There were a ton of awesome viewpoints along the hike. Some parts of the hike were super steep and you actually had to climb up what was essentially a ladder that was carved into the rock face...don't slip! Also on the way up I had to use the bathroom and in China they don't have western toilets they use what Jamie and I like to refer to as squatty pottys. Essentially they're just holes in the ground...so that was a fun experience 😷.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1679.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-706" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1681.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-704" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1685.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-703" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1690.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-705" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1693.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-707" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1698.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-708" width="3024">

At the top of the peak there was an overhang that you could climb out too and get your picture on. It was exhilarating, my feet were dangling over 6000' in the air.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1703.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-710" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1704.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-709" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1707.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-711" width="4032">

There were many more great views and of course we were asked a few times to get our picture with a few more asians 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1716.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-712" width="4032">

The highlight of the hike was when we hiked the "plank in the sky" which was by the South Peak.

The plank in the sky is where Mount Huashan gets the reputation for being the deadliest hike in the world! By the time we made it to the plank we had hiked for at least 2-3 hours so my legs were already exhausted and on the verge of shaking not only that but I was still not feeling he greatest from all the drinking the night before 😂 definitely not the best conditions for this section of the hike!

The plank walk was essentially 2-4 2x4s nailed into the side of the mountain that you walked across to get to a semicolon spot in the mountain. The problem is it's crowded! So even though it's not a super long distance it takes a while because everyone that goes out has to come back the same way!

We got our harness which cost about $5 US (it went around your shoulders and I'm really not sure how effective it would be if you fell...I wasn't going to find out!). On the way down you had to stop and wait for people coming back up, it was torture! I would have much preferred to go through it and come back fast, sitting up there contemplating all the things that could go wrong and how bad it would hurt to fall made it worse!

Surprisingly Jamie hiked the plank like a boss, even though she's afraid of heights she was less framed out than I was! She was the one that took all the pictures, I was way to nervous I'd drop my phone so I didn't take it out 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1726.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-715" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1731.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-716" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1769.jpg" height="960" class="wp-image-717" width="1280">

Even though I was freaking out I was able to stop myself from shaking long enough to hang off the wall with one arm, so that I good get some good insta and Facebook pics! 😁

The funny thing is the viewpoint the planks take you to wasn't even that cool!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1745.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-718" width="3024">

The worst part was on the way back you had to pass everyone who was coming down. This meant you had to step farther off the wall and move your clips around them 😳.

The whole plank hike took around and hour - hour and a half to finish because of the crowd. It was definitely on of the most thrilling and adrenaline pumping experiences I've done in my life. When we got back I was relieved to be back on solid ground. There was a restaurant nearby so we all stopped for lunch.

We continued hiking to the top of the South and West peaks. One funny thing was the Chinese really like screaming when the get to the top of the mountain it was pretty hilarious.

The mountain was getting noticeably more crowded as the day went on. After the West peak we made our way back down on the West cable car. In total I think we hiked for around 5 hours on the mountain. According to apple we did about 2,300 feet.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1754.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-719" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1752.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-720" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1761.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-721" width="4032">

Once we got down we had to take a bus back  down to the park entrance and then shuttle to the train, train back to the metro, and metro back to the hostel...were getting pretty good at using the public transport in china!

At the hostel we rested for a little and went down to hang out in the lobby. While there we met some cool people, one from another part of China, Italy, and Belgium. They invited us out to dinner with them.

For dinner we went back to the Muslim quarter and found a secluded restaurant that the Chinese guy we met heard was good. It was awesome having someone who could speak Chinese with us! We ended up trying a soup that had bread, noodles, and lamb. It was delicious and super cheap!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1764.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-713" width="4032">

After dinner we walked back through the Muslim quarter and tried some rice cakes with rose jelly that our Chinese friend recommended to us. They were really good but very sugary!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1767.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-714" width="3024">

I also tried a drink that was made from sugarcane that was alright. It kind of had a waterway coconut taste to it.

Once we got back to the hostel we hung out and talked with our new friends over beer. One interesting thing we learned was that a lot of people in china actually really like Trump?! Apparently they really like his "Make America great again slogan" 😂. We also learned that people spitting in public is actually considered very rude and it's usually the uneducated that do it. Another funny thing I forgot to mention is what we like to call the "Beijing bikini". A lot of men like to fold up the bottom of their shirts (holding it up with their belly) too cool off and walk around in public like that 😂. I'll see if I can get some good pictures of it, supposedly it's also mostly the uneducated people that do it.

Tomorrow we head back to Beijing to see the wall one last time to finish off our trip!</p>
