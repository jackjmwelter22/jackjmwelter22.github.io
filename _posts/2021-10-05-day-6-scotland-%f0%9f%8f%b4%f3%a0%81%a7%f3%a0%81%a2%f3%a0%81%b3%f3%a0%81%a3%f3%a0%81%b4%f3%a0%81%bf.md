<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I was woken up at 2am when my dad decided to turn on all the lights to the room to go to the bathroom and then again at 4am 😡. We ended up actually getting up at 6:30 and got some breakfast at the hotel. After breakfast we tried to dry some stuff with the hairdryer one last time before packing everything and loading up the car for the 3 hour drive to Dublin airport. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The ride to the airport went off without a hitch. We returned the car rental and dropped our bags off. We were about an hour and a half early so we grabbed a quick sandwich for lunch at the gate. The flight was a quick 1 hour. Sadly the weather at Dublin when we left was perfect and sunny while the weather in Edinburgh was rainy and overcast. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Getting into the UK was super easy no one even asked us about Covid. Once we arrived we got our bags and then got picked up by a shuttle to take us to the rental car. Really no hassle which was nice because we were a little worried about getting into the UK. The drive to St. Andrews took about an hour. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hotel we’re staying at is the historic old course hotel, commonly referred to as the “road hole” hotel that golfers must hit over on the 17th tee. The views from our view of the course were amazing!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2169,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After getting settled in we took a free cab to dinner at a place right next to the first tee and 18th green called hams hame (it was only a few minutes away). I had a burger with chili on top, it was pretty good! Apparently there’s a law in Scotland that you must cook beef well done. After dinner we went back to the hotel and went to bed. </p>
