<p>Day 5 Saturday</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today was another diving day - after eating breakfast at the VRBO we walked over to Reef Gliders and did two more morning dives with Gigi - the first dive was further away at a site called Tortuga Terrace (turned out it was right next to the moray channel). The dive had a max depth of 77' and average of 47'. It was a really fun dive that had lots of swim throughs and coral canyons along the wall.</p>

<p>Becca ended up going back over to one of the shops. She had seen a good deal for a dive computer and wanted to ask about a cash discount (which they did do - tax here is ridiculous at 19%). The second dive was at a spot called Blue Moonshine, Gigi briefed the dive and then we loaded back up onto the boat. The dive wasn't particularly special, we did see a stingray at the very beginning of the dive..... and at the end of the dive when we were getting back to the boat the ladder broke off (due to all the waves) so we ended up having to take off our gear in the water and climb/get pulled up onto the boat.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After diving we met up with Jamie and Nate (who did two dives with their dive shop) and then we went to pick up the dive computer for Becca. After getting the computer we walked back to the VRBO to see mom and Bennett. We had a snack to hold us over and then we drove to the Gumbalimba park (we didn't want to miss it before it closed which is why we skipped lunch). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The park was about 20 minutes away and cost ~$30 per person to enter. We were the only ones there so we basically had a private tour. The whole tour took about an hour. We started with the Iguanas - there were a bunch of them in an outdoor area. The next stop was the insect museum - which was basically a bunch of different dead insects on display. Then we crossed a big suspension bridge over a river to get to the more interesting side of the park.</p>
