<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up early and got all our clubs and stuff packed, then we took our at home covid tests for our return to the US (which we both passed). After the tests I went to down to check out the spa/hot tub area. Honestly I expected more from a fancy hotel, the hot tub was barely lukewarm. Jamie drove over from her Airbnb to have breakfast with us at the hotel. I had an omelet and waffles plus fruit. After eating I got packed up while dad and Jamie went to see the course and walk around the hotel. When they got back we loaded up the car and drove to downtown Edinburgh, a little over an hour away. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When we arrived we stopped and parked at the Airbnb which happened to be right next to Arthur’s seat (a hike overlooking the downtown). Jamie and I started hiking up while my dad decided to walk into the city. It was raining pretty steady so the hike wasn’t the best experience, but we had an umbrella so it could’ve been worse. The views on the way up and the top were great you could see the whole city. </p>

<p>After eating my dad wasn’t feeling great so he went back to the Airbnb while my sister and I walked around and explored the city. The royal mile is really cool, it looks like it’s straight out of Harry Potter (JK Rowling lived here), the architecture is awesome! We stopped at the Giles cathedral, the Scott’s monument, the castle, and the national museum. </p>

<p>After walking around we made our way to the meeting point for the Edinburgh underground tour. The tour took us under the streets of Edinburgh to the vaults which were originally built as store rooms for the shops above but later abandoned when they realized water would leak in when it rained and ruin anything in them. Also supposedly the vaults are haunted. The guide had a ton of ghost stories about the vaults and would jump out of places to scare everyone. </p>

<p>After the tour we walked back about a mile to the Airbnb to meet up with dad. We hung out at the Airbnb for a while then Jamie and I walked to a nearby Turkish restaurant for dinner. We had hummus and shish kebabs, it ended up being a ton more food than we expected, but it was really good! After dinner we went back and relaxed at the Airbnb before going to bed. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 11</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next morning I woke up early around 6:30. I drove with my sister to the airport so she could drop me off and then drop off the car. My flight was at 9 and the car rental opened at 8 and you weren’t allowed to drop the car off after hours for some reason. The Edinburgh airport was kind of a pain. They charged you to drop someone off and then took forever to check in and check my bag even though the airport was basically empty. The flight to Dublin was pretty uneventful everything went to plan and we landed on time…then the fun began. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So I had called United the day before to make sure my connection from dublin to Chicago was okay, it was only a 50 min layover and United actually called me twice saying I needed to call them back about my connection. Well the lady I talked with said it would be no issue, that was a lie. After landing in Dublin it turned out I couldn’t just walk to my next flight and check in. I had to exit through immigration and get my passport stamped, then go downstairs to check in at the United desk. At the desk they told me the boarding was closed, but luckily they gave me the tickets anyway and I sprinted through security and then through a second passport control and ran the rest of the way to my gate. I swear this was the farthest possible gate in the airport. Somehow I arrived justtt before they closed the gate and was able to get on the flight. Unfortunately, as I would find out later, my golf bag wasn’t so lucky. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My flight from Dublin to Chicago was smooth. I had a 4 hour layover in Chicago so my mom came and picked me up. We ended up getting lunch and hanging out at the house for a few hours before she took me back to the airport. I finally arrived in Savannah around 9:30pm. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Overall it was an awesome trip! I’m glad my dad and I were finally able to go! Looking forward to the next one!</p>
