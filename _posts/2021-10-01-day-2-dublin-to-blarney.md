<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we got up at 7. I slept really well after yesterday’s exhausting day. We ate at our Airbnb, our host had a spread laid out for us in the kitchen. I had eggs, toast, and a banana. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we packed up and loaded into the car and made our way to Killeen golf course. This course was a steal for only 20 euros each. It was about 30 min away. It was a bit chilly to start the morning but the weather turned out to be amazing! All sun no rain, it was great! I didn’t play too well I ended up with an 89 but at least I had a few pars and a birdie. My dad played a little better than me with an 86. The wind was super strong. It was honestly kind of ridiculous at times, I think it was around 20 mph or more with gusts. Which was nice only when the wind was coming from behind you. besides the wind there were a ton of trees, I swear I’ve never hat to hit so many punch shots in my life!</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We finished eating around 12:30 or so and made our way south towards blarney, our first stop along the way was about an hour drive to the rock of Cashel. This was the ruins of an old cathedral on top of a hill. I think it’s famous for st Patrick baptizing people there. Anyway, it was really cool to see with great views of the countryside. And the best part was free entry again…thanks covid!</p>

<p>After the cathedral we made another quick stop at a castle 15min away called Cahir castle. This castle was tiny and probably could be skipped, but it was free so what the heck!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2093,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_1994.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-2093" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the small castle we finished driving to blarney. Along the way we had a little snafu with the tool booth (I went in the wrong lane and had to back up and try to squeeze over to the other lane) and made it to the blarney castle at 4:20. This was perfect timing because the last entry is 4:30. This gave us an hour to see the castle and explore the grounds. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our first stop was the castle. It was really cool with tons of rooms and of course at the top of the castle was the Blarney Stone which you can lean over and kiss for the gift of gab. It honestly surprised me they still let people do it with all the covid restrictions today. The best part was that we were the only ones in the whole place besides the two workers that make sure you don’t fall off when you kiss the stone (normally the wait can be longer than 90 minutes to kiss the stone!). </p>

<p>After the stone I walked to see the blarney house (dad ended up getting a hot chocolate and waiting for me). I’m not sure what the story is with it but apparently it’s a private residence and it’s super nice!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2098,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Finally we walked around the grounds and saw the “waterfall” which was little more than a few drops into a pond (guess it’s out of season). We also walked through the woods where the witches live. There’s a special staircase that if you walk down and then walk up backwards with your eyes closed and thinking of a wish it’ll come true, tbd on if it works…I’ll let you know. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After all that we made our way back out to the car right at 5:30 when they closed. We drove to our hotel which happened to be like 2 blocks from the castle parking lot. After we got to our room and settled, we went down the street to a restaurant called musketry arms. I had the bangers and mash, it was good!</p>
