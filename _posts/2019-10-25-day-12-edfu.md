Today we sailed through the night from Kom Ombo north to Edfu. We woke up at 5:30am for our tour...another early morning. We met our new guide in the lobby to start the day.

From the boat everyone was loaded on to small horse carriages to the get to the Edfu temple which was about 10min away. The temple was completely covered by mud when it was discovered and because of that it is the best preserved temple in Egypt. The temple was a Greek temple for the God horus (the falcon God). Although the temple was built by the Greeks its original location, building plans, and foundation were Egyptian. The temple was finished in 57 BCE and took 180 years to complete. The birds eye view of the temple looks like a step pyramid with the main alter at the very top where only the pharaoh and high priest were permitted. Each day they performed rituals 3 times for the God.

After seeing the temple we took the horses back to the boat and stopped to buy some waters before re-boarding and going to breakfast. Breakfast was really good luckily we got here early before the crowd and were able to get an omelet without having to wait in a huge line. After breakfast we watched the rest of Indiana Jones and took a nap.

After napping we went up to the top deck of the boat to hang out in the sun as the boat sailed to the famous city of Luxor.

Sailing was relaxing, we went through locks at one point in order to get to the lower water level portion of the Nile. When we got to the locks a bunch of small tow boats pulled up next to our ship to try and sell rugs, shirts, towels, etc. their sales tactic was to put the item in a plastic bag and throw it up to the top deck. This caused a bunch of unsuspecting passengers to get hit by the items 😂. While on the top deck we also went in the pool which was super cold but refreshing.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3313.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1583" width="3024" height="4032">

After getting roasted on the deck we had lunch and then went back to our room to watch the new movie Spider-Man movie.

We went back up to the deck when the sun wasn't as strong to play some ping pong. Sam is about the same skill level as me so we had some pretty good games. After that we met our guide in the lobby to talk about the plan for our next two days at luxor.

After meeting our guide we decided to go for a walk around Luxor near the port with one of the people in our group named Ravi. The walk was not very relaxing because Luxor is filled with people trying to sell you something so from the minute you step off the boat you are constantly, aggressively approached by some of the most insistent 'salesman' I've ever seen. After walking through the local souk we made our way back to the boat. On the boat we played some more Egyptian rat screw and then went to dinner and finally to bed.
