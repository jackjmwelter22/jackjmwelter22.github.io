<p>Fully vaccinated and ready for another exciting year of traveling. Unfortunately the golf trip I've been planning for the last year and a half with my dad got cancelled for a third time and Becca's dive trip got cancelled (both were supposed to happen this month). Fortunately, that meant time to plan a last minute trip! El Salvador isn't really talked about as a popular Central American travel destination, but we decided on El Salvador for a few reasons: it's super close, easy covid restrictions, very cheap, and lots of interesting things to do from Mayan ruins to volcano hikes to surfing on the coast. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
