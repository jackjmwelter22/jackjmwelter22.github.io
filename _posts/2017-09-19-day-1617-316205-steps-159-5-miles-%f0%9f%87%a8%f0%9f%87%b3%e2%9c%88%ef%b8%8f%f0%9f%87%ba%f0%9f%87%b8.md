<p style="text-align:justify;">One last post to close out the trip!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">According to the Apple health app over the course of our trip Jamie and I walked/hiked 159.5 miles and over 10,000'! Overall it was a hugely successful trip and I'm glad I got to experience with my little sis (even though I'm sure I got on her nerves more than a few times 😂)!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">Today was a very relaxed day we slept in had the 'American; breakfast again at the hostel and then left by metro/train for the airport at noon. I ended up getting to the gate like 3 hours early.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">The flight went smoothly, just after take off I'm pretty sure I saw the great from the plane.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1937.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-797" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1938.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-798" width="4032"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">it was about 12 and a half hours to Detroit and I think I watched about 6 or 7 movies. I'm really getting to be a pro at skipping past the opening Ad that Delta puts before each of the movies haha! I got into Detroit just before 5pm local time and had a ~2 hour layover until my flight to Atlanta. The first thing I did now that I was back in America was get a massive burrito, it was beyond amazing!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">Once I got to Atlanta I hopped on the shuttle to get to the parking lot where I parked my car. The place was called Peachy Airport parking and if you ever need a cheap place to park near the Atlanta airport I definitely recommend them. The drive from Atlanta back to my house was another 3 and a half hours so I didn't make it home until ~2:30am. To make matters worse the AC in my house wasn't working 😳! after a couple unsuccessful attempts to fix it I went to bed (I had to wake up at 6am for work!).</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">I think the biggest surprises for me were the lack of people that understood English, China was probably the most difficult country to communicate out of the 23 countries I've traveled to. Another surprise was not accepting American credit cards; having to use only cash was such a pain!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">My favorite parts of the trip by far were the Great Wall and hiking mount huashan. I'd highly recommend them if you ever visit China!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">Now it's back to reality for at least a few few days then on Friday I head back to Chicago to visit the fam!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"></p>

<p style="text-align:justify;">That's it for now, I Can't wait to start planning the next adventure! Thanks for reading ✌️🙃✌️🇨🇳</p>
