<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Starting the year off right with a two week trip down to New Zealand and the French Polynesia for our honeymoon. Technically we had a mini-honeymoon right after our wedding but that was mostly hiking so it didn't count.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here's the high level itinerary and Google map of what were planning to cover</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p></p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Days 1-5 North Island</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Flying into Auckland seeing the Watimo caves, Hobbiton. Then heading to Rotorura to see the geothermal reserves/pools and the kiwi hatchery. We make a stop in Tongariro national park to hike "Mount Doom" from lord of the rings and then drive down to Wellington for a quick look before flying to the south island.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Days 6-13 South Island</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We fly into Queenstown and spend some time there doing the adventure activities and then take a quick trip over to milford sound for a cruise and kayak. After queenstown we drive up to Franz Joesef (with a quick stop in wanaka) to do some heli hiking and skydiving! After the Glacier we make our way to Christchurch before flying out.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Days 14-17 Bora Bora</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We end the trip relaxing on the beaches in Bora Bora (an island in the French Polynesia)!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
