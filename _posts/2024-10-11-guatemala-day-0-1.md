<p>In the midst of hurricane Milton I made it to the airport around 5am. It was a refreshing surprise that all of the flights went smoothly. I arrived in Dallas almost 30 minutes early and had no problem making the connecting flight. In Dallas I met up with Patryk and Jimmy at the gate, it was good to see everyone!</p>

<p>The flight from Dallas was less than 3 hours to Guatemala City. The flight was smooth I watched a few shows, pat and I happened to be sitting next to each other. Once we landed getting into Guatemala was painless, we filled out a customs form online. I went ahead and got the rental car and jimmy and pat followed behind shortly after. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove from Guatemala City to Antigua Guatemala, the drive took about an hour and a half and we arrived at our hotel around 2:30pm. Traffic driving here was pretty bad getting out of the city, but in general the drivers weren’t that aggressive. We’re staying at the el Carmen hotel, it’s a great location right in the center of the city. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We didn’t have anything really planned for today so we got settled and rested in the hotel for a bit before heading out to the Antigua brewing company for a beer. The place had a rooftop with nice views of the city. The city is pretty small and nestled in the mountains surrounded by volcanos. We had a drink and hung out there. </p>
