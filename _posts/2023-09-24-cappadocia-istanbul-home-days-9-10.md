<p>Day 9 Cappadocia</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up at 5:20 and left the room at 5:40 to make the short hike back up to the antenna to watch the balloons fly. There was someone already there with a full on picnic setup, but luckily our spot right under the antenna was still open. We set up our blanket and enjoyed the show.</p>

<p>I think this spot is probably the best place to watch the balloons- they’re literally all right there above the city. Of course we took a ton more photos and drone shots. I even got a cool Timelapse of about half of the balloon flights (I wish I thought to start it sooner).</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After about an hour of watching the balloons most of them landed, so we made our way back down to our hotel for a quick nap and then another breakfast buffet. Our host was kind enough to let us have a late checkout today, so after breakfast we napped some more and hung out in the room until 12:30. After packing our things we checked out and made our way to the car. Since we had already done all the things we really wanted to we decided to go check out the Goreme open air museum. This one was right under where we watched the balloons yesterday. We avoided paying for parking by parking a block away on the street and then walked up.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We were able to walk into one of the main churches that was filled with lots of paintings. The paintings were supposedly preserved over the years by pigeon poop (according to Becca) and were only found after they started cleaning the walls.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After seeing the church we walked up to the main entrance (where you actually had to pay). The price was close to $20 USD per person and since we already saw the church paintings (which was the main differentiator from Zelve) we decided to skip it. Of course Becca took advantage of their free bathroom before we went back to the car.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove towards causvin, only a few miles away, and stopped for lunch at a place called Causvin pide. The food was pretty good and there seating was outside underneath a ceiling made from the grape vines and trees. I had the meat pide and chicken skewers. The pide was good but the chicken was pretty dry. There were also a bunch of flies that would not leave our food alone which was annoying.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we walked into the center of the town, it was a little further than we realized and super hot. Supposedly there was a church there, it ended up being on top of the ridge, I made it there and took some pictures while Becca waited for me at the bottom of the steps. The church was cool, some deteriorating paintings and really big, all carved into the side of the mountain. Cool to see and kill time but not worth going super out of the way to see.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the church we made a quick stop at the market to get some water before making the ~1 hour drive to Kayseri airport for our flight to Istanbul. Before getting there we made a quick stop for gas.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our flight to Istanbul went smoothly, unfortunately we had to check our bags for Turkish airlines so we ended up having to wait ~20 minutes at the baggage claim before getting our bags. To make matters worse the line for taxis was MASSIVE. We ended up waiting at least another 20 minutes or so before finally getting a cab. I wish that was the end of it…but no. I showed the cab driver our hotel on google maps and he acknowledged it, which to me indicated that he knew where he was going…I couldn’t be more wrong. Not more than one minute after we got into the cab he said something in Turkish which seemed to indicate he had no idea where he was going. I helped him out showing him my phone again and he tried unsuccessfully to get the hotel in his phone’s GPS. No problem - I gave him my phone and we were off again, the hotel was only 10 minutes away so we should be there soon….nope. I loaded the directions in my work phone as well to “check” where he was going. Even with the directions from my phone he missed the turn. Becca and I immediately were telling him to take the exit and he seemed to have no idea what we were talking about. He continued driving, this time we were hyper aware of where he was going. The GPS re-routed us to have us go back the way we came and even with us literally yelling at the cab driver to take the exit, he decided to continue going the wrong direction. At this point we were livid and screaming at him, I even pulled out google translate to tell him to turn around. Finally he obliged, the 10 minute cab drive turned into a 30-45 minute ordeal. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When we finally got to our hotel the cab driver came inside with us and the receptionist helped translate. After all of that the drive still wanted us to pay for the whole bill. We told him tough luck and paid him 200 lira (which was in my mind a fair price if he went the correct route - the bill on the meter was 400). I later read that the taxis in Istanbul are basically terrible and not to use them lol. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once we checked in (around 9:30) we got our room and went to bed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 10</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up at 5:10. After the night before we were paranoid about the taxi to the airport so we wanted to give ourselves ample time. Luckily the taxi showed up quickly and the ride to the airport went smoothly. We had to check-in for our flight in person and the line took quite a while. After checking in we made our way to our first flight, to London, which left on time. We made it to London and the caught our next flight ~2 hours later to Miami. The Miami flight was relatively empty so I had an aisle seat with in the middle section with two open seats in the middle which was nice. After about 8.5 hours we made it to Miami. Unfortunately we had quite a long layover until our flight to Savannah ~9:15pm.</p>
