Today was a pretty uneventful day so I'll keep it short.

We slept in till around 10:30 this morning. I woke up feeling a lot better, I guess all the American food I ate yesterday did the trick! This morning I had the Oreo pancakes for breakfast. They were okay, they definitely could've used some more syrup because they were kinda dry.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1496.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-609" width="3024">

In china they really don't seem to like the stuffing. All the Oreos I've had here have basically been two cookies with the tiniest amount of filling between them.

After breakfast we checked out of the hostel and walked around the main strip, picked up some snacks for our flight, and then hailed a taxi to the airport. Again we had some communication difficulties with the driver 😂. You'd think the taxi would at least know what airport was in English! After what was essentially a mini game of charades he figured out what we wanted. China has surprisingly been the most difficult country to communicate in that I've been to.

We made it to the airport and got through security with no problems this time! We ended up boarding our plane then having to de-board because our flight was delayed again because of too much traffic. We waited another hour or two before they let us board again and head to chengdu.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1497.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-610" width="4032">

When we arrived at Chengdu we took the airport shuttle downtown and then walked to our hostel. Our hostel ended up being hidden in an alley behind a parking lot for a much larger hotel. Of course google couldn't figure this out so we had to talk to a couple different people and wander around before we finally found it.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1498.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-611" width="3024">

After dropping our bags off we went to a restaurant recommended by one of the hostel receptionists. Chengdu is in the Sichuan region of China which is known for its very spicy cuisine. Luckily at the restaurant there were two waiters who went to college in the US and spoke very good English. They showed us how the restaurant worked. It was a hot pot style restaurant where you picked out all the food you wanted and cooked it. The pot we got was half spicy and half not spicy. I ended up trying the spicy side which started off okay but by the end of the meal my mouth was on fire!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1502.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-613" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1501.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-612" width="3024">

Overall it was pretty good food and very unique. The whole meal only cost ~$12 for both of us including drinks!

After dinner we made our way back to the hostel and tried to plan our trip to Leshan for tomorrow. </p>
