<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After an arduous day/night of travel we finally made it to Auckland New Zealand! The journey here we flew from Savannah to Dallas (~3 hours) and then after a ~2 hour layover we flew from Dallas to Auckland, which ended up being a 15.5 hour flight! We flew in a 787-9 which is a newer airplane, but the seats still seemed tiny. The plane does has really cool tint-able windows instead of pull down shades. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We watched the entirety of season 1 of white lotus (HBO show) wouldn’t recommend, a few other random movies, and listened to an audio book. Unfortunately I didn’t get much sleep but Becca was able to. After we landed passport control/ customs was super smooth. It took us some time to figure out how to get to the enterprise rent a car (needed to take a shuttle). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After getting our car we drove to the Victoria street parking garage downtown (about 20min away) to explore the city and fight the jet lag. Our first stop was Albert park, fairly small but it was right next to the parking lot. Next we walked down to the ferry port area. </p>
