Today we woke up at 7 to get breakfast and check out of our room on the ship. Our guide was meeting us in the lobby at 9 so we hung out for a bit of the upper deck of the boat until he arrived. This gave me some time to get caught up on my blogs which I haven't been able to post since the ship doesn't have WiFi.

Our first stop was the valley of the kings which took about an hour to get to since it was on the opposite side of the river.

The valley of the kings is where they buried a TON of the Acient pharaohs. There are 62 tombs (and 3 others they just found) including the famous tomb of king Tut (the boy king).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3561.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1593" width="2576" height="1920">

In the valley we ended up seeing 3 of the different tombs. They are all very similar so this was more than enough in my opinion. The tombs were basically really long tunnels dug into the mountain and filled with hieroglyphics and at one point tons of treasure. Some of the tombs were packed with tourists which was a little frustrating!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3580.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1599" width="2576" height="1920">

The tombs all dead end into the burial chamber which has a huge sarcophagus that the pharaoh was buried in. I have no idea how they got the sarcophagus in but they're massive and obviously super heavy. Another interesting thing is the workers would continue the construction of the tombs for as long as the king was alive and ruling. So the longer the tunnel means the longer that king lives and ruled. This is the reason that king Tut's tomb is so small and basically just a room without a long tunnel.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3568.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1595" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3577.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1598" width="2576" height="1920">

After exploring the tombs in the valley of the kings we drove 10 minutes or so to the Temple of Queen Hatshepsut. This temple is where the queen was buried. Hatshepsut was significant because she was a female pharaoh, the first one to do so (well before the time of cleopatra).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3583.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1597" width="2576" height="1920">The temple was really massive and also had a huge stage being set up for a concert they were doing this weekend that was rugot in front of the temple. After walking through the temple we left to head back across the river.

We made a quick stop at the Colossi of Memnon for a picture. The colossi are two massive statues of the pharaoh Amenhotep III and were very well preserved.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3585.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1596" width="2576" height="1920">

After the tour we got dropped off at the Hilton Luxor for our final night before flying back to Cairo. We got checked in and had lunch at the restaurant down by the main pool. I got the chicken shawarma which ended up being hung up on a skewer. It was pretty good! The views of the Nile from the hotel were also great.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3342.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1620" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3345.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1619" width="4032" height="3024">

After lunch we went over to hang out by the other pool till sunset and relax. Relaxing has been a common theme for the last half of this trip! After sunset we went back to the room to watch a few movies and then get to sleep. Tomorrow we have an early flight back to Cairo.
