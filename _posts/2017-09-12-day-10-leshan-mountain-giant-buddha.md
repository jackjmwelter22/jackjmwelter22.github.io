After sleeping on probably the thinnest mattress I've ever seen I woke up 6:30 and made our way to the lobby. I had another American breakfast before we left to see the giant Buddha in a town about 2 hits south of Chengdu called Leshan.

Another side note about China: they don't accept credit cards anywhere. It's super annoying! Being one of the worlds super powers and largest GDPs you'd think credit cards wouldn't be an issue. I'm pretty sure all of the Southeast Asia countries I went to even accepted credit cards. It wouldn't be such an annoyance if the atms worked, but of course half of them don't so if you need to get cash you have to wander around until you get one that works. On the plus side everything here is super inexpensive!

Our plan to get to the big Buddha was to take public transportation. This turned out to be a little more complicated than we thought. At first we got on the right bus number but going the wrong direction so we had to get off and catch another bus going back. There is absolutely no English lettering or words for any of the stops so trying to explain where your trying to go is next to impossible.

We finally made it to our stop, the bus station south of the city. We went to buy tickets and they asked for our passports...I didn't have mine 😫. Luckily the lady hesitantly accepted my drivers license!

Another funny note about China is you have to be careful of the water fountains/coolers. The Chinese like their water hot (I guess for their tea and noodles) so a lot of the times the water fountains will have steaming hot water!

The 2 hour bus ride to Leshan was pretty uneventful. When we got there we were approached by a "taxi driver" who said he could take us to the Buddha. I was skeptical, like I usually am with people that approach me out of nowhere. We go to his car and it's unmarked with no meter...double red flag. He negotiates a price of 40 rmb to take us there. I had a feeling it was a bit high but we didn't see any other legitimate taxis so we figured we might as well. Surprisingly we made it to our destination!

We had to hike a little to arrive at the base of the giant Buddha. It was massive and carved into the mountain side. It looked like something you'd see in an Indiana jones movie or something.

Besides the giant Buddha there were a bunch of other random temples and caves we hiked to see. While we were there we ran into the ditch couple that we met on our tour of the Great Wall...small world!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1540.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-627" width="4032">Jamie super stoked to see a random temple

On the way back to the bus station we hailed a taxi (from farther away then we were dropped off) and it only ended costing us 17 rmb! The first guy totally ripped us off! At least 23 rmb only comes out to be like 3 US dollars it could've been worse.

The 2 hour bus back to Chengdu was orettt uneventful. Another observation about the Chinese is they never will get up from their seat to let you out. So if you have the window seat on the plane and need to get out you have to awkwardly shuffle past.

The bus station in Chengdu is right next to this famous bridge (don't ask me what it's famous for) so we decided to walk over there and check it out.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1546.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-628" width="4032">

The bridge ended up being closed so we weren't able to walk across it. Afterwards we caught a bus back to where our hostel was (more communication struggles!). Nothing in Chengdu is close and although there is a metro it's very limited as far as where you can get to with it so you are pretty much forced to take the buses everywhere.

After resting at the hostel for a bit we got recommendations to eat at a nearby restaurant. The place was all in Chinese and of course no one spoke any English but at least the menu had pictures! Our waitress was a good sport trying to use google translate to understand what we meant. I ended up getting some chicken and peanut dish that was pretty good and had a little bit of a spicyness to it.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1548-1.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-632" width="3024">

Jamie had some sort of pork ribs and struggle to try and eat them with her chopsticks 😂.
