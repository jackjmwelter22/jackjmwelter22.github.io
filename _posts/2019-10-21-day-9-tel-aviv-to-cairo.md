Today we slept in till 9ish. We didn't have too much planned for today. We went upstairs to have some breakfast at the hostel again. After eating we decided to check out the Kamel market. It was about a 10min walk from our hostel.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191020_101121_original.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1492" width="2268" height="4032">

The market was really crowded and was your typical street market. They had just about everything you could think of. We spent maybe 15 min walking through the market to the other end. After walking through we decided to head over to the beach which was only a couple blocks away. This beach was not the same one we went to yesterday, it was a little further north and much bigger. We decided to walk up the beach to check it out. Along the way there were some random body weight gym machines. Sam and I had a contest to see who could do the most consecutive pull-ups.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191020_110809_original.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1493" width="4032" height="2268"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191020_111559_original.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1494" width="4032" height="2268">

When we got to the end of the beach we decided to try the lime scooters again. This time we were able to get it to work so that everyone had their own! We cruised down the beach and then over to a street called Rothschild street which was supposed to be a street with really fancy houses? Idk we really didn't see anything to fancy honestly but at least there was a nice lane for scooter riding and no one crashed! After scooting around we stopped for lunch at another place Jamie's coworker recommended called cafe noir. It was a French themed restaurant that was known, surprisingly for their schnitzel. It was pretty decent! The waiter definitely judged us when we asked for fries instead of mashed potatoes, typical American move 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2990.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1496" width="3088" height="2320">

After lunch we scooted back down to the old Jafa city to check out the "famous" flea market. The market ended up being basically a bunch of junk that people were trying to sell. It was seemed to me like the community garage sale held by the homeless people or something....kinda weird. Needless to say I didn't see anything that I wanted. However, I did see a gelato shop right next door which I obviously wanted. So we all got some gelato.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2989.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1497" width="3088" height="2320">

After exploring the market we walked back down to the beach to hang out and enjoy the view until we had to leave for the airport.

We ended up grabbing our stuff and checking out of the hostel around 3 and then grabbing some scooters and scooting the mile or so to get to the train station that takes you to the airport. Our flight wasn't until 7 but we heard the security in Israel is super strict and takes forever to get through so we gave ourselves some more time for that. Also tomorrow is another Jewish holiday so the last train to the airport was at 4 and then everything shut down till Tuesday.

The security at the Tel Aviv airport turned out to not be a big deal at all. I'm really not sure what all the hype is about. We got asked maybe two questions and the line for security was so short. It was literally faster than going through Savannah's tiny airport line. Once in the airport I went to the executive lounge with Jamie (she gets in for free and can take a guest). They had free food and drinks which was great. It was also way more comfy than hanging out at the terminal waiting for a flight.

Our first flight was to Athens, since Israel and Egypt have a tumultuous past their are no direct flights from Tel Aviv to Cairo. The flight took a little over two hours, I ended up listening to some podcasts and playing sudoku on my phone.

Once at Athens we were supposed to only have a 2 hour layover and leave at 11:45. I'm not sure exactly what happened but we didn't end up taking off till almost 1am 😫. This flight was a little shorter than the last one. When we landed we were planning to get our visa on arrival, of course there were no signs and we ended up waiting in line at passport control only to be told we had to go to a separate line first to buy the visa...ugh! We eventually made it out of the airport and got an Uber to the hotel which was only a few minutes away. I think it was close to 3am by the time we went to sleep.
