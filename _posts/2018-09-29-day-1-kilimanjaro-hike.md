Today was the first day of our Kilimanjaro hike. I woke up around 4am because the roosters outside were making a ton of noise. Luckily I was able to get a little more sleep until we woke up around 7. Unfortunately the place we're staying in is basically a hut so warm water essentially doesn't exist which meant my shower was awful! But I enjoyed it because it's probably the last shower I'll get to take for the next 10 days.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2296.jpg" class="wp-image-835 size-full" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2297.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-834" height="3024" width="4032">

We ate breakfast at 8 and finished packing up around 9. There's a really cute dog that lived at the house named Frankie. So obviously I became friends with him.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2293.jpg" class="wp-image-836 size-full" height="4032" width="3024">

Our guide, Moses, met us at our house and gave us a quick brief of the hike. We're doing the 6 day Machame route (called the whiskey route because of its difficulty) which basically starts at the east end and works it's way up to the peak. Kilimanjaro is the tallest peak in Africa at over 19,300 feet and it's a volcano.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2301.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-837" height="3024" width="4032">

After the briefing we loaded into a van with about 10 other people including our guide, assistant guide, chef, waiter, and porters. We made a quick stop in the town to pick up some things and then headed to the Machame gate, the start of the hike.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2306.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-838" height="3024" width="4032">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1087.jpg" class="wp-image-840 size-full" height="4032" width="3024">

We arrived at around 11 and had a quick lunch while preparing for the hike. Everything seemed to be going smoothly...so we thought. Well it turns out they switched to some new payment system for the permits and our guide wasn't able to get the permit for us to climb the mountain. We ended up having to wait OVER 5 HOURS for them to get it sorted out! 😫 while we were waiting we met some cool people from Sweden (who were also stuck waiting for their guide to get payment through), played a board game that pat brought, and took a picture with some random Kenyans who really wanted a picture with us for some reason...must be my stunningly good looks.

Anyways after we finally got the payment sorted we began the hike. The guide told us this hike should take around 5-8 hours to get to the first camp. Great, that meant getting to camp at 10pm, well after sunset! We ended up hiking a much faster pace and arrived at 7:40 only 3 hours! I didn't think the hike was that bad but Patryk was definitely struggling. I ended up carrying his pack for about half the hike to help him out. The elevation ofthe first camp was ~11,000 feet. Pat had some diamox (altitude sickness pills) so I felt really good.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2326.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-843" height="1932" width="2576">

When we arrived at camp the porters had everything already set up so we quickly washed up and changed and the had some dinner in the meal tent. Dinner was actually really good we had hot chocolate, cucumber soup, potatoes, veggies, tilapia, and salad.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_2327-1.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-845" height="1932" width="2576">

After dinner our guide did a quick health check and then briefed us about tomorrow's hike. I'm pretty exhausted now so I'm gunna get some sleep!
