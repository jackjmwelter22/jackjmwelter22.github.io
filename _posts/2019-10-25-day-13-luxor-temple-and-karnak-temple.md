Today we woke up at 7:30 to get breakfast. Breakfast ended at 8 and the dinning hall was mostly empty which was nice but there also wasn't much food left. Fortunately the omelet station was still open so I had two of them.

We met our guide shortly after breakfast at 8:30 to head to our first stop, the Karnak temple. The Karnak temple is the largest temple in Egypt and is actually a complex of multiple temples. The complex had a massive entry road that was lined with Sphinx statues. The open area of the main temple had 134 columns representing 3 for each of the 42 governantes of Egypt at the time plus 8 for the Gods. This temple also had two large obelisks in the center.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3540.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1586" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3544.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1587" width="2576" height="1920">

There was also a small statue of a Scarab beatle next to the sacred pool (used to cleanse the pharaoh and high priest before the daily rituals). Legend has it if you walk around the statue 7 times then all your wishes will come true. So of course, like the tourists we are, we walked around 7 times. I'll let you know if my dreams come true 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3546.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1589" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3548.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1588" width="2576" height="1920">

After exploring the temple a little longer we got back in the bus and made our way to our next stop the Luxor temple. This temple was considerably smaller but was interesting because it had been converted to a Christian church at one point and also was covered by dirt and a mosque was unknowingly built on top of a portion of the temple and is still there to this day

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3331.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1601" width="4032" height="3024">

After walking around the temple we went back to the boat for some lunch (the usual buffet style). After lunch we hung out on the upper deck at the pool again to relax. This portion of the trip is considerably slower than the first half and I enjoy it! Sam and I played some more ping pong until my paddle unexpectedly broke :(. After that we decided to play cards instead. We played a trick taking game Jamie and I used to play all the time as kids called up the creek.

After getting more than enough sun we went back to our room and watched the movies 48 hours (a movie with Eddie Murphy from the 80s) and then Jamie and Sam watched hotel Mumbai which I already watched on the flight over.

Dinner tonight was way more crowded than usual because new people were boarding the cruise ship. After dinner I talked to my dad on the phone for a while before heading back to the room to sleep.
