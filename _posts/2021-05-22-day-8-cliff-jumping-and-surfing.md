<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up and had breakfast at the hotel at 7:30. This time the breakfast was a buffet style which was way better because I was able to load up my plate with a ton of everything: eggs, bacon, hash browns, pancakes, yogurt, and fruit. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast we drove to Taminique to meet Pedro for a waterfall tour. Taminique is only like 20 min away up the mountains. I found Pedro’s number in a random blog online and messaged him the day before, he only spoke Spanish but agreed to meet us in the park at 8:30. Sure enough when we pulled up to the park in Taminique Pedro was there waiting for us!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After meeting Pedro we made our way directly to the trail which was only two blocks away from the park. Another kid was with us, he was from the town and wanted to come. Pedro was super friendly I got to practice a lot of Spanish with him. It took about 20 min to hike down to the waterfall. In general the hike was pretty easy. At the first waterfall there was a cliff jump that was 4-5 meters high, not that high but still a lot of fun! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>We jumped a few times and also did the “water slide” down the waterfall. The water was super refreshing after the hike down. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_5493.jpg?w=576" alt="" class="wp-image-2004" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After jumping and sliding we hiked another few minutes down to the second waterfall that was much higher. There was no jump on the waterfall because the water was too shallow but we did swim in it and get a “free massage” from the falling water. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2005,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_5499.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-2005" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After enjoying the final waterfall we made the hike all the way back up to town, it’s way more tiring going up! we said goodbye to Pedro and drove back to the hotel to hang out at the pool. At 12:30 we drove to tunco town to meet for our second surf lesson. This time we went to majahal beach (right next to where we were the previous day). The instructors started us with some stretches and then had us do some drills on the beach before heading out into the surf. Our instructors (Wayo and Gato) were much better than the ones yesterday constantly giving us advice and tips. We were probably put on the water for 45 min, I was exhausted! The waves were constant and strong! Just getting back out through all the waves was a struggle, and then when you finally made it far enough out you had to focus on your form and balance. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Needless to say we were dead afterwards, much different than yesterday’s lesson. After finishing we rode back to tunco (in the back of the truck, el Salvadorian style).</p>

<p>In Tunco we got some smoothies and pupusas from our favorite place, la esquina, which happens to be right next door to the surf shop. After getting the pupusas we went back to our hotel and hung out at the pool watching the surfers. We also saw one of the hotel guests propose during their dinner (the restaurant is right next to the pool). Finally we went back to our room and got packed up for our journey home early tomorrow morning. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 9: We checked out and left the hotel at 6:30. Dropping off the car was a bit confusing, we had to call the Avis guy to come meet us in the parking lot to get the car. Our first flight to DC was a breeze, we had a 3 hour layover that we spent in the United club, becca had passes, it was really nice! Overall it was a great last minute trip! Definitely one of the least “touristy” places I’ve been but still a fun adventure! Can’t wait for the next one!</p>
