<p>This morning we woke up around 7:30, breakfast wasn’t open till 8 so we explored the grounds of the hotel. There was an old concrete water slide that looked like a snake, we climbed up for some good views. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In general everything was deserted in the hotel, I think we might be the only ones staying here. We had a pretty standard breakfast of eggs and then got ready for our tour. The driver picked us up at 9 to go to the tour office in Banos. We tried getting cash to pay for the tour but after 5 failed attempts at different machines we gave up and they said we could pay later. The firsts tour was canyoning at Churasco. The drive from Banos took about 45 min. Are guides, Manuel and German (pronounced Hermon) we really funny. After a quick review of the different rope techniques we hiked down to the start of the canyon. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We started by jumping into the water of the river and scrambling down. We end up doing a few jumps off cliffs into the river, my favorite was a 10m jump. We also did one that was kind of a zip line and a bunch of other repels down waterfalls, it was a blast. We finished with a small cliff jump into the the river where you could do a flip. </p>

<p>Faster we finished we hiked back up to the car past a little trout farm. Then we drove ~10 min to a lunch spot with a nice view of the mountains. We had beef, rice, beans, salad, soup, yuca, and some sort of exotic looking fruit. While eating we learned our guide German is a bit of a player when it comes to dating. He told us about all his different European girlfriends and how he has a daughter in Spain that he goes to visit. Also today was his 31st birthday so he was going to fiesta later haha. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch we drive back to banos and to the tour company. Luckily we were able to get the ATM to work so we paid for our tours and then walked around the city for a bit before getting picked up for our next tour, paragliding!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove about 45 min to a place called Niton hill. There were really great views of the surrounding cities. The wind was too strong so we had to wait for it to die down a bit. Becca and I walked around while we waiting and found a cow who like getting his nose scratched.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_0805-2.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1720" width="4032" height="3024"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After about 15 min we went back and waiting in the car until it was time to fly! I was up first because I was heaviest. We flew for about 20 minutes around the hill with some really cool views! The landing was right back in the same place we started and was flawless.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<figure><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_6724-1.jpg" class="size-full"></figure>
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next up was Becca. The winds weren’t as crazy so her takeoff was much smoother. Once she took off I talked to the other guy that was there (he only spoke Spanish). He was 23 and from a coastal town in Ecuador, also traveling with his girlfriend. I also learned that he owned some sort of strip club/brothel with his cousin 😳. Anyway...after talking for 10 min I looked over and saw that Becca was really low and that the wind really died down, then a minute later I saw them crash i a nearby field further down the hill! 😂 the assistant drove down to get her and the pilot. When they returned the wind picked up a bit again so they went for another quick flight, this time the landing went as planned!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove 45min back to Banos and were dropped off at the tour office. We ended up running over to the grocery store and a nearby restaurant to get some drinks and dinner. The restaurant was and Argentinian steak place, the owner understood what no gluten meant so that’s how we chose it! We got a ride back to the hotel and ate, the food was delicious! We went down to the hot tub after for a little and then went to sleep. </p>
