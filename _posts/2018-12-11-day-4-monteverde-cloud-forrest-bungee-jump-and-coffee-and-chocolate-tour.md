Today we got up around 7:30 and made our way to the Monteverde cloud Forrest. It was about a 15 min drive from our Airbnb. When we arrived we got some coffee and bakery snacks and made our way down the trails. There's a reason they call it a "cloud Forrest" the whole hike we were basically in a cloud! The whole trail was a little over 4 km and took us about an hour and a half to complete. The scenery was really nice but not a whole lot different from the hanging bridges park from yesterday. This park actually had one hanging bridge also.

After the hike we drove back into town and stopped at a small sandwich shop called panderia Jimenez. The plan was to get picked up by the bungee jump company at 11, however after like 10 min no one showed so I called them up. Apparently there was a misunderstanding and we missed our bus so we ended up driving to the park. The place was called Monteverde Extremo and was only like a 20 min drive away so it wasn't a big deal. Once we arrived I started getting a bit nervous about the jump. This bungee jump was off a platform suspended between two ridges of the mountain 470 feet in the air. The actual drop itself was about 200 feet! 😳 Despite our best efforts the girls did not want to jump with us 😂 so they ended up being our photographers instead.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1733.jpg" class="wp-image-1011 size-full" width="3024" height="4032">

There was a concerning lack of preparation before we jumped, we basically just walked up to the platform and we started heading out as they put our harnesses on. I ended up jumping first and Pat went second. It was a really awesome view and I was surprisingly a lot less nervous about the jump then I though I'd be. The worst part was hanging by your feet after the jump waiting to get pulled back up.

I'd definitely bungee jump again if I get the opportunity. After we got back on solid ground we went back to the Airbnb to rest. We had about an hour and a half to relax before getting picked up to go on a coffee, sugarcane, and chocolate tour.

We got picked up around 2:30 and drove out about 25 minutes to the farm. The tour was pretty interesting, we got to see how coffee and chocolate were made and of course got to try some samples!
