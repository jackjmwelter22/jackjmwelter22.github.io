<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 0</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 3:30 am thanks to Bennet - we weren’t upset though because it just meant a few more snuggles before we left. We officially got up and ready at 4am and got packed into the car and left just after 5am.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove 2 hours up to Charleston for our flight (it was much cheaper). The drive was smooth and the Charleston airport is really nice. Becca had a surprise for me when we got there, more on that later. Our first flight to Atlanta went off on time without a hitch. We were forced to check our big backpacks which was annoying but not a huge deal. It was a short flight so I just listened to my audiobook on the way. The next flight was to LAX and an hour layover in Atlanta which meant we basically started boarding right when we got to the new gate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The flight to LAX was also uneventful- about 3.5/4 hours. We didn’t see any fires while flying in. The layover here was also an hour so we boarded our final flight to Lihue (pronounced li-who-ee) relatively quickly. Unfortunately while we were racing to the runway the pilot announced there was a problem with one of the 4 fuel pumps and we needed to return to the gate so maintenance could take a look at it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Long story short we ended up staying on the plane and they ended up getting the issue solved resulting in about a 2hour delay. Besides that slight hiccup the flight was uneventful. We landed in Kauai around 9:30pm. I went to get the car rental from Avis which took longer than anticipated and then we made our way to our motel which was only 5 minutes from the airport.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After flying along the north coast we reached hanalei bay and the turned to head towards the middle of the island. Our last main stop was the kawaikini crater. This was a massive, half blown up crater in the middle of the island, super cool!</p>

<p>After the the tour we made a quick stop back at Avis (the car rental agency) they had charged me the wrong amount when they gave me the car last night and were able to fix it. </p>

<p>After eating we drove up north to where our Airbnb is, in princeville. We made a quick stop at the foodland grocery store to get some refrigerator items like milk. We also got some sushi and poke to have for dinner later (it was super inexpensive here!). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We had a bit of a difficult time finding our Airbnb due to the wrong address listed and the poor directions from the host. The Airbnb was also really quirky. It was basically a room in a condo with the only entrance being a sliding glass door into the bedroom. You could also tell they put a door in the halfway to partition off the room - but they didn’t separate the hallway light switch which we found out later when whoever is living in the other side of the hallway turned on their light :D. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After settling in we decided to go for a walk. We first checked out the shared pool, it wasn’t anything special. Then we went on a small hiking trail in the neighborhood that took us to the sea lodge beach. </p>
