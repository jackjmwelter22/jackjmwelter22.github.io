<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 6:30 courtesy of Bennett.  We had breakfast in the room (I had cheerios), then we packed up for the day and got loaded up in the car.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we are hiking Rawson Lake and Sarrail Ridge in the Kananaskis Park (boarders Banff). The drive to the trailhead took just over an hour. We made a quick stop on the side of the road before we lost cell service to try and get bus tickets for Lake Moraine when they released that morning, but had no luck :(. The drive in was mostly cloudy and foggy so we didn’t see much of the mountains.</p>

<p>We made it to the parking lot around 8:30am, put on our layers, and loaded Bennett in his hiking pack. We bought an Osprey Poco Plus used on facebook marketplace- it is comfortable for Bennett, comfortable for me, and has plenty of storage space. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the star of the hike it was very overcast and hard to see much of the surrounding mountains.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There were a decent amount of people on the trail including a big group of high schoolers that we passed. We also got some hiking recommendations from a girl who lived in Calgary.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After a short break at the upper lake we continued on ~1 mile up to the Sarrial Ridge. This part of the hike was very steep, slippery, and muddy. Luckily we brought our micro spikes which made it significantly easier to climb. There was a small section that had a rope because it was so steep.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We finally made it to the top of the ridge around 11:30. Bennett slept most of the way :D, it was definitely a workout carrying him up in the pack! The views from the top were stunning now that the clouds had all cleared out.</p>

<p>We continued back down to the parking lot which is at the base of the ridge we had just climbed and by a lake.  During the hike the fog burned off and the views from the parking lot were incredible. It was a good spot for our picnic lunch. </p>

<p>After eating we got back in the car and drive about an hour and a half to the next hike, Tunnel Mountain. We made a quick stop for gas and some delicious hot chocolate from Tim Hortons (I was getting super tired and it was the perfect pick me up).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Luckily we got a parking spot right at the trailhead. This hike was significantly shorter. Only 3 miles and ~850’ of elevation. The trail was groomed and dry. We got to the top in about 30 minutes. There were some really nice views of the town of Banff and the surrounding area.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>We enjoyed the views and took some pictures before heading back down. On the way we saw a wedding, a rock climber, and a nice looking golf course.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once back at the car we loaded up our things and drove back to Canmore. We stopped for dinner at a brewery called the Grizzly Paw. Becca and I both had burgers and I tried one of their IPAs. It was tasty! Bennett really liked all the pictures of grizzly bears on the wall.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we went back to the hotel. We stopped at the room and rested for a while before walking across the parking lot to the pool. The pool was massive! There was a big 3 story water slide and an indoor/outdoor hot tub.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We each tried the waterslide and then hung out in the hot tub. We brought Bennett in the car seat with us. While we were there a family came in with a lot of kids. He was happy watching us splash, but he loved watching all the kids. After the pool we went back to the hotel and relaxed before falling asleep.</p>
