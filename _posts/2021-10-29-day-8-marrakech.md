<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 8 and made some scrambled eggs with cheese, tomato, and mushrooms for breakfast. After that we had a pretty relaxed  morning in our Riad. We ended up leaving the Riad around 11 to go explore the Medina. Our first stop was Jemaa El fna, the main square in the Medina. To get there was about a 10 minute walk through the souks (markets where they sell various goods). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2332,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0977.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-2332" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The square was pretty massive there were tons of stands selling juice or food. There were also people with monkeys and snake Charmers. We didn’t take any pictures with them because we heard they will aggressively hound you for money if you do. Jamie downloaded some information on the square and gave us a brief “tour” around the square. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next stop was just down the road to the Koutoubia mosque. This is one of the oldest mosques in Marrakech with a huge minaret and some gardens around it. We couldn’t go in the mosque but we took some pictures and walked around. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2328,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0970.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2328" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our next stop was the Saddiens tombs. These sounded cool but it was pretty underwhelming. Basically a courtyard with some intricate rooms you could look into and a bunch of graves  scattered around. I would probably skip this in the future (I cost about 70 Dirhams to enter which is only ~$8 US but kind of a lot for Morocco). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2329,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0973.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2329" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After that we tried to navigate to the badii palace. A road was closed which threw us off but after some wandering around we were able to find it (the entrance was not obvious at all). These were more like ruins of a palace. We ended up looking inside the gate briefly and decided to skip it since there was another palace we wanted to see next that was supposed to be more beautiful and didn’t feel like paying the 80 dirham entrance fee. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next we walked to the Bahia palace. Which was only a few blocks away (we had a much easier time finding the entrance). This palace was pretty expansive, but it’s not what you typically think of when you hear palace. Basically it was a bunch of big courtyards (I think 3) with rooms around them. It was okay nothing I would’ve been sad to miss. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2330,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0975.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2330" /><figcaption>The largest courtyard</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the palace we stopped for lunch, first we went to a place just outside the palace  entrance, but while waiting to order we found another nearby place online that had a lot of good reviews so we decided to leave and try that one instead. The restaurant was called henna cafe and they had hummus surprisingly (we have struggled to find any here) and some gluten free options which is why Becca was excited to go. I ended up having the Moroccan version of a burrito which was good!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2331,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0976.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-2331" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch we walked back to our Riad. Jamie and her friends needed to get a PCR test for their flight (since they were connecting in Italy) so they left to go do that while Becca and I relaxed for a bit and then went for a quick walk to explore the area around our riad before they came back. I was able to somehow find my way around without a GPS this time!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When everyone was back we ended up cooking and eating dinner at the riad. Becca and I made a curry chicken with rice and the others had pizza in the oven. We all hung out on the rooftop terrace and played some uno. Becca and I went to bed early because we had to wake up early the next day. Overall a nice less hectic day!</p>
