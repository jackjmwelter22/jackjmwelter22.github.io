We had a quick connection in Atlanta and then landed in Belize around 12:30!</span></p>

The weather was awesome today. Once we got out of the airport we tried to pick our our rental car from Avis....what a hassle. First they told us the didn't have a car for us and they traded our booking with another company (for the same rate). Well the new rental company, Crystals rentals (not joking), wasn't prepared either and we ended up having to wait another half hour to get our car!
<div></div>
<div>
<div style="margin:0;">Once we finally got our rental we started on our way to see the Xunichtunich ruins. On the way we got stopped twice at "check points" that were literally run by middle schoolers who supposedly worked for the department of transportation to ask us a survey about our experience with the roads, it was quite odd.</div>
</div>
</div>

<div>
<div style="margin:0;">We drove a little over two hours and made it to this ferry/bridge thing that was supposed to take your car across the river to get to the ruins. However the guy said it was broken and we'd have to try tomorrow (pretty sure he was lying and just wanted to leave early). So we ended up driving to San Ignacio and finding our hostel. They told us about some ruins called Cahal Pech that were about a 15 min walk away and a good restaurant to eat at.</div>
</div>
</div>
