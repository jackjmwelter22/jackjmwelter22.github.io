<p>Today I was feeling better than the day before but still pretty exhausted since I didn’t get great sleep last night. For breakfast I just ended up ordering room service (it was complimentary) I had some scrambled eggs, toast, and a banana. Nothing too crazy to start getting my stomach back to normal. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating I got ready for golf and we drove about 15min to kingsbarns golf course. Kingsbarns is known as the “Scottish pebble beach”. Our tee time was 9am, we got really lucky with good weather again! We played with two other people, Ian and Nicky. My dad had a caddie, Jim, who was very Scottish and apparently played pro in the older division. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The course was great! Not a single hole that had a bad view. Lots of hills and cool tee shots! I had one birdie and played okay overall. Ian, in our group actually got a hole in one on 15! We didn’t know it though because the pin was over a ridge so we couldn’t see it. It wasn’t until we were walking up that the group on the other hole was yelling and cheering (they had seen it go in). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We finally had a little drizzle on last hole, but it wasn’t bad at all. We were able to stay virtually completely dry. After golf we drove back to hotel after and I rested until dinner time. We went to dinner at the Jigger inn, which is basically attached to the hotel. I had a burger and fries, again nothing too crazy. After dinner we walked back And I went to bed. Jamie arrived late in St Andrews by train (from London) and stayed at an Airbnb a mile or two away so we’ll see her tomorrow.</p>
