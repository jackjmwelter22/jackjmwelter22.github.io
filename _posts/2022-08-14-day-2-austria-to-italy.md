<p>Today we woke up around 7am, got ready and then had breakfast at the hotel. The breakfast was really good! All you could eat and they had a really fancy coffee machine that made delicious hot chocolate, seriously some of the best I’ve had. We stepped outside to enjoy the amazing views of the mountains while we drank. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we got ready, packed the car, and drove about 30 minutes to another kletterstieg called <a href="https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/crazy-eddy-klettersteig/">Crazy Eddy</a>. This one was a bit more difficult with some fun features including a wire bridge, spider chains, and of course great views the whole time! I forgot my gloves in the car and decided to not go back and get them (definitely don’t do that, the gloves are immensely helpful when gripping the cable). </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We completed the route in about two hours. We drove a bit into the town and stopped to get some gas and snacks before driving onto Innsbruck to pick up Pat and Sarah. They had flown into Munich and then took the train to meet up with us. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>From Innsbruck we headed south towards Italy. It was funny, crossing the boarded you had to pay a toll but included with your toll apparently were apples and water. The apples were very tasty! 10/10 would recommend Italian tolls. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The drive to our next stop, Orteisei (a small town), was about 1.5 hours. We attempted to find parking for the gondola to see Secceda ridge, after some trial and error we managed to find the right spot and took the gondola up to the top (the gondola was v expensive but worth saving 3 hours of hiking). The views of the ridge were amazing! No matter with way you turned you saw impressive mountains and cliff jutting out of the earth. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We spent probably an hour and a half at the top before heading back down to the car. It was around 4pm so we decided to drive about an hour to lago di carrezza, a “rainbow” lake with tons of myths and legends related to it. The lake was a bit underwhelming, I wouldn’t recommend going out of your way to see it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/d6fdjqJ.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We walked the small path around the lake and then had some gelato to relax before heading back to ortesei and our hotel. We checked in and dropped off our bags before heading to the restaurant next door called “tennis pizza”. Fittingly It was a restaurant with a bunch of tennis courts. The pizza was amazing. I had the “Vesuvius” pizza which had cheese, pepperoni, and a over easy egg! It was so delicious. They even had gluten free pizza for Becca! Pat ended up ordering a cheese pizza with pepperoni, apparently pepperoni is called “spicy sausage” in italy, and our waiter thought he wanted peppers 😂. </p>
