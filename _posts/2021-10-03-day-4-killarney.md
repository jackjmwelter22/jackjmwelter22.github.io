<p>Today we woke up at 6:45 and had “breakfast” at 7, which consisted of some cereal and questionable milk. Needless to say it wasn’t the greatest meal haha. After eating we packed up the car and drove about an hour and a half to our hotel in Killarney national park. We were way early but they were still able to get us checked in. After we got settled my dad decided to stay at the hotel and rest while I decided to go hike. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I drove about 25 mins to a place called Cronins yard and parked to hike a mountain called Carrauntoohil, the highest peak on the island of Ireland (includes Northern Ireland) at 3,407’. The mountain is actually privately owned but is free to access by the public. The hike was about 8 miles and 3,000’ elevation gain. The start was really flat and the weather was great. There were a ton of sheep grazing along the way all spray painted different colors (presumably to identify which farm they belong to). </p>

<p>The only bad news was it poured rained for about 10 min so I was soaked and the wind was ripping. Also the peak was another 1,000’ and clearly the views weren’t going to be there since a cloud was engulfing the top of the mountain. I almost decided to turn around but I didn’t want to climb down the devils ladder and knew there was another way down on the other face of the mountain which I assumed would be easier. The rest of the way to the top wasn’t too bad. As expected the visibility was awful. There was a steel cross marking the peak but that was it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2124,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_2164.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2124" /><figcaption>Great views from the summit!</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On the way down it was really hard to find the trail (the whole hike the trail wasn’t very well marked you just kinda went in the general direction you needed to go). Luckily I had my GPS working and was able to find a path and started down. As I descended the ridge the wind was blasting me, I had to stop a few times to not get blown off the mountain! I later found out that this section is called “howling ridge”.  Because of how windy it was I was rushing to get off the ridge and took what I thought was the right trail. It was a super steep gulley, even steeper than the devils ridge. I went slow and eventually made it out. As I came down I ran into a guide who told me that was the central gulley and is dangerous to go down…Whoops!</p>

<p>After enjoying the view for a few minutes I got back in the car and drove back to the hotel to pick up my dad. Our first stop was Castlelough Castle which was actually not the castle I was trying to go to, it was tiny ruins of a castle. Still cool I guess. </p>

<p>After the mishap we drove to Muckross house. I’m not sure why it’s so popular but it has over 6k reviews on google. Basically it’s a cool old mansion. We parked and walked around for some pictures. </p>

<p>Next stop was Torc waterfall. Google ended up trying to send us down a road that was closed. We ended up parking at a lot next to the gate. I walked the rest of the way the google directions had us going and then followed the trail signs to the waterfall. We’ll it ended up looping back right to the parking lot! Not sure how I missed the trail head haha. The waterfall was down about 10 minutes, it was cool, nothing too crazy. My dad was waiting in the car so I just took a few pictures and then made my way back to the car. </p>
