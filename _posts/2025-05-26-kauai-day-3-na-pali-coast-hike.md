<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up early at 5:20, we ate breakfast in the room and got all our stuff ready for the hike. We loaded up the car and left around 6am to drive to the shuttle parking lot. Our tickets were for 7am but we were told you could go on standby and get an earlier shuttle (the earliest starting at 6:20). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Luckily the early shuttle was practically empty so it was no problem getting on. The shuttle started in Waipa and we further along the coast to Haena park and the start of the Kalalau trail. The parking is very limited which is why a shuttle is required. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After dropping us off a park ranger gave us a quick briefing on the trail before letting us head out. We started on the trail just before 7am, it was still pretty dark so we used a head lamp for the first 30 minutes or so. </p>

<p>The trail was really nice, it basically weaves in and out of the mountains along the coast with great views. The first stop was past the first mountain ridge at a beach called Hanakapi’ai beach. There wasn’t much of a beach here due to the high surf which also meant no swimming allowed (the waves were enormous). </p>

<p>Here the trail splits one way goes down the valley between the two ridges to the Hanakapi’ai waterfall and the other continues down the coast until the Kalalau beach. To continue down the coast you need to have a permit (which we had) however no one checked it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We continued hiking up and around the ridges of the coast for another ~3.5 miles taking a couple stops to enjoy the views and to get some drone shots. </p>

<p>I ended up hiking by myself a bit further (Maybe a mile and a half) while Becca rested. We had the walkie talkies with us so we could communicate and Becca was able to see me when I was out on the points. I hiked to a spot that jutted out pretty far towards the water and hiked down for some pictures. It was really windy and a steep ridge so I didn't even try to take the drone up.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike to the waterfall was relatively short, 2 miles in 2 miles out with a couple river crossings. There was also a section with tons of bamboo.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>By the time we left the falls it was about 1pm. We made in back to Hanakapi’ai beach around two and had a short break before continuing back to the shuttle stop, arriving back at ~3:30. We made a quick stop at ke’e beach (right before the shuttle) and refilled our waters. We were really lucky with the great weather we had for the hike, perfectly clear skies and sunny. There were parts of the trail that you could tell would get really muddy if it rained. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The shuttle took us back to the car. We decided to make a stop at wishing well shave ice. I got “the local” and Becca got the Hanalei sweetie. The shave ice was delicious, it’s on top of ice cream and is drizzled with condensed milk, so yummy, I highly recommend!</p>

<p>We ended up going back to the room to shower and eat before going to sleep. We noticed that someone clearly was in our room (the adjoining door to the main house had moved the microwave we had set in front of it) so that was concerning. We contacted the folks from Airbnb and they told us that the cleaning crew had mistakenly opened the door, nothing seemed to be missing so that was good! Also they sent us $100, I guess to bribe us with a good review lol. The Airbnb is definitely strange - we have to enter through the sliding glass door and there is virtually no window treatment, the screen they have is completely see-through when the lights are on at night!</p>
