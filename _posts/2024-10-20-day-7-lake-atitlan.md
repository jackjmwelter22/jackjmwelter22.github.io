<p>Today I woke up around 6 and wrote my blog. My stomach was not feeling too well so I didn’t sleep great. I made some breakfast around 7 and we hung out until about 8:30 before walking down to the dock to catch a lancha to take us over to San Juan, a town on the other side of the lake. In the way to the lancha we saw a pup on the roof of a building.</p>

<p>After walking around we decided to walk over to the mirador (viewpoint) of the city. It was about 1km to get to the start of the trail and then about 0.25 miles up the mountain to the viewpoint. There was a big cross at the end of the viewpoint with a platform that had really good views of the lake and the city. On the trail we had another trail dog keep us company. The entrance to the hike cost 30Q each which was annoying but the views were nice. </p>
