<p>The final day of the hike! I was ready to get back and see Becca! We woke up and had breakfast which was eggs bread and fruit.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast we packed up our things before starting the trail back. We hiked back past the Kogi and Wiwa village and made a stop at for watermelon after about 30 minutes. The guide decided to lead the first part of the hike today so we were going a much slower pace. However, after the fruit stop he allowed Pilar to lead and she along with a translator from another group decided to run the trail back, so of course I followed. It was a fun trail run, tough with my large pack but it wasn't too bad. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made it to the first camp we stayed at in record time. Since we got there so early I decided to jump into the swimming spot, I was drenched in sweat anyway so I didn't really care if I dried. We had a quick snack of chocolate cake and filled up on water before finishing the rest of the hike (I didn't run the final bit).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>On the way back a younger dutch guy, Lars, from one of the other groups caught up to us and I talked to him for most of the hike back to the village. He was most surprised to hear about how little vacation we get in the US (he had been traveling for over a month).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We finally ended back at the same restaurant that we started at. I took a quick shower and then waited for the rest of the group to arrive (they weren't that far behind us). We ate some lunch which we had picked on day one. I had beef which was delicious.</p>

<p>After eating the cars came and picked us up. I said bye to half of the group (that was in the other car) and then we drove the 40 minutes to get out of the mountains to the main coastal road. At the road a driver from the beach hotel Becca was staying at met our car and drove me back to the beach resort to reunite with Becca! It was only about a 15min drive.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once I met back up with Becca she informed me of how bad my bag smelled and made me put everything outside to air out before showing me the resort. There was a nice pool and it there was also direct access to the beach.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
