<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We actually ended up in the ruins of an ancient city called Phaselis. It was ~$15 to get in for the two of us and ended up being super cool. There were so many ruins and 3 beaches. The ruins were actively being restored in parts but you could climb and wander around the ruins to your hearts content. We learned the city was founded in 690 BC and was known for its harbors. It was an important center for trade and commerce. The city was influenced by   The Persians, Egyptians, Roman’s, and the Greeks (a melting pot of culture). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There actually was a Hadrians gate at the site. We  later found out there are several monuments known as Hadrians gate built to commemorate the the Roman emperor Hadrian’s visit to the city. This gate wasn’t really a gate- more like the ruins of one. It turns out the most famous gate is in Antalya. Other famous gates are in Athens and Jeresh (Jordan) which I think I’ve seen both. </p>

<p>After the beach we backtracked a little ways to the town of Cirali to see mount chimera. We planned our day so we would get there just before sunset, and our timing ended up being perfect. The mountain is known for the continuous flames that come out of holes in the rock at the top. It’s caused by methane leaking out of the ground and igniting with the air. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Mythology says a guy named Bellerophon tamed Pegasus, then used a lead spear to kill the Chimera- a mythical creature with the body of a lion, head of a goat, and tail of a snake….. and it could also spew fire. One version of the myth says Bellerophon was tasked to kill the chimera after declining the advances of Queen Anteia, who then turned around and accused Bellerophon of trying to seduce her (the ol’ switcheroo). The king, Proteus, then tasked Bellerophon with killing the Chimera in hopes he would die in the process (he couldn’t just sentence him to death because he was a hero). Anyway this is supposedly the mountain he killed the Chimera on and that’s the real reason for all the fires. </p>
