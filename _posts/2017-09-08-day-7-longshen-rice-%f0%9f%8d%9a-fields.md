I think my body hates me, Today I woke up around 1am feeling like death, I'm not sure if it was something I ate (maybe the scorpions or the dishwater 😂) but my stomach was not feeling good at all. I was able to get a little bit more sleep and eventually got out of bed around 6am. Luckily we had a 3 hour bus ride ahead of us in order to get to the famous rice terraces so I could relax for a bit.

What I didn't realize was that our tour guide spoke absolutely no English and for the first hour of the bus ride spoke completely in Chinese which I can only assume was about the history of the rice fields. Some where in the middle of talking he started singing haha! Surprisingly not a bad voice but again no clue what the hell he was saying 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1321.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-555" width="3024"> Side note: One thing I noticed about china is that the Chinese don't do lines. I'm not sure if they just don't understand them or if they don't care. I guess when you live in a country with nearly a billion and a half people if you don't push and shove your way to the front you'll never get in. Another thing is they really like to spit... like an abnormal amount. Which wouldn't be that annoying except when they spit it's like they're in some sort of competition to see who can make the most noise while spitting. It's weird and kinda nasty haha.

Anyway, The 3 hour bus ride took us deep into the northern mountains. We finally stopped at what we thought was the entrance to the rice terraces but it turned out we had to switch over to another smaller bus. I was feeling a bit better until we left in the new bus. This ride took almost another hour and the driver was flying up one of the curviest roads on the side of a mountain that was just small enough to fit the bus and had sections of the road that had collapsed in what I would assume was some sort of landslide 😳. On the plus side though the views were pretty incredible!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1324.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-556" width="3024"> When we finally made it to our destination in one piece. I wasn't feeling good again, but thankfully Jamie had some meds that seemed to help. We were given the choice to take the cable car up to the peak or to hike. We of course chose to hike it! We had a ton of time so we figured we might as well (it's more fun if you have to work for a view). The only other people in our group that chose not to take the cable car were three French girls. They told us they were going to follow us as they were smoking their cigs (good luck 😂). They were able to keep up with us for all of about 10 min of the 40 minute hike. Along the way we saw some pretty cool views and ran into some Chinese people from another tour group that we made friends with and of course wanted to take a selfie with us.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1326.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-557" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1329.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-559" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1340.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-558" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1344.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-560" width="2576"> Exhausted we finally made it to the top which had more amazing views of all the rice terraces that my photos don't do justice.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1353.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-561" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1358.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-564" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1359.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-563" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1363.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-562" width="2576"> We walked around the mini village at the top enjoying the views and then stopped at one of the restaurants. Jamie and I both tried the bamboo rice and I also had the beef and fried rice. The bamboo rice is essentially rice and some meat and veggies shoved into a little bamboo stick, cooked, and then split in half. It was actually pretty good!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1367.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-570" width="3024"> While we were eating it got super foggy out and started raining (glad we were able to make it to the top before that started!). The rain didn't last too long and once it cleared up we left the restaurant to walk around some more. Near one of the hotels at the top Jamie and I found the 4 cutest little puppies! So we ended up playing with them for a while, I was tempted to try and take one with me!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1372.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-565" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1373.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-566" width="4032"> At this point it was about time to head back. Before we did we stopped and had some ice cream. We decided to take a different route back to the bus which ended up being longer but turned out to have much better views than the one we hiked up. It took us through a couple different villages on the way back. All of the locals were super friendly and fun to talk to.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1380.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-567" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1385.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-568" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1393-1.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-573" width="4032"> We made it back to the bus which left almost immediately but we ended up having to wait a good 40min at the second bus before we left.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1396-1.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-574" width="4032"> Once we got back to Guilin we went to our hostel to drop off some stuff and then walked to try and get to the elephant hill. It was closing soon so we rushed over as fast as we could. One thing I like about Guilin is that crosswalks aren't really a thing. Instead if you want to cross the street you have to just go for it and hope you don't get hit, it's like playing frogger on steroids. Unfortunately they closed the entrance to the hill earlier than advertised so when we got there we weren't able to make it in :(. On our walk over however we ran into more Asians that wanted to get a picture with us 😂 this must be what it feels like to be famous.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1400-1.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-572" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1401-1.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-571" width="4032"> We ended up walking back to the main strip and found what looked like a really cool restaurant from the outside. When we got in we realized no one there spoke any English and the menu was completely in Chinese. 'That's okay' we thought, we can use google translate to figure everything out...that didn't work so well. I successfully ordered a beer 🍻 while Jamie, who tried to order wine, ordered a fifth or some kind of hard liquor 😂. The food we ordered to share was alright, there wasn't any meat in it. We also order dumplings that ended up being filled with some kind of lettuce. I'm pretty sure the place was a vegetarian restaurant haha. Luckily all the food was super cheap so we didn't feel bad about not eating a lot of it.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1402.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-569" width="3024"> After dinner we stopped at the smoothie shop across the street and then made our way back to the hostel. According to the phone we walked 10.7 miles today. Hoping tomorrow I feel better because we have another busy day planned!
