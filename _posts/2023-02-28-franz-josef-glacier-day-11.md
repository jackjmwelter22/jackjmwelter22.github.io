<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we didn’t have to wake up quite as early. We had breakfast at the Airbnb and then got the car loaded and over to the meeting point for our sky dive! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sarah had changed her mind overnight and decided she didn’t want to go. The guy at the checkin counter did his best to try and convince her but she ended up staying with her decision not to go. After we were all checked in we ended up driving ourselves to the Franz Josef airport (the shuttle was too full) and then getting geared up for our dive. They made us wear these jump suits and silly padded helmets that looked like rugby helmets. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Fortunately Becca and I were on the first plane up while Pat ended up having to wait for the 4th plane. The flight up to 13,500’ (our jump altitude) was quick. The weather today was perfect! Very little clouds which meant great views of the mountains and the sea. Becca was the first jumper out of the plane while I followed her shortly after. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/6pVB5Ed.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The jump was awesome! Really makes me want to learn more about the sport. The free fall lasted around a minute, we did some turns/spins. I was surprisingly not scared at all on this jump (I guess I’ve become desensitized). After the minute free fall the instructor pulled the chute and then let me do some canopy flying, steep turns left and right and flares. As we got closer to the landing site he took over and we landed on our butts. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/MEDTgv6.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Since I was heavier we landed shortly before Becca so I got to see her landing. After we were back on the ground we got all our gear off. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We realized it was going to take a while for Pat to jump so Becca and I decided to go do the gorge and tunnel hike which wasn’t far. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We did the gorge hike which was about two miles. There was a cool suspension bridge over the river and a path down so you could get to the water, it was very cold!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Since we still hadn’t heard from pat, After the gorge we did the tartare tunnel hike. The hike is connected to the gorge hike and is another 2 miles hike. The hike took us to an old tunnel that was used to transport fresh water to the town.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The tunnel was cool but was filled with ankle deep water. I went a good ways into it (not sure if it ends somewhere) and my feet were pretty soaked so I turned back. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/NofEMn8.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the tunnel we hiked back to the car and finally heard back from pat that he survived his jump so we went and picked them up. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We decided to do another slightly longer (6.5 mile) hike called Robert’s point with pat and Sarah. The hike was pretty cool, lots of long suspension bridges and very steep! Becca wasn’t feeling great and I was the only one who made it to the end of the trail. Unfortunately the clouds had rolled in so you could barely see the glacier. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
