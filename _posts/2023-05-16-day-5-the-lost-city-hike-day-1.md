<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I woke up around 6:30am and got everything ready for the hike. Becca and I went to a nearby cafe for breakfast. We had omelets and a big bowl of fruit and hot chocolate. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/MMfZZBM.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Becca was not feeling great so she decided to not do the hike and go to the beach resort instead while I did the lost city hike. I walked down to the tour office at 8:30 to pay and meet up with the tour. After paying we got loaded up in the cars around 9 and drove about 2 hours to the town of mamey to start the hike. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Quq9hIM.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There were 8 other people on my tour and one guide (Antonio) and one translator (Pilar). Two were an older couple from Germany (Evelyn &amp; Christian), two Dutch friends (Ann Marleen &amp; Marlijn), two English friends (Amanda &amp; Darren), and a young English couple (Bea &amp; Tom). Everyone was super nice and friendly!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We started with a quick lunch in Mamey that was chicken rice and then our guide, Antonio explained us the plan for the hike (in Spanish) which our translator, Pilar, translated for us. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/S2VdAsY.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The basic plan is a four day hike where we reach the lost city on day 3 and then hike all the way back to the start. The total hike is just under 30 miles and over 8,000’ of elevation gain. It’s a lot but over 4 days should be no problem. After the brief explanation we grabbed all our bags and started! This was only the second serious multi day hike I had done and the first where I had to carry my own gear (the Kilimanjaro hike we had porters to carry everything!). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The first part of the hike is in the farmerlands. The trail was a fairly wide rode and we frequently were passed by motorbikes and mules transporting people and various supplies. </p>

<p>We ended the day at camp Adan just across the buritaca river. The hike today was only about 4 miles, mostly uphill, but overall not too bad. When we arrived at camp we got our beds (bunk beds) and then went for a swim in the river. It was a little pool with a ~12ft cliff jump. It was super refreshing!</p>
