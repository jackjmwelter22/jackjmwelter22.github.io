<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I woke up around 6:30 and wrote my blog for the day before Becca and Bennett woke up. We eventually all got up at 8am and made some eggs and bacon for breakfast. After eating we loaded up the car and left for our first stop around 9am.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our first stop was at a natural bridge. Basically a rock that was cut through by the river forming a bridge. It was about a 45 minutes from our resort. It was cool to see but super crowded - you didn’t have to hike at all to get to it which makes it too easy to access. There was a girl taking selfies on a dangerously slick rock right next to where the water rushed though - I swear she was going to fall in.</p>

<p>The lake was very picturesque. They had canoes you could rent, but it was pretty overcast and cold so we gave it a miss. After hanging out at the lake we got back in the car and drove to our next stop, Takawa Falls.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The falls were a 30 minute drive from the lake. Parking here was also crowded,  but we got a spot on the side of the road relatively close. The trail to  and from the falls was only about a mile total.</p>

<p>After seeing the falls we walked back to the start of the trail and ate our sandwiches for lunch on a bench.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we got back to the car and drove back towards Banff to another hike called Johnston Canyon. On the highway there are land bridges over the road for the wildlife to avoid traffic - really interesting.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Once again, parking was crowded. We were able to park in the lower lot. The hike itself was only 3.5 miles round trip.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The trail was mostly paved with railings. Because it was so accessible it was very crowded. There were queues of people to take pictures at a few of the viewpoints (which were not near as pretty as some of the other hikes we had done)- Becca and I didn’t not wait to do that haha.</p>

<p>Going back down was a bit of a pain - it was very crowded with slower hikers not in the best of shape, and the path is very narrow. Luckily we were able to get though without too much delay.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got back in the car and made our way back to the hotel. We decided to checkout the  indoor “playroom” with Bennett. It was super random - picture the old McDonald's play areas on steroids.  It was a massive 3 story playhouse with slides, tunnels,  and other things you could climb around on/through. I took Bennett through to the slide, which was more difficult than I expected - the playhouse wasn’t built for a 6’4” 33 year old haha. Nonetheless it was fun.</p>
