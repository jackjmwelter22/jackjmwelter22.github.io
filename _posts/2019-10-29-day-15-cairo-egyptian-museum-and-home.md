Today we woke up at 6 and went down to eat the breakfast at the hotel. The breakfast was a buffet and had a ton of options and even some delicious strawberry smoothies. After quickly eating we took a cab to the airport to head back to Cairo.

The weird thing about Egypt as I mentioned is they love metal detectors and the airports or no exception. At the airport you have to go through security twice! Once when you get in and then again when you go to your gate. The second security line was extremely long today, luckily we arrived early enough it didn't matter. Another weird thing is they Have separate security lines for men and women.

After finally making it through to our gate we boarded and took the quick flight to Cairo, only ~1 hour. Somehow we got first class tickets so we were in the front of the plane with a ton of legroom!

Once we landed we took and Uber to hotel to check in and drop off our stuff. This was the same hotel we stayed in our first stop in Cairo.

Next we called an Uber again to take us to the Egyptian museum. The museum was MASSIVE and had lots of sarcophagi. It also looked a little disheveled and cluttered since they are moving everything to the new museum that opens next year.

The exhibit they had on king Tut was incredible. Even though the boy king didn't live or rule for long his tomb was absolutely PACKED with treasures. Here's a picture of his sarcophagus which was completely covered in gold!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3350.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1622" width="3024" height="4032">

The other thing that was particularly interesting was the mummy exhibit which was filled with actual mummies from thousands of years ago. It's incredible how well they were preserved considering how old they are.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3348.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1612" width="3024" height="4032">

After walking around the museum we had some lunch at the food stand just outside. I of course had to have one last chicken shawarma before leaving Egypt (I highly recommend if you ever go). Once we finished eating we Ubers back to hotel, traffic here is crazy and getting back out of the city took about twice as long as the trip in.

At the hotel we watched the movie the mummy (original) and then went to the steam and sauna to relax. The one employee there was all about the sauna and was super enthusiastic telling us to lay down and sleep in the sauna and bringing us water to not overheat 😂. By the way this sauna was super hot compared to any I've ever done so it was a struggle to stay in for the full 15 minutes.

Afterwards we went back to hotel room to watch the new version of the mummy movie (with ton cruise) and ended up ordering room services once we were lazy and didn't want to dress up and go down to one of the restaurants. I ended up getting the butter chicken which was good, but didn't have any naan bread with it!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3353.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1613" width="3088" height="2320">

After eating we slept for a few hours and then made our way to the airport and said our goodbyes. My flight back home was at 4:30am so I took the shuttle to airport at 3am. The security was slow again but I made it to my flight on time. My first flight was to Rome and then I was supposed to go to Atlanta but was rerouted through Detroit since the flight was over booked. I ended up getting back to Savannah around 10:30pm.

This was another great trip that I'm super grateful I was able to go on. Traveling with my sister and Sam was a blast and hopefully will have the opportunity for some more adventures in the future! Nothing is planned yet for next year, but my dad and I are thinking about maybe going out to Scotland to try and play the famous St Andrews golf course.
