Today we woke up around 6:30 to get ready and had a small breakfast in the main house at 7. The brothers of the house were staying at drove us to the airport. We left at 7:30 and got to the airport around 8:30. Patryk was struggling to get his bags to be the correct weight so he had to frantically reorganize everything and give me some of his stuff so that he was allowed to check his bag.

We ended up flying on a small ATR which is a propeller plane. The flight to Zanzibar only  took a little over an hour.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_1460.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-937" height="2320" width="3088">

Once we arrived we met our driver outside the airport (which was one of the smallest worst looking airports I've seen haha). The driver took us straight to our resort, the Royal Zanzibar beach resort. The drive took about an hour and 15 min. On the way we passed by tons of tiny, very poor villages that. It was really strange being on our way to a beautiful resort.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_2495.jpg" class="wp-image-942 size-full" height="4032" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_2496.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-941" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_2485.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-940" height="750" width="1000">

We got to the resort a little after 1. It was freaking awesome! It had like 5 pools, an awesome beach, and everything was all inclusive! The first two days we just relaxed by the pool and beach. Pat and I played some vball against the staff and they were really impressed with how good we were 😂. I don't think they were used to losing.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_2511.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-944" height="3024" width="4032">

On the third day we went scuba diving near another island about 45 min from our beach called Menemba. It was known to be the best diving in Zanzibar, we enjoyed but we weren't too impressed, I think it was a little over hyped but it was still a lot of fun and much needed break from laying at the beach.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_2519.jpg" class="wp-image-947 size-full" height="1530" width="2040">

On the last day we woke up early to have a quick breakfast and then make our long journey back home. We left the resort by taxi around 7am to drive us to stone town on the south end of the island to catch the 9:30am ferry. The ferry was a complete cluster f*ck. I'm honestly not surprised they had issues with overcrowding. Once we were on the ferry however it was a smooth ~2hour ride to dar es salaam.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_2513.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-943" height="3024" width="4032">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_2515.jpg" class="wp-image-946 size-full" height="1932" width="2576">

At the port we met our second driver who took us tow the airport. The airport in dar es salaam is one of he worst airports for such a large city that I've ever seen. We arrived like 4 hours early so we had a lot of time to kill. We ended up watching the movie sky scrapper which was so over the top and not realistic it was hard to watch.

The flight from dar to Dubai was on a 777 and only ~5.5 hours. Which would've been easy.... if the smelliest person in the world didn't sit next to us. I'm not really sure what was wrong with this man but he wore a winter glove on one of his hands and it smelled like death. I'm not sure what exactly was going on with it but I didn't want to know. On the flight I watched American animals (pretty interesting) and dear dictator (not very good).

When we arrived in Dubai shortly after 11pm and made our way to the complimentary hotel emirates booked us (it was a big hassle to book). The hotel was a 2 min shuttle from the airport, the shuttle was super packed. This layover was not long enough to go to the city (plus we were exhausted) so we just slept and woke up around 6am to eat breakfast and catch the flight back to Chicago at 9:40am.

After a long 14 hour flight (and plenty of movies later) we finally made it back to Chicago! My parents picked me up from the airport. Tomorrow I head back to Savannah to get back to reality for a little while.

Can't wait for the next trip. Looking at Costa Rica! But for now, as they say in Tanzania, Hakuna Matata!
