<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 0 - Thursday</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made our way to the airport after work. This time it was much smoother checking in on our Delta flight to Atlanta. Delta actually let us check in before we even got to the airport, but we still ended up getting there two hours early just in case. The only small hiccup we had was while waiting for the airplane. Bennett pooped on Becca :).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Other than that everything was on time and the weather was great. We even boarded a little early and got our seats, but unfortunately, shortly after everyone boarded the pilots announced there was a problem with the GPS that they had to troubleshoot...you literally can’t make this up! Fast-forward two extremely frustrating hours later and we’re still sitting at the gate when finally the pilot announced they fixed the issue and we can go. So we left Savannah at 7 pm instead of 5 pm, and our connecting flight in Atlanta was leaving at 8:10. This meant essentially zero chance of us making the direct Rio flight.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We landed and got to the gate around 7:50. Of course the terminal we were at was on the complete opposite side of the airport from the Rio flight. I still had some hope since the plane hadn’t left yet, but by the time we de-boarded our connecting flight was taxiing away from the gate. It was super frustrating and stressful.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Fortunately, Delta automatically rebooked us onto another flight. The only bad thing was this flight went to São Paulo instead of Rio, and then had a three hour layover in São Paulo before going to Rio. This put us into Rio about seven hours later than we expected. To put a cherry on top when we got to the gate, they told us we would not have a bassinet, couldn't guarantee seats next to each other, and Becca would not have a gluten free meal.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This new flight departed at 10 PM so while we were waiting, Becca went back to the Delta service desk and they were able to get her some meal vouchers so that she could have something to eat on the flight. She ended up getting three Qdoba steak bowls with the $50 in vouchers Delta gave her (This was generous compared to the $70 we got for the two of us from American when we had to spend the whole day in Miami).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The new flight departed on time and in our first bit of luck, we ended up getting the whole four seat middle row to ourselves!  This was a lifesaver! I was able to get a little bit of sleep on the flight by laying across the seats and even on the ground at one point. Bennett on the other hand, slept the entire nine hour flight until just before landing when they turned on all the lights and made a bunch of announcements.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once we landed in São Paulo it took forever to get our gate checked stroller back. Fortunately we got to go to the front line for immigration so it kind of balanced out. We had to re-go through security (fortunately they didn’t care we had water in our backpacks). And then made it to our gate. Our flight to Rio left at 12:20pm and took about an hour to get to Rio. It took us an hour to figure out where enterprise rental car was, there were absolutely zero signs for it. We finally realized that enterprise was called localiza here, and then we had to wait for a shuttle at the very far end of the terminal (there were literally zero signs for this). We finally made it to the localiza office and got a “ password” (basically their version of a next in-line ticket). We waited for about 10 minutes . While waiting there was a friendly lady talking to us who basically said why are you going to Rio (implying that it’s not great for tourist), she also reminded us (probably the fourth person) to be careful of pickpockets and which areas you drive/grow go through. After the 10 minutes, they finally called our password and we checked in and got our car!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We loaded up and drove towards the Christ the Redeemer tram. Fortunately, we found a parking lot near a Pickleball court/church. It was only a few blocks away to the station where we able to get tickets for the 3:40 PM tram which departed 5 minutes later. The tram ride took about 20 minutes to get to the top. The statue itself was massive and really cool to see, also the views from the top were really nice. We could see Ipanema and the main center of Rio. Supposedly, it wasn’t very crowded, but there were still a ton of people at the top (I would hate to be there when it’s actually crowded). we took a few pictures and then left shortly after (way too many people and we were exhausted). We couldn’t figure out how to pay to leave the parking lot so we ended up following another car out.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Airbnb was only 15 minutes away, and the traffic actually wasn’t too bad. We parked at the Airbnb lot and made our way up with all of our stuff. We unpacked a bit and I showered then we went to a steakhouse on the corner called Garota de Ipanema. Becca saw the people next to us with a sizzling plate of meat and told the waiter we want that. I also tried a chopp (basically a small cold draft beer) and Becca had a caprinha (their version of a mojito). The food was really good. You actually had to cook the meat on the sizzling pan. It was sliced really thin so by the time you moved it to the middle of the pan it would cook in a few seconds and then you would flip it to the other side. It was super delicious.</p>
