<p>Today we woke up at five again and cuddled with Bennett. We got most of our things packed and ready, and then went up to breakfast around 6:30. It was a little early for breakfast, but they were able to actually get our breakfast a little before seven. We had the same thing as before-I had the continental, Becca had the Nicaraguan, and Bennett had pancakes.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yoga ended at 9am, Becca said the yoga class was really good. We all went back to the cabin while Becca showered and packed, and then we left around 10am to go back to the ferry at Mayogalpa.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The drive took a little under an hour. We made a quick stop at a bank right before the ferry. There was a strange looking dude as we pulled up to the ferry entrance who helped us get the tickets and everything settled for the car which was slightly confusing. We were supposed to have a reservation for 12 but since we were there early, they were able to load us on the 11:30 ferry, which was super nice.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This ferry had an open air top, which was a lot nicer.  On the ride back we had some really good views of the island and the volcanoes. After just over an hour on the ferry, we made it to San Jorge. We loaded back into the car and started driving towards the Pacific coast-  San Juan del sur.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>It was about 45 minutes to San Juan Del Sur. We stopped at Pali - the local grocery store-  then made a quick stop at the gas station before driving into town for lunch.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ate at a place called King Curry which was right on the beach. While waiting for our food, I went out onto the beach to put my feet in the water and to check it out. The views were really nice and the water was the perfect temperature.</p>

<p>I had their version of butter chicken, which was really good. It was like a fusion between Asian and Indian curry that had a sweet flavor to it with pineapples. Which sounds kind of weird, but it was actually really good. The naan bread with the dish was also delicious. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating, we got back into the car and drove another 30 minutes to Playa El Coco, a small beach south of San Juan del Sur where we were staying. The road to get there was a mix of really nice new highway and bumpy dirt road. It honestly is kind of in the middle of nowhere and I was very pleasantly surprised to see how nice the condos were that we are staying in.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The place was literally a compound- a private gate with at least six security guards patrolling. The unit we were in was very nice. Downstairs there is a large kitchen, dining room, living room, and a sliding door that opens to the garden area with the pool and beach. On the second floor there are three bedrooms and an upper deck. And most importantly it had air conditioning!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The condo also has a really nice shared pool that had a big shallow end, which was perfect for Bennett, and was directly on the beach. This beach is much more secluded and private. On the busiest day we maybe saw ten other people there. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
