<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The flight from Lima left around 1am and took ~2 hours plus and hour time change. I slept most of the flight (no one was in the middle seat next to me) and we arrived in La Paz around 4am. The immigration process took FOREVER they needed to scan a copy of our passports and itinerary before we were allowed to pay for the visa on arrival which was $160 USD. After finally getting out of the airport around 5:30am. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After a 30min cab ride to our hotel we unpacked and took a much deserved nap. We woke up around 10:30 and Becca and I decided have some coca tes upstairs on the deck of our hotel which had great views of the city. After we went for a short walk around the city to explore. The hotel is really central so we walked down the witches market and Calle srganara both were kinda dead since it was Sunday I guess. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>We also walked to plaza mayor de San Francisco and saw the San Francisco basilica. There was a mass going on inside so we just popped in for a quick look. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/SMx3dlT.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After seeing the church we walked back up to the hotel and then head out again to meet a friend of a friend, Maria for some lunch at a restaurant called La tranquera. The food was really good we ordered two big plates of meat that had everything from steak, chicken, and pork to blood sausage, intestine, cow tit, and liver! We tried everything but I think the steak was my favorite. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next Maria took us on an informal tour of the city. Because the city is so mountainous the easiest way to get around is to take the gondolas. They also give you a really nice view of the city. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/vo6XVs9.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We went from the centro area to calacoto which is much lower than the city. We walked around there and stopped at a local coffee shop before heading back up to the city to a restaurant near our hotel for some food and drinks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/ApokL50.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I had a chicken burrito and a drink called te con te. It’s a popular drink here, basically tea with a brandy type liquor called singani. After dinner we walked back to the hotel for an early bedtime to catch up on some sleep. </p>
