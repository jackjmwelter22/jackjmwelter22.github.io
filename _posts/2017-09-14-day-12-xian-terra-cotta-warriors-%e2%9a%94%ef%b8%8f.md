This morning we slept in until about 8:30. We got ready and made our way down to the lobby of the hostel for breakfast. It was only 30 yuan (~$5.50 US) for an all you can eat  breakfast, it was delicious and I definitely stuffed my face 😁.

After eating we got picked up at the hostel by our tour guide to go see the terra-cotta warriors. We picked up about 12 people and made our way by bus. It was around and hour and a half drive to get out there. Along the way our guide, JaJa (definitely not how you spell it but that's what it sounded like), gave us a rundown on the history behind the warriors.

Here's the 2min version: The first emperor  to unify china had ~7,000 people build all these clay soldiers to surround his grave. He believed they would help protect him in the afterlife. And in case that didn't sound crazy enough, the emperor decided to kill ALL of the people that helped build all the soldiers so that they would remain a secret. Fast forward to the 1970s and a farmer in china trying to dig a well on his farm stumbles across one of the warriors. For his efforts the Chinese government gave him 10 yuan (~$1.5) in exchange for his land...sounds pretty fair 😂.

The terra-cotta warriors were very cool to see. There were 3 pits in different stages of restoration. To date there's been ~8,000 warriors that have been found. Most of them were completely shattered so essentially it's a massive puzzle to put them back together and restore them.

After viewing all 3 pits we made our way to a nearby restaurant for lunch. It was another big table with a lazy Susan and all different kinds of dishes. On the tour we met 3 people from New Jersey (the first Americans we've met on our whole trip!) and also some people who were from Brussels.

After lunch we got a free tea tasting session. One of the teas (I think it was called black lychee) was delicious! The second tea we tried...not so much haha. It was a really fun experience.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1620.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-672" width="3024">

After tea we took a short bus ride to see the hill where the emperor was actually buried. His tomb had still yet to be opened because they're worried once its exposed to air it will deteriorate. Also you're not allowed to go too close because the emperor had mercury rivers in his tomb and the land is supposedly still toxic.

It wasn't really much to see honestly, kinda just a hill, but hey it was included in the tour!

We took the car back to Xi'an and got dropped off at the east gate of the wall. Xi'an city center is surrounded by a pretty large (I'd say about 40-50 foot) wall. We went up to the top and rented bicycles and rode around the whole wall which was around 8miles. It was a bumpy ride but was a cool way to see the city! The one difficulty was that the bikes only had one gear 😫.

After dropping the bikes back off. We walked back to the hostel to relax for a bit. We decided to meet the friends we just met from New Jersey at the rooftop of their hostel which was really close to ours. They had surprisingly good food there! After dinner we all caught a bus down to the wild goose pagoda to try and see the free show they do there every night. Unfortunately we just missed it! :(

We walked around a bit to see the pagoda then went back to their hostel to play beer pong 😂. Even though we missed the show it was a fun night! Tomorrow we wake up early to head to mount huashan to hike "the worlds deadliest hike" hopefully we survive!</p>
