Today I woke up early again around 4am luckily I was able to fall back asleep for another couple hours before we left the hostel at around 8 am. The plan was to take the ferry over to Macau for the day. On our way to the port we stopped at a small little bakery and picked up some things to eat for breakfast. I got like 5 different pastries and they were all really delicious and super cheap! Definitely going to stop there again tomorrow.

Again google struggled to give us good directions (they really need to fix that!) and finding the ferry was not very obvious but we eventually made it! The trip to Macau was an hour ferry ride each way and cost about $40 US, definitely worth the trip if you're ever in Hong Kong. The ferry was super nice and even had wifi!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1228.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-496" width="2576">

For those of you that don't know and are too lazy to google it (probably my mom 😜) I'll help you out- Macau was a territory of Portugal up until 1999 when it was given back to China. Macau is technically considered a special administrative region (SAR) of China but it's essentially it's own country. It has its own currency, flag, and most of the people speak Portuguese as well as Chinese. It's a really interesting mix between the two cultures.

On the ferry ride over I blogged the whole time to try and catch up with everything that happened yesterday and was able to finish just as we pulled into the Macau port. Once we got off we had to go through customs and everything like you would if you left or entered a new country. Once we got out we were able to find a bus that took us too the historic district of Macau. Side note: Macau is known for all its casinos but there is an old section of the country with forts and ruins that is preserved. The bus ended up dropping us off in front of the Grand Lisboa (a really cool and massive casino just outside the historic section). You can see the incredible disparity between rich and poor. Some areas look super run down almost like the favelas in Brazil but then you see these massive casinos and super nice and exotic cars everywhere.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1237.jpg" height="2576" class="wp-image-498" width="1932"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1236.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-499" width="4032">

We ended up walking through the Grand Lisboa casino and it was really cool. I'm not much of a gambler but it seemed like the game of choice was bacarat which I know absolutely nothing about.

We then made our way into the historic district to see the ruins of St. Paul's Cathedral. It almost looked like a prop set of a town from an old western movie. It was literally just the front wall of the church that remained standing.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1243.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-500" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1245.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-505" width="3024">

Next we hiked up an old Portuguese fort which was right next to the ruins called Monte Fortelza. I forgot to mention it was EXTREMELY hot and humid. I'm used to Savannah weather but this was another level. By the time we got to the top I was drenched in sweat and exhausted but we had some cool views of the city which made it worth it.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1247.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-506" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1254.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-507" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1250.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-508" width="4032">

We hiked backed down and made our way towards the center of the historic district, senado square. The walk was really interesting and the houses all looked like bird cages because their balconies were barred, I guess there worried about criminal ninjas or monkeys or something.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1257.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-501" width="3024">

The architecture was really interesting in the historic area, you could definitely see the Portuguese influence. We also stopped at two cathedrals: Se cathedral and Igreja de sao domingos. Both churches had an 'island' style to them (lots of pastel colors) that was really unique and something I've never seen before in a church.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1258.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-509" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1261.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-502" width="2576">

Next we made it to senado square where we stopped in a small cookie shop that offered samples of basically every cookie they had, which was a lot. I must've had at least 30 samples 😂 they were mostly good but I had a few that were pretty terrible and I'm not sure how anyone could like them.

The free samples didn't completely fill us up so we went to a Portuguese restaurant called boa mesa (it was rated highly on google). Jamie and I both ended up getting the daily special which consisted of a bean soup, pork, salad, and desert. We also split a pitcher of sangria which was perfect for such a hot day.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1263.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-504" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1264.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-510" width="3024">

After eating (the food was delicious btw) we tried to walk to another old Portuguese fort about a mile a way. We ended up hiking about halfway up and couldn't find a way to get to the top! Every entrance was blocked off so we weren't able to see it :(.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1266.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-503" width="2576">

Our final stop in the historic district was the A-Ma temple which is apparently how Macau got its name and that's all I really know about it. Well we tried to take a bus over to the temple but instead of taking bus 18A we took bus 18 which got us about a mile away from where the temple was. We grudgingly walked the rest of the way. We stopped at a small store to get some water and I also tried a Portuguese egg tart. The thing was I didn't realize it was egg, it looked more like a Rene brûlée so I was expecting it to be much sweeter, so it took me by surprise and I wasn't a big fan of it. The temple was pretty cool it was in the middle of city buildings kinda built into this little mountain/cave.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1268.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-511" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1269.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-512" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1272.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-513" width="3024"> Jamie's I'm annoyed at you face 😂

After checking out the temple we caught a bus to the Venetian casino. The Venetian is actually the biggest casino in the world, it's pretty incredible how massive it is. Even though I'm not much of a gambler I decided to test my luck at this weird dice game which I ended up losing on my first try :(. We also walked over to the Parisian casino which was also huge and had a giant replica of the rifle tower outside. Jamie and I both played the penny slots, which are probably the dumbest things I've ever seen...is it even possible to win money on those?! I played a dollars worth and jamie ended up cashing out at 30 hon Kong cents (about 4 US cents worth) 😂I'm still not sure why she even bothered.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1273.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-521" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1274.jpg" height="2576" class="wp-image-522" width="1932">

We finally made our way back to the ferry by bus to return to Hong Kong. The ferry ride was smooth but it ended up taking us to a different port than we left from which was super confusing!

Once we got back we made our way to a restaurant called the Australia Dairy company. I absolutely loved the place! It was jam packed and Jamie and I were the only foreigners in the whole place. We even had to share a table with two other people who were very friendly and gave us recommendations on what to order. The food was so weird but good. I had a soup with macaroni noodles, egg, and ham and I also got the 'famous' steamed milk pudding which I though was delicious but Jamie didn't really enjoy. During the meal I was thirsty so I decided to drink the water that was on our table in a cup, seems normal right? Well I start to look around and nobody's drinking the water...it was weird. Then I realized the cups were supposed to be used for your silver ware when you were finished. So essentially I was drinking dish water looking like an idiot 😂😂. Another plus was the food we got was superrr cheap I think the meal was a total of $12 US for the two of us. Maybe they gave me a discount for drinking the water haha!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1281.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-526" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1277.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-523" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1278.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-524" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1280.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-525" width="3024">

Cup I was drinking from 😂

After dinner we walked to see the night market which was your typical tent shops selling all sorts of crap you don't really need. It was a massive market though! We also went to the ladies market, which was essentially the same as the night market and finally pet street. Pet street was a street that...had a ton of pet shops on it...shocker right?! 😂 there were a ton of cute puppies and lots of cats for sale. If only I could bring them back on the plane with me!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1282.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-527" width="3024">

We ended up taking the metro back to the hostel and hanging out on the terrace for a bit which has an awesome view of the city.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1285.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-528" width="3024">

According to my phone we did another 12.6 miles today! Good training for the spartan race! Tomorrow we've got about half the day to hangout in Hong Kong and then we're off to Guilin china!</p>
