<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We decided we liked Lake Louise so much that we wanted to spend another day there hiking Mount Fairview. So we woke up at 4:30 am to get there in time to get a parking spot.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got to the parking lot around 5:45 and there were a ton of spaces still open. We ended up eating breakfast in the car and waiting until 6:30 am to start the hike to catch sunrise on the way up the hike.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It was still dark out, and I left my head lamp in the other pack, so Becca used her phone to light the trail. After about 30 minutes it was bright enough that we didn’t need the light anymore. The trail we were hiking was the Mount Fairview trail, a friend of mine from golf recommended it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike up to the pass was well groomed. We caught some nice views of the sunrise over the mountains.</p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Overall the trail was good but steep!! Becca was feeling tired so I took her pack for her (I looked ridiculous 😂). Talk about a good workout!</p>

<p>We then continued back down the trail to the lake. We had a drink by the lake. At this point the wind at the surface was picking up and it was funny watching the canoers struggle in the wind - there were white caps on the lake. A few of them had no idea what they were doing and crashed into the rocks near the shore. The canoe rental workers were running about furiously trying to get to the canoes before they crashed, it was hilarious.</p>

<p>Becca and I had considered canoeing but the price was $200 an hour…no thanks! I can’t believe people pay that much, especially people that clearly have no idea how to canoe. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After relaxing at the lake shore we got back into the car to head to Jasper - about 3.5 hours away. The drive is on a road called the icefield parkway and the views along the way are absolutely stunning. We made a few stops to break up the drive. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The first stop was about 45 minutes away at Peyto Lake. It was a short hike from the parking lot to two different view points. The first viewpoint was pretty crowded but the second one (better view in my opinion) wasn’t as crowded - probably because the trail isn’t paved to get there.</p>

<p>The views of the lake were awesome. An interesting fact we learned is that the lakes get their color from something called rock flour - basically as the glacier on top of the mountain grinds away the rock it makes a fine silt that flows in the runoff down and feeds the lake. This silt refracts the light in a way that makes the water look super blue. The color can change depending on the time of year and amount of silt in the water. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After taking some pictures we hiked back to the car. And drove another 35 minutes to a place called mistaya canyon. Mistaya canyon is a deep river that cut through the limestone rock over the years. It was a super short hike down. We took some pictures and got back to the car. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next to the glacier a few minutes up the road was the Columbia ice field skywalk. Basically a glass walkway. The view of it from the road did not look impressive and I think it’s about $20 a person to do!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We continued on to Jasper. There was a massive fire there this year so we had considered changing our plans. The fire damage really was noticeable until about 30 minutes from the city. As we got closer we could see massive amounts of trees burnt - it was pretty crazy. When we got to the town there was still a ton of damage - building completely leveled. It was surprising to see how some blocks were completely destroyed while others were untouched. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We finally got to our airbnb and unpacked and rested. We went to get dinner around 6pm. The first place we tried to go to was a bbq place that was closed. We ended up going to another restaurant called maligne range. The atmosphere in side was really cool (definitely newer build). Becca and I got the meat platter for two. It wasn’t a whole lot of food for two people - chicken and short rib with fries, carrots, and broccoli. The short rib was the best in my opinion. </p>
