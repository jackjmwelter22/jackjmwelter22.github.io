Today we slept in till around 9:30AM, I finally caught up on some sleep! Sleeping in the cave room was pretty cool! It was super dark in there which made it difficult to get up. After getting ready for the day we left around 10:30 to stop at the local bakery for a quick breakfast. I had a pastry with Nutella and another bagel type thing with ham and cheese, both were delicious!

After the bakery we made a quick detour to a nearby town to look for a shirt and hat for Patryk who's sunburn wasn't getting any better. Unfortunately we had no luck in the town (we were told to go here by the bakery cashier). So we decided just to continue on with our day.

Our first stop was Ancient Thera. It was ~20 min drive up and down some very windy switchback roads. The weather today was perfect again without a cloud in the sky. Ancient Thera is the ruins of a very old town from around 600 BC (if I remember correctly) and it's situated a top a mountain, hence the switchback rounds. Once we made it to the top we parked the ATVs and continued on foot. At the top of the mountain it was SUPER windy. The hike to the city ruins was very short. The ruins themselves were really cool and the views from the top of the mountain were awesome!

After exploring the ruins we made our way back down the mountain and made a quick stop at Kamara beach, which was practically at the base of the mountain. This beach was much bigger than the previous ones we'd seen and was unique because it had black sand (which was actually sand and not pebbles).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2978.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1140" width="2576" height="1920">

After the quick stop at the beach we made our way back towards where our house was to stop at the Santo winery for a tour and tasting. Santo winery is situated on the side of a mountain and is the biggest winery in Santorini. Their facility was really interesting and the wine was almost as good as the views, which were amazing! Definitely recommend.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2989.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1141" width="2576" height="1920">

After the winery we drove the ATVs to Thira, or Fira? Or Thera? We're really not sure the name of the city, but at any rate it's the largest city on Santorini. We finally ended up finding some clothes for Pat and stopped for some more delicious gyros for lunch. After eating we walked around the city some more to enjoy all the views and see where we need to meet (at the port) for our boat tour tomorrow.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190901_153359.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1152" width="3840" height="2160">

After Thira we drove another 15 min to skaros rock (on the way to Oia). Once there we climbed down and then up to the top for some more awesome views of the island and all the house built into the side of the mountain. Hiking up the rock had some sketchy parts that required some skill and actually climbing to make it up, but it was definitely worth it! Probably one of my favorite things to see on the island so far.

After hiking back from Skaros rock to our ATVs we drove the final ~15 min to oia. Once there we walked around the city and saw the Ancient ruins. The city in general was super crowded which was somewhat annoying. That being said the city is really cool, it's like a vertical maze, I have no idea how they were able to build everything into the side of the mountain like it is.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2345.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1153" width="4032" height="3024">

After walking around we went to a bar called Pelekanos to watch the sunset. Again the views were awesome!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190901_194453.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1154" width="3840" height="2160">

After the sunset we made our way back to the house by ATV, this time none of the ATVs broke which was good! Once back at the house we rested for a bit and then went down to the main square of our town for dinner. I had the sea bass and some wine. The food and the atmosphere was fantastic. After dinner we walked back and hung out for the house then went to sleep.
