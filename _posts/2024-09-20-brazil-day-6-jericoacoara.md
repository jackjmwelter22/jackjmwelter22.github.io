<p>Today we slept in till 8 AM. After we woke up, we went to the beach restaurant where breakfast was included. The breakfast was a buffet style with all sorts of pastries, scrambled eggs, etc. Becca's favorite was the "tapioca"- a crepe made from tapioca flour. The breakfast was good and had a great view of the beach. After eating, we decided to do a small hike to Pedra Furada (basically a big rock arch on the beach) which was about a mile and a half away.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before walking out of town we took a quick picture at the Jericoacoara sign in the main square.</p>

<p>Back at the hotel we decided to relax and hang out by the pool. Our hotel had two pools and both were really nice. I also decided to take the drone out and get some shots of the town in our hotel. The wind at Jeri is really strong- it is one of the top places for windsurfing, kite, surfing, and all other wind related activities. Unfortunately drones don’t do the best with wind and after about five minutes of taking some drone footage I realized the drone wasn’t able to come back because the wind was too strong blowing it further offshore! I quickly ran from the pool down to the beach and brought the drone down to a lower altitude- which thankfully wasn’t as windy - and I was able to get the drone back. It was a close one, lesson learned!</p>
