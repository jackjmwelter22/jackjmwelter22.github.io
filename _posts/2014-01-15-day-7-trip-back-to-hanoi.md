<p style="text-align:justify;">Today we woke up around 7 and went down to breakfast at the hotel. I tried the Vietnamese breakfast today which consisted of a big bowl of beef soup, which was a weird thing to have for breakfast but nonetheless was good! After lunch we got on the bus and headed to the dock. The trip back to Hanoi was the same routine as before but reverse and after about 4 hours we made it back to the Hanoi bus station where we had to haggle to get a cab for a decent price. Finally after a short cab ride we made it back to our hotel. They had the room set up with flower petals on the bed and everything, so romantic! Hahah</p>

<div>After lunch we went back to the hotel to rest and watch tv. Today was a rest day since we had already done most of the things we wanted to do in Hanoi. Since we had a late lunch we went out to dinner at a place called Bun Bo Nam Bo. Like most of the shops in the old quarter it was very skinny and long, almost like a long hallway. When we arrived they didn't give us a menu instead the waitress asked "how many". At first we we're confused about when we should order however after a couple minutes we were handed a big bowl of Bum Bo. This turned out to be one of the only things they served which is why we weren't offered a menu. The food was awesome! We were the only white people there and the place was packed with tons of local Vietnamese so we figured the place must be good.</div>
