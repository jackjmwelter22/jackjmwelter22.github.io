Today we woke up at 5 AM, I slept surprisingly well and did feel much jet lag at all. I had a quick snack for breakfast and finished off the rest of the wine (couldn't let it go to waste!). We finally cleaned and packed up and left around 5:30 for the airport with an Uber. We got to the airport about 30 min later and made it through security super fast and got to our gate.

We needed up flying on this super old Boeing 717 that had seen better days. The flight took about 45 min and we flew over the island before turning and landing at the airport at ~8:45AM. We got some great views of the island from above.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2271.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1115" width="4032" height="3024">

After landing we grabbed a taxi and took it a little over a mile to an ATV rental place. Even though it was a short trip it still cost 20 euros, definitely more expensive than Athens! To make matters worse the rental company we got dropped off at was super sketchy and told us we could only rent ATVs for 50 euros a day instead of the 35 euros advertised on the website. Because of this we decided to walk to another place our VRBO hosts suggested and rented atvs from them. One note, if you ever come to Greece make sure you have your international drivers permit otherwise they won't rent to you.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190831_133642.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1116" width="3840" height="2160">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190831_104348.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1127" width="2688" height="1512">

Once we got our ATVs we Drove to a place called the family bakery to meet our VRBO host and get the keys to our house. The island is super confusing to navigate so we got a little lost on the way but nevertheless made it on time. One funny thing about Santorini is that they don't have addresses for anything, so I guess you kinda just have to know where you're going 😂.

After meeting our host he showed us our place which was ~2 min walk away. The house is super cool it's in this little maze of a neighborhood packed with houses and has a really big courtyard and is built like a cave! We chilled at rental house for about and hour and a half and ate some lunch we picked up from the bakery.

After resting we decided to take the ATVs to the south end of the island to explore the red beach which was the rocky beach that looked like it was cut out of the side of this big red rock. It looked really cool, but the beach was packed with people and it was more rocky than sandy, not somewhere I'd really hangout for the day.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190831_130809.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1117" width="3840" height="2160">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2944.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1129" width="3024" height="4032">

Our next stop was the furthest sourthern point of the island to see the old light house. We hiked around and got some good views of the island and had smoothies for a snack while sitting and enjoying the view.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2954.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1130" width="2576" height="1920">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190831_135232.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1126" width="3840" height="2160"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190831_143818.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1125" width="3840" height="2160">

Next we drove to the "black beach" which was another little beach famous for its black sand, which and was more like stones than anything but it was still cool to see! This beach was much less crowded and bigger than the red beach.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2961.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1131" width="2576" height="1920">

At this point it was around 3PM so we decided to head back to the house to grab some things and then make are way all the way to the most northern part of the island Oia (confusingly pronounced ee-ah) to relax and wait for the sunset. The drive took about 40min and had some really cool views of the island. We got lost only a few times, I actually ended up just driving with my phone gps in my lap to navigate. We ended up at a place called Lioyerma pool bar. It was a really nice restaurant with an awesome view of the ocean and a pool. We ended up hanging out here swiming, resting, and eating gyros till sunset.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2280.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1124" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2282.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1123" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2284.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1122" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190831_194326.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1121" width="2688" height="1512">

I also made friends with a cat at the bar by giving him so if our gyro that fell in the ground. Side note: there are a ton of cats we've seen here. About an hour before sunset the place started to get more crowded. The sunset itself was really nice and we had an awesome view of it. Once the sun dropped we quickly left in hopes of making the long ride back before it got to dark. This plan however did not work out. About halfway back we were cruising home when all of the sudden I heard a loud clunk! Sound and immediately felt my ATV lose power. The road was smooth so I didn't hit anything, just something went very wrong with the transmission. We pulled over and tried in vain to get the ATV into gear with no luck. We frantically called the rental company and explained the situation and they sent a 2 guys with a replacement rental over in less than 30 min!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2286.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1119" width="4032" height="3024">

The new replacement ATV was bigger which was nice. We could tell the guy that came was super mad about the ATV breaking down. At this point it was super dark out so we slowly made our way back to our house for the night. Once back we decided to walk around the little town for a little and then played a board game in the courtyard once we got back to the house (pat and Sarah never have a shortage of new games!). I got lucky and somehow won (beginners luck) and then we hung out inside for a little before going to bed.
