<p>Today we woke up early at 5am to get to Tre Cime di Lavaredo and hike. The weather forecast was looking bad, so we debated wether or not to postpone the hike till tomorrow but we ended up deciding to risk the weather in order to have more time tomorrow getting to Slovenia.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The drive took about 40 minutes to get to the park. The entrance fee was 30 Euro (a bit steep, but not bad split 4 ways). We drove into the park and up right next to the 3 peaks, there was a parking lot right next to the Refugio Auronzo. The trailhead to get to the other side of the peaks started right there and wrapped around them to the right. We hiked about 20 minutes before catching our first glance at the peaks.</p>

<p>We continued to hike up along the ridgeline heading towards the summit of Monte Paternokofel. It wasn’t long before we reached another set of tunnels and Klettersteig wire (we were loosely following this <a href="https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/via-ferrata-de-luca-innerkofler-und-schartenweg-klettersteige-paternkofel/">route</a>). The views along the way were great, we were lucky the clouds and bad weather was holding off.</p>

<p>There was a steeper section of the feratta that involved some climbing that led us up to the summit of paternokofel. There was a cross at the peak as per usual. We were in the clouds while climbing up, but they cleared again when we reached the top and gave us some good views of The Valley below but unfortunately the 3 peaks were covered.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sarah and Becca ended up following the correct path down through some tunnels and ferattas while Pat and I ‘created’ our own off piste route down the gulley of the mountain. It was a fun hike down but filled with tons of loose shale that would create mini rockslides as you rocked. Probably not the smartest route down, but we survived. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pat and I stopped for a break at the bottom on a small crest near the second Refugio called locatelli to do some droning.</p>

<p>After our break we started to make our way back to our car, this time we took the route around the north side of the mountain, which had more views of the peaks and the surrounding landscape.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made it to the car about an hour later, we passed by a field filled with a ton of cows. After loading up we drove back to our airbnb in Cortina and walked around the town. We walked all the way up and down the Main Street, mostly window shopping. We found a place to eat but the kitchen didn’t open until 7pm so we sat and played a small board game while having a drink.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When the kitchen opened we got dinner, we all tried Spatzle and Candederli (basically dumplings) as an apetizer (they were recommended to us by our Italian friend Maria). I also had another pizza…when in Italy! Everything was really tasty!</p>
