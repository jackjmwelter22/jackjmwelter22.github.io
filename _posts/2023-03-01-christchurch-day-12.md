<p>We spent about 1 hour there hiking around. Then we finished up the 2ish hour drive to Christchurch and made it to our Airbnb. Our Airbnb was close to downtown and also had a hot tub in the backyard. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We settled in and then decided to go do another escape room since we didn’t have much else to see in the city. We did the room called Nikola where you try and recover the designs from nikola tesla. The room was cool, very high production value but the clues weren’t very unique. We would have finished without any clues but one of the items wasn’t working correctly and needed some excessive force to activate. We ended up finishing in 46 minutes, but would have been much quicker if we didn’t get stuck on that one item. </p>
