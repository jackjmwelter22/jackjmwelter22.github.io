<p style="text-align:justify;">I decided the best strategy was to wait until the last minute to pack all my stuff so I finished packing last night around 10pm, slept for ~4 hours and then hit the road to the Atlanta airport at 2am 😫. ATL is about a 3.5 hour drive from my house, which sucks but the plane ticket was $800 cheaper than flying from Savannah! The ride up was smooth, I had to chug red bull to keep myself awake.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">Getting to the airport from the parking lot and through security was super easy and I got to my gate like 3 hours early...shoulda slept another hour! First I flew to Detroit which was about a 2 hour flight and then had an hour and a half layover until my 14 hour flight to Beijing. I think I caught a few minutes of sleep here and there but for the most part I stayed awake and watched wizard of lies, unforgettable, split, and gone girl.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-393" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0824.jpg" width="3024" height="4032"><img class="wp-image-394" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0818.jpg" width="1932" height="2576"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">Jamie and I ended up landing right around the same time (~2pm), however we were arriving in different terminals. The plan was for Jamie to take the shuttle and meet me in my terminal. Seemed simple enough, but the shuttle signs were not obvious and there were a ton of different buses all going different places. Finally after about an hour she finally made it to my terminal!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-392" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0829.jpg" width="2576" height="1932"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">From there we took the train into city and then the subway to get right by our hostel. They lady at the front desk was super helpful and gave us the overview of what all we needed to see and what to avoid. After quickly dropping off our bags we walked a few blocks to Jingshan park.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">The park is pretty massive and right in front of the forbidden city. Also there are 5 pagodas you can hike up to that give you a great view of the city.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-396" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0841.jpg" width="1932" height="2576"><img class="wp-image-397" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0837.jpg" width="4032" height="3024"></p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">After hiking for a bit we were hungry so we walked to a nearby restaurant district which wrapped around a big lake that had paddle boats you could rent and weird old Asian dudes in speedos swimming 😂.</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-398" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0842.jpg" width="4032" height="3024"> We ended up stopping at this hot pot restaurant (basically China's version of fondue). We were the only white people in the whole place which made us stand out. Not only that but let's just say my chopstick skills are not up to par (I'm blaming some of it on the jet lag/exhaustion) and I was constantly dropping the noodles and meat while trying to move them from the pot to my plate. Basically this meant the table became a big plate for me. Needless to say the people next to us were laughing at me...oops!</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;"><img class="wp-image-399" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_0846.jpg" width="4032" height="3024"> One thing we noticed is that it seems like nowhere accepts cash. They all use this app called wechat which is basically China's version of iMessage combined with Venmo. I forgot to mention that Facebook, google, Instagram, etc are all blocked in china unless you use VPN. Crazy! Another weird thing we noticed on the way back was there were a lot of old Asian dudes walking around without there shirts on, apparently this is a thing in China?</p>
<p style="text-align:justify;">Well I've been up for about 30 hours now (trying to fight the jet lag). Hopefully this post is at least semi coherent, I promise I'll do better with the next ones!</p>
