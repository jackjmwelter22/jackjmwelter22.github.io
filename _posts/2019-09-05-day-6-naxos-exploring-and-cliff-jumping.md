Today I woke up around 7, I decided to go for a run to clear my head, I ended up doing 6.5 miles up and down coast, mostly on the beach but I also ran around the Apollo portara which is a famous arch ruin in the town. It was a really nice run and good to get some exercise and a great way to see the city. part of the run was through a dry lakebed so it looks like I was running in water on the map.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2498.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1220" width="1074" height="1074"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2460.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1221" width="3088" height="2320">

After my run I got back and cooled down on the roof of the Airbnb then showered and made some Greek yoghurt mixed with oats and honey for breakfast. Pat and Sarah woke up shortly after around 10ish and once everyone was ready we decided to go take the scooter and ATV and explore the island.

Our first stop we drove to engares to see an olive oil museum. There was a free tour that was surprisingly really good and we got to try some free samples at the end. They had this olive marmalade that was delicious but unfortunately the containers were too big to take back :(.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2464.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1224" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2467.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1223" width="3088" height="2320">

After that we drove to some small ruins called kouros that are famous. It's basically a statue of a guy made in marble laying down. To get to the ruins we had to drive through little towns and the mountains which was really fun. Also next to the ruins we decided to take a path which we thought would lead us to an old quarry but it turned out to not go anywhere. Oh well, the scenery on the path was nice at least!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2468.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1226" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2477.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1225" width="3088" height="2320">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190905_122504.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1241" width="2688" height="1512">

After Kouros we drove to try and find a church called panagia (I think that's what it's called) but instead we ended up in a random town in the mountains so we had lunch at a local restaurant called the ARTernative bar. It was a really cool atmosphere and the food was great I ate calzone that was more like open pizza with all the toppings under the cheese.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2484.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1227" width="3088" height="2320">

After lunch we drove to the southwest area of the island to a beach Called Prygaki. We found through googling that there was cliff jumping here. After a short hike on a jetty like coast we found the spot! Pat was too nervous to jump at first so he had me be the guinea pig. There were basically 3 heights to jump from so I started on the smallest and worked up to the largest. It was a ton of fun! And after I priced it was safe pat and Sarah both jumped too.

After the cliff jumping we continued to drive up the west coast of the island and made a stop at an abandoned building we found that ended up being filled with graffiti art. It was really cool! there were some really talented ones and some that were clearly amateur haha!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_3062.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1235" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190905_164341.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1234" width="3840" height="2160">

Next we made a very quick stop at okros beach because we a ton of people kite surfing. We asked about renting/taking lessons but they said you need at least 6 days to learn so that was out.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2488.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1228" width="4032" height="3024">

Finally we Made it to Plaka beach at this point is was ~5:30. We ended up relaxing here for about and hour or so. I ended up getting a nice nap in.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2489.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1229" width="4032" height="3024">

After the beach we made our way back to the Airbnb to watch the sunset again on the roof. Tonight was a bit cloudy so the sunset wasn't as nice but we still enjoying hanging out in the roof. A bit later we went down to the square for some gyros that were delicious (they included fries of course) and super cheap I got 2 for like $6. After dinner pat and Sarah got some gelato and then we went back to the Airbnb and watched some Friends before bed.
