Today we woke up around 6:30ish and got all our stuff packed up. Today we were heading to the city of Xi'an, but first we were going to see some pandas!

We went downstairs to the lobby at 7, the time breakfast is supposed to start and when you're able to checkout. Of course they weren't ready for us 🙄. It took another 15 minutes before the guy showed up to check is out and there was no sign of breakfast being ready anytime soon so we left for the bus stop.

This time we were sure to get on the correct bus, however we had to transfer to bus 87 at the end of the route. Well of course bus 87 was no where to be found. I asked at least 5 people, all who pointed us in different directions 😂. Well it turned out the #87 bus driver slept in too late or something because he finally showed up about 20 min after he was scheduled to. The whole trip probably took just over an hour, but we made it!

I forgot to mention we had to carry our bags with us all day. Our hostel said we could leave them at the hostel, which usually means there's a secure bag room or something...nope we could leave them in the middle of the lobby for anyone to grab. I really didn't trust their competence considering they couldn't even make breakfast on time, so that's the long story as to why we had all our bags.

Anyway, the place is called the Chengdu panda breeding research center and is world famous for being able to very successfully breed pandas in captivity. Another thing I think they're famous for is having the most white people in all of Chengdu, maybe even all of china! Seriously though, I guess white people really like pandas 😂.

The place was pretty massive and had all different types of enclosures with different types of pandas (unfortunately the red panda exhibit was closed) and also the split the panda up by age group.

If you really wanted to you could pay ~$300 US to play with some of the pandas. It was a little out of our budget, but maybe next time! I forgot to mention we ran into the same ditch couple again! Third time in a row haha, it was pretty funny.

Overall the pandas were a cool thing to see. I feel like a trip to china wouldn't be complete without them 🐼.

After spending the morning with the pandas we took the bus back to the city to see the Wenshuyuan monastery. We were finally starting to get a hang of the bus system! On the way we stopped at a Chinese fast food restaurant called dicos. I had a pineapple chicken sandwhich that was pretty good. The monastery was really interesting, we hung out there for about an hour, I had a coffee and then we walked around the grounds to see the big temple.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1569.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-646" width="3024">I didn't know monks could have iPhones!<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1576.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-647" width="4032">

After the monastery we decided to check out one of the parks. There was a metro from the monastery that went straight there so we decided to take it. Before this according to google chengdu's metro line was very limited and not extensive at all. Well it turns out Google was wrong again! Jeeze! The metro (which to googles credit seemed very new) had a ton of different lines with stops all over the place. They even had a stop at the airport!

We made it to the park and walked around a bit. There was. Bunch of people dancing and a nice lake.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1578.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-648" width="3024"> Since we found out the metro basically went everywhere we decided to head further south to see the new global center, which is supposedly the biggest mall in the world! On the way we also saw tianafu square which has a big statue of Mao (the founder of the Chinese communist party that took over power in the 40s).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1577.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-649" width="3024">

We entered the mall from the basement t and at first glance didn't seem like much, but when we got to the main entrance we saw just how massive it was! The place has a ginormous indoor water park, ice stake rink, movie theater, and tons of shops and stores. We got a bunch of snack food and walked around for about an hour.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1581.jpg" height="3804" class="wp-image-651" width="13054"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1583.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-650" width="3024">

We ended up taking the metro to the airport which went really smoothly and our flight even left on time! (A rarity in china).

Once we arrived in xi'an we caught the bus downtown which took about an hour and then a taxi from there to our hostel. Our hostel was packed!! There were so many people, most of them seemed to be British.

Since we had some time we decided to drop out bags off and go explore the city. We saw the bell and drum towers which were all lit up since it was night time, they looked really cool.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1587.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-652" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1588.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-654" width="3024">

We also explored the nearby Muslim quarter which is we'll know for all its street food. Of course now that my stomach was feeling better I had to try some! 😂. I had some sort of grilled meat and potatoes along with a rice cake desert. They were all super delicious!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1589.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-655" width="4032">

Afterwards we made our way back to our hostel. Trying to find an ATM that would work for us (that took foreverrrr). But eventually we found one and made it back. Tomorrow we're doing a tour of the famous terra-cotta warriors!</p>
