Rachel and I were a little delayed getting to San Jose arriving around 11:30 pm. Pat and Sarah were already at the airport so we met with them and got the rental car. We had about a 3 hour drive to La Fortuna, where we were staying.

The road to La Fortuna was pretty nice it looked like it was recently paved, the only problem was that it twisted and turned through the mountains the whole way. To add to the misery it was also super foggy out due to us basically being in the clouds, needless to say it was very difficult to see. The other obstacle we encountered were rouge speed bumps, they were massive and literally would pop out of no where. They weren't painted or anything so seeing them in the low visibility was nearly impossible. We hit a few of them full speed in our little SUV. The SUV was a small Japanese car I never heard of that barely had enough power to make it up some of the steep roads.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_2542.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-961" width="1000" height="748">

Finally we arrived at our Airbnb around 2:30 am. We were exhausted and feel right to sleep. We woke up the next day around 9 and walked to a nearby breakfast place for the "typical" breakfast which consisted of egg, rice and beans, and chicken. After breakfast we drove around the town to check out the area. La Fortuna is fairly small but has lots of outdoor activities. The town is right next to a super large volcano called Arenal that looks really cool!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_2544.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-962" width="2576" height="1932">

At 12 we made our way a few miles up the road to the tour company to do a canyoning trip. I had no idea what canyoning was but my sister recommended it so we figured we'd give it a shot. We had a quick lunch, more chicken, and then began the tour. It ended up being a ton of fun! It was a lot of different types of rappelling down waterfalls. We ended up getting soaked!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_2546.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-963" width="4032" height="3024">

On our way to start the trip, it was a super bumpy ride to get there!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_2555.jpg" class="wp-image-964 size-full" width="3024" height="4032">

All geared up!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_2560.jpg" class="wp-image-965 size-full" width="3024" height="4032">

The first descent! Over 160 feet! They ended up swinging us underneath the water fall on the way down which got us absolutely drenched!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_2580.jpg" class="wp-image-966 size-full" width="3024" height="4032">

The tour was a really cool experience and we had a ton of fun, would definitely recommend! We used a company called pure trek.

Afterwards we made our way back to the Airbnb and relaxed in the jacuzzi for a little while. We went back into town for dinner at a really tasty restaurant called Pollo Fortuneño (recommended by our tour guides), Rachel and I spilt a big plate of ribs and chicken. After dinner we stopped at the super market to pick up a few things and then went back to the Airbnb to sleep, we were pretty exhausted from the lack of sleep from the previous night.
