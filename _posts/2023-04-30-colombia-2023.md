<p>Time for another trip! This time Becca and I are heading south to Colombia to explore the country, hike, and relax at the beach! Here is a google map of all the spots we plan to hit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p></p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here’s our rough itinerary:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Day 0 Thursday May 11<br />
Drive Sav to Jax<br />
Fly Jax to Miami to Bogota (Depart KJAX 8pm)<br />
Arrive 1:10 am<br />
Stay Bogota - Cranky Croc Hostel</p>
<p>Day 1 Friday May 12<br />
Bogota Activities (8,600’)<br />
Walking tour - La Candelaria Barrio: Museo del Oro, Museo Botero, Plaza Bolivar, Graffiti on Calle de Escudo<br />
Food Tour - ajiaco (soup), chocolate santafreño (hot choc w/cheese), chica (fermented corn drink), Plaza de Mercado la Concordia<br />
Hike Mt Monserrate- 3.2 miles, 2637 ft elevation gain<br />
Dinner @ Puerta Falsa (anthony bourdain, open since 1816)<br />
Stay Bogota - Cranky Croc Hostel</p>
<p>Day 2 Saturday May 13<br />
Fly Bogota to Medellin 6:30a<br />
Fruit Tour 10 am<br />
4:30pm Comuna 13 Tour<br />
To See: Plaza Botero, Parque Lleras, Palacio de Cultura Uribe Uribe, Parque de la Luces, Plaza Minorista market<br />
Stay Medellin - Los Patios Hostel</p>
<p>Day 3 Sunday May 14<br />
Day trip to Guatapé - El Peñón de Guatapé<br />
Stay Medellin - Los Patios Hostel</p>
<p>Day 4 Monday May 15<br />
Fly Medellin to Santa Marta 6a<br />
Sailing trip - Tayrona National<br />
Stay Santa Marta - Masaya Santa Marta</p>
<p>Day 5 Tuesday May 16<br />
Lost City Hike Day #1<br />
SANTA MARTA - EL MAMEY - CAMP 1 8:30am start<br />
4 hours day trek, 6.8 km/4.2 miles<br />
Stay Hiking Camp 1</p>
<p>Day 6 Wednesday May 17<br />
Lost City Hike Day #2<br />
CAMP 1 – CAMP 3<br />
Hike along riverside of Buritaca River, see Kogui indigenous village called Mutanshi. 7 hours (14.7 km/ 9.1 miles) going through the jungle of Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta<br />
Stay Camp 3</p>
<p>Day 7 Thursday May 18<br />
Lost City Hike Day #3<br />
LOST CITY- CAMP 2<br />
Climb 1.200 steps to Lost City. Guided tour of ruins. Hike down, lunch camp 3, hike to camp 2<br />
7 hours day trek (6 km/3.7 miles, uphill and downhill).<br />
Stay Hiking Camp 2</p>
<p>Day 8 Friday May 19<br />
Lost City Hike Day #4<br />
CAMP 2- EL MAMEY- SANTA MARTA<br />
Hike to El Mamey, lunch<br />
Drive to Buritaca (beach resort)	</p>
<p>Day 9 Saturday May 20<br />
Checkout at 12pm<br />
Travel to Cartagena<br />
Stay Cartagena - Holiday Inn Cartagena Morros</p>
<p>Day 10 Sunday May 21<br />
Kite surf lessons 12-5p<br />
Explore Cartagena- Old City, Plaza de Trinidad, Getsami (Parque del Centenario-sloths), Playa Blanca, Calle de la Sierpe (graffiti)<br />
4pm Getsami neighborhood walking tour (tips, ~2.5 hrs) meet at the big statue of Camellón de los Mártires Square (Red Umbrellas)<br />
Stay Cartagena - Holiday Inn Cartagena Morros</p>
<p>Day 11 Monday May 22<br />
10 am Old City Walking tour<br />
Fly Cartagena to Jax (Depart 3p)<br />
Arrive 11:56 pm<br />
Drive Jax to Sav</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Excited to explore another new country! Stay tuned for updates!</p>
