<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At 8am I met Ian the divemaster and Joshua, another diver. We checked in and then got our gear fitted. Lake Atitlan is a lake in a crater of the caldera of a volcano, its high elevation (5,100’) so there are specific things you need to be careful with while scuba diving at high altitude. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After getting our gear and getting briefed about the dive we loaded up on the boat. The boat was small but it was just the 3 of us. The first site we went to was called el árbol. It’s an underwater tree. Ian did some drills under water with Josh as a refresher and then we started the dive.</p>

<p>Overall the dive was interesting super unique the max depth was 78’ with an average of 45’ for 50 minutes total. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We took off our gear in the water and then climbed back up onto the boat. The ride to the site was only 10min tops. We did a surface interval of about 40 minutes before changing the tanks and then heading back out to the next dive site, Ramb II.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After the chimney we swam along the wall. We saw volcanic rocks, a weird fungus, some small fish, a crab, and some stuff that looked like destroyed building materials. The dive had a max depth of 87’ and an average of 40’ for 47 minutes. </p>

<p>Overall it was a unique dive. The lake itself was formed by the explosion of a massive volcano 85,000 years ago with a max depth of over 1,000’. Apparently there is also an underwater Mayan city - however you’re not allowed to dive there unless you get special permission from the government. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the dive we went back to the dive shop to drop off the gear and settle up. From there I took another lancha to the town of San Marcos to meet Pat and Jimmy. It was about 1:30 so we went to lunch in the town at a place called the hidden garden. It had super hippy vibes. We met a guy there named river who tried to sell us his “Nero coding” massages haha. Besides the interesting atmosphere the food was good. I had the teriyaki chicken bowl and a peanut butter smoothie. Both were good! We also had a very friendly and cute pup sit by us and kept us company. </p>
