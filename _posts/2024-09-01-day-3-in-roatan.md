<p>Day 3 - Thursday</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up early for our two morning dives with Reef Gliders. I ate some eggs and toast for breakfast before walking over to the dive shop with Becca. Jamie and Nate started their open water dive training at a place called West End divers. Our first dive was the Aguila wreck which was a bit further away from the dive shop. Gigi was our dive guide again for this dive. She gave us a quick brief of the site at the shop before we left.</p>

<p>The max depth of the dive was 107' and an average of 61'. At the start of the dive we saw a bunch of grass eels and some sucker fish that were all over another one of the divers in our group. The wreck itself was super cool! It was a massive cargo ship that was sunk in 1997 (a typical thing they do to promote reef growth). We got a bunch of cool pictures with the GoPro.</p>
