<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up relatively early and left our place in Cortina around 8am. We drove about 2.5 hours and made a stop at a granite pit off the main motorway to hike a via feratta called <a href="https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/ferrata-ferrante-di-ruffano/">Ferrante di Ruffano</a>. We were able to park right near the start of the feratta (only ~5min walk). There were some rock climbers climbing that were climbing near the start.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/gXIIHUR.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Feratta was really difficult, Sarah and Becca both quit after the first pitch (and it’s a good thing they did, it got significantly harder!). Pat and I continued on. It was super hot and the rock face was extremely exposed so I ended up climbing fast to get to the shady spot and wait for pat.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The feratta ending up taking around 1 and a half hours, Pat and I made it back down and met up with the girls in the dry river bed below to have some snacks and do some more droning before heading back on the road.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove another hour to try and go to another via feratta, but it turned out you had to pay 20 euros to get in so we decided against it (we were only planning to stay for a short time anyway). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We continued on in the car and made a stop along the motorway for food for Pat and Sarah at a place called Maria’s diner. It was an American themed diner from 50’s and they had just about every typical American dish you could think of.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After they ate we got back in the car and drove to Stoeska Vintgar, known as the Grand Canyon of Slovenia. We didn’t pre-book tickets (we didn’t know you needed to) but luckily we were able to get online and get some last minute tickets so we could do the hike. Also we were able to get a good parking spot right near the start of the trail. It was surprisingly crowded, but since it was the end of the day I think we missed the main rush of people.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The trail was really nice and had a path built on the side of the canyon wall that followed the river. The views were great and there was a nice cool breeze through the canyon.</p>

<p>We stopped for drinks near the top of the waterfall. I tried the local crazy duck beer and a pastry. After resting we went to the bottom of the falls for a quick photo.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/nAOMbSj.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After seeing the falls we walked back above the gorge (they don’t allow you to take the same path back. Also the path back is mostly uphill so they try and get you to pay for a shuttle ride back to the entrance.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Mnddb2I.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once we got back to the car, we drove another 10 minutes to the hotel we’re staying at (in lake bled) called Villa Ana. The villa was a super old house (I think our host said the 6th one built on the late) so it was like living in a historical house with all the old furniture and everything.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After checking in we stopped at the bakery across the street, I tried this chocolate coconut ball that was really good. We walked to the grocery store to pick up things for breakfast and some snacks before going back to the villa.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Becca and I walked down to the lake and hung out on one of the small boats at the dock until Pat and Sarah met up with us. The four of us went to dinner on the main road at a restaurant called Central Bled house.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The food was good! I had the ‘Central Plate’ which was a variety of meat and dumplings and also tried the local beer.</p>
