Best sleep of the trip so far! Woke up around 9am and went downstairs to get some breakfast. It was a full buffet style so we loaded up on everything. After breakfast we went back down to the Dead Sea to get another float and mud session in.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2902-2.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1377" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2908-2.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1378" width="4032" height="3024">

Afterwards we hung out at the pool for the day. It was really nice to have a day to rest and recharge! We ended up checking out of the hotel at 2.

Next we drove to mount Nebo which was about 30 min away and kind of on the way to Amman. Mount Nebo is where God showed Moses the promised land and also where Moses was buried (although the exact location is unknown). There was a really cool view of the surrounding area as well as a nice church with lots of restored mosaics.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3455.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1389" width="2576" height="1920">

Next we drove to Amman which took about an hour. The closer we got to the city the worse the traffic got and the crazier the drivers were. We dropped off the car at the rental place, it turned out we had it booked for an extra day we didn't realize but we returned it anyway because we really didn't want to drive around the city. To get to our hotel we tried to get an Uber but the driver cancelled 5 times so we ended up hailing down a random taxi. The ride to be hotel was about 30min because the traffic was so terrible. Also the taxi driver was a smoker and was smoking in the car which wasn't enjoyable. I think he realized we didn't like the smoke because he pulled out a perfume bottle and sprayed us all with it 😂 he didn't even ask he just did it which was really weird.

Once we got to the hotel we dropped off our stuff and then walked to rainbow street to get some falafels at a place called Al-Quds. The place was recommended to us by one of Jamie's friends. The restaurant was super small and they only offered one thing: falafel sandwich and either spicy or no spice.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2911-2.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1383" width="3088" height="2320">

The falafel was delicious so I ended up ordering 2 more since I was pretty starving. After the falafel we went to the ice cream store right next door for some delicious gelato. Side note: walking around Amman is not the nicest, lots of people seemed to be eying us so my guard was up also the city is very dirty, luckily we're only here one more day.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2912.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1375" width="3088" height="2320">

After eating the gelato we walked back to our hotel. We ended up hanging out in the room and watching the movie driven for the rest of the night.
