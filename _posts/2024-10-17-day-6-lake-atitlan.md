<p>Today I woke up around 6 and wrote my blog. I made some eggs and toast for breakfast at 7. Pat and jimmy woke up slightly later. The weather outside looked great so we decided to rent some ATVs and dirt bikes. I wanted to switch to a dirt bike since we already drove ATVs in Antigua. They required that you have a motorcycle license to rent them so Pat and jimmy had to stick with the ATVs.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On the walk to the rental place we stopped for an obligatory picture at the I love pana sign and also got followed by a pack of cute street dogs. </p>

<p>We left the rental shop a little before 9 and cruised about 30 minutes to a spot called ojo de agüilla. Unfortunately the gps took us down a wrong turn and we ended up going up a steep dirt road that was a big pain with the dirt bike -I didn’t want to have the dirt bike slip and hurt myself so I went slow which stalled the engine a lot :D. After finally struggling to the top we figured out that we were at the wrong spot!</p>

<p>To make matters even worse the spot we stopped was absolutely covered with fire ants which proceeded to destroy my ankles! We quickly left and cut our losses and decided to go to the next stop instead of trying to continue to find the ojo de agüilla. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next stop was Santa Catarina a small town just south of pana. The views on the ride to the town were great! We ended up parking for free just outside the town and walking in and exploring. The main square was cool, tons of uniquely painted buildings.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After walking through the reserve we got a tuktuk back to town and walked to the Airbnb. We rested for a bit and then decided to walk to a nearby supermercado that we saw to pick up some snacks - popcorn, gummies, chocolate. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got back to the Airbnb and relaxed a bit more before heading to get massages at 4. The massage place was great! Jimmy and I ended up sharing a room and Pat got his own. Although the masseuses were tiny they had super strong hands and the massage was probably one of the best I’ve had. They did some unique things like stretch you and scratch your head haha!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the massages we walked down to the lakeshore and found a hotel/restaurant to stop at for dinner. We started sitting outside but moved inside because of the rain and wind. I ended up getting the fettuccini pasta with crab and shrimp and it was really good!</p>
