We somehow managed to survive the night in the cave. The sandstorm seemed to last pretty much all night. The makeshift front wall/door of our cave was falling down 😂. Luckily nothing flew in and hit anyone. The whole room was COVERED with sand though. My headscarf thing really came in handy to keep my face protected while I attempted to sleep. I think I did end up getting a few hours of sleep in somehow.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2862.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1343" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2861.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1344" width="3024" height="4032">Everything was layered in sand!

We packed up our stuff into the car and made our way out of the cave fast! We went back to Petra for another day of hiking. This time the plan was to go all the way to the monastery, which is a bit more of a hike with lots of stairs (800 to be exact). To get to the start of the hike we walked back through the siq past the treasury building to the main city center of Petra. There were a ton of buildings here the biggest was the ruins of this old church.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2868.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1345" width="4032" height="3024">

From there we took the Al Dir trail to get to the monastery. I forgot to mention we got to Petra around 7/7:30 so there was a lot less people and it wasn't as hot so hiking was enjoyable.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2871.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1346" width="3024" height="4032">

After about an hour of hiking we made it to the monastery. The monastery is very similar to the treasury except it's larger and a little less intricate. It's also not nearly as busy since the hike is farther and more difficult.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3431.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1372" width="2576" height="1920">

As a reward for making the long hike up we treated ourselves to some ice cream.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2877.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1347" width="3088" height="2320">

We hiked around the area of the monastery to get some other view points. We found another spot that advertised "the best view in the world"...it's funny how many of them there are here 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3421.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1362" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3424-2.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1364" width="2576" height="1920">

Afterwards we made our way back down to the city of Petra. This time once we got to the city we took a different trail that goes south of the city that goes to the temple of high sacrifice. Along the way there were more buildings cut out of the rock.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3435.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1365" width="2576" height="1920">

There was a cool temple I think called the garden temple that was in a siq on the way to the high temple. Also on the way was an old "waterfall" that was dried up so it wasn't really much to see.

After another 30min or so of hiking we made it to the temple of high sacrifice. It was surprisingly crowded. The temple itself was basically a square cut into the ground really not much to see but the views from the top of the siq looking down on the city were nice!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3437.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1366" width="2576" height="1920">

After the high temple we took another route down to the main route to leave Petra. We passed by the treasury building one last time on our way out, it really is cool and I definitely recommend going to see it if you get the chance.

We left Petra around 12:30. I realized I left some clothes in the cave so we made a quick stop back to get them and then continued on our way to the Dead Sea.

The drive to our hotel was about 2.5hours and had some cool views going over the mountains and down into the valley where the sea is.

One interesting thing we learned is that the Dead Sea is actually technically a lake (since it's no longer connected to the ocean) and also it's the lowest land point on earth at -431 meters below sea level.

The hotel we stayed at was the Hilton Dead Sea luxury resort and it was bad ass! Jamie has a bunch of points through her work travel so she was able to book it for us and since she's a gold status we were able to get an awesome room, free drinks at the executive lounge, and free breakfast!

After we checked in we made our way down to the Dead Sea. The Dead Sea is "dead" because it has a super high salt content and nothing can survive it. The other thing the high salt content does is make it super easy to float. Once we got in we floated around for about 15min. It was insane how easy it was to float. You could literally "stand up" without touching the ground, it was impossible to sink!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3451.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1367" width="2576" height="1920">

After floating you're supposed to rub this mud on you and let it dry for 15min, I guess it's good for your skin or something? We looked like idiots doing it but it was kinda fun, and I guess my skin felt softer afterwards 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191014_165719.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1369" width="2592" height="1458">

After floating in the sea we went up to the pool and got some drinks at the swim up bar. I tried a drink called the Dead Sea breeze, it was good, not great. Then after the pool we went to the executive lounge for some free drinks and food. We stayed in the lounge for about 2 hours and then we checked out the rooftop bar but it was pretty dead so we went back to the room shortly after to get some sleep.
