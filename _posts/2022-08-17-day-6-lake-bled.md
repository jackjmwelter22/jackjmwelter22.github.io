<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we slept in for a rest day (Pat’s favorite type of day) and woke up around 8am to cook and eat breakfast in the hotel  I had cereal, yoghurt, and bread with jam. After eating I went downstairs to check on pat and Sarah who were surprisingly also awake. Pat and I took the drone for a quick spin around the hotel (called villa Anna and one of the oldest on the lake). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After getting ready we slowly made our way down to the lake to walk clockwise around the lake ~6km. We walked a little ways and came across a boat rental shop. We rented an oar boat for a bit over an hour to paddle out to the island in the middle of the lake (Slovenia’s only official island).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/dbMckNX.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The ride took about 20 min (i rowed). Pat took some cool drone footage of us on the lake. We docked at the small island and made our way up to the church (the only thing on the island). There’s an old myth that if you can carry your bride up the 99 stairs to the church then you will have a blessed marriage and fertility. Of course pat and I decided to not only carry Becca and Sarah on our backs but also race up the steps. It was a run race, we both made it to the top, I barely beat pat. People were cheering us, it was a fun time! </p>

<p>After running up the steps we took a quick and we’ll deserved dip in the lake. The water is super blue and refreshing!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got back on the boat and circumnavigated the island before making our way back to the dock.  I stopped and jumped in the water on the way back for another quick dip (rowing is exhausting and difficult!). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After getting off the boat we continued around the lake. We saw some people jumping and rope swinging off a tree into the lake so of course I had to try. </p>

<p>After dipping in we hiked up to mala osjenica (a small mountain with a nice elevated view of the lake). The hike itself took about 30 min to get to the top and an uncountable amount of complaints from Pat about hiking during our rest day :). In my opinion the view from the top was well worth it!</p>

<p>After lunch we continued walking down the lake and decided to hike up to the castle that’s on the lake  the hike was quick and had some good views at the top (Pat was upset with all the hiking on our rest day). The castle cost 13 euro to enter and Becca and I weren’t that interested so we ended up walking back down to the lake to swim and relax while we waited for pat and Sarah to tour the castle. </p>
