Today we woke up at 11 pm (I probably got like 2 hours of sleep) and then got all our stuff ready to hike by 11:30 and ate “breakfast” which was the typical porridge. We left for the hike a little late, around 12:15. It was dark but the sky was clear and the moon was almost full so it lit everything up. It looked awesome, I’m so thankful that we had great weather today!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1247.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-893" height="2320" width="3088">

It was really cool to see all the groups of people making their way up the summit lit up by only their headlamps. I tried to get some shots of the hike but it was really difficult because of how dark it was.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1253.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-883" height="4032" width="3024">

The hike was super steep and seemed to last forever. We moved slowly one step at a time. I was focused on following Moses the whole time. The higher we got the more difficult it was to breath. Up to this point I hadn’t had any issues with the altitude, but now my head was pounding if I moved too fast or didn’t breath deep enough. It was crazy. We reached Stella point at around 5:30am just as the sun was starting to come above the horizon. From there we hiked to uhuru peak and got there right at 6:20am. Finally reaching here the peak was simultaneously the best and worst feeling ever, the best because we finally made it and the worst because we had a longggg way back down.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1259.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-884" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1260.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-885" height="2320" width="3088"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1262.jpg" class="wp-image-886 size-full" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1268.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-887" height="3024" width="3024">

The hike back down took about 3.5 hours and it was the most miserable I’ve ever felt, I just wanted to stop hiking. After what seemed like forever we finally made it back to base camp around 10. We had 1 hour to rest and then lunch. After waking up from the nap I had the worst headache I’ve ever felt in my life, I can’t even describe how bad it felt. For lunch we had soup but I only had like two bites because I felt like I was gunna puke, my body hated me.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1285.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-888" height="3024" width="4032">

The best part is That after lunch we had another 4 hour hike down to Mweka camp. It was pretty miserable but I started feeling a lot better the more we descended. As we got lower we got into the clouds which created a mist which was kinda nice but got everything wet and also made the trail muddy and slippery.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1293.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-890" height="2320" width="3088"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1295.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-889" height="2320" width="3088"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1302.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-891" height="3024" width="4032">

We finally made it to our final camp just as the sun started to come out. We checked in and set up our tent, everything was superrr muddy. We had dinner around 6:30 which consisted of soup, rice and veggies, pineapple. After dinner we passed out in our tents. It was the best sleep I had all trip.
