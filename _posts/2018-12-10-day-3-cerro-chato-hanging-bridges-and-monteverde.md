Today was a busy day! I woke up around 7 and called the green lagoon resort (an entrance to cerro chato) to see if it would be possible to hike today. The guy on the phone told me he didn't speak English and very clearly said it was illegal to hike. I asked him if we could hike the observatory trail and he said sure. So Pat and I decided to drive over there and give it a shot. This time there was no guards in the parking lot so we decided to just go for it. However after a min someone caught up with us and told us we had to pay...whoops. We ended up seeing the same guy at the reception as the day before however this time he told us the rangers were gone and it should be okay to hike cerro chato! I was pumped! We made our way up the observatory hike for about 30 min, there were great views of La Fortuna.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_20181209_110659.jpg" class="wp-image-998 size-full" width="3264" height="2448">

Once you get to the end of the hike you have to take a small side trail which leads you to the edge of the green lagoon resorts property. Eventually we arrived at a barbed wire fence with signs that clearly say don't keep going.<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1650.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-982" width="3024" height="4032">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1654.jpg" class="wp-image-983 size-full" width="3024" height="4032">

We obviously kept going. The trail was very obvious but also very rutted out. As we continued hiking up it started raining which caused the trail to get really muddy and slippery. After about an hour we were almost at the top when we heard people up ahead. Fearing that they were rangers and we're going to send us back pat and I ran off the trail to hide. As they approached we could tell they clearly weren't rangers...they were Americans who were also hiking the volcano 😂. After a quick laugh we finished our ascent to the rim and then hiked down into the crater where the lagoon was. The hike down was super sketchy and slippery. If you weren't careful you could easily get hurt.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1665.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-984" width="4032" height="3024">

The view of the lake was amazing. We ended up going for a quick swim just to say we did and then hung out and enjoyed the view. Luckily the weather cleared up so the visibility was decent.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_20181209_094332.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-994" width="3264" height="2448">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_20181209_094824.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-995" width="4032" height="3024">

After about 20 min we made our way back up to the rim and then started our descent back to the parking lot. On the way down we ran into about 20 or so more climbers. Before driving back we rinsed off (we were super muddy from the hike).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_20181209_100243.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-996" width="3024" height="4032">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_20181209_100807.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-997" width="3264" height="2448">

We met up with the girls at the Airbnb and got all of our things packed up in the car. We drove about 20 min to a place called mistico park, know for its hanging bridges. The park was very well kept and the bridges were also pretty fun.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1678.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-985" width="4032" height="3024">

The whole trail was about 2 miles and took us about an hour to complete. After the mini hike we had lunch, the views of Arenal volcano were incredible.

After lunch we continued our drive to monteverde. The drive took a little under 3 hours. We had to drive all the way around Lake Arenal and through the mountains with plenty of windy and pothole ridden roads. It was quite the experience! Monteverde is a small town in the middle of the mountains with lots of poorly maintained and very steep roads. I had a couple of close calls maneuvering our car which was a stick shift. Our Airbnb was really nice and about a half mile away from the main strip.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1693.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-986" width="3024" height="4032">

After settling in we decided to walk down to town to eat. We ended up eating at a really cool restaurant called the tree house, it literally has a live tree growing in the middle of it! The food was also delicious and there was a live band playing a mix of Spanish and popular American songs.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1696.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-987" width="4032" height="3024">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1700.jpg" class="wp-image-988 size-full" width="4032" height="3024">

After dinner we took a taxi back to the Airbnb and went to bed.
