<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 7:30 and had breakfast in the apartment. The first stop was to the other side of the lake which took about 45 minutes to drive to. We were planning to try and do some cliff jumping, but by the time we got there it was pouring rain and we still had to hike from the parking to the jump spot (which we only had a general idea of where it was). So with all that we decided to pass on the cliff jumping and continue on. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our next stop was another 45 minutes away to Berchtesgaden Germany. Or more commonly know as the eagles nest, basically Hitler’s evil secret hideout that’s built into and on top of a mountain. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We had to park at the bottom of the mountain and then take a bus up. It’s possible to hike but it takes like 3 hours one way and we were short in time so we paid the outrageous ($25 bus price each). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The bus dropped us off at the top and then we walked through a tunnel into the mountain and then got on an elevator that took us straight up into the kehlsteinhaus (the eagles nest). </p>

<p>After eating we took the elevator back down and then took the bus back to our car. We got back in and drove about an hour to our next stop, Salzburg (back in Austria). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Salzburg was a cool town, built right on a river and famous for being the birthplace and home of Mozart. When we first drove in there was a ton of traffic and we couldn’t find parking anywhere. We thought we had found a spot, but upon further inspection and translation of the sign it turned out it was for residents only. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Since the rain seemed pretty light we decided to head over to the <a href="https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/city-wall-klettersteig/">city wall klettersteig</a>. It’s a klettersteig that ascends the old wall of the city! Luckily we were able to find some street parking close by. The girls were tired of klettersteigs and weren’t a fan of the rain so just Pat and I went. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The route was short but really fun! The wall was pretty slick from all the rain but we made it to the top without falling ha! The top had some good views of the city. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

We also saw Mozarts birthplace and residence, walked the main shopping street, crossed the river bridge, saw a small market, and saw the Mirabell palace. </p>

<p>After exploring the city we made our way back to the car for another two hour ride back to Munich. Pat and Sarah weren’t able to get a room at the same place as us (pat waited too long to book). So we dropped them off and then went to our hotel to check in. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Apparently there’s some sort of huge music festival and also the Europe games going on in Munich right now so the city was packed. We just ended up going to an Indian restaurant by our hotel that was sooo delicious, seriously the best chicken tikka masala I’ve had (and I’ve had a lot). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After dinner we ended up taking an electric scooter back (we were only a few blacks from the hotel. Becca had never been on one before and I’m not sure she enjoyed it haha. At least we didn’t crash like my sister and I when we tried to share a scooter. Back at the hotel we got packed and went to bed. </p>
