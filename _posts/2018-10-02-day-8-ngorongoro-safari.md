Today we woke up around 6 to get ready. I slept really well tonight (we actually had beds!). I went to the little view point area to catch the sunrise before breakfast, it was pretty cool. For breakfast we had the typical food eggs, sausage, pancakes, toast. I'm pretty sure they only eat one type of meal here it's starting to get a bit monotonous haha.

We left camp around 7am to head to the Ngorongoro Park. The drive took a little less than 3 hours to get to the park.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_1393.jpg" class="wp-image-919 size-full" height="2320" width="3088">

The park is essentially an old volcano that blew up a million years ago and now is a crater that is filled with a ton of wildlife. We drove up the rim and then down into the crater where the animals were. The views at this park were stunning and totally different from tarangire.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_1445.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-920" height="3024" width="4032">

We saw a bunch of new animals in the park, most notably lions, hippos, and hienas. At one point we got within a couple feet of two lions who were crossing by our truck!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_1423.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-921" height="2320" width="3088"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_1433.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-922" height="2320" width="3088"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_1440.jpg" class="wp-image-924 size-full" height="3024" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/img_1441.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-923" height="3024" width="4032">

We stopped and ate lunch in the crater in our car. It was basically the same lunch as the day before (like I said the meals are kinda monotonous). We drive around more after lunch and then made our way back to panaorama camp.

At camp we ended up playing board games with the two French people that were in our group until it was time for dinner. Dinner was fried potatoes and fish. After dinner we went to bed. Tomorrow we start a little later and go to a nearby lake for our last day of Safari-ing before heading to Zanzibar!
