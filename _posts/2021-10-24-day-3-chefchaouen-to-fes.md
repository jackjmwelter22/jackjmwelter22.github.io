<p>After exploring we hiked up to the Spanish mosque again then made our way back to the city to pack up and meet our tour guide to head to the next city, Fes. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We took a bus about an hour to a random restaurant for lunch. The food was good, I had grilled chicken and fries. They also had beer here which is uncommon because of the strong Islamic influence. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we drove another hour to the volubilis ruins. These are ruins from the Roman Empire and supposedly are the largest Roman ruins outside of Europe. We spent some time walking through the ruins and taking pictures. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It was super hot and sunny out so we were glad when we got back to the air conditioned van. We drove another hour to a city called Mekenes. Here we stopped to see the Bab Mansour, which is essentially a really big gate to get into the palace</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2261,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0389.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2261" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We also drove inside the outer walls of the gate and stopped at the Kara prison. The prison is a vast underground labyrinth that would hold 40,000 prisoners. They were fed by dropping food down the holes. You could look into the holes and see it was a long way down, at least 30-40’. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2260,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0390.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2260" /><figcaption>Little holes looking down into the prison</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Supposedly if you could find your way out you gained your freedom, however it is thought that no one ever was able to escape. Apparently a group of French researchers tried to map out the prison in the 1990s and never made it back out. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We drove past the rest of the palace which was massive, according to our guide the King has at least one palace in every city and sometimes more than one. Also every palace prepares food for the king each day just in case he shows up. After getting past the palace we drove through the rest of the city and then continued on to the city of Fes. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We arrived at our Riad around 8pm and ended up getting dinner of the roof terrace. I had a meat and vegetable couscous that was really good. </p>
