<p>Today we woke up around 7 and then made our way downtown by car (it’s only about a 5min drive). We had breakfast at a place called cafe honey. I had a sandwhich and a hot chocolate (it looked more like a milkshake. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_4365.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1722" width="3024" height="4032"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After we ate we drove down the main road to see all the waterfalls (it’s called the Ruta de las cascadas because there are so many). At the first two we stopped at Agoyan waterfall and monato de novio we to a cable car over the river to get a closer view of the falls (it was only $2 to ride it). Everything in Ecuador is super inexpensive, I highly recommend if you’re looking for a budget trip. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_4367.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1723" width="2320" height="3088"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_4380.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1724" width="3024" height="4032">The final falls we stopped to see (also the largest) was called Pailin del Diablo (the devils cauldron). We parked where the GPS told us to go for the entrance but ended up walking around the town for a solid 20 minutes before we figured out where the entrance was (we totally would’ve failed if this was an amazing race challenge) to add to our misery it started raining. The falls were really cool! And as we later found out there are two different entrances to see them. The first one was higher and had a bunch of suspension brides. The other entrance (the original and main one) took you lower and right underneath the falls. I think the second one (main entrance) was the best but each only cost $2 to enter so might as well do both if you have the time. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_0878.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1731" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_0887.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1732" width="3088" height="2320"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the falls we made our way back toward Banos. On the main road we stopped to do Ecuador’s longest and fastest zip line. It was something like 1.8 kilometers over the rio verde and the cool thing was you got to go down on your stomach! I’m not the biggest fan of zip lines but Becca had never done it and it was only $10 so totally worth it. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_4406.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1725" width="3520" height="1980"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the zip line we made a quick pit stop in Banos to get more cash from the ATM and then drove up the nearby mountain to casa del Arbol (tree house). This place is famous for its “swing at the end of the world”. It lives up to the hype. We had a ton of fun swinging and taking fun pictures and it only cost $1 per person to enter! Another nice thing was it was, like many of our other activities, mostly empty, thanks COVID! There were so many great views of the volcano and surrounding mountains. After swinging we decided to eat at the restaurant there. The most expensive thing on the menu was $1.50 so we ordered one of everything to try, the whole meal was only $9! Definitely recommend checking out this place if you’re in Ecuador. </p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After casa del arbol we drove a little ways back down towards Banos and stopped at a really nice hotel/spa called Luna volcano. The day pass for the pool was $20 but totally with it (this place was super fancy). We spent most of our time in one of the hot tubs overlooking Banos and had a few drinks from the bar. I had a beer and a “chocolate Oreo explosion” it was delicious. We hung out there and enjoyed the sunset. Oh yea and they made you wear these swim caps, so you don't get hair in the pool...I guess they don't care about Oreos. </p>
<p><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_7003.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1728" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/img_6970.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1729" width="3088" height="2320"></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up leaving a little after 7 and made our way back down to town and walking to a new restaurant on the second floor with a balcony overlooking the park. I got some super tasty dish with eggs, avocado, chorizo, and a pancake thing. Becca had the trout and shrimp dish. After dinner we drove back to our hotel and got somewhat packed in preparation for our early drive tomorrow to quilotoa. </p>
