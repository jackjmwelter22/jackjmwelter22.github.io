Today was another early day, we had a flight to catch at 5 am so we woke up around 2:45 to take the 3am shuttle to the airport. The flight down took about an hour and relatively smooth. Getting out of the Aswan airport was a breeze and our guide, Big Mac met us outside without bus to start our tour of Aswan.

The first stop was the Aswan high dam. It's a huge damn that was finished in like the 1970's to help control the flooding of the Nile and generate power for Egypt. The dam was only 15 min from the airport and it was heavily protected by guards since it's very important for Egypt. The dam created a large lake behind it called lake nasser, after the president who was in office when it was built. Our guide warned us not to swim in the lake because it was filled with lots of Nile crocodiles.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3512.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1561" width="2576" height="1920">

Up next we went to the Philae temple aka the temple of love. Along the way we picked up two Italians who were joining us on the tour. To get to the temple we had to take a small motor boat to the island the temple was on.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3516.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1562" width="2576" height="1920">

Interestingly the temple had to be relocated because of the construction of the Aswan dam (along with 17 other temples) so that it wouldn't flood. Archeologists had to disassemble the entire temple piece by piece and reassemble it ~500m away on higher ground. The temple itself was cool. It was built for the God Isis and had lots of hieroglyphs covering it. There were also lots of Christian symbols in the temple because later in history the Christians came and took over the temple.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3518.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1563" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3519.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1564" width="2576" height="1920">

After finishing Philae temple we went to see the unfinished obelisk. If you don't know an obelisk is what the Washington monument is, basically a big square pillar with a pyramid top. Anyway the unfinished obelisk was going to be the largest ever built, however during construction (basically carving it out of a massive rock) it cracked and was abandoned. We also learned about how they carved out the rock and moved the massive piece. It's amazing that they were able to do all this thousands of years ago. There are quite a few mysteries that we still haven't figured out today about the Egyptians, some people even believe that aliens helped 😂!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3521.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1565" width="2576" height="1920">

After the obelisk we went to the herbs and spices shop. The shop was filled with just about every spice and herb known to man. It was cool to learn about all the different ones and the effects they supposedly have. I ended up getting some hibiscus tea, it's really tasty, if anyone wants to try some let me know!

The next stop was the essence/perfume and essential oils shop. Again they showed us a ton of different oils and what all the benefits of each were. One thing I learned was the difference between perfume and essence is that perfume is just essence mixed with alcohol (only about %15 essence). Jamie ended up getting one she liked made from the lotus flower, it's also known as the sweat of the Gods haha.

Finally we got to our tour boat to check in. Also side note: basically everywhere in Egypt has metal detectors, but none of them are actually used! You walk through them at every single tourist site and it goes off every single time and no one seems to care or check.

The cruise boat was surprisingly nice! We were on the first floor in a 3 bed room. The breakfast/lunch/dinner room was in the sub level and there were 2 levels above us and a deck that had a pool! After settling in we ate lunch downstairs, it was a buffet style all you can eat and was pretty decent.

After lunch we walked around the boat a little. One weird thing about the pool is that there are windows in it so people in the bar/lounge on the floor below can see you swimming. After walking around a little we went back to the room to take a nap.

While we were napping the boat started sailing to Kom Ombo, our next stop. We arrived around 5:30 and met our new guide in the lobby to start our tour of Kom Ombo temple and the crocodile museum.

Kom ombo means mound of gold in Egyptian and as you'd guess, the city had a gold mine that provided gold for all Egypt. The temple took 400 years to build which was the longest time for any temple. The temple itself was built for two gods: the evil crocodile god Sobek and Horus (the falcon God). The temple also had a well that was used to determine the water level of the river Nile. Another interesting feature of the temple was the tunnels they found underneath it that went all the way to the other side of the Nile.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3522.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1575" width="2576" height="1920">

Next we stopped at the crocodile museum that had a bunch of the mummified crocodiles that they found buried in the area. The mummification of the crocodiles were a tribute to the God sobek. Archeologists found over 1000 crocodiles buried in the area.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3524.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1576" width="2576" height="1920">

After the museum we went back to boat and watched the movie Jumper before going to dinner. Dinner was a similar buffet style. After dinner we went to the lounge on the second floor and played cards. Fittingly we played the game Egyptian rat screw, Sam was actually really good at the game so him and I were good competition, Jamie on the other hand, didn't make it far. After we went to bed early watching Indiana jones as the boat sailed to the next stop Edfu.
