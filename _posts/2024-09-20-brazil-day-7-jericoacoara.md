<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast, we got ready and were picked up from our hotel to do the East Side tour of Jeri. Basically there are two different tours you can do: East Side and West Side. Our driver recommended we do the East Side tour since it’s not as active and we had Bennett to bring along with us.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For the tour we were driven around in a small dune buggy. Normally you sit on the back, but because we had Bennett, we had one person sit up front with the driver. The first stop was the sloth tree, which was about 20 minutes away from our hotel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The sloth tree (aka Árvore da Preguiça) is basically a big tree that grew sideways due to the strong wind - kinda cool I guess!</p>

<p>After the sloth tree we drove through the nearby town Preá and stopped at Lagun Beach, which was a lagoon beach club you had to pay about four dollars a person to get into. The lagoon itself is really cool The water was super blue and there are lots of spots to take cool pictures. I took my drone out and got a few shots. We also jumped in and swam for a bit. The tours normally start at 9 AM so we were the first people there. </p>

The water here was more blue and they had a platform you could jump off of into the lagoon. I flew my drone for some more shots. </p>

<p>After I flew the drone, a helicopter came and landed right next to the lagoon - good thing I wasn’t flying! After hanging out for about 45 minutes, we got back in the buggy and made our way to the next stop, yet another lagoon called Lagoa Azul. This lagoon was more natural less built up than the other two. There was a beach club area that you needed to take a raft to on the other side, but Becca and I elected not to go there because it didn’t look like anything special and you had to pay 40 rials per person.</p>

<p>Instead, we continued to the next spot which I think was just across the water at another lagoon called Lagoa do Paraíso. Here there was yet another beach club that cost about seven dollars per person. We ended up staying here for about two hours and eating lunch. There were quite a few people at this club. It was called the Alchemist beach club. We had lunch basically in the water on the beach of the lagoon. I wasn’t super hungry so I just had fries. </p>

<p>We left around 1:30 pm and made our way to the final stop- Lagoa do Amancio. This Lagoon was a natural lagoon in the middle of the dunes caused by rainwater. It was super cool to see. I ran around the lagoon twice and up-and-down the dunes, jumping into the lagoon. we hung out for maybe 20 minutes. I flew my drone, got some really cool pictures,  and then we made our way back to our hotel in our dune buggy.</p>
