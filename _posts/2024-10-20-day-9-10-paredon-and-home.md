<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we slept in, we ate some breakfast at the room (cereal and milk) and then hung out in the net watching some tv on the iPad. Pat went ahead and scheduled us surf lessons at 11. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up going to the pool around 10 and hanging out until our lessons started. The lessons were much of “lessons”, we each had an instructor (who didn’t speak much English). My instructor didn’t even get in the water while Pat and jimmys at least helped them. We surfed for 1 hour, it was totally exhausting, the waves were constant and even though my instructor wasn’t good I was able to consistently catch the waves. The lessons were only $20 so I wasn’t too worried about the instructor. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After surfing we went back to the pool to relax. We chilled for a bit and then went to the pizza place across the street for lunch. I had a meat pizza. Pat showed us a fun card game to play while we waited. There was also a cute pupper there hanging out with us.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The pizza was good! After we finished we finished playing the game and then went back and hung out at the hostel. Pat really wanted to play some volleyball so we recruited one of the beer pong players and played on the court right in front of the hostel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It was a pretty fun game Pat and Timo vs Jimmy and I. We ended up winning. After the game we went back to the pool. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After relaxing we hung out in the room and then went out for dinner. We tried to go to a papusa place but it looked pretty bad. We stopped at the super24 for some more snacks and then went to another restaurant called Saya. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This was a fancier looking restaurant with fancier dishes. I got the ribs. They were pretty meh. It was also super hot, there was absolutely no breeze so we were all sweating!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ate quickly and then went back to the room to hang out and watch some more of the TV show. Not Jimmy and I ended up passing out during the show so we went to bed for an early night.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 10 </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up and got packed and checkout of the hostel. We got on the road at 7:30 to drive back to the airport. The drive took about 3.5 hours with pretty bad traffic once we got to the city. We ended up stopping at a gas station about 5 miles from the airport to fill up. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We dropped the car back off - I had seen a lot of bad reviews about this Avis car rental but everything went smoothly. We got through the airport security and to the gate. I stopped at subway to use the rest of my Quetzales on a sandwich for lunch. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Jimmy ended up on a different flight connecting through Dallas while Pat and I were connecting through Miami. Our flight to Miami was slightly delayed but we made up a lot of the time in the air. Once in Miami I went in to Savannah and Pat went on to Chicago. Overall smooth trip back. I ended up getting back to Savannah at 9:20 and got back home just before 10. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once again an awesome trip with my good friend and cousin! Glad we were able to sneak it in before the end of the year! I’ll see them both again next weekend when Becca, Bennett, and I all head up to Chicago to visit the family! As always looking forward to the next Adventure!</p>
