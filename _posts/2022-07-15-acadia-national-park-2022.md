<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here’s the quick overview of our long weekend trip to Acadia national park with Clint and Kate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><span style="text-decoration:underline;">Day 0 (Thursday)</span></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We left after work and got to the Savannah airport around 4:30. Everything was going smoothly, our flight arrived to Savannah on time and we even finished boarding early! We were all ready to leave…That’s when thunderstorms decided to pop up, localized literally right over the airport. At first I wasn’t worried because Savannah in the summer is notorious for quick afternoon storms and I figured it would blow over in 20-30 minutes tops (which happened the day before). Unfortunately today the thunderstorm lingered over Savannah and we didn’t end up leaving for 2 hours! Also after the weather had finally subsided our first attempt to take off was aborted! The pilots told us they had received some warning messages on the takeoff roll so they ‘debugged’ the issue on the taxiway. Takeoff attempt number 2 was successful! Unfortunately all of these delays compounded and we completely missed our connecting flight from Philadelphia to Bangor. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When we arrived in Philly (around 10:30pm) we had to assertively talk with customer service manager to get us a hotel voucher. After some back and forth (Becca was told on the phone by American customer service that they’d get us a hotel in Philly while the manager at the service desk in Philly told us they will not do that for a weather delay) she eventually relented and was able to get us a hotel that was about 10 minutes from the airport via shuttle. The hotel itself was pretty dumpy (0/10 would not recommend) but we were happy to have a bed to sleep in! We ended up finally getting to sleep around midnight</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p><strong><span style="text-decoration:underline;">Day 1ish</span></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we had some time to kill in Philadelphia before our flight to Bangor (at 1:30 pm) so Becca and I took a taxi to the city to see the sites in Philadelphia. I had previously visited for a wedding around 4 years ago but Becca had never been. Our first stop was breakfast near Washington square. I had bagels (which for some reason are really good in PA) at a small store called Knead’s bagels. I had a bacon, egg, and cheese everything bagel and a cinnamon cream cheese flaxseed bagel, both were delicious! Becca of course wasn’t able to eat bagels so she went to a restaurant across the street called pain quotidian to have an omelet.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast we walked over to the Independence hall to get a tour of the East wing Assembly room which is where they signed the constitution. The guide was actually really good and told the story leading up to the American independence, very inspirational! </p>

<p><strong><span style="text-decoration:underline;">Day 4</span></strong></p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On our way to the airport we found out that our connecting flight from DC to Savannah was cancelled :(. We ended up talking to the gate agent in Bangor and he was able to get us a flight to Charlotte from DC and then a flight to Savannah. We boarded our plane confident we would make it back. Unfortunately the airlines had different plans. It turns out the flight from DC to Charlotte was going to be late because of airplane availability so there was no way we would make the connecting flight to Savannah and we would end up stuck in charlotte….not cool. We ended up talking to another gate agent in DC and he was able to get us on a flight from DC to Charleston and then American Airlines gave us a voucher to take a taxi from Charleston all the way to Savannah! (In case you don’t know its ~2.5 hour drive. I’d personally never heard of an airline doing that before, but I was just happy to be getting back to Savannah that night. In the end we made  it back home around 9pm (only a few hours later than our original plan).</p>

<p>Unfortunately Clint and Kate were not so lucky (they flew Delta) and ended up getting stuck in Maine with the only available flight to Savannah from Portland (~2 hours from Bangor) the NEXT day.
