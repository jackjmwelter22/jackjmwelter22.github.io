<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up early and met Scott at the new course (right by old course) at 7:45. This was our final round in Scotland and for the trip! Weather was great again, just a little drizzle for a hole or two but really not much. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We didn’t have many people in front of us so we played fast and finished in 3 hours. The new course is similar to old course but a little tougher in my opinion (tighter fairways). After golf we picked up Jamie from the Airbnb went back to hotel to change out of our golf gear.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Jamie and I wanted to hike so we went to a town an hour and a half north called pitlochry. The town is just on the outskirts of the highlands, which has a lot more elevation (which is why we went there). In the town we stopped at small sandwich. They had a really good ham sandwich, however the horrible chocolate milkshake I ordered was awful, it kinda tasted like they just microwaved some chocolate ice cream and put it in a glass. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2188,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_2388.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-2188" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After we finished eating w went to a place just north of town (2min away) to do a hike called Ben verackie. It’s a 5.7 mile hike with 2,400’ elevation gain (so pretty much uphill the whole way). There were really nice views of the town to start then, probably halfway up, we go into the clouds so the visibility was nothing. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The whole hike took just under 3 hours. Once back at the car we drove back to the hotel (about an hour and a half). Back at the hotel I stayed in and rested the rest of the day, the hike kinda wore me out. Dad and Jamie ended up walking around the town since she hadn’t seen it yet and then went to dinner.</p>
