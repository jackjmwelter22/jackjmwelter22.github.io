<p style="text-align:justify;">It's day 5 and we still aren't quite adjusted to the time difference. Today I woke up around 5 (better than 12am I guess!) we took the morning slow eating breakfast and than laying around watching the movie channel. Around 9:30 we went and rented motorbikes again. The first stop today was the Cat Ba national park. The park is huge and has a trail that goes to Ngu Lam Peak. The hike was very fast and steep and only took about 20 minutes to reach the top. The views were awesome as expected.</p>

The pier itself wasn't anything special compared to the other.</div>

Before dinner I decided to take the motorbike out one last time for fun and stumbled upon a cool shirt hike to the top of this mountain with a viewing deck and statute thing. There was an awesome view of the main strip where our hotel was.</div>

<div class="separator" style="clear:both;text-align:justify;">After hiking back down I drove around the town a little more and decided to go up this hill, however when I tried to make it up the hill the bike stalled and shut off. I started the bike back up and tried again, the bike stalled out again. However this time the bike wouldn't start back up! It had plenty of gas so that wasn't the problem. I ended up having to coast all the way down the hill back to the rental place. They didn't seem to care which was good and they didn't have my name or anything to rent the bike so I figured I was good. After dropping the bike off I went back to the hotel.</div>
<div class="separator" style="clear:both;text-align:justify;"></div>
<div class="separator" style="clear:both;text-align:justify;">We decided to eat dinner at the restaurant right outside of our hotel. I ordered a hot pot. I wasn't quite sure what is was when I order it but I was cold and hungry and the name was appealing. The hot pot certainly did not disappoint!! It was essentially a large pot heated on the table where the waitress cooked the meat and other ingredients for me and served them to me in a bowl. It was a ton of food and it was awesome!</div>
