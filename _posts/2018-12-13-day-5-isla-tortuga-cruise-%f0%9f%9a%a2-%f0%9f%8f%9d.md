Today we woke up early, around 6 AM and hit the road at 6:15 for a 2 hour car ride to punta arenas, the port we were leaving from to go on our cruise to isla tortuga. The drive took about two hours and only about half of it was on rough roads. We arrived at 8 AM. The tour included a typical breakfast Of eggs rice and beans and fried plantains.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1818.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1024" width="800" height="493">

We boarded the catamaran at 9. There was about 40 people on our tour, which was "low" they can hold up to 100 people. The boat ride over to isla tortuga took about an hour and a half. The views along the way were really nice. The boat was also pretty nice it had a net area in the front you could lay out on and two little mini pools you could sit in. We layed out on the net area the whole way.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1817.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1025" width="800" height="490">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_20181211_102122.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1040" width="4032" height="3024">

The island was very remote, it appeared to be uninhabited besides the beach area which was really nice. When we arrived we unloaded our stuff to the picnic area and then got back on the boat to do some snorkeling.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1819.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1026" width="800" height="492">

The snorkeling was not the best I've done but it was still fun. There really wasn't a whole lot of fish where we went. After an hour we boated back to the beach for lunch. Lunch consisted of a surprisingly good pasta salad and chicken and rice. After lunch we hung out on the beach. I worked on my tan while Rachel applied as much sun screen as humanly possibly to try and avoid burning. It was a perfect day out with no clouds in sight.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1799.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1030" width="4032" height="3024">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1796.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1031" width="3088" height="2320">

About an hour or so after lunch they had banana boat rides that we went on. We chose the "fast" ride of course. It went for about 15 minutes and we fell 3 times.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1822.jpg" class="wp-image-1028 size-full" width="800" height="487">

Back on the beach they also had hammocks and a slack line which is basically a tight rope you try to balance on. I tried it a few times and failed miserably 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1809.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1029" width="3024" height="4032">

Around 3:30 we boarded the boat to head back to the mainland. We went a slightly different way back but it still took about an hour and a half. Once we got back we packed up all our things and headed to Jaco. A small beach town about an hour south of punta arenas. We arrived around 7 PM. The Airbnb was super nice and massive! It was the 6th story on a beach front condo with great views from the balcony.

After a quick shower we walked down the main strip of town to go to tacobar jaco for dinner. I had 3 tacos that were all really good. The place was also unique because it had swings for seats instead of bar stools. After dinner we went back to the Airbnb and crashed for the night.
