<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we slept in and took it easy. We ate breakfast at the Airbnb and then cleaned and packed up before heading out. Today the weather was perfectly clear, not a cloud in sight! Perfect for hiking up mountains. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We left the Airbnb around 10a, the drive up to the remarkables took about 45 minutes, the drive itself had great views of the city and surrounding area. We parked at the lot for the ski area, currently closed for the summer - though there was snow it wasn’t nearly enough to ski. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/GGGN2ez.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike up to Alta lake took about 30min and was around a mile. Apparently the lake was one of the film locations for LOTR. The lake was really nice and had great views of the peaks surrounding it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the lake the other decided to hang out while I decided to attempt to hike to the top of one of the double cones (the name given to the two peaks). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike up was very rough lots of scree (big loos boulders), no clear path, and snow. The snowy scree was very difficult to traverse. My shoes are pretty old and had no grip on the snow. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/YgrFgmF.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">The “path” up behind me</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After a certain point (probably 20/30 min) I hit the ridge of the peak. There was less snow because it was steep and require a scramble/climb up. It was really fun, I ended up making it about 10m from the final summit which required some climbing that was too sketchy for me. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Also the wind on the ridge was ripping and I was start to get cold. I took out the drone for some quick photos and videos (I tried to cicle the peak and lost signal with the drone, thankfully it was able to return to home automatically but it freaked me out for a minute!). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/uv7d2KO.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">If you look hard you can see me</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After I successfully got the drone back I decided to hike down. The hike down was much faster. However I went a little too quick and my foot slipped on some snowy scree and I got a nearly gash on my ankle. I was able to hike down the rest ok and Becca was able to patch me up with my mini first aid kit (glad I always carry it!). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/vd0FFnU.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I ate a quick sandwich at the lake and then we hiked back down to the car. After loading up we made the ~2 hour drive to our next stop, te Anau. Te Anau is basically a stop over town on the way to the famous Milford sound. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We arrived at our Airbnb around 4p and got unpacked. The Airbnb layout was really strange. After getting settled we walked into town. I made a quick stop at the gas station and got some cheap replacement sunglasses then we walked down the main strip to the lake. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On the way down we saw an escape game so we booked a time for 7p. We made another stop at the grocery store for breakfast and lunch tomorrow and then brought everything back to the Airbnb (we’re only 5min walk from the town). We rested for a bit before heading back to do the escape game. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/PGTXmmZ.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The game was actually really cool, the theme was moose hunt we had to collect evidence that moose still exist in NZ and then escape (apparently it’s based on a true story where they brought moose to the island and then tracked them).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/GjHSvqk.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made it out in 48 minutes without any help or clues! All the puzzles were really unique and fun so we decided to reserve the second room for tomorrow (apparently it’s more difficult).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the game we stopped at an Asian fusion restaurant called ditto. I had the spicy tuna sushi bowl and a bao bun. Both were delicious! We got the food to go and ate it at the Airbnb. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
