Today was an extremely long travel day. I packed up everything last night and went directly from work to airport around 6pm. The first leg of the trip was Savannah to Atlanta which was fast and painless. I listened to a Jocko podcast which if you haven't heard of him, and you want to get motivated, do yourself a favor and look him up.

I only had a 2 hour layover till my Atlanta to Paris flight so I really just only had time to get to the terminal and eat a snack before boarding. The flight to Paris was ~8 hours. I fell asleep a few times watching a couple good movies (the last breath and supreme) and listened to a few more podcasts.

Once in Paris I had a 5 hour layover until my final flight to Amman. I did some reading and watched another movie (hotel Mumbai) to kill the time. The flight to Amman was on an older aircraft that didn't have any TVs in the seat-backs but I had my tablet so I watched two movies: in the loop and when Harry met sally. This flight was only about 4.5 hours and there was no one in the middle seat which was nice and let me stretch out a bit. The dinner on the flight was some vegetarian meal which wasn't that great, but since the flight was from France at least the wine was good!

I finally landed in Amman around 11:45pm it's been ~24 hours since I started traveling so the exhaustion was starting to set in. Going through the passport control was a pain. Apparently even though I already bought a visa I still had to go through the "purchase visa" line. I conveniently found this out after waiting in the passport line so I had to go all the way back, and at this point the line for visas was long 🤬.

While getting through all the lines I ran into Sam. After we finally made it out of the airport we took the shuttle to the hotel (which was like 2 min away) and checked in to meet Jamie. We had booked a two person room because on the website there wasn't an option for 3 but when we got there the reception was able to change us to a bigger 3 bed room. At this point it was about 1am so we pretty much passed out right away.

The next morning we got up at 6am for breakfast which was a buffet style so we loaded up on everything. After we took the shuttle back to the airport to pick up the rental car and head on our way to Wadi Rum.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2564.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1295" width="3088" height="2320">

We got on the road around 7am and the trip down was about 3.5 hours. The driving here is about what you'd expect, a cluster*. Apparently there are no lanes and whatever lines are on the road are purely there for decoration and at best are a suggestion of where you should drive. However, the funny thing is that there are everywhere and apparently they are very strict on the speed limit...I found this out the hard way 😂.

Less than an hour into the drive we see a cop ahead of us run out into the middle of the highway and start waving at us to stop and pull over to him. It seemed really sketchy at first. After a quick interaction he told us that we were going 121kmph in a 110kmph zone, which is like 6mph over the speed limit.

We thought that we were going to have to bribe him or something but Luckily I guess I was charming/convincing enough (aka I had a US passport) that he decided to give us a warning and let us go on our way.

As if that was enough to teach me a lesson about an hour later we get pulled over AGAIN! The same things happened, once he found out we were tourists from the US he was super nice and let us go on our way.

Fortunately we didn't get pulled over by any police after that and made it to Wadi Rum around 10:20 to meet our tour guide. He drove us through the desert in his Jeep to the camp and we planned out the rest of our day over some tea.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2568.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1296" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2575.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1298" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2577.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1297" width="4032" height="3024">

After resting for a few hours we had some lunch at camp which was pita with tuna and some veggie mixes. At 1pm we began our Jeep tour of Wadi Rum.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3285.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1308" width="2576" height="1920">

Our first stop was this giant sand dune. Our guide brought a sandboard with so that we could hike up and board down. It was a blast. I only went down twice though because hiking up the dune was a lot of work!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2615.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1300" width="1968" height="3520">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3301.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1309" width="2576" height="1920">

Our second stop was this old carving in the rock that Lawrence of Arabia carved as directions to get through the desert. It was up on a rock so we had to do some climbing to get to it.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3302.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1310" width="2576" height="1920">

Our next stop was the Lawrence house. This was a cave in the side of the mountain where Lawrence of Arabia supposedly lived. Our guide told us that wasn't really true, they were actually Nubian caves and they did some of the filming for the Lawrence of Arabia movie there. Side note: a lot of famous movies have been filmed in Wadi Rum including Star Wars, Prometheus, and the Martian. We ended up climbing on top of the caves for some stunning views and of course some good photos.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191012_144516.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1301" width="4032" height="2268">

After the Lawrence house we made our way to Um Frouth rock bridge which was a cool rock formation in one of the mountains that looks like a bridge.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/20191012_152444.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1302" width="4032" height="2268">

Next we went to Mushroom rock. This ones pretty self-explanatory, it's a rock that looks like a mushroom....really not that cool honestly haha.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3329.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1311" width="2576" height="1920">

Our final stop on the Jeep tour was a drive through a canyon and to see some faces carved in rock. The faces were supposed to be Lawrence of Arabia and the first kind of Jordan. They weren't super realistic looking but the area was cool. Finally we made our way back to camp around 5. We washed up quickly and then got to our camels for a short sunset tour.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3334.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1312" width="2576" height="1920">

For the camel ride the 3 of us rode out about 30 min from our camp, stopped to watch the sunset, and then rode back. Our camels were the best at walking, I was in front but Jamie's camel kept trying to get in front of me messing up the line. Overall I thought the camels were fun but you definitely don't need to ride them that long to get the experience.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3343.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1313" width="2576" height="1920">

Once the sunset Jamie wanted to take some "artsy photos, one of which was a handstand with the sunset. I'll let you decide who did it better....

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2722.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1315" width="3662" height="2744">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2724.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1316" width="3024" height="4032">

Once we got back to camp we had a traditional Bedouin dinner where they cook the food under ground. The meal consisted of rice, chicken, and veggies.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2732.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1305" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2733.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1306" width="3088" height="2320">

After dinner we hung out in the main camp tent then showered and got ready for bed. It's super quiet in the desert and also great for star gazing since there's hardly any light pollution. Tomorrow we have another early day hiking around Wadi Rum.
