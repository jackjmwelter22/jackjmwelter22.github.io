I woke up this morning still feeling bad, hoping I start feeling better soon, my plan today was to eat as much American food as possible to fix my stomach...that'll work right? We started it off right by getting the 'American breakfast' at the hostel.

After breakfast we hailed a cab outside to take us to Yaoshan mountain. The cab ride over was very strange, the car in front of us kept randomly throwing fires peeks out of their window!? I have no idea why but they did it at least 6 times and our driver acted like it was just normal.

Yaoshan mountain is the highest peak in Guilin. Our plan was to hike it and then take the tobaggon slide down. However when we got there we were told we couldn't hike and that the slide was closed because of the rain so we ended up taking the cable car up to the top which was essentially an old ski lift. The views on the way up were pretty spectacular.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1404.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-586" width="3024">

Creepy advert for the tobaggon 😂

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1407.jpg" height="1280" class="wp-image-584" width="720"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1409.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-585" width="2576">

At the top the weather got a lot cloudier and the view was completely obscured. We spent the rest of the time walking around the top which had a few temples. Some of the temples had thousands of what I assume were some sort of prayer flags tied to the trees. It was really cool to see, I wish I was able to read some of them.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1410.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-588" width="4032">

Wonderful view at the top!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1413.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-587" width="4032">

After exploring for a bit more we decide to catch a taxi back to the hostel. The Chinese are the master of the concept 'exit through the gift shop'. In order to leave the mountain we had to go through a gift shop that was essentially a binge maze that zig zagged as much as possible forcing you to walk past rows and rows of stuff that they're trying to sell you. You basically have to walk 100 feet to get to an exit that (if you were to just walk straight) is 5 feet away 😂.

Finding a taxi from the mountain was pretty easy. Trying to explain to them where we were going however was next to impossible. I have a suspicion that the locals here don't know what a map is and have never used one before. I showed our driver the exact google maps route on how to get back to our hotel and she looked at it like it was some weird kind of picture. I even showed her the address to the place figuring that would help. Nope, the thing is everything has both a Chinese and English name and the address is not an exception. So even bough I showed her the name of the street and where it was on the map she still had no idea where to take us. Finally she ended up calling someone who knew English and he helped translate where we wanted to go.

Another side note: all our drivers here have been terrible! I mean texting and driving, talking and driving, one of our drivers was even taking pictures of something...while driving!

After making it back to the hostel we decide to give elephant hill another try. This time it was early enough so we were able to get in! We hiked up the hill first which gave us a cool view of the city and then hiked around it to get some good views of the hill itself. It's called elephant hill because well...it looks like an elephant 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1427.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-596" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1430.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-591" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1439.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-590" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1442.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-589" width="2576">

We ran into more people outside who wanted to take their picture with us so of course we obliged.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1445.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-597" width="4032">

After elephant hill we went back to the main strip for lunch. I was determined to eat American food so I went in search for McDonald's. Unfortunately as I've mentioned google maps really sucks here and both places that were supposed to have McDonalds didn't 😔 so I ended up settling for Kentucky fried chicken. Apparently asians really like KFC because inside was a madhouse, it was packed! The chicken sandwich I ordered definitely had a bit more spicyness to it than what you'd get in the US.

Next spot on the agenda was the reed flute cave. Again we took a taxi out there, this time we were more prepared and had our hostel write the Chinese names of places down. When we were waking to the entrance of the cave this Chinese guy approached us asking us if we wanted to take a bamboo raft. His English was surprisingly very good and supposedly the raft was going to take us to the entrance of the cave and save us time walking. It was only $3 so we decided why not, hell I would've gave the guy money just for being able to speak English as well as he did!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1455.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-595" width="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1456.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-594" width="4032">

The bamboo ride was fun but also kind of a scam. Basically they took you 100 yards across the lake and there's no way it saved you any walking 😂.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/d7cc386b-6864-44ea-aacb-3d423ffc07a5-11171-00000ab3d0a1862b_tmp.jpg" height="1280" class="wp-image-592" width="960">

The cave itself was just alright. It was super commercialized, everything was lit up in different colors, there was a speaker system, and even photo booths inside the cave.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1469.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-598" width="3024">

I definitely think it would've been cooler to see the cave units original form. All of the rock formations even had little name tags of what they were supposed to look like, some of them were definitely a stretch.

Outside the cave we found stairs and were able to do some more hiking! This spot gave us some amazing views of the city.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1473.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-593" width="4032">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1495.jpg" height="3021" class="wp-image-599" width="3451">

Once again we were forced to exit through the gift shop 😂, still haven't bought anything yet! We caught another taxi to take us back toward the city to Diecai hill (more hiking).

This hill gave us more awesome views a little closer to the city and even had a makeshift slide you could take back down that was kinda fun!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1484.jpg" height="1932" class="wp-image-601" width="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1481.jpg" height="3024" class="wp-image-600" width="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/img_1490.jpg" height="4032" class="wp-image-602" width="3024">

After all the hiking we did we were exhausted and made our way back to the hostel to relax and hang out. Jamie and I played some pool and chess and had a pizza (more American food!) which was surprisingly good! Then we called it a night. Tomorrow we make our way to the city of Chengdu. </p>
