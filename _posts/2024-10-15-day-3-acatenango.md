<p>Today I woke up way too early again (4am) I wrote my blog and then everyone got up at 6:30am. We left the hotel just before 7 and walked to the car and drove ~1 hour to the village below Acatenango (the volcano) to meet our tour group. Apparently they didn’t realize we drove ourselves even though I whatsapped them the day before and waited extra for us in town.</p>

<p>We filled our waters and rented some poles for like $2. After we got all our stuff organized we had a meeting in front of the tour house explaining the hike. We’re hiking from 2400m to about 3500m stopping at the camp tonight and then leaving early tomorrow morning for the summit. There’s an optional hike to the el fuego volcano that we plan on doing after we get to camp. There’s group was massive 32 hikers in total with 6 guides. We took a quick group picture before getting in the vans to drive to the start of the hike. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the quick first stop we continued up to the entrance to the park. Overall the trail was superrr crowded and very tight which made passing difficult. The pace was also very slow. Luckily one of the guides broke off a group of 8 of us so that we could hike at a more reasonable pace. After stopping at the entrance for a quick break and to get our wristbands we continued up. The trail itself is only like 8 miles to the peak and back but 5,000’ of gain - pretty steep but I’ve definitely done steeper. </p>

<p>Lunch was chicken and rice, pineapple, an apple, and Oreos. It was pretty good, we also got some hot chocolate. We were supposed to do the optional el fuego hike at 4, however the weather was so bad the guides said we couldn’t do it today but instead could do it tomorrow instead of the peak.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We hung out at in the cabin and at camp until 7pm to get dinner. The guides made a fire using strange strategies to get it started by pouring gasoline and throwing plastic things in it haha, eventually they got it going. Dinner was spaghetti pasta and fried chicken, it was okay nothing amazing.</p>
