<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up and took our at home covid tests. We needed to do it in order to get back into the US. The test was relatively straightforward nose swab that you do while FaceTiming a test “proctor” to make sure you do it correctly. My test kit was missing the reagent but luckily beccas had enough for both of us. Luckily both our tests came back (after 15 min) as negative. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After testing we had our complementary breakfast at the hotel on the balcony facing the ocean. I had the “americano” breakfast and Becca had the traditional. Nothing too special about either. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1996,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_5331.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-1996" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast we changed and met our surf instructors at the hotel parking lot. There were 3 guys, two instructors and one photographer (I can’t remember their names). We ended up going to the next beach over called San Blas, because the waves weren’t as big there...they were still really big though! They actually almost cancelled our lesson because of the size of the waves. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The beach we went too had this really cool black sand that shimmered in the sun. We ended up doing some stretches and going over the basics of how to position yourself and how to pop-up while we were on the beach. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1993,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_6814.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-1993" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After going over the basics we tried it out for real in the waves. I’ve only been surfing one other time (in Costa Rica) and the waves were way farther out, here by the time you popped-up you were almost at the shore, this made for some very short runs. Also the waves were pretty powerful. The instructors weren’t all that great either, I kept asking him for tips and what to do but he didn’t other up much advice. We still had fun though!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Afterwards we drank some coconut water and relaxed on the beach. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1990,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_6941.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1990" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After our tour we hung out at the hotel for an hour and then drove to the other nearby town called La Libertad. We walked along the beach and onto the pier, there were a bunch of random shops lining the beach and a big seafood market that we explored. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1992,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_5465.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1992" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We made our way to the end of the beach to a place called punta roca. There was a little shop there with drinks where you could watch some of the really good surfers catch waves. After all the surfers came in we made our way back to the center of town and stopped at a restaurant with a view of the water. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I had some sort of beef and cheese sandwich, it was delicious, and Becca had a fish stuffed with shrimp. One thing to note is restaurant service in El Salvador is superrr slow it took about 40 min just to get our food after we ordered!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1991,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_5468.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1991" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we drove to El Tunco to look for some potential surf lessons again tomorrow afternoon. The traffic to get back was really bad, and for no apparent reason, it seems like they just like to block the road sometimes, not really sure why. In el tunco we picked up some pupusas for dinner later from the really good street shop and also found a different company to do surf lessons with. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After el tunco we went back to the hotel and hung out at the pool and our room until bed.  Tomorrow we’re planning to go to another waterfall about 30 min away and then surf again in the afternoon!</p>
