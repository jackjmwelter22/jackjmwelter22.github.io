<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I woke up early again (still on eastern time I guess). Becca ended up waking up around 7a. We ate some breakfast in the room and then got in the car and drove back to the east side of the island to get our kayak rental at 9a. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The kayak shop we rented from was xxxx, they were super friendly and helped us load up the two person kayak on top of the roof of our car with foam pads. They also gave us a map with directions on where to put the kayaks in and how to hike to the secret falls.</p>

<p>After loading up the kayak we drove about 2 miles to the Wailua river boat ramp to put in. It turned out there was some sort of school or club rowing race so the ramp was packed with people. Luckily we were able to unload and find a parking spot in the small lot. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once on the river we paddled upstream for about 45 minutes and took the fork to the right and beached our kayaks at the start of the “secret” falls trailhead. Secret is a bit of a misnomer as just about everyone knows about as evidenced by the 1000 kayaks on the google maps aerial photo. Luckily for us they don’t do any guided tours on Saturdays so it was essentially empty. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The falls were really cool. There were only two other people there when we arrived. I went in the water to about my waist, it was really cold and I didn’t feel like going all the way in. We took a bunch of pictures and I flew the drone around for a bit. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Another thing I forgot to mention was there were a bunch of chickens/roosters at the falls. In general we’ve seen tons of roosters around Kauai, they’re literally everywhere. While resting at the falls I took the drone out and got a few shots.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After a nice break at the falls we hiked back to our kayaks. Unfortunately as we left one of my sandals randomly broke. It was still kind of wearable so I kept it on till we go to the kayaks. On the way back we passed a ton of other people (glad we went early!).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the kayaks we paddled back to the fork in the river and this time went down the other side to see the fern grotto. The landing area for the kayaks was tiny and very sporty. Basically and muddy ledge you had to pull the kayak up onto. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once on shore we walked 2 minutes to see the grotto. The other way to visit the grotto is by boat in a tour group which costs $35 per person. I’m super glad we didn’t do that, the grotto was cool but definitely underwhelming and I would have been really annoyed if I payed money to see it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After taking a few pictures we went back and loaded into the kayak and continued paddling down the river. We heard there was a cliff jump spot so we wanted to check it out. After about 5–10minutes of paddling we found the spot. The cliff was pretty small and the water was cold so I decided to skip jumping. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After floating for a minute we turned around and started the paddle back to the dock. It probably took us an hour or so to make it back. Once at the dock we loaded up the kayak and then drove back to town for a quick lunch stop. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up stopping at a Thai food truck. Becca got the fried rice and I got a chicken skewer. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After eating we returned the kayak to the rental shop and then drove to do a small hike called the sleeping giant. There are two trails for the hike and we did the west side. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike was short and relatively steep 1.7 miles and 600’ elevation. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After enjoying the views we hiked back down to the car and drove to a nearby beach called kealia beach. We relaxed and the beach for a couple hours. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the beach we drove back and changed and then went to downtown Hanalei to the Kaylpso Island grill for dinner. I had a burger with grilled onion and Becca had a tuna salad.</p>
