Today we woke up early (5am) to get on the trail early so we could make it to the safari on time. We had breakfast at 5:30 which was porridge, lentils, and pineapple. We gave Moses the money for tipping and after breakfast gave it to the team. In total we had 11 people helping us: 1 guide (Moses), assistant guide (Jackson), 1 chef, 1 waiter (Francis), and 7 porters. We definitely couldn’t have done the trek without them. After breakfast we made the 3 hour hike back down to the gate where we stopped in a small gift shop and then got picked up by our van. Right before you get to the van little kids run up to you on the road and ask you to give them basically anything. One kid walked up to me and asked me for my hiking poles, I said no, then he asked for my hat, I sad no again, he basically asked me for anything he could see 😂.

The drive back to Moshi took less than an hour. It turns out there was some confusion with the safari and now we have a day in Moshi to relax. We cleaned and dried most of our clothes and then hung around the property the rest of the day.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/img_1326.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-904" height="3662" width="2744">

For lunch we ended up just eating at the main house. It was chicken and rice with fruit. After lunch we played a board game in this treehouse they have that has an awesome view of the mountain.

For dinner we walked to a nearby restaurant called the kitchen which was surprisingly nice. I ended up getting the "American burger" which had egg, cheese, and everything on it. It was delicious. Afterwards we got our stuff packed and ready for the safari tomorrow. It's a far drive to get out for the parks so we have to leave at 5am.
