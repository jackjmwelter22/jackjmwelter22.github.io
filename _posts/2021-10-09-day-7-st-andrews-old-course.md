<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today was the old course day! Luckily we had Amazing weather! Almost 60 degrees and super sunny! We got up and had breakfast at the hotel (which was included). It was a buffet plus a menu that was really good! I had eggs Benedict and waffles. We met Scott (the guy who set up our golfing here in Scotland) at 9a to get a quick debrief of our itinerary and plans for our trip. After talking we went back up to the room and got ready for gold then met him back down at the lobby to go to the driving range. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The driving range is actually on the other side of the course. It was a really fancy range, every stall had a trackman that would tell you each shots ball speed, height, launch angle, carry, total distance, and a bunch of other cool stats. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After the range we drove over to the first tee to putt and get ready for our tee time. We were playing with a couple from Florida, George and Catherine, neither were very good, George almost missed and rolled one off the first tee 😂, but they were really nice. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My dad and I both had caddies, mine was Sam who was +1 golfer (really good). Apparently he’s caddie for a bunch of pros and celebrities, including Trump multiple times (he said he’s actually a really good golfer). My dad and I both played really well, I had 3 birdies and was 1 over going into 17th then doubled and bogied to end 4 over at 76. </p>

<p>On our way back we stopped at Dunvegans, a famous pub with tons of pictures of pros and celebrities on the walls, for dinner. I ate about half of my burger and really didn’t feel well so I walked back to the hotel to get the car and then picked my dad up with the car. Back at the hotel I ended up puking a lot, not sure if it was something I ate but it was not fun! Needless to say we stayed in for the rest of the night</p>
