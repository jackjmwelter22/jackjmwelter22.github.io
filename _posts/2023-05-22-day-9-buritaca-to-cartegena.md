<p>Today we slept in and had breakfast at the hotel. I had pancakes! They were great!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we hung out in the room until our drive came to pick us up and take us to Cartegena. We decided to get a driver because we needed one regardless to get back to Santa Marta and an airplane would have cost more and likely not saved anytime (4 hour drive) and no direct flights. Also the bus would have cost only slightly less total. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unfortunately we ran into some bad traffic on road which according to our driver was due to protests. We were dead stopped in traffic and it was a somewhat sketchy area. In typical fashion Becca really had to pee so she went to a small shop near the road. The drive ended up taking 5 hours which I still think was faster than flying, but we basically spent an hour sitting completely stopped in traffic. We finally made it to our hotel to check in at 3:30. The best part about the hotel was that it had air conditiong!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We relaxed in the room and got some Powerade's and snacks from a nearby store. After relaxing we took a cab downtown (about 15 minute drive) and ate dinner at cafe Del Mar a restaurant on the city wall known for its great sunsets. Unfortunately it was cloudy so the sunset wasn't great but it was still a good spot to eat. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
