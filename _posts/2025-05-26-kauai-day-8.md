<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 7 and made a big breakfast in the condo of eggs and bacon. I ended going out for a run down the coast at 8. I ran past shipwreck beach where there is a super nice hotel called the grand hayatt. And then I ran down the Maha'ulepu (Heritage) Trail which took me through a deep sandy bluff and past some really nice coastline and onto a golf course! </p>

<p>The view from the course was really nice. The trail continued back down to the coast and ended near the Makauwahi Cave Reserve. I ran through and took a picture of the “cave” it wasn’t much to look at but I think the main cave was closed. The trail was blocked by a massive puddle of water. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After seeing the trail I decided to take another route back this time following the roads. The views weren’t as good as the coast but it was definitely easier not having to contend with the sand. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I made it back about an hour later and 5.5 miles. After showering we walked down to the hot tub to relax for a bit. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For lunch we decided to get some Thai food from the Anatta’s thai food truck. We both got the pad Thai which was really good. There were a bunch of chickens and chicks walking around begging for food while we ate.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch we decided to drive to see the spouting horn (just a few minutes west of us). The horn was definitely a tourist trap, there were two massive tour buses there so it was packed with people. It’s basically just a blowhole that shoots water up in the air occasionally when the waves hit. We took a quick picture and then got out of there.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>We stopped at Lawa'i Beach on the way back to relax. It was a really small beach but not quite as crowded as Poipu. There was a shave ice food truck across the street from the beach called Riptides so we had to try it. I got strawberry, Blue raspberry, and cherry with ice cream and condensed milk. It was really good but still not as good as wishing wells shave ice.</p>

<p>After relaxing at the beach we made our way back to the condo and then decided to walk over to shipwreck beach. It’s a little farther than poipu (about 0.5 miles) but it’s still connected to the greenway so very easy to walk to. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We rested at the beach for the rest of the afternoon. There was a big cliff that juts out at the end of the beach. I hiked out there to see about jumping off. It was really hard to tell how deep the water was below it so I decided to not jump. I did see a cool turtle swimming by though!</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the beach we went back to the pool/hot tub for a bit. Then for dinner we went and picked up some Indian food from a nearby restaurant. Becca and I both got the chicken tikka masala, it was really good. </p>
