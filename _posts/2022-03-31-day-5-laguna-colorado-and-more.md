<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up at 6 to get breakfast, turns out breakfast was actually at 6:30 (our guide has trouble keeping time lol). Breakfast consisted of toast m, eggs, fruit, and tea. Pretty basic but we weren’t expecting much. After breakfast we showered, got all packed and ready to go (we were supposed to leave at 7 but ended up leaving at 7:30). We made a quick pit stop back in Uyuni for the drivers to pick up our lunch and then started our drive south. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The first stop was about 1.5 hours away called San Cristóbal, we basically just here to pee and there was a market if you wanted a snack or something. I did find a cute dog to pet so that made it worth it. Then we drove another hour or so in the car to a place called valle de las rocas. It was a huge area with tons of volcanic rock scattered everywhere from the nearby volcano. It was a really unique landscape and we had about 30 minutes to explore and climb the rocks. The rocks were really grippy and fun to climb, we took a bunch of pictures. </p>

<p>After climbing we loaded back into the car and drove another hour to a lake nestled in the mountains called lake cunyapa. It was cool because it had a bunch of pink flamingos in it. </p>

<p>We stayed for about 15 min before getting back into the car and driving another 30 minutes to stinky lake (thats literally what it’s called lol) to eat lunch. We arrived around 2pm This lake was also surrounded by mountains and had a ton more flamingos! Probably like 1000 of them and apparently the lake is stinky because of all the flamingo poop. </p>

<p>Apparently it’s actually not a deep lake but it’s called that because it’s lower in elevation than the surrounding lakes. We also learned that the white stuff in the lakes is actually borox. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After some pictures we drove 45 minutes to stop at the Solie desert. It’s the highest and driest desert in the world. Apparently it only rains once or twice a year, I decided to help out and water the plants 😁. It was super windy so we didn’t stay long just some pictures before getting back in the car. </p>

<p>Our next stop was the stone tree, a really cool rock formation. We stopped for some pictures and climbed a few of the rocks (it was prohibited to climb the stone tree rock). It was super windy here as well. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got back in the car yet again and made our way about 45 minutes to the border of the national park where the laguna colorada is at around 5:30pm. We had to pay the 150 boliviano entry fee and have our passports checked to enter the park. We drove a little ways past the entrance to see the laguna colorada (colored lagoon), the lake gets its red color from micro-organisms that live in the lake. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/rXDP4vi.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the lake we drove to our hotel around 20 minutes away. It was a pretty basic accommodation, the shower didn’t work quite right but at least our bed was comfy. For dinner we had a spaghetti without meat, it had a mushroom tomato sauce. We also had a soup that was pretty good. After dinner we went outside to look at the stars, with such little light pollution you could see everything, it was awesome! We went to bed pretty early since we decided to start early tomorrow (at 4am). </p>
