<p>Today we woke up early, around 6 am, ate breakfast in the hotel. I had cereal and a yoghurt. We loaded up the car and left around 7am making our way to Austria to do the famous sky ladder klettersteig called <a href="https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/intersport-klettersteig-donnerkogel/">Donnerkogel</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The drive took around 3 hours. We parked at a parking lot near the gondola and took it up to the hut (it saved at least an hour of hiking). Sarah decided she wasn’t going to do the feratta, so instead she did the hike on the back of the mountain to meet us at the summit. </p>

There were some other people right at the start, luckily we were able to get on before them. We also passed a couple on the way up who were going really slow.</p>

<p>When we made it to the ladder I decided to go first, it was really fun! Unfortunately Pat wasn’t able to get the drone working while I went but Becca still got some good photos. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I ended up hiking back down (an alternate route) to take photos and drone footage (it worked this time) of Becca and Pat. The drone footage turned out really good! Unfortunately by the time pat made it up the ladder a huge group of people arrived so I decided not to do the ladder again. Also weather looked like it was moving in and we didn’t want to get stuck on the ridge during a rain storm. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the ladder the feratta got a bit more difficult but we were nearly at the top. It took maybe a other 20 minutes to reach the summit where we met up with Sarah. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the summit we took a break to snack and enjoy the view (the weather looked like it cleared up a bit). We took a couple of group photos and then started our way back down the mountain. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The hike back down was a bit of a pain. It took us about an hour to make it dow to the hut. Becca and I made it back just before it started to downpour. Unfortunately pat and Sarah descended a bit slower than us and had to hike a good 15-20 minutes in the rain. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/mvpZXAv.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the hut Becca had a sprite and cappuccino while I had a winter schintzle and hot chocolate. Once pat and Sarah got back we hung out for about half an hour till the rain subsided and made our way back to the gondola to head down. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/epf9rP1.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Back at the base of the mountain we walked over to the lake (only a few hundred feet away) and went to do another via feratta called <a href="https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/laserer-alpin-klettersteig/">Laserer Alpine Steig</a>. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This one was short, but fun. We were directly above the lake for about half of it and there was another 2 wire horizontal ladder that was fun and of course great views of the surrounding area.  </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>I hiked ahead a bit to get some drone footage of everyone else making the climb. Once we all finished we went back to the car and drove about 45 minutes to strobe to check into our Airbnb. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up parking the car in an electric vehicle spot (per our hosts instructions); parking seems to be an issue with all our accommodations lol. We showered and did a quick drone of the area before heading into town for dinner. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The town is small and quaint but apparently there was a “summer in Strobl” festival taking place so there were tons of people at the lake shore. They were doing a water skiing demonstration that was pretty impressive. One guy water skied on top of 3 chairs and another guy was doing all sorts of flips. </p>
