<p>Day 1</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We woke up at 3:45am to get ready, pack the car, and head to the airport. I dropped Becca and Bennett off with the luggage, and then parked the car so they could get checked in quickly (we couldn’t check in online because we had a lap infant).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After parking the car I walked in and met Becca at the check-in desk for American. It turned out that whoever Becca had talked to on the phone from American to add Bennett as a lap infant only put him on the first flight to Miami and not the second flight to Roatan?! It made absolutely no sense and the agent she was talking to was SUPER rude and unhelpful acting like it was our fault. It took over an hour and the manager to come by - they made us pay for Bennett again at the desk and the best part was - after we paid again  he still wasn’t on the ticket! At that point I think the manager realized that something was wrong with their system and let us go (using the receipt as proof to board with Bennett) and try to fix it on their side while we were in the air. I also forgot to mention that they charged Becca a $30 bag fee even though she is an American card member and it was supposed to be free. Not happy with American Airlines today!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As a result of all this craziness, we barely made it through security in time and got to the gate just as they were about to close. Even though Bennett wasn’t listed on the second ticket we were able to show the receipt that we had paid and they let us in the flight. STRESSFUL!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The flight to Miami was mostly empty. Becca and I each chose two random empty rows at the front of the plane (we honestly didn't care we just grabbed the first seats we saw). Bennett did really well on the flight, I held him in the chest carrier and he slept the entire 1.5 hour flight arriving in Miami at ~7am.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once we got to Miami we went to the American help desk since we had 5 hours before our next flight. Unfortunately they weren’t able to help us but told us to fill out some forms on the website. So TBD on the compensation process.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My mom arrived in Miami a few hours later at 9:45 and met us at the gate (we're all on the same flight going into Roatan). Bennet had fun hanging out with my mom at the gate until our flight which left just after 12. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Luckily for him Bennett did great and slept all the way through the short 2 hour flight over to Roatan. We landed around 12pm - thanks to Bennett we got the priority lane through immigration/passport control.  Becca and my mom waited for the bags while I got the rental car. The airport is super small so it didn't take long before we got everything. We had to squeeze everyone into the tiny rental car (a Kia picanto), but luckily the drive to the VRBO checkin was only 15 minutes away. The property was ready for us early which was nice, so we drove another 15 minutes to the VRBO and got everything unpacked and settled in.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The VRBO was very nice! Just a short walk away from the main strip of West End where all the shops and restaurants are. The place itself was massive, it was on the second floor and had a big open kitchen and living room with 3 separate bedrooms and a big balcony looking out into the Caribbean sea. It was right on the beach and had a small pool on the first floor deck.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After unpacking and settling in we walked ~2 minutes into town. Our first goal was to find a shop to dive with for the week. Unfortunately it turned out to be more difficult than we originally anticipated - the first few shops were either already closed for the day or were full. Fortunately we stumbled onto the Reef Gliders dive shop (which turned out to be awesome). They had a phone number on the door so even though they were closed for the day we were able to contact them and get on the schedule for the following morning. They also said they would be able to take us to one of the wreck dives that we were interested in!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After figuring out the diving we stopped for dinner at a place called Anthony's chicken (highly recommended on Reddit)</p>

<p>Day 2 Wednesday </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We woke up early today and ate breakfast at the VRBO, I had some scrambled eggs. We left at 8am to walk to the Reef Gliders dive shop to check in for our morning dives and get our gear in order. The plan was to do two morning dives, one at 9 and one at 11.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We met our dive guide, Louden who was young and really friendly. He briefed us on our first dive which was going to be at a dive site called Chief's Quarters. The dive site was only about a 5 minute boat ride from the dive shop (that's the best part about diving in Roatan - all the dive sites are right off the shore)!</p>
