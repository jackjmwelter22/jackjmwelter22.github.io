Last night was pretty rough. We went to bed around 10 and I fell asleep pretty fast but then woke up to the sound of a car revving it's engine for a solid 15 min. I checked the time...10:50 I couldn't believe it. Of course after that I couldn't fall back to sleep. It was really hot in the room so we opened up the windows....that let the mosquitos in. Literally all night they were biting me and I couldn't hide under the covers because the room was so hot! I ended up listening to a podcast. I think I fell asleep for maybe like 2 hours :/.

We finally got out of our sauna...I mean tent... at 6am for a sunrise hike. We hiked away from our camp for about 30 min to this large dune and hiked up to the top for a cool view.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2785.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1324" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2789.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1325" width="4032" height="3024">

Hiking out here is like hiking on another planet it just looks so surreal. Hiking in the morning also has the benefit of it being way cooler than the afternoon making the hiking much more enjoyable.

After getting off the dune we continued hiking through a canyon (they call it a siq here) our guide told us about his family and a few stories but his English was not very good so it was very difficult to understand him think. From what we could gather was he has like 4 brothers and 3 sisters and is training to be a hot air balloon guide? I'm not really sure on the last one though 😂. Our guide also showed us how to make soap from some of the desert bushes, it was a little distracting though because there were a TON of flies. We finally hiked back to our camp around 9am and ate breakfast. Today we had eggs, pita, a meat vegetable mix, and hummus.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3355.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1334" width="2576" height="1920">

After breakfast we cleaned and packed and left the camp around 10:30. We decided to do Petra today instead of tomorrow since we finished seeing/doing all the things we wanted in Wadi Rum. The drive took about 1.5 hours. We stopped at a grocery store right before to pick up some snacks for the day. We ended up getting in to Petra around 12. Oh and I bought a head scarf thing to try and fit in.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2802.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1327" width="3024" height="4032">

Once inside we walked down the main trail which leads you to the famous treasury building. Along the way there are other various buildings carved in the rock and you walk through a big siq (canyon) to get there. The whole thing doesn't seem real and when you finally get to the treasury it's breathtaking. I ended up taking off my hat for a little and trying to put it back together, it was quite the ordeal but I think jamie got it right.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3374.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1335" width="1920" height="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3376.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1336" width="1932" height="2576">

After enjoying the view we decided to walk around the area. Petra is pretty huge and the treasury building is just a small portion of it. We ended up seeing the coliseum and then took the trail to see Petra from above. The trail was a lot of stairs and took probably about an hour but the view was worth it. One of the guys at the spot must've takin like 1000 pictures (including flexing poses) so you know the view was good! 😂

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3383.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1337" width="1920" height="2576"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3387.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1339" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3391.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1340" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3399.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1338" width="2576" height="1920">

After the trail we hiked back down to the entrance. On the way out there were tons of people trying to sell horse rides back, some even went as far as saying the ride was included with your ticket...yea right. Once we got out we stopped at the cave bar which is right next to the entrance for a beer. The cave bar is supposedly the oldest bar in the world, idk if that's true or not but the beer was tasty.

After the beer we went to meet our Airbnb host. We met him at little Petra which was about 15 min away. When we met him he told us to go check out the little Petra ruins quick before taking us to the cave. Little Petra was very similar to Petra, just smaller (get it?) There was not nearly as much hiking and way less people which was kinda cool. It also advertised the "best view in the world" the hike up to it was short but really cool, Sam choose difficult path at first until we realized there was a way easier way to go.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2853.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1328" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2855.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1329" width="2320" height="3088">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3408.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1341" width="2576" height="1920">

After checking out the ruins we made our way back and then followed our host to our cave Airbnb. Not gonna lie it was a little sketchy...okay a lot sketchy. It looked like the guy literally picked a random cave and decided to make it his. It was a big cave room with no bathroom and a light powered by car battery 😂 his advertisement of an "authentic" experience was no lie! words truly can't describe this place.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2858.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1330" width="3024" height="4032"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2857.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1331" width="4032" height="3024">

After dropping our stuff off in the cave we had dinner on the roof. To their credit it was a really delicious stew of rice with goat meatballs and potatoes and vegetables.

We ended up hanging out on the roof for a bit as the sun went down it was really nice honestly, the weather was perfect...for now....

We were super exhausted so we went to the cave to try and get some rest. That's when the weather started picking up. It was SUPER windy outside and sand was blowing into our cave at some points because the top half of the door area was open.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2863.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1332" width="4032" height="3024">

We were originally supposed to stay here two nights but because of all the running around we decided to treat ourselves and booked a room and the Hilton spa at the Dead Sea for tomorrow instead. Looking forward to a night of air conditioning and no bugs!
