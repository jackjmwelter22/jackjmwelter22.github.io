<p>Today we slept in - well Bennett woke us up earlier but we went back to sleep and got up around 8:30 am. We ate some breakfast in the airbnb and then drove about an hour to the northeast to do a hike called the sulphur skyline. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike was really nice, through the forest and up to the peak. There was snow most of the way up on the trees and off the trail making it look very picturesque.</p>

<p>We continued hiking back down to the parking lot. Once there we had a lunch break and unloaded most of our hiking gear. Conveniently there is a hot spring right on the same parking lot so we went there to relax.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://i.imgur.com/BIqUAPe.jpeg","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"embed-handler","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hot spring was really nice, basically two big hot pools and they also had two smaller cold pools. This hot spring is supposedly the hottest in the Rockies. They estimate that the water comes from 1500m below the surface based on its temperature.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:embed {"url":"https://i.imgur.com/f2q4EXj.jpeg","type":"rich","providerNameSlug":"embed-handler","responsive":true} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We hung out at the springs for a few hours. I bounced between the hot and cold pools a few times. We ended up leaving to head back to town around 4:15. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Right before making it back into town we saw some Elk so we stopped to get a quick picture from the car. There are a ton of warning signs about the elk because it’s their mating season. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>It’s called pyramid lake because it’s right below pyramid mountain. Which is a big mountain made out of a rare quartz. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After seeing the lake we drove into town and stopped for dinner at famosa pizza (many of the restaurants still aren’t open so the options are limited). The food was really good, I had the meat lovers pizza and we both got hot chocolate. </p>
