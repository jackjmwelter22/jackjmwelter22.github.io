<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up and had the typical breakfast at 7, same as yesterday’s. Then we packed up and hit the road for our final stop, the surf town El Tunco (recently renamed surf city, I suppose this is a marketing ploy by the el Salvadoran tourism department). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>On our way to Tunco (~2hrs) we stopped about halfway at a beach called los Cobanos. On the drive down we saw a ton of stray dogs along the road. Also there’s a really weird trend we noticed, people just walk casually along the road with machetes, I mean literally everyone young, old, man, woman. I’m not sure what they do with them. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Los Cobanos was a small beach known for its golden sand. Parking in the Tony town was confusing, we ended up parking by a restaurant in a tiny alley. There’s really no direct access to beach here you have to go through a building of some sort. We walked down the beach and then relaxed for a couple hours. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1982,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_5310.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1982" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The waves at the beach were massive! Also there were a ton of large rock formations in the water so we didn’t end up going in very far. After a few hours we decided to venture over to the other side of the cove. We parked in another alley and ate at the restaurant there. The restaurant was literally inches from the water. I’m pretty sure that where we were sitting would be in the water at high tide. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1984,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_5319.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-1984" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The waiter told us they only had to options for food: fish or shrimp. I went with the fish, which I got to pick out from a cooler filled with fish, and Becca had the shrimp. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The food took a while to get but it was really good! There were also a bunch of stray dogs and cats scouring the restaurant for food, some of the little puppies were really cute. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1985,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we walked around the beach for a little longer and then packed up to continue on our way to our hotel in Tunco,  Los Farallones, another hour away. This hotel was super nice and had awesome views of the ocean. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1978,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_1305.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-1978" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our hotel was right on the coast with a couple pools and our room even had its own balcony with a hot tub! We decided to go to the city to eat and get some groceries since we heard the reviews of the hotel restaurant weren’t great. The little city of Tunco was really cool! By far the most touristy part we’ve seen yet, there were also a ton of white Americans we saw for the first time. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ate at a small shop in town, I had a “gyro” that was basically a weird shaped burger in a tortilla and Becca got tacos. Unfortunately we didn’t realize the tacos were fried so they became veggie tacos. Afterwards we ended up grabbing some pupusas in town so that Becca could get some more food. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1976,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_1310.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-1976" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We walked around the town and beach some more and watched the surfers catch some waves. Apparently the world surfing championships are next week so lots of professionals are out here practicing, the top 5 men and top 7 women will get a bid to the Olympics in Japan (the first time surfing will be in the Olympics). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":1977,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/img_1311.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-1977" /><figcaption>Famous rock formation, supposedly looks like an upside down pig</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After exploring the town we made our way back to the hotel and hung out in our hot tub for a bit before watching some more of the god father (it’s a really long movie) and then fell asleep. Tomorrow we’re taking surfing lessons! </p>
