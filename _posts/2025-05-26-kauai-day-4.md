<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we slept in and had breakfast in the room again. Today was a much more chill day with no plans except to relax on the beach. We ended up FaceTiming with Bennett and my parents in the morning and taking our time getting up. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We left around 9am to go to Hanalei beach, which is the big beach right near the main town. We parked sort of in the middle of the beach and walked all the way to the west end and then back to the east where the pier is. There were really big waves near where we parked and much more mellow waves near the pier which is also where all the surfers were, besides a few really good surfers who were way out. </p>

<p>We decided to head back to the beach, this time we parked further east, closer to the pier. We relaxed on the beach some more and went in the water a few times. </p>

<p>We stayed until ~4 a went to the car when a quick rain shower hit (it passed in ~5min). We decided to stop again for some shave ice. This time we tried tropical bliss and rainbow, both were good but the local is still my favorite. </p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We are back at the room and after eating we walked out on the golf course (backyard of the condo area) to see the course and watch the sunset. Right by our place there were a few albatross dancing and clucking their beaks to each other. We found out later that they mate for life and do their mating ritual to each other to strengthen their bonds. </p>
