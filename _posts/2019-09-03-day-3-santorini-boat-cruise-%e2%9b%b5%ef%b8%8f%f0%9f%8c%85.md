Today we woke up around 8:30 And got ready to head off on our boat cruise. The boat left from the old port in Fira so we drove our ATVs ~20min and parked in the town. Since we were there fairly early we were able to get really good parking spots.

While in the city we picked up our tickets from the tour office and then stopped at a small restaurant with a great view of the coast for breakfast. I had eggs and pancakes that were tasty.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190902_094623.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1169" width="2688" height="1512">

After breakfast we made the long walk down to port. It's 587 steps supposedly and the walk wouldn't have been bad besides the fact that the path was filled with tons of donkey poo! Instead of walking you can pay to ride a donkey up or down the steps. It must be very popular. While we were walking down we passed at least a few dozen donkeys heading up.

Once we got to the port we found our boat and boarded. Our first stop was the small island in the middle of the crater called Na Kamena. The whole island formed in the last 400 years so the name literally means new island. The boat ride took ~20min and once at the island we got off and did a short hike around some of the craters. There were also some great views looking back at Santorini.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190902_114745.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1170" width="2160" height="3840">

I will day our boat tour probably had about 50 people and it felt very touristy so it wasn't my favorite, but the tour was cheap so I couldn't really complain. After the island we got back on the boat and we to the back of the island where there is a "hot" springs. We jumped off the boat and swam over to it but in my opinion it wasn't really hot. It also had a lot of mud that people were rubbing all over there body, I guess it's supposed to be good for you?

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_3016.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1167" width="4032" height="3024">

After hanging out at the hot springs for ~30min we loaded back onto the boat to head to our final stop Thirasa. At the island we walked around the port area and found a nice little spot for lunch. I had some meatballs that were served with fries. It was an interesting combination!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/83386465-4926-4dc1-b627-cc5d07bd992e.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1171" width="1440" height="2560">

After lunch we took the boat back to the port in Fira. On the was we went past Oia and skaros rock and we're able to get some nice views from a different perspective. Once we landed we decided to take the donkeys up since we were not feeling like walking back the whole way. The donkey ride was pretty fun actually and was much faster than walking up ourselves.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190902_163848.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1173" width="2160" height="3840"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2366.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1172" width="3088" height="2320">

When we made it to the top our first stop was to get some gelato and then we walked around the city to do some shopping with Sarah, there are a tonnn of small shops throughout the whole city. We also found a great spot to drink a beer and watch the sunset. Honestly I thought the sunset in Fira was better than Oia, it was much less crowded with tourists and had some great views of the city and the water.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2370.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1177" width="4032" height="3024">

After sunset we took our ATVs back to the house. We stopped to drop off our stuff and then went down to the little town square for dinner. I ended up eating some lamb and fried feta cheese with wine. While at dinner we ended up making friends with a British couple that was sitting at the table next to us and ended up talking and drinking with them till nearly 2AM. It was a really fun time! Tomorrow, sadly, we leave Santorini to head to Naxos for a few days.
