<p style="text-align:justify;">Today was a very lazy day. So I'll keep this post short! We woke up late and had the standard scrambled eggs and bread for breakfast and then we laid around in the room until 12 (when we had to check out). We didn't really have anything left we wanted to see so we decided to go to a restaurant on pub street for lunch called warehouse. The service was reallyyy slow (seems to be typical here) but the food was pretty good! We walked around the town markets a little longer and decided to go back to the hotel and chill in the lobby watching tv.</p>

<div style="text-align:justify;"></div>
<div style="text-align:justify;">Finally we got picked up by our tuk tuk driver at 630 who drove us to the airport. Unfortunately we were too early and couldn't check in and get our ticket for a half hour so we had to wait. Once we got through security we had an overpriced ham and cheese panini for dinner that actually wasn't too bad. They also had a Dairy Queen at the airport so we decided to get some blizzards for dessert as well, they were delicious!</div>
<div style="text-align:justify;"></div>
<div style="text-align:justify;">We finally boarded the plane around 915 and were heading to Bangkok!</div>
