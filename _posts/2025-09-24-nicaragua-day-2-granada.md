<p>Today we had our typical Bennett 5:30 am wake up call :). The hotel buffet breakfast opened at 6 am and they had an omelette station today which was tasty. The hotel is square shaped with a big courtyard in the middle and a fountain. The fountain had a bunch of fish in it that Bennett loved looking at.</p>

<p>After the pool, we got dressed and walked to the main square. We tried to go to the top of the main church in the city center, but we had a lot of difficulty finding the entrance. We looked everywhere with no luck and thought it was closed. I called the phone number on google maps to be sure and found out it was indeed open- the ticket office was in a tiny alcove on the back of the church.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After visiting the church we went back to the hotel to relax. We ended up checking out and loading up the car around 10:30am to head towards the city of Granada, a ~2.5 hour drive.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We arrived in Granada around 1pm at the hotel. They were able to hold our bags for us and let us park the car while we waited until 2pm for our room.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We ended up waiting at the hotel pool which was in the courtyard. The pool/courtyard area was beautiful. Bennett loved splashing and blowing bubbles in the pool.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>Our room was finally ready just after 2 - it was actually one of the rooms on the ground floor of the courtyard just steps from the pool. After settling into our room we walked to a nearby restaurant called La Frontera for a late lunch/early dinner. I had a burger that was really good!</p>
