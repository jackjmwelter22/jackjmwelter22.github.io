<p>Day 4 Friday</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up early again and made some eggs for breakfast. Becca and I drove to a different dive shop today called Blue Island Divers so we could dive the Odyssey wreck. It was about ~15min drive north/east up the coast from where we were staying. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The dive shop had a resort vibe to it and was very nice. We met our dive guide, Daniel, who was very nice and knowledgable. He debriefed our first dive which was the Odyssey wreck.</p>

<p>After briefing we got our gear and loaded up onto the boat. This boat was much smaller than Reef Gliders, but it was just us and Daniel on the boat. The dive started in the reef and we swam out towards the bow of the wreck. The dive was 96' max and 61' average depth. We got a bunch of good pictures with the GoPro.</p>

<p>After swimming through the bridge we went up the mast and did our safety stop. Daniel is really into "macro life" - basically really small organisms. He pointed out some really small jelly fish - I literally had no idea they were they until he shined his flashlight on them and pointed them out. There were a bunch of them!</p>

<p>After surfacing we took a quick boat ride back to the dock and did our 1 hour surface interval at the dive resort. The second dive was at a spot called the Moray Channel - Daniel briefed the dive and then we got back on the boat for a quick trip to the site. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The dive was fun - there were a bunch of different channels - Daniel was again very into the macro life - which was cool but got a bit old after he kept turning over little leaves :D. The water at the start of the dive was a deep green until we hit the main channel where it turned deep blue - it was really cool to see!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the dive we got back in the boat and made our way back to the dive shop. The bar at the dive shop had a deal where if you left them a good review they would give you a free drink - so Becca and I both left them a good review! I had the mokey lala drink - a Honduran specialty that is a chocolate/coffee/coconut flavored drink, it was delicious!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After our drinks we drove back to the VRBO to see mom and Bennett. After connecting with them we decided to walk down to Half Moon Bay - just a bit past West End - to go to Loretta's. Unfortunately Loretta's was closed so we ended up getting lunch at an Argentinian grill that was close by instead. The food there was good! I had a steak sandwhich.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating lunch we decided to try the iSoar - which is a boat anchored in half moon bay that has a tall platform you can jump off of or swing off of with a rope (it was named iSoar because the locals thought it was an eye-soar and I guess it stuck hah). To get there we ended up going to the dock where the half moon bay submarine was - the guy there was not very friendly. After checking out the sub, Becca and I swam out to the boat to jump off while my mom hung out with Bennett in the shade on the shore.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The boat was really fun! There were a few other people there and they would rock the boat (quite a lot!) while you jumped. You had to time the rope swing right so that the boat was rising as you were at the bottom of the rope.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After we jumped/swung off a few times we swam back to shore. We walked back down the town and stopped at a few dive shops for Becca to check out dive computers and then met up with Jamie and Nate - now freshly certified SCUBA divers! After meeting up with them we went back to Half Moon bay and hung out on the beach taking some pictures.</p>

<p>Becca and I had planned to do a night dive with Reef Gliders tonight so we left mom, Jamie, Nate, and Bennett at the beach and walked over to the dive shop around 6 and got loaded up onto the boat around 6:30 - just before sunset. The dive site was Chris Fisher's and Blue Channel, our guide Louden briefed the dive before we left.</p>

<p>After the dive we walked back to the VRBO to meet up with everyone else- they ended up going to Ginger's - a restaurant near where we were on the beach in Half Moon Bay. They got Becca and I some food to go which we ate once we were back at the VRBO.</p>
