<p>Today we slept in a little later. We got up around 8 and packed and left the Airbnb and got on the road with the car around 8:30 am. The drive to the coastal city of Paredón was supposed to take about 3.5 hours. The route google had us going was on some pretty spicy roads! Very steep dirt/rock roads. There were a few that were super tight, I’m not sure we were supposed to drive down. Google also tried to get us to take a couple one way roads the wrong direction. </p>

<p>Paredón is a small surf town on the pacific coast of Guatemala. We arrived at our hostel called Cocori lodge, parked the car in their lot and checked in. Unfortunately it was around 12 and our room wasn’t going to be ready until 3 so we walked out to the beach (which has black sand - very hot!) and then went to a nearby restaurant called Pez paredón. The food was decent, I had some tacos and loaded fries.</p>

<p>Once we got our room we unpacked and got settled in. The room is a private villa that is a big circle with one bed downstairs and another upstairs. The upstairs also has a big net that you could lay in, very interesting layout!</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After relaxing we decided to go eat some dinner at the hostel. I got a burrito and fries. They were pretty good. After dinner we hung out a bit more, we walked out of the hostel to a small convenience store called super24 to get some sunscreen, cereal/milk for breakfast, and a few other snacks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We hung out at the room until 8 when the tournament started. Jimmy and I ended up playing pats team first. There were quite a few teams - at least 16. They had the first 3 rounds of “pool play”. Jimmy and I won our first game, got destroyed in the second game by some Guatemalans, and then tied the last game (there was a 4 minute time limit). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We thought there was no chance of making it out of pool play (only the top 8 moved on), but somehow we did! We came back and won our first playoff game and then won the semifinal game handily. The championship game came down to two cups each but we were able to come away with the W!</p>
