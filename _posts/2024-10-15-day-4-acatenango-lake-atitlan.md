<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up at 3 am after a night of very little sleeping - we didn’t have much space and the mattress was super thin and uncomfortable, I maybe got 2-3 hours of sleep.  3am was the planned time to leave for the hike but the visibility was still awful so the guides said that we would wait until 4am to decided. We ended up watching some more of a movie until 4 am and luckily when we got out of our cabin the visibility had started to improve so the guides said we could go. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unfortunately we had to choose either the Acatenango summit or el fuego. We all decided to do the el fuego hike. It was about a half hour downhill and then an hour uphill, really not bad. At the top we waited for sunrise- the visibility was kind of in and out. After sunrise we hiked up a little farther but the visibility just got worse and worse :(.</p>

<p>We ended up getting to the end and taking the van back to the tour house. At the tour house we had a snack and still had to pay for the “extra” fuego hike which was a bit annoying. After we got to our car and started the 2 hour drive to panajachel on lake Atitlan.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The roads were actually pretty good for the most part considering this is Guatemala. However as we got closer they got a bit worse - sections where landslides covered up the road and some dirt roads. There was even a part of the road where you had to drive through a small river! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We hung out and relaxed at the airbnb before going to an Indian restaurant in the main town about 5 minutes away. The restaurant was called Delhi 6 I had the chicken tikka masala and garlic naan it was really good!</p>
