<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up early at 5:20, ate a quick breakfast, then loaded up the car and drove to the Lake Moraine shuttle area about 50 minutes away.  The only way to get to Lake Moraine is by shuttle- they do not allow any private vehicles. We were very relieved we were able to get spots on the shuttle. Lake Louise does allow private vehicles, but you have to get there very early if you want to get a parking spot.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We arrived a little early and were able to get on a shuttle to Lake Moraine without an issue.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>First we climbed the Rock Pile trail. It’s super short- only a few hundred meters. At the top it was absolutely packed with people and photographers. Becca and I took a couple of photos and then got out of there. We made at least one photographer angry for ruining their Timelapse :D.</p>

<p>We continued along the lake shore trail which was flat and only about a mile long, stopping for pictures at various points along the way. This was a very nice hike, and not crowded because everyone else was up at the rock pile. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the end of the trail was a small river that fed the lake from the glacier on top of the mountain. We walked back down the shore a little ways and then stopped to rest. I took out the drone and flew around to get some nice aerial shots of the lake.</p>

<p>After resting for a bit we made our way to the back of big beehive to hike to the devils thumb.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once the uphill started we quickly realized that it would be very difficult/sketchy to bring Bennett along for this hike. Since Becca was already tired we got the walkie talkies out and I went ahead while Becca waited at the base for me to run up to the peak.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hike itself was a lot of scrambling and up some pretty loose scree. I went at a pretty fast pace and it only took about 15-20 minutes for me to make it up to the top (it wasn't very far, just steep!). It was much easier to hike without ~30lbs of Bennett and gear on my back! At the top I took some selfies and flew the drone for a bit to get some really epic shots. Unfortunately there were a bunch of people at the top so getting pictures was kind of a pain.</p>
