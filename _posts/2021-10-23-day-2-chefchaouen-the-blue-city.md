<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 6:45 to get get packed and ready. We had breakfast down stairs at the hotel. It was a buffet style but because of COVID you had to tell the waiter what you wanted. Breakfast was ok, afterwards we grabbed our stuff and met our driver and tour guide in the lobby to start our drive to Chefchaouen. The drive was almost 6 hours, the city is all the way up north right across the water from Spain. At least the van was massive and comfy, it even had usb charging ports at every spot. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2231,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0039.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2231" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our tour guide, Said, was very friendly! The first thing he asked us was if we believe in COVID 😂. Not sure what exactly he meant by that (his English is a little rough). We stopped once on the way at a gas station. Becca and I watched a few episodes of an HBO documentary about a cult church in the neighborhood she grew up in and I read a bit. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got to the city around 2:30, we also got word from my sister that they all had landed and made it into Casablanca! We checked into our hotel and they served us a welcome tea and showed us to our room. The hotel is a Riad which is like a big house with a court yard. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After settling in we went and ate some lunch at a nearby restaurant in the main square called Marisco. Becca and I both got the Tajine, which is like a pot roast served in a hot clay bowl. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2233,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0047.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2233" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The restaurant (and the city in general) has a ton of stray cats and there were a few surrounding our table trying to get some food. After eating we went across the square to the Kasbah, basically a small fortified castle. It cost $6 to enter and was kinda small, but it had a tower you could climb up to get views of the town. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2234,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0058.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2234" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The castle also had a little garden area in the center.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2235,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the castle we decided to walk around the town and find all the recommended photo spots (there were a bunch of them). The whole city is painted blue and the town is like a little maze. It’s really cool! </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There is also a bunch of elevation change in the city so not only are the streets winding all over but they’re also going up and down. After a few pictures of the streets we made our way to the top of the city and hiked to the atlas hotel which was on a hill just above the city. The views were okay but not as cool as the ones in the city. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After a few minutes we hiked back down to the city to take a few more pictures. On the way down we ran into a random herd of goats coming up the street. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2239,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0109.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2239" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>One place we took a picture was a “paid set”. It only cost like $1 US so we decided we might as well do it. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2240,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0119.jpg?w=768" alt="" class="wp-image-2240" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After that we managed to somehow find our way back to the hotel (the GPS signal doesn’t work well in the narrow streets and half the streets don’t show up, so Google maps isn’t very helpful). There we took a little break before heading back out to hike to the Spanish mosque which was about 20 min away to watch the sunset and get some views of the city. On the way to the mosque we stopped at a crepería and I got a Nutella crepe, it was tasty! </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After the sunset we made our way back into town and explored (aka got lost) and eventually made it back to our hotel. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2245,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_0173.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2245" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After changing (it was getting cold out) we went to the main square and found a cafe, hamas cafe, to get a snack and drinks. I had hummus and hot chocolate and Becca had tea and French fries. We sat in the very top of the cafe roof which had a nice view overlooking the square. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Right after we finished, around 9pm, Jamie and her friends all finally arrived! We met them all back at the hotel, they got unpacked, and then our guide met us and took us to dinner. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We went to a place a few minutes away called bab ssour , we all had different types of tajine and we also tried the goat cheese (what this town is known for, they’re also known for their hashsih according to Said 😂). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After dinner it was around 11pm, we made our way back to the hotel and went to sleep. </p>
