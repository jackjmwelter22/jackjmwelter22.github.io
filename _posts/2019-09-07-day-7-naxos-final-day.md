Today I woke up around 7 and had some more Greek yoghurt for breakfast. I watched some Netflix till ~10 when pat and Sarah finally woke up. Today we didn't really have anything planned. One thing we were debating was doing a day trip to Mykonos, however it's about a 2 hour boat ride each way and it really didn't seem like there was anything too unique on the island so we decided it wasn't worth the trip. So instead we decided to explore Naxos some more.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190906_121521.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1256" width="2688" height="1512">

We ended up driving up the northwest coast all the way up to a town called Appollonos (about an hour away) to see another kouros ruins. The ride was very picturesque as usual here and on the way we found a cool looking tower that we stopped at and explored.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190906_124740.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1257" width="2688" height="1512"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190906_134106.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1258" width="3840" height="2160">

We continued our drive to kouros and stopped for some pictures, it was another large guy carved out of marble.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190906_131036.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1262" width="2688" height="1512">

After seeing the Kouros we were going to stop at a mountain town just south called Kronos. However we weren't really hungry yet so we just drove though the town and then kept going all the way down to Prigaki again on the south end of the island. On the way we found a cool church called Pagiani Drossani. One thing about this island is there are tons of little churches everywhere and in the most remote places.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2504.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1249" width="3088" height="2320">

We relaxed at the beach for a while and then decided to go do some more cliff jumping. This time we had Pats GoPro so we shot some fun videos and took more pictures of us jumping. We also met a German couple who was at the cliffs. The guy dove headfirst off the highest spot which was a little crazy since the water wasn't that deep!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/00007xtr_00007_burst20190906171440.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1264" width="2160" height="3840"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/00024xtr_00024_burst20190906171440.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1263" width="2160" height="3840">

Once we got tired of cliff jumping we drove up to plaka beach again to relax some more before the sunset. We napped on the beach for about 3 hours and then drove back drop off scooter and ATV at the rental place. Then we went up to our roof to enjoy one last Naxian sunset. Tonight was clear and the weather was perfect.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2506.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1250" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2507.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1251" width="3024" height="4032">

After the sunset we ate at gyro gyro again and then got some gelatos for desert. Finally we got back to the hotel and hung out and watched some Netflix before sleeping. Tomorrow we head back to Athens :(
