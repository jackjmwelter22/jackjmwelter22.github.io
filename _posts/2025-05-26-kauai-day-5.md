<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today was another relaxing day we slept in, which for me was 6am :D, but to be fair we didn’t actually get up out of bed until 8 ish. We ate breakfast in the room and then got in the car to explore. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This first stop we tried to go to was called the queens bath. It was only about 5 minutes from our Airbnb and unfortunately we found out it was closed when we got there (there surf is too dangerous). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The next beach we tried was a little further down the road called hideaway beach. The parking lot for the beach was only 4 cars and was completely full so we ended up not going to this one either!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Next we decided to drive east about 20 minutes to Kauapea Beach, aka the secret beach. We noticed the light house was right nearby the beach trail so we made a quick stop there only to find out that it was closed as well!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Luckily the lot for the secret beach was open and had parking. To get to the beach we had to hike down for about 5-10minutes. </p>

<p>The beach itself was really nice, there wasn’t many people there and there were a bunch of good surfers. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The beach was relatively small with big rocks on either side. I walked out to the rocks which had good views of the coast. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After relaxing at the beach we hiked back up to the parking lot. There happened to be a right foot sandal sitting at the trailhead (the same side that broke for me) so I took it as a replacement!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We got in the car and drove to the foodland back in princeville for lunch. I had the poke bowl (thankfully they still were in stock!) and Becca had sushi. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating we drove to another beach a little closer east of princeville called Anini beach. This beach was a much longer beach and had more people. The water was also super shallow for a long way off the beach. I ended up going out and snorkeling. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unfortunately I didn’t see anything except for a sea slug and two little fish. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We relaxed in the beach until about 3:30 and then drove to Hanalei for some shaved ice at wishing well. This time Becca got the local and I decided to try a smoothie from their coffee shop next door. The smoothie was banana and peanutbutter, it was pretty good but the local is still the best in my opinion!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After we walked two blocks down to the Tahiti Nui restaurant, we made reservations for 5pm, but they were able to seat us early (4:30). We saw this place in the movie The Descendants with George Clooney so we figured that we’d give it a try. I ended up getting the Tiki Man Pizza which had a bit of everything on it including pineapple, meat, and BBQ and Becca had the traditional Hawaiian pizza, gluten free of course.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>The pizzas were good but a bit small and expensive, but they were tasty. After eating we drove back to the room to shower. I decided to go for a run. I ended up running down to the hideaway beach (it was only 2 miles away). The beach was cool, very small, and some steep steps to get down to. </p>
