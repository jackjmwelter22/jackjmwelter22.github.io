Today we woke up at 8 I made some breakfast eggs and bread. Then we packed up and went down to the square to rent a scooter and ATV. The rental place was pretty slow so we didn't end up getting on the road till a little after 9. The trip to mount Zas was very picturesque and took about 30 min. Zas is the name for Zeus and the reason the mountain is named after him is because there is a cave in the mountain where Zeus was born.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190904_095441.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1198" width="3840" height="2160">

Once we got to the start of the hike we parked our vehicles and started. The trail itself was super beautiful. The beginning of the hike was paved but then quickly turned to shale and we had to scramble our way up. About 15 minutes into the hike we found the cave of Zeus. It was surprisingly large. Another cool thing about the mountain is there were goats climbing all over. They all had bells attached to them so we could hear them jingling.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190904_100715.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1201" width="2160" height="3840"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2417.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1200" width="3088" height="2320"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190904_102639.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1202" width="2160" height="3840"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2421.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1199" width="3088" height="2320">

After the cave we pressed onward. The trail quickly got steeper and the rocks were looser making it more difficult to climb. It was almost like climbing up a dune but instead of sand it was rocks. Eventually we had to actually climb. I went ahead of pat and Sarah since they were taking their time. Soon I was high enough to realize that we went the wrong way and the trail was supposed to go around and then up the mountain ridge. I called pat and Sarah to let them know and decided I would try to keep pressing forward since I already went so far. This was probably not the best idea. The more I climbed the steeper it got and I'm not going to lie there were a few points I got really nervous, but at this now trying to climb down would've been even worse. So I just kept on going making sure all my foot and hand holds were solid and didn't look down! I was also worried I wouldn't be able to actually find a way up from here but luckily I was eventually able to make it to the top!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2425.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1203" width="3088" height="2320">

I ended up waiting at the top for about 45 min for pat and Sarah, who took the longer way. The views at the top were awesome! The weather was clear so we could see everything.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190904_114157.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1204" width="3840" height="2160"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190904_114821.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1205" width="3840" height="2160"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190904_121506.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1206" width="3840" height="2160">

After enjoying the views for a bit we made our back down, this time using the actual trail! We made it back to our vehicles around 1 and decided to head to the nearby town of Filoti to have lunch. We stopped at a local restaurant and I had grilled pork and pita, it was really good.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2444.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1207" width="3088" height="2320">

After lunch we decided to make to quick stops at two historic sites: Bazeos tower and dinitras temple. The tower was more like a little house so we ended up just looking at it from the outside. The temple was very typical Greek ruins but looked cool in the middle of the country side.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190904_142803.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1209" width="2160" height="3840"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_20190904_144600.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1208" width="2688" height="1512">

Once we got back to the Airbnb we changed and drove to agios propios beach which was about 15 min away. This beach was even nicer than the beach from yesterday. We hung out for about 3 hours. I went for a walk down the coast to check out the area.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/img_2445.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1210" width="3024" height="4032">

After the beach we went back to the rooftop of our Airbnb to enjoy another sunset with some wine, Greek yoghurt and conversation. After the sunset we went down to the square to try another restaurant for dinner. I had the house special which was a really good chicken and cheese baked thing, it was tasty. After dinner we walked around a bit before heading back to the Airbnb to hangout then go to bed.
