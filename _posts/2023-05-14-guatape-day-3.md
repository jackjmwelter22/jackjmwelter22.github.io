<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we had a relatively late start and woke up around 6:30am for our 7am pickup for a day tour to guatape. We took a large tour bus with ~30 people. The guide was very punctual which I liked! Our first stop was a cafe called Delicias de la Abuela where we ate breakfast - empanada, scrambled eggs, arepa with cheese, and hot chocolate. It was really good!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/J0tkFQD.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our first stop was the town of Peñol. It was a pretty small basic town, the only notable thing was they had a church that was built into the side of a rock that looked similar to the famous piedra (stone) but smaller. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/wiYogvW.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">The church rock</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our next was the famous piedra. It’s basically a big rock in the middle of a man made lake. It was extremely crowded (likely because it was a weekend). The hike up was about 700 steps and at the top there was a lookout with great views and some shops/restaurants. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The way down from the top took longer than the way up! There were a bunch of out of shape people in front of us who were going extremely slow and there was no room to pass (unlike the stairs going up). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We eventually made it down and met at the meeting point, the rooftop of a restaurant near the base of the rock. The next stop was in the town of Guatape. We walked around the town there was an old church with the inside made all of wood, an umbrella street, and a boardwalk. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After exploring and taking some pictures we got some ice cream and then a few snacks from the grocery store before getting a beer and hanging out at a restaurant near the meeting point. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/PczyGB5.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our next stop was the replica of the old Peñol town. As mentioned the lake was man made. It was made with a hydroelectric damn and in doing so it completely submerged the old town under water so they decided to make a replica of the town. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We had lunch in the old town. It was soup, beef, rice, salad, and a plantain. Not too bad, there was a super cute pup there who begged us for a bite so we obliged. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>After lunch we walked around the town to get a few pictures and then made our way down to the boat for a quick tour of the lake. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/2Cu5GM6.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">The old town replica </figcaption></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The tour of the lake was okay, I could’ve done without it, but we got another view of the piedra and a couple mansions. One peninsula had 3 mansions next to each other one was a famous soccer player the other was his teammate and the third was his ex wife’s (which was the biggest lol!). We also saw the ruins of one of Pablo Escobars mansions. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Fw02qq0.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the boat tour we took the bus back to the hostel. We rested at the hostel for a bit before going out to grab some dinner. We ended up at a nearby taco shop and ordered like eight tacos and some elote to share. It was all really good!</p>
