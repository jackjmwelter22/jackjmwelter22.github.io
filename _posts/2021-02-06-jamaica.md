<p>Well I never ended up doing a blog for Jamaica, but I wanted to make at least one post before I forgot about all the stuff we did. A Hawaii blog will hopefully follow soon. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 1</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We arrived in the airport and made our way through the coronavirus check line (basically they made sure that you had proof of a negative test) and then out through customs. Once everyone made it we picked up our rental car and drove to our airbnb in Negril (about 1.5 hours). Driving in Jamaica took a little bit to get used to, not only does everyone drive super aggressive (to be expected in a developing country) but the cars all drive on the opposite side of the road and the steering wheel is also on the other side! I only messed up by getting in the wrong side of the car a dozen times haha. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our Airbnb was a little sketchy but it was super close to Rick's cafe which was our stop for the night. I forgot to mention that Pat and Sarah drove separately and ended up popping a tire on their way to the Airbnb! Luckily they had a spare and were able to get it changed without too much hassle. Once Pat and Sarah reunited with us at the airbnb we drove 2 minutes down the road to Rick's cafe for dinner and cliff jumping!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

You had to start on the 10' jump to prove that you weren't an idiot and then you were allowed to go higher. Of course I did all of them multiple times!</p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After eating and jumping at Rick's cafe we made our way to a little grocery store to pick up some drinks and snacks for the Airbnb. Once we got back we hung out for a bit and started playing a board game but never finished it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Day 2</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We slept in a bit and then got all our stuff packed up in the cars and drove to nearby cafe for breakfast. I had a spanish omlet which was really tasty. Today was a longer driving day from Negril all the way to our resort in Ocho Rios (3-4 hours). We had plenty of stops planned along the way to break up all the driving. Our first stop was the blue hole, which is creatively named because it is basically a big hole in ground which of course you can jump in.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/C1YETr2.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We jumped in from the edge a few times (which was about 25') and I also did a few jumps from a tree over hanging the hole that was about 35' to the water, it was a ton of fun! Pat jumped with his GoPro on his wrist which proceeded to fall off when he hit the water sinking to the bottom of the hole. Luckily the guides at the blue hole were able to dive down and grab it for him (the bottom of the hole is about 30 feet deep. After jumping one of the guides took us to the roof of the hotel for some nice views. Also inside the blue hole was some 'exfoliating sand' which you could rub on your face, it felt nice afterwards to be honest haha.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/LyQmFOt.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p> After the blue hole we drove through the mountains to our next stop called the roaring river and cave. Unfortunately the cave was closed, we conveniently found this out after we had paid the entry fee. Instead of showing us the cave the "guide" showed us the river (which allegedly Bob Marley would swim in) and then did some sort of weird baptism thing to each of us throwing water on our heads and saying that it had magic powers lol. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/2vGUZx3.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After seeing the river he was going to give us a tour of the local village and 'ganja field'. As we proceeded to follow him we realized we were getting farther and farther away from the cars to we ended up just turning around and leaving.  Our tour guide just seemed super sketchy and the whole experience had a bit of a scam vibe to it, I think if the actual cave was open it would have been more fun.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After quickly driving away from the sketchy river and cave we made our way through even more mountains and tiny little roads to Mayfield falls. On the way there we made a wrong turn and luckily quickly realized our mistake as the road became superrr skinny, after somehow managing to turn around we made it the rest of the way without a hitch. Mayfield falls is a waterfall hike, there were tons of different falls that you got to climb up, under, and around. Overall it was really fun and not crowded at all (probably due to covid).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the waterfall hike we made the final drive to our resort in Ocho Rios. By the time we made it there it had just gotten dark. We were all starving so after checking in and dropping off all our stuff in the room we grabbed dinner at the all you can eat buffet. After dinner we hung out near the main stage for karaoke, Sarah was the only one who sang, she was awesome! The whole rest of the trip random people would come up to her to compliment her on her singing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<p>Day 4</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast Clint, Pat, Sarah, and I decided to go explore a place called secret falls. Basically it's a hike to a waterfall with some place to jump in. Well because of all the rain the night before the water level of the river was SUPER high and the water (which is normally clear blue) was brown from all the runoff coming from the mountains. This made the hike to get to the waterfall extra fun. instead of walking across the river in a few inches of water, the water was up to our thighs and flowing fast! We had to be super careful crossing at some parts so we wouldn't get swept away down the river.</p>

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the rope swing we drove back to the resort to relax and eat. Sarah had brought everyone Christmas light necklaces so we were all very festive! Since it was Friday the 'fancy' restaurants were open so we went to the sushi/asian fusion restaurant. The food was really good! </p>

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After dinner the hotel had a game in the main lobby so of course we tried. The challenge was to use a fishing pole type looking thing (a pole with a string and a ring attached to the end of it) and get the ring around the top of a liquor bottle. It sounds easy but it was actually really difficult. A ton of people attempted it but no one was able to do it. On my first attempt I failed, but my second time around (with a new strategy) I got it and everyone nearby went wild! Sadly the liquor bottle wasn't the prize, the prize was a fanny pack with the name of the hotel on it lol. Jess really wanted it so I just gave it to her.</p>
