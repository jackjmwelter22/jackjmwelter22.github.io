<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Since we were able to fit in all the activities yesterday, we woke up early and had breakfast at 8am (right when they opened) after breakfast I went for a quick hot tub and sauna and then we loaded up and started the drive up to Lahinch (on the west coast of Ireland). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our tee time was originally supposed to be at 2pm, but we were able to call at get an earlier time at 12:45. We arrived at the course around 11:45 and warmed up on the range and putting green. The weather was looking iffy. Right about when we started off the first tee the rain started. It was pretty rough, eventually everything soaked through which made it really difficult to grip and swing the clubs. Needless to say it was a challenging round. We played with two other people James and his wife loretti. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Besides the weather the course was really cool! It was a traditional links style course with lots of thick rough! There were some really cool holes with blind tee shots. One of the par 3 holes had a huge mound in front of the green so you couldn’t see it, so after you hit you had no idea if your ball was on the green or not. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Around 3p the weather finally cleared and it was sunny for the last 5 or so holes, though everything was still soaked it was nice to enjoy the sun. I ended up with a generous 88 for the round. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2141,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_2265.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2141" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After we finished we drove up ~15 min to see the cliffs of Moher. We got there just in time for good weather, we could tell rain was coming in just off the coast. My dad and I hiked to a few view point for some pictures. I wanted roses the final view point on the other side, but my dad was tired so he decided to go back to the car and I went on by myself. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2142,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_2271.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2142" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the other viewpoint there was a trail that continued along the cliffs. It looked really cool so I figured I might as well walk it. After 10 minutes or so I could tell that the trail went way farther than I expected so I checked the map and saw it ended at a separate parking lot. I called my dad and he agreed to meet me there with the car (getting to experience a bit of driving in Ireland himself). Along the way it started raining again (luckily I had an umbrella so I stayed mostly dry). The trail ended near the ruins of a castle towering the cliff. There were some pretty cool views of the cliffs, cows, and even a rainbow after the rain died down again. </p>

<p>I met my dad at the parking lot and we drove to our hotel in Lahinch. We unpacked all our stuff into the room so we could pick everything up for our flight tomorrow. After settling in we went to the hotel restaurant for dinner. It was delicious! I had the steak sandwich and it was huge. We didn’t really eat lunch so I was pretty starving at this point (it was about 7pm). </p>
