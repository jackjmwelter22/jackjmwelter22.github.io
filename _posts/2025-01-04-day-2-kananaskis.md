<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I woke up around 5:30 and started to work on the blog. Around 6:30 Becca and Bennett woke up and we had some breakfast in the room. Unfortunately Bennett had a fever,  so we gave him some Tylenol and then started getting everything packed up (we were moving to a hotel in Banff that day). We waited in the room until 8am to have wifi so we could get the shuttle tickets when they released for Lake Moraine - I used both my phones and iPad while Becca used her phone. Only Becca was successful in getting tickets for Tuesday!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After we secured the tickets we loaded into the car to head to the Smutwood Peak trailhead. Before leaving town we made a quick stop at a pharmacy for some more medication to have on hand for Bennett. The drive to the trailhead was about an hour on an unfinished (gravely and bumpy) mountain road.</p>

<p>This hike started in the forest and was mostly flat. At parts the trail was very tight and a bit difficult to maneuver with Bennett's big pack on my back. It continued around the mountain through a pass (open field) called Birdwood Pass. Bits of the trail were pretty muddy, but overall not too bad.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the hike, we made our way to our resort in Banff. We also called and got dinner reservations (typically its so busy you need to have reservations).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After unpacking in our room we drove into town to have dinner at Bear Street Tavern. They had a Sunday deal - 50% off pizzas and $10 Stein beers (and had gluten free dough!). We both got the big bird pizza and I had a stein of the Tokyo drift IPA. Everything was really good and the atmosphere of the restaurant was nice.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Just before we were getting ready to leave Becca reached over for her bag and spilled about a quarter of my stein all over myself and Bennett (who I was holding at the time)! I have suspicions she did it on purpose :D.</p>

<p>After cleaning up and paying we walked down the town and stopped at a grocery store to get some eggs, bacon, and ice cream. Our room has a kitchenette so its easier for us to cook a more filling breakfast.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bennett fell asleep almost instantly when we got back to the car and drove to our resort. We left him in the car seat sleeping and brought him to the pool/hot tub. This place had TWO big waterslides. Indoor water slides seem to be very popular in Canada.  We hung out in the hot tub and took turns testing out the water slides while Bennett slept. They were both fun but the yellow one was definitely the best.</p>
