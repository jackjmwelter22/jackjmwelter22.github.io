<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we woke up around 7:40 for breakfast at the hotel at 8. Breakfast was good, I had an omelet and fruit. The views of the Mediterranean were awesome. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/ZMZoaNS.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast I went down to the room and got ready before heading into town to SCUBA. Unfortunately Becca’s not allowed to SCUBA so she hung out at the hotel to relax. I surprisingly found a parking spot in the city close to the dock. I got to the boat a little before 9, got all my gear and we were off around 9:30. The boat itself was pretty massive, there were about 25 divers on board and the boat even had its own air compressor on board. Kas is a huge diving town because of how clear the water is, there are about 25 dive companies in the small port of Kas- all with their own dive boats. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/EalcXWC.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Bottom deck of the dive boat</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We cruised for about an hour to the first dive spot. We were going to dive at Bisemi but the current was too strong, so we went a little further to Conan brunu-tunnel. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/qqwdSLu.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Dive map</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Our group was the last to go in, there were 5 of us Rory (England), Ramona and Dorian (Swiss), and our guide Aytun. Everybody was super friendly and nice. Our first dive went smoothly. We were under for about 45 minutes with a max depth of about 105’. The highlight was a swim through- a very large tunnel that was formed by the rocks. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Nb46AY8.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Going through the tunnel</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The current was pretty strong, so I was tired after the dive. Ramona was actually low on air, so Aytun shared some with her at the end of the dive. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the dive we relaxed and had lunch. They made a big spread of rice, salad, chicken, and bread. It was good! At lunch I got to know our group better, apparently Dorian and Romana are road tripping in their camper van for 6 months,  and went all the way from Switzerland to Mongolia (crossing through Russia and Kazakhstan)- wild! Every time I talk to Europeans I’m jealous of how much vacation they get, and how easily they have access to other countries in Europe and Asia. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After lunch we cruised back towards the port and stopped for our next dive at Hevbeli Ada island. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/e4L1naU.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Hanging out on the boat</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/KdTJ4rU.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Dive map for the second dive</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Again our group went in last. This is a much shallower dive than the first with and average depth of 30’ and a max of 60’. There were small pieces of wreckage from a boat. We dove down and around the seagrass and then between the two islands. The main highlight for me was the calamari (squid). We saw a big group of them at the end of the dive. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/vKUPSHY.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the dive we got all of our gear sorted and then cruised back to the port. We got back around 3:30. I went back to the shop to pay (they only took cash) and then hung out with our dive group for about 30 minutes before leaving to meet back up with Becca at the hotel. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/hKzv961.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Port of Kas</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Becca and I hung out at our pool and relaxed for a while. A little before sunset we drove towards the city and made a stop at Antiphellos Ancient City- old ruins of an amphitheater that were cool and had a good view of the sunset. Parking there was super easy and only a 1 minute hike to get to from the car. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/K90gHH7.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We hung out at the top for a bit and I flew the drone around to get some cool shots. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/eOK8frZ.jpg" alt="" /></figure>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After hanging for ~15min we walked back down to the city to get some dinner. We found a cute little restaurant tucked away in an alley called Naturel. The food was really good, we had humus appetizer and I had the falafel balls and lamb ravioli, Becca had the lamb shank. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/j2ps05b.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After dinner we wandered around the town some more and stopped again for some ice cream. We also found a cool pillar looking column (we later found out was an old tomb) in the middle of one of the streets. It’s really cool how preserved some of these sites are and how the town is build around them. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/n6N2Wfw.jpg" alt="" /><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Ignore the rando wanna be influencer in the background</figcaption></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today’s ice cream shop was better than last nights. After eating we walked around the town a little longer before going to the car and heading back to our room to relax (watch some Indiana jones) and then go sleep. </p>
