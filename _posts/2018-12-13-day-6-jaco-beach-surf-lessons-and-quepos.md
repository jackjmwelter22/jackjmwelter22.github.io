This morning I woke up around 7. I decided to go for a run along the beach to get at least some exercise in on this trip. I ran from our condo to the south end of the beach and back around 3.5 miles. Today the weather was again super nice!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1813-1.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1034" height="4032" width="3024">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1814.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1033" height="3024" width="4032">

After the run we all hung out at the condo until 11, when we had to check out. After packing into the car we walked down the main strip to get some lunch. We stopped at a pizza place called totally baked. It was really delicious and the owner was super nice! After lunch we walked a little more around town, got some ice cream, and then went back and chilled at the pool till around 3.

At 3 we drove to the south end of the beach to meet for our surfing lesson! We did a 2 hour sunset lesson where we did some drills on the beach and then went out on the water with an instructor.

surfing actually wasn't that difficult! It definitely helped that the instructor told you exactly when to start paddling and when to pop up on the board. My instructor looked exactly like what I picture a surf instructor to look like, he had super long hair and was from Venezuela. He'd been teaching surfing for over 10 years and had just moved to Costa Rica a year ago. We surfed for about two hours and got to see the sunset at the end of the lesson. The water was actually really warm which was nice.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_20181212_172743.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1035" height="3840" width="2160">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_20181212_172840.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1042" height="2160" width="3840">

After the surf lessons we drove for about an hour to our final stop, Quepos. We arrived at the resort and got settled in our room and then got dinner at one of the restaurants on site. The resort is super nice!
