<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today we got up at 7 and went down for breakfast at 7:30. The hotel we stayed at had a nice spread and a menu to choose a main dish from. I had some cereal and the “full Irish breakfast” which consisted of egg, toast, sausage, bacon, and pudding. It was super filling!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After breakfast we hung out in the hotel and relaxed until 9:30. Then we packed back into the car and drove into Cork to a small castle called the black rock castle observatory. Luckily they weren’t doing tours today but allowed us to go in a climb up the castle by ourselves. It was super tiny but cool, and some nice views of the town/river. As a side note I’m getting much more adjusted with driving on the left and shifting with my left hand after driving through all these little towns. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2106,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the castle we jumped back into the car and drove down to a place called Charles Fort just outside Kinsale. The fort was really cool, like a much nicer looking fort Pulaski. We walked around the perimeter and took some pictures. There were some good views of Kinsale just across the coast. Kinsale is apparently known as a fishing town and there were tons of sail boats in the harbor. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":2107,"sizeSlug":"large"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/img_2032.jpg?w=1024" alt="" class="wp-image-2107" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the fort we drove over to old head golf course. We were originally supposed to play tomorrow morning at 7:45am, but the weather was so good, so we called the course and luckily we were able to play today instead at 12:45. We ended up getting to the course right around 11:45. The drive to the course was really beautiful. The course is on this little peninsula attached to the coast with shear cliffs, very picturesque. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We warmed up at the driving range and putting green. We ended up playing with two other guys, James and Ian, both were super nice and just out to have fun (not serious golfers). My Dad and I both played really well and took a ton of pictures! I shot an 81 and my Dad shot an 82 and I think we each had a birdie. Basically every hole you played was on the edge of a cliff with crazy tee shots and super large changes in elevation. There were so many fun shots to hit! By far the coolest course I’ve ever played. </p>

<p>We ended up taking 5hours to finish, which is really long for my dad and I (as a reference, yesterday took 3 hours), but it really didn’t seem that slow. After the round we hung out on the clubhouse patio and James and Ian showed up with a hurling stick as a souvenir for us. It was superr random, but apparently they’re both huge hurling players/fans and wanted us to have it! They explained the game to us, patently hurling is like a mix of lacrosse, field hockey, basketball, and baseball and is super popular in Ireland (one of the oldest traditional sports. </p>

<p>After hanging out we drove back to Kinsale ~15min away and checked into our b&amp;b and then walked to dinner. We ate at a nearby pub called Kitty O Se’s bar. I had a Muphrys beer and the fish and chips, both were very good! </p>

<p>After dinner we were both tired so we walked back to the b&amp;b to go to bed. One quirk about the place we’re staying is the shower is absolutely tiny you basically have to crouch in to use it. Tomorrow we’re heading up to Killarney national park to do some hiking, at least I’ll be doing some hiking while my dad takes a rest day. </p>
