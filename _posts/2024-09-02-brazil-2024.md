<p>Day 4 Monday 8/26<br>Drive to GIG &amp; return car<br>✈️5:45 am Fly Rio (GIG) to Foz do Iguaçu (IGU) Arrive 9:45 am<br>Pick up car<br>See Iguazu falls Brazil side (9a-6p)<br>Macuco Safari Boat Tour Tickets- has “dry” and wet boat option<br>Hiking (2 miles)<br>Time permitting see Iguazu falls Argentinian side Hike upper &amp; lower falls (4 miles)<br>Parque das Aves ( Bird Park ) 8:30a-4:30p, $15 pp<br>Restaurant: ParkFoz Steakhouse<br>Stay Foz do Iguaçu: Hotel Rafain Centro</p>
