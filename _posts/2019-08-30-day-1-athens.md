The trip from Savannah to Athens was long! First I took a shirt 2 hour flight to JFK and arrived around 9:30. My next flight wasn't till 5 PM 😫 while waiting I watched a few really bad movies, walked around the terminal and listened to some podcasts.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/img_2200.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1095" width="4032" height="3024"><strong>Leaving Savannah</strong>

When it was finally time to board I of course had a very large dude sit next to me and basically squish me into the window for the entirety of the 9ish hour flight...not the most comfortable arrangement! I dosed off a little bit mostly couldn't sleep and watched a few more movies.

The torment finally stopped when we landed early at 9:30am (2:30am EST). Getting though passport control was pretty quick and painless. I took the metro from the airport to the city, almost getting on the wrong train heading out to the country, oops. The trip from the airport took another ~1.5 hours. I was able to get into the Airbnb with no issues and Pat and Sarah arrived shortly after (they stayed in a different Airbnb the night before).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/img_2211.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1098" width="3024" height="4032">

After getting unpacked and a quick rest we made our way out to explore all the sites. First we walked to olympeion, then the Athenain pantehnon, aristotles school lycma. These sites were two ruins and a stadium.<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/img_2215.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1102" width="4032" height="3024">

Then we rented some electric scooters and scooted up to the base of Mount lybcabettus (the highest point in Athens) and took the short hike to the top which had some awesome views of the city. There was also a restaurant there that we had lunch at. I had chicken and potatoes and a beer that was really good and surprisingly cheap!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/img_2908.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1099" width="2576" height="1920">

After lunch we took an Uber to Monastiraki square which seemed to be the epicenter of all the tourist sites and was packed with people. From there we saw Haydrians wall, the ancient agora, and the Roman agora which were all ruins in various stages of restoration.

Finally we walked up to see the acropolis, stopping at a nearby hill on the way for a good view.<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/img_2929.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1100" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/img_2247.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1103" width="4032" height="3024"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/img_2261.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1104" width="3840" height="2160">

The acropolis is where the famous Parthenon is and is on a plateau that is a short hike above the city which also had some great views. The acropolis was destroyed multiple times and the restoration of the site is still ongoing.

After the acropolis we walked back to the Airbnb stopping at the local grocery store for the essentials: wine and gum and then spent some time resting and relaxing.

after about an hour and a half we ubered back up to Monastiraki square. It's interesting to note that all the Uber's here are "Uber taxis" and they use meters like a typical taxi, you just can call them from the Uber app. At the square we went to a popular restaurant know. For their gyros called Bairaktaris. The food was delicious and way more than any of us could even come close to finishing. The nice thing about restaurants in Greece is that the portions are large and the prices are cheap!

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/img_20190830_200607.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1105" width="3840" height="2160">

After dinner we walked around the flea market and then grabbed some gelatos and made it back home to get ready to fly out tomorrow morning to santorini!
