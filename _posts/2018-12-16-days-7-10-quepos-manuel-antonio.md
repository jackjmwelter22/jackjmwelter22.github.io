We didn't do a whole lot at the end of our trip since we were mostly relaxing so I'm combining all the days into one post.

The first day at the resort we got up and had breakfast. It was included with the room and it was a big buffet that was really delicious so naturally I stuffed myself. After breakfast we hung around the pool relaxing. The resort has 3 main pools: 2 big pools for everyone and a more private adult pool.

<img class="wp-image-1051 size-full" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1833.jpg" width="3088" height="2320">

The resort also had a variety of activities to do. We played mini golf, which ended up being pretty competitive (I won of course 😂) we also played tennis and went on the 1 mile walking trail. The trail has a bunch of animals and plants. We saw a lot of monkeys and a few sloths too.

For dinner we ended up driving into the center of Manuel Antonio and went to a place called Samui Thai I had the pad Thai and it was really good. At the end of the meal the waiter ended up giving Sarah his Snapchat handle 😂, I guess he didn't realize her and pat were dating? After dinner we went back and played some board games and crashed in our room.

The next day we had another big breakfast and then got picked up from our hotel to go white water rafting on the naranjo river. Rachel was nervous because this was the same river a bunch of tourists died on two months ago but our guide assured us the conditions were much different. We went with a company called H2O and our guide had been rafting for 9 years all over the world and told us he was going to try out for the Olympics in 2024 when rafting is added.

Overall the rafting was really fun! We did class II-III which was a little to easy for me I think class IV would've been fun but the river was really low so no parts of it were class IV yet.

<img class="wp-image-1046 size-full" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1846.jpg" width="1920" height="1280">

We got back from rafting around noon. Pat, Sarah, and I decided to go check out the nearby beach while Rachel hung out at the pool. The beach was a golf cart ride and a steep 5 min hike. The views were pretty nice but the beach itself was really rocky so after about 20 minutes we ended up going back and hanging out at the pool.

<img class="wp-image-1047 size-full" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1835.jpg" width="4032" height="3024">

For dinner we ended up driving a little farther to a rest called Ronny's which had a great view of the coast and sunset. I had a burger that was really good!

<img class="wp-image-1048 size-full" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1839.jpg" width="4032" height="3024">

Our final day at the resort we had another big breakfast, checked out, and then hung out by the pool till around 2. At 2 we decided to check out the Manuel Antonio Park which had been highly recommended. Our experience was a little different 😂. The minute we got near the park people were flagging us down to try and get us to park in their lot. We ended up parking about 10 minutes from the entrance (we later found out there was free parking right at the entrance 😠). The place to buy tickets was hidden inside a random bank which didn't take credit cards for some reason. The ATM also didn't work for the majority of our cards. Luckily pat was able to get one of his to work and get enough cash out to buy all the tickets.

The park itself was mediocre at best. The real attraction is the beach itself which was nice. We only had about an hour to explore before the park closed so we did the small loop to check out the beaches.

<img class="wp-image-1049 size-full" src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/img_1845.jpg" width="4032" height="3024">

After the park we drove to our Airbnb in San Jose near the airport. The drive back took about 3 hours, but the roads were mostly good. The town our Airbnb was located in was absolutely packed! It also had the most cop cars I've seen so far in Costa Rica, there was one every block. Luckily our Airbnb had a secure parking spot. We ended up showering and getting settled then walking about 3 blocks to a Columbian restaurant our host recommended. The restaurant was a weird mix of flavors (I had a cheese, guacamole, and sausage arepa) but I liked it! After dinner we went back and slept.

We Woke up around 4 the next day to drop off the car and head to the airport to go back to Savannah! Another great adventure in the books! Can't wait for the next one!
