Today we woke up at 8 and got some breakfast downstairs at the hotel. The breakfast was the typical pita with cheese and a hard boiled egg, kinda strange but it was super cheap so can't complain! After breakfast we grabbed our bags and met our driver (the hotel set us up with) to go see the city of Jerash.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3458.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1417" width="2576" height="1920">

Jerash is about an hour north of Amman but our driver was fast so we made it there in 40min. Jerash is known as the "Pompeii of the Middle East" and the "city of 1,000 columns". It's basically a big city that has been around forever and has been inhabited by a bunch of different people, destroyed a few times from attacks and twice from earthquakes. I think the most recent inhabitants were the Romans and you can see their architecture style in all the buildings ruins. There are a bunch of different temples in the city, the main ones being the temples of Zeus and Artemis and there is a main road called the corda that's lined with hundreds of columns that connects everything together. There were also two big amphitheaters and a hippodrome (horse racing track).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2914.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1406" width="4032" height="3024">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3463.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1419" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3470.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1418" width="2576" height="1920">

After exploring the city for 2 hours we met back up with our driver who took us back to Amman and stopped at a famous mosque. Apparently when we got there it was prayer time so we weren't allowed to go in but we did get a picture of the outside (which was enough for me anyway to be honest).

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3472.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1420" width="2576" height="1920">

The mosque had a gift shop attached you had to walk through and the people there were trying really hard to sell you one of their overpriced trinkets. After the mosque our driver took us to the Amman citadel which are famous ruins that sit on top of one of the many hill tops in the city. The ruins were cool a bit more of the same kind we've been seeing so nothing too unique, but the views of the city were really cool. The city is so densely packed into the hills, the organized chaos is really cool.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3474.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1423" width="2576" height="1920"><img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3475.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1422" width="2576" height="1920">

From there we walked down the hill about 10 min to the Roman theater. Before entering we grabbed food at a nearby restaurant that had some kind of gyros. We all ordered 3 different things but they ended up all looking the same😂. We ate our lunch inside the Roman theater, it must be a popular place because there were a bunch of people (tourists and locals) hanging out. After exploring the theater and the attached museums we made our way back to our hotel.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2925.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1405" width="3088" height="2320">

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_3476.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1421" width="2576" height="1920">

We hung out at the hotel and played some cards/rested until dinner time. We went out around 7 for dinner and walked 15min to a place we found on google called wild Jordan. The restaurant had a really nice terrace with a view of the citadel. We all had some delicious smoothies and I had a cheese stuffed chicken. The food was pretty good. After dinner we walked back to the hotel and called it a night.

<img src="https://whereintheworldiswelter.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/img_2928.jpg" class="size-full wp-image-1404" width="3088" height="2320">

Tomorrow we head to Israel by car. Jordan was a super cool country and I definitely recommend it!
