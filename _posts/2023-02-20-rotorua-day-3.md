<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I woke up early so I decided to go for a quick run. There was a really cool trail right by our Airbnb that went by the airport and the lake. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After running Becca, Sarah and I made a quick trip to the grocery store to get some snacks and breakfast. We made some scrambled eggs and yoghurt for breakfast and then loaded up the car again to head to a place to try zorbing at 9am. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I wasn’t expecting much but the zorbing was actually a ton of fun! We ended up doing all 4 runs two were straight down and two were very curvy. We did both solo and double. The double was definitely crazy, you’re not allowed to stand up for the double and for good reason, you’d probably fall on top of each other. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After an hour of zorbing we finished just in time to run next door to the kiwi hatchery for our 10 am tour. The tour itself was okay we got to see a baby kiwi get its daily health checkup and I must admit they are super cute. Our tour guide however was very obnoxious making sure we knew she got to name the kiwi we saw. We did get a few interesting facts. One weird fact is that the kiwi has the smallest beak of any bird, this is because they define beak as the tip of the nose to the nostril, the rest is considered the bill so the kiwis have a tiny “beak”  but a really long bill. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We also got to see a more grown up kiwi running around in the terrarium. Unfortunately no pictures were allowed. This is the only picture we took. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We finished the tour in about 30 minutes and since we had some extra time we decided to make a quick stop at the downhill luge, also next door.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I swear New Zealand has all the coolest activities. The luge was a ton of fun! We only had time for one run but I totally would’ve done more. It’s basically like down hill Mario kart and the best part is there are no rules you can bump into each other as much as you want and go as fast as you want. There were quite a few times I almost flipped the cart or spun out of control. </p>

<p>After the luge we took the gondola back down and then drove to a thermal spring called hells gate. Most of the lakes in this region are old volcanoes whose craters were filled with water after they erupted, due to all the volcanic activity there are a lot of these thermal springs in the area. Hells gate had a ton of muddy thermal springs and about a 2km walk. </p>

After unpacking we went to the grocery store to pick up some lunch and breakfast for tomorrow. After grocery shopping we had some dinner at a place called the tongariro grill. The food wasn’t very good but there was a lot of it. </p>
