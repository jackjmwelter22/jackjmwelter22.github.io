<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Today I woke up around 7 am and Becca and I went upstairs for breakfast a little after 8. Breakfast was eggs, fruit, and coca tea. After breakfast we made our way to a nearby tour shop to try and find some fun activities for the end of our trip, we decided on hiking and ice climbing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the tour shop we went back to the hotel to meet pats and Sarah to catch a taxi over to The Valley of the moon. It’s a cool area about 30 minutes away with a small hiking circuit and really cool rock formations. The whole loop took about an hour and we took a lot of stops for pictures. </p>

<p>While hiking we got a message back from one of the rock climbing tour guides so I called him and we were able to set up some climbing for today at a spot right by valley of the moon. It was going to take the guide 2 hours to get there so we ended up going to the nearby town, calacoto (close to where we were yesterday) for a quick bite to eat. We settled on another meat place. Along the way I tried a sáltena from a street stand (Bolivian version of the empeñada) it was delicious!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The restaurant was called boca y vaca, I had the trout and Becca had a steak, both were good. </p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We finished climbing around 5pm and got a taxi back into town. We made a quick stop at the tour office to pay for our hiking and ice climbing and then walked ~10 minutes to do Urban Rush, basically rappelling face first down a 17 story building!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To start we got dressed in fun costumes, I was Superman Becca was Spider-Man and pat and Sarah were unicorns lol. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"sizeSlug":"large"} -->

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>After the urban rush, we rushed back to our hotel to pack, shower, checkout, and taxi over to the bus station for our overnight bus to the salt flats. We ended up getting to the station at 8pm (per the request of the guy from the company), turns out the bus wasn’t leaving until 9pm so we had some time to kill. Loading the bus was an ordeal to we probably didn’t leave La Paz until 10pm. The bus itself was really nice though! Dinner, good legroom, the seat lean wayyyyy back!</p>
