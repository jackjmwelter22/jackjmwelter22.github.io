Hi, I'm Jack — aerospace engineer who spends every free moment outside or on the move.

I'm an avid skydiver and scuba diver, ultramarathoner, and pilot. When things slow down you'll find me on a golf course or a beach volleyball court.

From backpacking Southeast Asia in 2014 to summiting Kilimanjaro and hiking the Na Pali Coast, this blog follows wherever I end up next. I'm drawn to new experiences, interesting places, and the kind of stories you can only get by actually showing up. I'm always looking to learn something new — whether that's a skill, a culture, or a corner of the world I haven't seen yet.

Always building, always moving, always planning the next one.
